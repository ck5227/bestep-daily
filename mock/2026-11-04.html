閱讀 Reading

1. Students who wish to ______ from a course must do so before the end of the fourth week.

  1. withdraw
  2. withhold
  3. withstand
  4. undertake
看答案與詳解

正解 A　withdraw from a course 是「退選課程」的固定用法，選 (A)。withhold 扣留、withstand 承受，都不接 from a course；undertake 承擔，語意不合。

2. The professor asked us to ______ our sources at the end of the essay so readers can find them.

  1. site
  2. cite
  3. sigh
  4. recite
看答案與詳解

正解 B　cite sources 指「註明引用資料來源」，選 (B)。site（地點）與 cite 同音是主要陷阱；sigh 嘆氣、recite 背誦，皆不合語意。

3. Because of the ______ number of applicants, the dormitory cannot offer rooms to all first-year students.

  1. limited
  2. decreasing
  3. overwhelming
  4. minor
看答案與詳解

正解 C　宿舍無法提供所有新生房間，表示申請人數「多到應付不來」，選 (C) overwhelming。(A)(B)(D) 都表示人數少或減少，與後半句矛盾。

4. Please keep your receipt as ______ of payment in case there is a problem with your registration.

  1. approval
  2. prove
  3. proven
  4. proof
看答案與詳解

正解 D　as proof of payment「作為付款證明」，需要名詞 proof，選 (D)。prove 是動詞、proven 是過去分詞；approval 是名詞但意為「核准」，語意不符。

5. The seminar was so ______ that many students stayed afterward to ask the speaker questions.

  1. boring
  2. tiring
  3. engaging
  4. engaged
看答案與詳解

正解 C　學生會後留下來發問，表示研討會「引人入勝」，選 (C) engaging。(D) engaged 用來形容人「投入的」，不能修飾 seminar；(A)(B) 與後句結果矛盾。

6. All laboratory users must ______ with the safety rules posted on the door.

  1. comply
  2. obey
  3. follow
  4. respect
看答案與詳解

正解 A　comply with 是「遵守」的固定搭配，選 (A)。obey、follow、respect 雖然意思接近，但都是及物動詞，後面不加 with。

7. The student club received a ______ from a local company to cover the cost of its trip.

  1. penalty
  2. refund
  3. debt
  4. sponsorship
看答案與詳解

正解 D　企業提供經費支付旅行費用，是「贊助」sponsorship，選 (D)。refund 是退還已付的錢；penalty 罰款、debt 債務，語意不合。

8. If you are unable to attend the interview, please ______ the office at least 24 hours in advance.

  1. inform to
  2. notify
  3. tell about
  4. announce
看答案與詳解

正解 B　notify + 受詞「通知某人／單位」，選 (B)。inform 後不加 to；tell about 少了受詞；announce 是「公開宣布」，不接人或單位當受詞。

9. The final exam will ______ all the chapters we have studied since the midterm.

  1. consist
  2. include in
  3. contain of
  4. cover
看答案與詳解

正解 D　cover 有「涵蓋（範圍）」之意，選 (D)。consist 須接 of；include 不加 in；contain 不加 of，三者皆為文法錯誤。

10. Free counseling is ______ to all students, regardless of their year or major.

  1. able
  2. capable
  3. available
  4. accessible of
看答案與詳解

正解 C　be available to + 人「提供給某人使用」，選 (C)。able、capable 形容人有能力；accessible 應接 to，不接 of。

11. Tom's essay was rejected because it was too similar to an article online; the teacher suspected ______.

  1. plagiarism
  2. criticism
  3. journalism
  4. tourism
看答案與詳解

正解 A　文章與網路文章太相似，老師懷疑「抄襲」plagiarism，選 (A)。其他三個字同樣以 -ism 結尾，是字形干擾。

12. Exchange students should ______ for a visa at least two months before departure.

  1. supply
  2. apply
  3. reply
  4. imply
看答案與詳解

正解 B　apply for a visa「申請簽證」，選 (B)。supply 供應、reply 回覆、imply 暗示，都是 -ply 結尾的字形干擾。

Students Turn Unused Campus Land into a Community Garden

Last spring, a group of environmental science students noticed that a piece of land behind the Agriculture Building had been empty for years. Led by third-year student Kevin Lu, they asked the university for permission to turn it into a community garden. After two months of discussion, the university agreed, on the condition that the students manage the garden themselves and keep it tidy.

Today, the garden has thirty small plots. Any student or staff member can rent one for NT$300 per semester. The money is used to buy seeds, tools, and a water tank that collects rainwater. Members must spend at least two hours a week working in the garden, and those who fail to do so for a month may lose their plot.

The garden has become more popular than anyone expected. There is currently a waiting list of over fifty people. Kevin says the biggest benefit is not the vegetables but the friendships. "Students from different departments who would never meet in class now share tools and advice," he explains.

The team now hopes to double the size of the garden next year. However, the university has said that part of the land may be needed for a new parking lot, so the plan is still uncertain.

看答案與詳解

正解 B　學校同意的條件是 the students manage the garden themselves and keep it tidy，選 (B)。(A) 租金是會員付給菜園的；(C) 師生都可租；(D)「兩個月」是討論時間，是時間錯置的干擾。

My Semester in Germany: What I Wish I Had Known

When I arrived in Leipzig last September for my exchange semester, I thought my biggest challenge would be the language. I had studied German for a year and could order food and ask for directions. What I did not expect was how different the classes would be.

In Taiwan, most of my courses had a midterm, a final, and regular homework. In Germany, many courses had only one final exam or one long paper at the end. There were no weekly reminders, so I had to plan my own reading schedule. In the first month I fell behind, and I only caught up after a classmate showed me how she divided the reading list week by week.

Another surprise was paperwork. Within two weeks of arrival, every student had to register their address at the city office. Appointments were hard to get, so I waited almost ten days. Without the registration, I could not open a bank account, which meant I paid my rent late in the first month.

My advice to future exchange students is simple: book official appointments before you leave home, and from the first week, treat each course as a long project rather than a series of small tasks. And don't be afraid to ask local students for help. Most of them were happy to explain how things worked.

看答案與詳解

正解 D　作者說原以為語言最難，沒想到課程差異很大：多數課只有一次期末考或一篇報告，選 (D)。(B) 是作者「原本預期」的挑戰，是主要干擾；(A)(C) 未提到。

Campus Shuttle Service: Spring Semester Update

Starting February 17, the university will run a new shuttle route connecting the main campus, the Medical Campus, and Riverside MRT Station. The service is free for anyone with a valid student or staff ID. Visitors may ride for NT$15 per trip, paid by EasyCard only.

On weekdays, buses leave the Main Gate every 20 minutes during rush hours (7:00–9:00 and 17:00–19:00) and every 40 minutes at other times. On Saturdays, the service runs only once an hour, and there is no service on Sundays or national holidays.

Each bus has 30 seats and space for one wheelchair. Bikes are not allowed on board, but there are bike racks at every stop. Because of high demand last semester, the university has added an express bus that goes directly from the Main Gate to the Medical Campus without stopping at the Library stop. The express bus runs on weekday mornings only.

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

Excuse me, do you know when the library closes tonight?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The fair was very crowded.
  2. Thanks, I'm feeling much better now.
  3. Not yet. Is there still time?
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問是否已報名下週的就業博覽會，(C)「還沒，還來得及嗎？」最自然。(A) 用過去式描述博覽會，時間不符；(B) fair 與 feeling 混淆，答非所問。

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  3. Statistics is in room two oh five.

正解 B　Why don't we...? 是提議，不是問原因，(B) 接受並提出時間。(A) 誤把它當成問「為什麼」而用 Because 回答；(C) 只重複 statistics。

4. （請選出最適當的回應）

正解 B　問誰負責借社團開會的教室，(B) 回答人名。(A) 談會議長度；(C) charge 有「收費」之意，誘答談費用，但題目的 in charge of 是「負責」。
