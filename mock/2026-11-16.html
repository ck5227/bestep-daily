閱讀 Reading

1. Students who wish to ______ from the course must do so before the end of the fourth week.

  1. attend
  2. remove
  3. withdraw
  4. retreat
看答案與詳解

正解 C　withdraw from a course 是「退選課程」的固定用法，選 (C)。attend 是出席（不接 from）；remove 是及物動詞，需接受詞；retreat 指撤退，不用於選課。

2. The library has ______ its opening hours during the final exam period, so it now closes at two a.m.

  1. extended
  2. exceeded
  3. expressed
  4. exposed
看答案與詳解

正解 A　延長開放時間用 extend，選 (A)。exceed 超過、express 表達、expose 暴露，字形相近但意思不合。

3. All participants must wear safety glasses ______ they are working in the lab.

  1. unless
  2. although
  3. despite
  4. whenever
看答案與詳解

正解 D　「只要在實驗室工作就必須戴護目鏡」，用 whenever（每當），選 (D)。unless（除非）邏輯相反；although 表讓步不合；despite 是介系詞，後面不能接子句。

4. The professor asked us to ______ our sources carefully, so that readers can find the original articles.

  1. describe
  2. cite
  3. recite
  4. excite
看答案與詳解

正解 B　cite sources 是「註明引用來源」，讓讀者找得到原文，選 (B)。recite 是背誦、excite 是使興奮，皆為音近干擾；describe 描述不符目的。

5. The committee will ______ each application based on the student's grades and a short essay.

  1. elevate
  2. evaluate
  3. evacuate
  4. estimate
看答案與詳解

正解 B　委員會依成績與短文「評估」申請，用 evaluate，選 (B)。elevate 提升、evacuate 撤離、estimate 估計（數量），皆不合。

6. Freshmen are ______ to attend the orientation; those who miss it must make an appointment with their advisor.

  1. forbidden
  2. delayed
  3. prevented
  4. required
看答案與詳解

正解 D　後半句說缺席者必須另約導師，可知新生「必須」參加，be required to，選 (D)。forbidden／prevented 意思相反；delayed 不合語意。

7. The new dormitory is ______ located, only a five-minute walk from both the library and the main cafeteria.

  1. conveniently
  2. comfortably
  3. constantly
  4. carefully
看答案與詳解

正解 A　距圖書館與餐廳都只要走五分鐘，表示「位置便利」，conveniently located，選 (A)。comfortably 舒適地、constantly 不斷地、carefully 小心地，皆不搭配 located。

8. Because the two events ______, students will have to choose between the concert and the debate final.

  1. complete
  2. compete
  3. coincide
  4. collect
看答案與詳解

正解 C　兩個活動「時間撞期」才需二選一，coincide，選 (C)。compete（競爭）字形相近但不表示時間重疊；complete、collect 意思不合。

9. Thanks to a generous ______ from a former student, the art department was able to buy new printers.

  1. donor
  2. dominance
  3. doctrine
  4. donation
看答案與詳解

正解 D　a generous donation from... 「一筆慷慨的捐款」，選 (D)。donor 是捐贈「人」，不能說 a donor from a former student 來買印表機；dominance、doctrine 意思不合。

10. If you are not ______ with your grade, you may ask the instructor to review your exam.

  1. satisfying
  2. satisfied
  3. satisfaction
  4. satisfy
看答案與詳解

正解 B　人「感到滿意」用過去分詞 satisfied，be satisfied with，選 (B)。satisfying 是「令人滿意的」；satisfaction 是名詞；satisfy 是原形動詞。

11. The workshop is open to all students, ______ of their major.

  1. regarded
  2. regarding
  3. regardless
  4. regard
看答案與詳解

正解 C　regardless of 「不論、不管」為固定片語，選 (C)。regarding 意為「關於」，後面不接 of；regarded、regard 詞性不合。

12. The student union plans to ______ a survey to find out what students think about the new meal plan.

  1. carry out
  2. give up
  3. put off
  4. turn down
看答案與詳解

正解 A　carry out a survey「進行調查」，配合目的「了解學生想法」，選 (A)。give up 放棄、put off 延後、turn down 拒絕，都與目的矛盾。

Library's 24-Hour Study Zone Returns for Finals

Last spring, the university library tried something new: during the two weeks of final exams, the first floor stayed open 24 hours a day. The trial was so successful that the library has decided to repeat it this semester, starting December 29.

According to library director Paul Shih, more than 6,000 students used the space between midnight and 6 a.m. during the trial. "We expected students to come late at night, but we were surprised by how many came early in the morning, before their 8 a.m. exams," he said.

Not everything went smoothly. Some students complained about noise, as groups discussing assignments disturbed those who wanted to study silently. This time, the library will divide the floor into two areas: a silent zone near the windows and a discussion zone near the entrance. Security staff will also check student IDs at the door after 10 p.m., since several non-students were found sleeping in the library last spring.

Food will still not be allowed, but drinks in covered cups are permitted. A vending machine selling snacks will be placed outside the main entrance.

Learning to Cook in a Shared Dorm Kitchen

When I moved into the dormitory in my second year, I had never cooked anything more complicated than instant noodles. In my first year, I had eaten every meal at the cafeteria, but after my meal plan ended, I realized I was spending almost half of my monthly budget on food.

Our floor has a small shared kitchen with two stoves and one refrigerator for thirty students. At first, it was frustrating. The fridge was always full, and someone often "borrowed" my eggs. I almost gave up after a week.

Things changed when a senior named Andrea suggested starting a cooking group. Five of us agreed to take turns cooking dinner on weekdays, so each person only had to cook once a week. We shared the cost of ingredients, which turned out to be much cheaper than buying meals alone. Andrea, who had grown up helping in her parents' restaurant, taught us simple dishes like fried rice and tomato egg soup.

Now, a year later, I can cook about fifteen dishes, and my food spending has dropped by about forty percent. More importantly, the cooking group became my closest group of friends. If you are living in a dorm, my advice is simple: don't cook alone.
