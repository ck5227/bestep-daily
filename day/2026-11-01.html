校園網路與數位學習平台

今日單字

expire v. 到期；失效
Your campus password will expire if you don't change it every six months.
upload v. 上傳
The professor uploads the lecture slides to the platform after every class.
verification n. 驗證；核實
Two-step verification makes it much harder for others to use your account.
back up phr. v. 備份
Remember to back up your thesis drafts before your cloud space is deleted.
maintenance n. 維修；維護
The online course system will be down for maintenance on Saturday night.
simultaneously adv. 同時地
Two hundred students tried to log in simultaneously, and the system crashed.
suspend v. 暫停；停權
Your network access may be suspended if you set up a personal router in the dorm.
interfere v. 干擾；妨礙
Personal hotspots can interfere with the campus wireless network.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before installing any software on university computers, students must get ______ from the lab administrator.

  1. admission
  2. permission
  3. submission
  4. commission
看答案與詳解

正解 B　get permission from 表示「取得某人的許可」，選 B。admission 入學／入場、submission 繳交、commission 佣金／委員會，皆不合語意。

2. The online exam system crashed because too many students tried to log in ______.

  1. previously
  2. occasionally
  3. gradually
  4. simultaneously
看答案與詳解

正解 D　系統當機是因為太多學生「同時」登入，選 D simultaneously。previously 先前、occasionally 偶爾、gradually 逐漸，都無法解釋系統過載。

From: Computer and Information Center

To: All students

Subject: New learning platform: what you need to know

Dear Students,

As announced earlier this semester, the university will officially replace our current learning platform, E-Campus, with a new system called LearnHub on November 16. All course materials uploaded by instructors will be transferred automatically. However, personal files you saved in E-Campus, such as notes and draft assignments, will not be moved.

Please download any personal files you wish to keep by November 14. After that date, E-Campus will become read-only, and it will be permanently closed at the end of December.

To help you get familiar with LearnHub, we will hold 30-minute introduction sessions in Room 204 of the library every weekday at noon next week. No registration is required. A short video guide is also available on the university homepage.

Best regards, Computer and Information Center

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To ask instructors to upload course materials
  2. To report a security problem with E-Campus
  3. To announce a system change and explain what students should do
  4. To recruit students to lead training sessions
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信件宣布 E-Campus 將由 LearnHub 取代，並說明學生需下載個人檔案、可參加說明會，選 C。教材會自動轉移，不需教師上傳；未提資安問題或招募。

4. What should students do by November 14?

  1. Sign up for an introduction session
  2. Save their personal files from E-Campus
  3. Upload their assignments to LearnHub
  4. Watch the video guide on the homepage
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第三段要求在 11 月 14 日前下載想保留的個人檔案，選 B。說明會不需報名；影片只是可參考的資源，沒有期限。

5. The phrase "get familiar with" in paragraph 4 is closest in meaning to

  1. become related to
  2. become popular with
  3. become worried about
  4. become used to
看答案與詳解

正解 D　get familiar with 指「熟悉、習慣」新系統，與 become used to 最接近，選 D。familiar 雖與 family 同源，但此處不是「有親屬關係」。

Faster Wi-Fi Arrives, But So Do New Rules

After years of complaints about slow and unstable connections, students returning to campus this month may notice a real difference. Over the summer and early fall, the Computer and Information Center replaced more than 600 old wireless access points across classrooms, the library, and all six dormitories. According to the center, average download speeds have nearly tripled, and the number of dropped connections reported to the help desk fell by 70 percent in October compared with the same month last year.

The upgrade was partly driven by the growing number of courses that use online tools. "Many professors now run live quizzes and discussion boards during class," explained Dr. Lin Mei-hua, director of the center. "When two hundred students connect at the same time in a lecture hall, the old network simply couldn't handle it."

However, faster Wi-Fi comes with new rules. To protect the network, students can now connect no more than three devices at once with a single account. Personal routers and hotspots are no longer allowed in dormitories, as they interfere with the new system. Students who break these rules may have their network access suspended for up to two weeks.

Reactions have been mixed. Chen Yu-ting, a junior in the Department of Foreign Languages, welcomed the change. "I used to go to a café to upload videos for my group projects. Now I can do it from my room," she said. Others are less pleased. Some graduate students say the three-device limit is unfair to those who use extra computers for research. The center says it will review the policy next semester and invites feedback through an online survey that is open until November 30.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A network upgrade and the rules that came with it
  2. Why students prefer cafés for group work
  3. How professors design online quizzes
  4. A survey of graduate students' research needs
看答案與詳解

正解 A　全文介紹校園無線網路升級成效與隨之而來的新規定，標題也點出主旨，選 A。咖啡廳、線上測驗、問卷都只是文中細節。

7. According to Dr. Lin, why was the upgrade needed?

  1. Dormitory students were using too many routers.
  2. Online activities in large classes overloaded the old network.
  3. The help desk did not have enough staff.
  4. The old access points were damaged by a typhoon.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　林主任說大教室兩百位學生同時連線做線上活動時，舊網路無法負荷，選 B。路由器是升級後的新規定，並非升級原因；人力與颱風未被提及。

8. What may happen to students who use personal routers in dormitories?

  1. They will have to pay a fine.
  2. Their devices will be taken away.
  3. They may lose network access for a period of time.
  4. They will have to attend a training session.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第三段說違規者網路權限可能被停用最多兩週，選 C。文中沒有罰款、沒收設備或上課的處分。

9. Which TWO statements are true according to the article? (Choose TWO.)

  1. More than 600 access points were replaced.
  2. Download speeds have roughly doubled.
  3. All students support the new rules.
  4. The center plans to review the device limit.
  5. The online survey closes at the end of December.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　第一段提到更換超過 600 個無線基地台（A）；最後一段說中心下學期會檢討三台裝置的限制（D）。速度是近三倍而非兩倍，反應褒貶不一，問卷 11 月 30 日截止。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, I logged my study hours in my notebook.
  2. Your password probably expired. You have to reset it every six months.
  3. The Wi-Fi café near the gate closes at nine.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問為何無法登入校園 Wi-Fi，B 指出密碼可能過期需重設，最合理。A 重複 log 但意思是「記錄」；C 重複 Wi-Fi 卻答非所問。

聽力原文

Do you know why I can't log in to the campus Wi-Fi this morning?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Not yet, but she said they'd be up by tonight.
  2. The slide on the playground is being repaired.
  3. I'd rather wait on the upper platform.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問教授是否已上傳講義，A 回答還沒但今晚會上傳，切題。B 的 slide 指溜滑梯；C 的 platform 指月台，都是同字異義干擾。

聽力原文

Has the professor uploaded the lecture slides to the learning platform yet?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I made the same error in my math homework, too.
  2. Yes, my assignment is to write a message to a pen pal.
  3. Your file might be too large. Try compressing it first.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　對方說線上繳交作業一直出現錯誤訊息，C 建議檔案可能太大、先壓縮，是合理回應。A 重複 error（計算錯誤）；B 重複 assignment 和 message 但無關。

聽力原文

I keep getting an error message when I try to submit my assignment online.

4. Why can't the man access the journal databases?

  1. His laptop is too old to run the VPN.
  2. His school email account has been locked.
  3. The databases are under maintenance.
  4. He is still using the default VPN setting.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女子說預設設定只涵蓋學校信箱，男子回答他什麼都沒改，可知原因是仍用預設設定，選 D。對話未提到電腦太舊、帳號被鎖或資料庫維修。

聽力原文

Hi, is this the IT help desk? I installed the campus VPN on my laptop, but I still can't open the journal databases from home. Did you choose the full tunnel option when you connected? The default setting only covers your school email. Oh, I didn't change anything. Where do I find that? Click the gear icon in the VPN app, and you'll see it under Connection Mode.

5. When will the man most likely take the quiz?

  1. On Wednesday
  2. Tonight
  3. On Sunday at midnight
  4. After the typhoon holiday
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男子最後說週末整天有社團活動，所以「最好今晚就做」，選 B。週三是原本的開放日，週日午夜是截止時間，颱風假是提前的原因，皆非他作答的時間。

聽力原文

Kevin, did you see the notice on the course platform? The statistics quiz is already open. Really? I thought it opened on Wednesday. It was supposed to, but the professor moved it up because of the typhoon holiday next week. It closes Sunday at midnight. Then I'd better do it tonight. I have a club event all weekend.

6. What will still be available during the maintenance?

  1. Wired connections in the computer center
  2. Wi-Fi in the library
  3. The online course system
  4. Wi-Fi in the dormitories
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播明確說電腦中心的有線網路仍可正常使用，選 A。圖書館與宿舍 Wi-Fi、線上課程系統都會暫停。

聽力原文

Attention, all students. The campus network will undergo scheduled maintenance this Saturday from eleven p.m. until five a.m. on Sunday. During this time, Wi-Fi in the dormitories and the library will be unavailable, and the online course system cannot be accessed. Wired connections in the computer center will still work as usual. If you have an assignment due this weekend, please submit it before Saturday evening. We apologize for any inconvenience.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A computer science professor
  2. A staff member of the computer center
  3. A librarian
  4. A student representative
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者自我介紹是 Computer and Information Center 的 Daniel，為電算中心人員，選 B。辦公室在圖書館大樓一樓，但他不是圖書館員。

8. Why does the speaker suggest installing the app this week?

  1. The app will stop being free next month.
  2. All passwords will expire next month.
  3. The help desk will be very busy near the deadline.
  4. The Wi-Fi system will be shut down.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說若等到截止日才安裝，服務台會非常忙碌，選 C。其他選項（收費、密碼過期、關閉 Wi-Fi）都未提及。

9. According to the talk, which TWO statements are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. One account is used for several campus services.
  2. Students get unlimited cloud storage.
  3. Cloud files are kept for six months after graduation.
  4. The help desk is located in a dormitory.
  5. Two-step verification will be optional.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　一個帳號可用於 Wi-Fi、信箱和學習平台（A）；雲端空間在畢業後六個月才刪除（C）。空間是 100GB 非無限，辦公室在圖書館大樓，雙重驗證是「必須」。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to this short workshop on our digital learning tools. I'm Daniel from the Computer and Information Center. First, every student has a single account that works for Wi-Fi, email, and the online learning platform. So if you change your password in one place, it changes everywhere. Second, let's talk about security. Starting next month, the school will require two-step verification. After you type your password, you'll need to approve the login on your phone. Please install the authenticator app this week, because if you wait until the deadline, our help desk will be extremely busy. Finally, a reminder about storage. Each of you has one hundred gigabytes of free cloud space, but it is deleted six months after you graduate. So remember to back up anything important, such as thesis drafts or project files, before you leave. If you need help, drop by our office on the first floor of the library building, or send us a message through the online chat.
