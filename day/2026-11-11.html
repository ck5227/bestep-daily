校園交通與單車規範

今日單字

permit n. 許可證；通行證
You need a parking permit to leave your scooter in this area.
tow v. 拖吊
Cars parked in the fire lane will be towed at the owner's expense.
abandoned adj. 被丟棄的；廢棄的
The office removes abandoned bicycles every semester.
reclaim v. 取回；領回
You can reclaim your bike with a valid student ID.
commute v. 通勤
Many staff members commute from nearby towns every day.
repave v. 重新鋪路
The road near the main gate will be closed while it is repaved.
inconvenience n. 不便
We apologize for any inconvenience caused by the road work.
peak hours n. phr. 尖峰時段
The shuttle runs every ten minutes during peak hours.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who park their scooters in fire lanes may have their vehicles ______ away at their own expense.

  1. delivered
  2. repaired
  3. towed
  4. reported
看答案與詳解

正解 C　停在消防通道的機車會被「拖吊」（towed away）且費用自付，C 正確。deliver（遞送）、repair（修理）、report（報告）都不能與 away 及 at their own expense 搭配成合理語意。

2. Because the shuttle bus is often crowded during rush hour, the university plans to ______ the number of buses on the Gongguan route.

  1. decline
  2. increase
  3. delay
  4. assume
看答案與詳解

正解 B　尖峰時段太擠，合理做法是「增加」班車數，B 正確。decline 作及物動詞是「婉拒」，delay（延誤）、assume（假定）語意都不合。

From: General Affairs Office

To: All Students

Subject: Removal of Abandoned Bicycles

Dear students,

Every semester, hundreds of bicycles are left unused in campus parking areas, taking up space that other riders need. To keep our bike racks available, the General Affairs Office will remove abandoned bicycles from all campus parking areas on December 3.

Between now and November 27, staff will attach yellow tags to bicycles that appear to have been abandoned, such as those with flat tires, rusty chains, or no lock. If your bike has been tagged but you still use it, simply tear off the tag. Bikes that still have a tag on December 3 will be moved to the storage area behind the Physical Education Building.

Removed bikes can be reclaimed until December 31 with a valid student ID. After that, they will be repaired by student volunteers and donated to new international students.

Thank you for helping keep our campus tidy.

General Affairs Office

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To advertise used bicycles for sale
  2. To introduce a new bicycle parking fee
  3. To ask for volunteers to repair bikes
  4. To announce the removal of unused bicycles
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中說明 12 月 3 日將清除校內廢棄腳踏車，故選 D。信中沒有販售或收費；志工修車只是後續處理，並非寫信目的。

4. What should a student do if their bike has been tagged but they still use it?

  1. Contact the General Affairs Office
  2. Remove the tag from the bike
  3. Move the bike to the storage area
  4. Show a student ID by December 3
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第三段說若車被貼標籤但仍在使用，只要撕掉標籤即可，故選 B。A、C、D 都不是信中要求的做法；學生證是 12 月 31 日前領回被移走的車時才需要。

5. The word "reclaimed" in paragraph 4 is closest in meaning to

  1. recycled
  2. repaired
  3. got back
  4. reported
看答案與詳解

正解 C　reclaim 指「取回、領回」，後文說憑學生證可在 12 月 31 日前辦理，故選 C。recycled（回收再利用）與 repaired（修理）是之後才發生的處理方式，容易混淆。

Fewer Cars, More Bikes: Campus Unveils Three-Year Transportation Plan

Last Wednesday, the university announced a three-year plan to make the campus safer and greener by reducing the number of cars on its roads. Under the plan, the large parking lot next to the main gate will gradually be turned into a green space with seating areas, and visitor parking will be moved to a new underground garage on the edge of campus.

The decision follows a survey conducted last spring by the Student Association. Of the more than two thousand students who responded, sixty-two percent said they felt unsafe walking on campus during busy hours, and nearly half reported having almost been hit by a car or scooter at least once. "These roads were designed decades ago, when far fewer people came to campus," explained Professor Chen Wei-ting of the Department of Urban Planning, who helped draft the plan.

To make up for the lost parking spaces, the university will add four kilometers of protected bike lanes and replace its diesel shuttle buses with electric ones. During peak hours, the shuttle will also run every ten minutes instead of every twenty.

Not everyone is pleased, however. Some staff members who commute from nearby towns worry that the changes will make their daily trips longer, especially on rainy days. "Not everyone can ride a bike for forty minutes," said one administrative assistant. In response, the university says it will offer discounted public transportation passes to employees and keep a limited number of parking spaces for people with disabilities and staff with special needs.

The first stage of the plan, the new bike lanes, is expected to be completed by next September.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A survey on students' favorite ways to travel
  2. The history of the campus road system
  3. A university plan to reduce car traffic on campus
  4. A protest by staff against new parking fees
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文介紹學校減少校內汽車的三年交通計畫，故選 C。問卷只是計畫背景；道路歷史只是教授的一句話；文中沒有抗議或停車費。

7. According to the survey, what did sixty-two percent of the students say?

  1. They felt unsafe walking on campus during busy hours.
  2. They had almost been hit by a car or scooter.
  3. They would like more parking spaces.
  4. They usually ride bikes to campus.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第二段指出 62% 學生表示尖峰時段在校內步行感到不安全，故選 A。B 是「將近一半」學生的經驗，不是 62%；C、D 未提及。

8. Why are some staff members unhappy with the plan?

  1. They will have to pay more for parking.
  2. They dislike the new electric shuttle buses.
  3. They think the bike lanes are too narrow.
  4. Their trips to work may take longer.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第四段說從鄰近城鎮通勤的職員擔心每天通勤時間變長，故選 D。文中沒提停車費上漲、電動接駁車或自行車道寬度的不滿。

9. According to the article, which TWO things will the university do? Choose TWO.

  1. Ban scooters from campus
  2. Build an underground garage for visitors
  3. Raise parking fees for students
  4. Close the main gate to all vehicles
  5. Make the shuttle run more often during peak hours
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　第一段提到訪客停車將移至新建地下停車場，第三段說尖峰接駁車由 20 分鐘改為 10 分鐘一班，故選 B、E。禁騎機車、調漲學生停車費、關閉校門都沒有提到。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The park closes at six.
  2. Yes, I really enjoy riding bikes.
  3. There's a bike rack behind the east entrance.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問圖書館附近哪裡能停腳踏車，C 給出具體位置最合理。A 用 park（公園）一字多義混淆；B 以 Yes 回答 Where 問句，答非所問。

聽力原文

Where can I park my bike near the library?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Only on Saturdays, and less often.
  2. I usually run in the morning.
  3. The shuttle was full of students.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問接駁車週末是否行駛，A 說明只在週六且班次較少，直接回答。B 重複 run 字面混淆；C 只描述接駁車很擠，沒有回答週末是否有車。

聽力原文

Does the campus shuttle run on weekends?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Did you buy it online?
  2. Oh no, how much is the fine?
  3. The scooter area is fine.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　對方說機車停錯位置被開罰單，B 詢問罰款金額是自然回應。A 把 ticket 誤解為「票券」；C 重複 fine 的另一個意思「很好」，語意不合。

聽力原文

I just got a ticket for parking my scooter in the wrong area.

4. Why can't the woman get a car parking permit?

  1. She applied after the deadline.
  2. She lives too close to campus.
  3. She doesn't have a driver's license.
  4. All the permits have been sold.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男子說汽車停車證只給住 30 公里以外或有醫療需求的學生，女子只住 10 公里遠，故選 B。對話未提截止日、駕照或售罄。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to apply for a car parking permit for this semester. Sorry, student car permits are only for students who live more than thirty kilometers away, or who have a medical reason. Oh. I live in the city, about ten kilometers from here. Then I'd suggest a scooter permit, or the free shuttle from the MRT station.

5. What will the speakers do first?

  1. Walk to the station by the gym.
  2. Check an app for available bikes.
  3. Take the campus shuttle.
  4. Wait at the dorm station.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男子提議出門前先用 app 查即時車輛數，女子同意，故先做 B。走到體育館站只是沒車時的備案；接駁車與在宿舍站等待都沒提到。

聽力原文

Are you taking a YouBike to the night market tonight? I was going to, but the station by the dorm was empty an hour ago. The app shows how many bikes are available in real time. Let's check it before we leave. Good idea. If there aren't any, we can walk to the station by the gym.

6. What will change during the road work?

  1. The location of a shuttle bus stop
  2. The shuttle bus schedule
  3. The opening hours of the main gate
  4. Whether bicycles are allowed on campus
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播說施工期間接駁車改停運動場旁的臨時站，故選 A。時刻表、校門開放時間都沒改；腳踏車只是改走湖邊步道，並未禁止。

聽力原文

Attention, all students and staff. Starting Monday, November sixteenth, the road between the Engineering Building and the main gate will be closed for repaving. The work is expected to last two weeks. During this period, campus shuttle buses will stop at a temporary stop beside the sports field instead of in front of the Engineering Building. Cyclists should use the path along the lake. We apologize for any inconvenience.

7. Why is the officer giving this talk?

  1. To announce a new shuttle bus route
  2. To recruit student safety volunteers
  3. Because bicycle accidents have increased
  4. Because many bikes have been stolen
看答案與詳解

正解 C　林警官開頭說校內腳踏車事故比去年幾乎倍增，因此來說明，故選 C。接駁車、招募志工、失竊皆未提及。

8. What will happen to a student who is caught using a phone while riding for the first time?

  1. They will receive a warning.
  2. They will pay a fine of three hundred dollars.
  3. They will lose their parking permit.
  4. They will have to attend a safety class.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者說第一次邊騎邊用手機會被警告，之後才罰 300 元，故選 A。B 是再犯的處罰；C、D 沒提到。

9. Which TWO are new rules mentioned in the talk? Choose TWO.

  1. Riders must wear helmets.
  2. There will be a speed limit of fifteen kilometers per hour.
  3. Bikes will not be allowed near the library.
  4. Bikes must have a working front light after six p.m.
  5. All bikes must be registered with Campus Security.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　新規定包括時速 15 公里限速與晚上六點後須有前燈，故選 B、D。安全帽、禁止靠近圖書館、登記車輛都不是講者說的規定；圖書館只是事故多發地點。

聽力原文

Good evening, everyone. I'm Officer Lin from the Campus Security Office. I'm here tonight because the number of bicycle accidents on campus has nearly doubled since last year. Most of them happen on the slope near the library, especially in the evening when students are rushing to class. So, starting next month, we're introducing a few new rules. First, the speed limit for bikes and scooters on campus will be fifteen kilometers per hour. Second, riding while using your phone, even just to check a map, will result in a warning the first time and a fine of three hundred dollars after that. And third, all bikes must have a working front light after six p.m. We know these rules may feel strict, but our goal isn't to punish anyone. In fact, this Friday from noon to four, we'll be giving out free bike lights in front of the Student Center. Just bring your student ID. If you have any questions, you can email our office or stop by our booth on Friday.
