宿舍生活與室友相處

今日單字

top up phr. v. 儲值；加值
I need to top up my meter card before the electricity runs out.
quiet hours n. 安靜時段
Quiet hours in our dorm start at eleven p.m.
leak v. 漏（水、氣）
The air conditioner has been leaking water since last night.
extension cord n. 延長線
Extension cords without safety switches are not allowed in the dorm.
claim v. 領取；認領
You can claim your package at the housing office with your student ID.
drawback n. 缺點；不利之處
One drawback of living off campus is the long commute.
questionnaire n. 問卷
Each freshman completed a questionnaire about their sleeping habits.
expand v. 擴大；擴展
The office may expand the program to all dormitories next year.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who wish to stay in the dorm over winter break must ______ an application to the housing office by December first.

  1. admit
  2. permit
  3. submit
  4. commit
看答案與詳解

正解 C　submit an application 是「提交申請」的固定搭配。admit 承認／准入、permit 允許、commit 承諾，都不能接 application 表示繳交。

2. The new dorm rules are intended to ______ conflicts between roommates by setting clear expectations from the start.

  1. reduce
  2. refuse
  3. reflect
  4. remind
看答案與詳解

正解 A　一開始就訂好明確規範，目的是「減少」室友衝突，reduce 最合適。refuse 拒絕、reflect 反映、remind 提醒，語意不通。

From: Housing Office

To: All Dormitory Residents

Subject: Changes to package pickup starting November 4

Dear Residents,

Because of the growing number of online orders, the front desk is no longer able to store packages for residents. Starting Wednesday, November 4, all deliveries will be sent to the new smart lockers next to the convenience store in Building A.

When your package arrives, you will receive a text message with a six-digit pickup code. Packages must be collected within 72 hours. Items that remain after this period will be moved to the housing office, where they can be claimed on weekdays between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. with a valid student ID.

Please note that the lockers cannot hold refrigerated items or packages larger than 60 centimeters on any side. These will still be delivered to the front desk. We appreciate your patience during this transition.

Housing Office

3. Why is the housing office making this change?

  1. The front desk staff are changing their working hours
  2. The front desk can no longer store all the packages
  3. Students complained about lost packages
  4. The convenience store asked for more space
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第一段指出網購包裹太多，櫃台已無法再保管，故選 B。工作時間、遺失包裹與便利商店需求都未提及。

4. What happens if a package is not picked up within 72 hours?

  1. It is returned to the sender
  2. It stays in the locker for another day
  3. It is moved to the housing office
  4. The resident receives a new pickup code
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段：超過 72 小時未領的包裹會移至宿舍辦公室，故選 C。退回寄件人、多放一天、重發取件碼都沒有出現。

5. The word "claimed" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. announced
  2. argued
  3. examined
  4. collected
看答案與詳解

正解 D　此處 claimed 指憑學生證「領取」包裹，與 collected 同義。claim 雖也有「聲稱」之意，但 announced、argued、examined 都不符文意。

Mixing It Up: Dorms Test Cross-Major Roommate Program

This semester, the university's housing office launched a pilot program that places first-year students with roommates from different departments. In the past, students were usually matched with classmates from the same major, which made it easy to share notes and textbooks. However, a survey last spring found that many students felt their social circles were too small by the end of their first year.

Under the new system, about two hundred freshmen in Building D were randomly matched with roommates from other colleges. An engineering student, for example, might share a room with someone studying literature or nursing. Before moving in, each student completed a short questionnaire about sleeping habits, cleanliness, and study routines, so that roommates would at least have similar daily schedules.

Early feedback has been mostly positive. "At first I was worried we'd have nothing to talk about," said Lin Yu-ting, a business major whose roommate studies physics. "But now we explain our homework to each other, and I've learned more about science than I ever expected." Some students, however, say the arrangement has drawbacks. Several told reporters that it is harder to form study groups when no one on their floor takes the same courses.

Housing director Dr. Chen Ming-hui said the office will review the results in January before deciding whether to expand the program to all dormitories. "We're not trying to replace academic communities," she explained. "We simply want students to meet people they would never meet in class." Students who wish to change roommates can still apply through the usual room-swap process.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A survey about students' sleeping habits
  2. The opening of a new dormitory building
  3. A new way of matching roommates in the dorms
  4. A study group program for freshmen
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文介紹讓新生與不同科系同學同寢的試辦計畫，故選 C。睡眠習慣只是問卷內容之一；D 棟並非新建；讀書會反而是計畫的缺點。

7. Why did students fill out a questionnaire before moving in?

  1. To choose which building they wanted to live in
  2. To make sure roommates had similar daily routines
  3. To report problems with previous roommates
  4. To request a roommate from their own major
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段：問卷是為了讓室友至少有相近的作息（so that roommates would at least have similar daily schedules）。其餘選項文中未提，且同科系配對正是舊制。

8. What drawback of the program does the article mention?

  1. It is harder to form study groups.
  2. Rooms in Building D cost more.
  3. Students are not allowed to change roommates.
  4. Roommates often argue about cleanliness.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第三段指出同樓層沒人修相同課程，較難組讀書會。費用未提；最後一段說仍可申請換寢；清潔只是問卷項目，非爭執。

9. Which TWO statements are true according to the article? Choose TWO.

  1. Lin Yu-ting's roommate is a physics student.
  2. The program currently includes every first-year student.
  3. Students in the program cannot use the room-swap process.
  4. The idea came from a request by engineering professors.
  5. The housing office will review the results in January.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、E　A：林同學的室友讀物理；E：宿舍辦公室將於一月檢討成果。B 錯，只有 D 棟約兩百名新生；C 錯，仍可申請換寢；D 錯，起因是去年春天的學生問卷。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The room is a little too bright at night.
  2. I'll pay attention to the schedule in class.
  3. Yes, I topped up the meter card last night.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問宿舍電費是否已繳，C 表示昨晚已儲值電卡，最合理。A 重複 room 卻答非所問；B 用 pay 字面混淆（pay attention）。

聽力原文

Have you paid the electricity fee for our dorm room yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Two of the washing machines are being repaired.
  2. I usually do my laundry on weekends.
  3. It's close to the elevator.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問洗衣間為何關閉，A 說明洗衣機正在維修，回答了原因。B 重複 laundry 但沒回答；C 用 close（靠近）與 closed（關閉）的音近混淆。

聽力原文

Why is the laundry room on the third floor closed today?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The exam room is on the fourth floor.
  2. Sorry, I'll use my headphones instead.
  3. I don't mind the morning class.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　對方請求把音樂關小聲，B 道歉並改用耳機，是合理回應。A 重複 exam、C 重複 mind 與 morning，都答非所問。

聽力原文

Would you mind turning your music down a little? I have an exam tomorrow morning.

4. What will the man probably do next?

  1. Fix the air conditioner himself
  2. Submit a repair request for the woman
  3. Help her reset her website password
  4. Move her to another room
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男子說 I'll submit it for you right now，即替她送出報修單。修冷氣的是明天來的技師；網站問題與換房都沒有提到要處理。

聽力原文

Hi, the air conditioner in room four oh six has been leaking water since last night. Sorry about that. Did you fill out the online repair form? I tried, but the dorm website keeps logging me out. Okay, I'll submit it for you right now. A technician should come by tomorrow afternoon.

5. Why does the woman want to move?

  1. The dorm is too expensive
  2. Her room is too far from campus
  3. She wants to live alone
  4. She and her roommate keep different hours
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女子說她和室友作息完全不同（十點睡 vs. 讀到兩點），故選 D。說「便宜」的是男子在比較租屋；距離與獨居都沒提到。

聽力原文

I heard you're thinking of moving out of the dorm next semester. Yeah. My roommate and I have totally different schedules. She goes to bed at ten, and I usually study until two. Have you asked the housing office about a room swap? It's much cheaper than renting an apartment. Not yet. I'll stop by on Friday.

6. Where should residents gather during the drill?

  1. In the main lobby
  2. At the nearest staircase
  3. In the parking lot behind the building
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播說樓長會在大樓後方停車場點名，故選 C。樓梯只是離開的路線，不是集合地點；大廳未被提及。

聽力原文

Attention, residents of Building C. A fire safety drill will take place this Thursday at nine p.m. When the alarm sounds, please leave by the nearest staircase. Do not use the elevators. Floor leaders will check attendance in the parking lot behind the building. Residents who are absent without a good reason may lose points on their dorm record.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To update residents on several dorm matters
  2. To welcome new residents to the dorm
  3. To explain how to apply for a room change
  4. To announce the results of a survey
看答案與詳解

正解 A　舍監依序提醒安靜時段、房間檢查與健身房問卷三件事，屬於多項宿舍事務更新。這是期中會議而非迎新；問卷仍在進行，尚無結果。

8. What does the speaker suggest residents do if they need to make calls late at night?

  1. Make them before eleven p.m.
  2. Keep their voices low in the hallway
  3. Use headphones in their rooms
  4. Go to the study lounge on the ground floor
看答案與詳解

正解 D　原文 please use the study lounge on the ground floor。走廊正是被抱怨的地點；耳機與十一點前通話都沒提到。

9. According to the speaker, which TWO statements are true? Choose TWO.

  1. Residents must be present during room inspections.
  2. Extension cords without safety switches are not allowed.
  3. The gym is already open twenty-four hours a day.
  4. The survey closes this Sunday.
  5. Room inspections will begin next semester.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　B：無安全開關的延長線因有火災風險而禁止；D：問卷本週日截止。A 錯，不需在房內；C 錯，24 小時開放只是提案；E 錯，檢查在 11 月 17 日，下學期指的是健身房可能的改變。

聽力原文

Good evening, everyone, and thanks for coming to our mid-semester floor meeting. I'm Grace, your resident advisor. First, a reminder about quiet hours. From eleven p.m. to seven a.m., please keep noise to a minimum. We've had several complaints about loud video calls in the hallway, so if you need to talk late at night, please use the study lounge on the ground floor. Second, room inspections will be held on November seventeenth. We'll be checking for things like electric cookers and extension cords without safety switches. These aren't allowed because they're a fire risk. You don't need to be in your room during the inspection, but please make sure your desk and floor are clear. Finally, the housing office is collecting opinions on a plan to keep the dorm gym open twenty-four hours a day. The survey link is in our floor group chat, and it closes this Sunday. If enough people respond, the change could start as early as next semester.
