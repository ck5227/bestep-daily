助教時間與課後提問

今日單字

consult v. 請教；諮詢
You should consult your TA before you rewrite the whole report.
drop by phr. v. 順道拜訪；臨時過去
Feel free to drop by my office if you have a quick question.
reserve v. 預約；預訂
Each student may reserve one slot per week on the course page.
in advance phr. 事先；提前
Please email me at least a day in advance if you need an online meeting.
clash v. （時間）衝突
My lab clashes with the TA's office hours on Friday.
specific adj. 具體的；明確的
Bring specific questions so we can use the time well.
pilot program n. 試辦計畫
The university will launch a pilot program for evening tutoring.
graded adj. 已評分的；批改過的
Bring your graded midterm if you want to discuss your score.

閱讀 Reading

1. If you have questions about the lab report, feel free to ______ the TA during her office hours.

  1. contain
  2. convert
  3. consult
  4. consume
看答案與詳解

正解 C　consult 意為「請教、諮詢」，consult the TA 指向助教請教，符合語意。contain 包含、convert 轉換、consume 消耗，皆不合。

2. Students who cannot attend in person may request a virtual meeting, ______ they email the TA at least one day in advance.

  1. unless
  2. provided
  3. although
  4. whereas
看答案與詳解

正解 B　provided（that）意為「只要、前提是」，表示提前一天寄信是申請線上會面的條件。unless 意思相反；although、whereas 表讓步或對比，不合邏輯。

From: Ting-Yu Wu (TA, CHEM 210)

To: CHEM 210 Students

Subject: Office hours before the second quiz

Dear all,

With the second quiz coming up on November 12, I expect office hours to be much busier than usual. To keep things fair, I will start using a sign-up sheet next Monday. Each student may reserve one twenty-minute slot per week through the course page. Walk-in visitors are still welcome, but students with reservations will be seen first.

Please note that I will not answer questions about quiz content by email after 6 p.m. on November 11. This is to make sure everyone has the same access to help. If your question is urgent, post it on the class discussion board, where the other TAs can also respond.

Best,

Ting-Yu

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To cancel the second quiz
  2. To introduce a new TA
  3. To ask students to grade each other's work
  4. To explain a new way office hours will be organized
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中主要說明下週起改用登記表預約時段、預約者優先，即 office hours 的新安排，故選 D。A 小考照常舉行；B、C 未提及。

4. The word "reserve" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. book
  2. protect
  3. delay
  4. refuse
看答案與詳解

正解 A　reserve one twenty-minute slot 指「預約」一個時段，與 book 同義。protect 保護、delay 延後、refuse 拒絕，皆不合上下文。

5. What should a student with an urgent question do on the evening of November 11?

  1. Email Ting-Yu directly
  2. Walk into her office without a reservation
  3. Post the question on the class discussion board
  4. Wait until the quiz is over
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中說 11 月 11 日晚上 6 點後不再以 email 回答，急件請貼到討論區，故選 C。A 正是被禁止的方式；B、D 信中未建議。

Empty Rooms, Full Inboxes: Why Students Skip Office Hours

Every week, teaching assistants on our campus spend about three hours in their offices waiting for students. According to a survey conducted by the Student Learning Center last month, many of them wait alone. Of the 1,200 undergraduates who responded, only 23 percent said they had attended office hours at least once this semester.

The reasons students gave were surprisingly consistent. Nearly half said the times clashed with their other classes or part-time jobs. Another common answer was embarrassment. "I don't want the TA to think I'm not smart enough," one second-year engineering student explained. Others said they were unsure what office hours were for, believing they were only meant for students who were failing.

Meanwhile, TAs report the opposite problem in their inboxes. Wang Yi-Chen, a graduate student who assists in two accounting courses, receives up to forty emails a week. "Many of these questions would take two minutes to answer in person but twenty minutes to explain in writing," she said.

To close this gap, the Learning Center will launch a pilot program next semester. Selected courses will offer evening drop-in sessions in the library, as well as small group sessions in which several students work through problems together. The center also plans to send short videos to first-year students showing what happens during a typical visit. Instructors who want their courses to join the program can apply through the center by December 15.

"Office hours are not a punishment," said center director Dr. Lisa Huang. "They are one of the most effective learning tools we have, and they're already included in your tuition."

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. How graduate students are chosen as TAs
  2. Why few students use office hours and how the university plans to respond
  3. Whether tuition should be reduced
  4. Differences between engineering and accounting courses
看答案與詳解

正解 B　文章先以調查說明學生少去 office hours 的原因，再介紹學習中心的試辦計畫，故選 B。其他選項只是文中細節或未提及。

7. Why does Wang Yi-Chen mention "two minutes" and "twenty minutes"?

  1. To show that answering questions in person is more efficient than by email
  2. To complain that her pay is too low
  3. To explain how long each office-hour visit lasts
  4. To point out that students often arrive late
看答案與詳解

正解 A　她指出當面兩分鐘能答的問題，用文字要解釋二十分鐘，說明當面問比 email 有效率，故選 A。B、C、D 文中都沒有提到。

8. The word "clashed" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. agreed
  2. ended
  3. crashed
  4. conflicted
看答案與詳解

正解 D　times clashed with their other classes 指時間與其他課「衝突」，同 conflicted。crashed 字形相近但意為撞擊、當機，是常見陷阱。

9. Which are part of the Learning Center's pilot program? Choose TWO.

  1. Required visits for students who are failing
  2. Evening drop-in sessions in the library
  3. Lower tuition for participating students
  4. Replacing email questions with online chat
  5. Small group sessions for working through problems
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　第四段提到試辦計畫包括圖書館晚間免預約時段與小組解題時段，故選 B、E。A 與文中「不是懲罰」的精神相反；C、D 未提及（學費只是說已包含在內）。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. He's still working on his officer training.
  2. No, he moved them to Thursday because of a faculty meeting.
  3. This week's homework is due on Wednesday.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問助教這週時間是否仍在週三下午，B 說改到週四並給理由，最合理。A 用 officer 與 office 音近混淆；C 重複 Wednesday 但談作業期限。

聽力原文

Are Kevin's office hours still on Wednesday afternoon this week?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Sure, let's look at your work together.
  2. I think three points is a fair price.
  3. You can explain it to the whole class tomorrow.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　學生請助教解釋第三題為何扣分，A「我們一起看你的作答」直接回應。B 重複 three/points 但談價格；C 重複 explain 卻把解釋責任丟給學生。

聽力原文

Could you explain why I lost points on question three?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The TA dropped the course last semester.
  2. You can mail the package at the post office downstairs.
  3. Dropping by is fine. She prefers questions face to face anyway.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　說話者猶豫要寄信還是直接去找助教，C 建議直接去並說明助教偏好當面問。A 用 dropped 與 drop by 混淆；B 用 mail 與 email 音近混淆。

聽力原文

I'm not sure whether I should email the TA or just drop by.

4. What will the man most likely do next?

  1. Go to room four oh two on Friday
  2. Email Jason to arrange an online meeting
  3. Switch to section A permanently
  4. Skip his chemistry lab
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生週五有實驗課無法去 402 室，女助教建議寄信給 Jason，他會安排線上會面，故選 B。A 與他週五有課衝突；C、D 對話中皆未提及。

聽力原文

Hi, is this the office hour for Calculus Two? I'm in section B. It is, but on Tuesdays I only cover section A. Your TA, Jason, holds his office hours on Fridays in room four oh two. Oh no. I have a chemistry lab every Friday afternoon. Then just send him an email. He usually sets up online meetings for students with schedule conflicts.

5. What is the woman's problem?

  1. She missed the assignment deadline.
  2. She left her code at home.
  3. Her file won't open in the lab software.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女生說程式在筆電上，但檔案在實驗室軟體中打不開，故選 C。B 錯，她有帶筆電與程式；A 對話未提截止日。

聽力原文

Thanks for meeting me. I'm stuck on the regression assignment. No problem. Did you bring your code? It's on my laptop, but the file won't open in the lab software. That's usually a version problem. Save it as a plain text file and try again. If that doesn't work, we'll go through the logic on paper.

6. What should students bring if they want to discuss their score?

  1. Their student ID card
  2. A written grade-change request
  3. Their textbook
  4. Their graded midterm
看答案與詳解

正解 D　廣播說想討論分數者請帶「批改過的期中考卷」，故選 D。B 是申請改分時才需書面提交，並非討論分數要帶的東西；A、C 未提及。

聽力原文

Attention, students in Introduction to Economics. Because of the midterm review session on Thursday, all TA office hours this week will move to the Learning Commons on the second floor of the library. Sessions will run from two to five in the afternoon. If you would like to discuss your score, please bring your graded midterm with you. Requests for grade changes must be submitted in writing by next Monday.

7. Why is the speaker giving this talk?

  1. The location of her office hours has changed.
  2. Many students contacted her after midterm grades were released.
  3. Professor Chang will be away next week.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　開頭說昨天公布期中成績後收到很多信，所以要說明 office hours 怎麼運作，故選 B。A 地點並未改變；C 未提及教授不在。

8. What can students do if they cannot attend the regular hours?

  1. Email Professor Chang for a meeting
  2. Visit the Science Building at any time
  3. Ask a classmate to attend for them
  4. Book a short online slot on the course website
看答案與詳解

正解 D　助教說若無法前來，可透過課程網站預約 15 分鐘線上時段，故選 D。A 寄給教授是用於成績申訴；B「任何時間」不正確。

9. What does the speaker advise students to do? Choose TWO.

  1. Point to the exact step where they got lost
  2. Make an appointment for regular office hours
  3. Bring their notes and the work they have done
  4. Ask her to change their midterm score
  5. Email her their grade complaints
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　助教建議指出卡住的具體題目或步驟，並帶筆記與已完成的作業，故選 A、C。B 錯，正常時段不需預約；D 她明說不能改分；E 申訴要交給張教授。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. Since midterm grades were posted yesterday, I've received a lot of emails, so I'd like to take a few minutes to explain how office hours work in this course. First, my regular hours are Monday and Wednesday from three to four thirty, in room two fifteen of the Science Building. You don't need an appointment for those times. If you can't make it, you can book a fifteen-minute online slot through the course website. Second, please come prepared. Office hours are most useful when you bring specific questions. Instead of saying, I don't understand chapter five, try to point to the exact problem or step where you got lost. Also, bring your notes and any work you've already done. That way, I can see how you're thinking, not just where you ended up. Finally, a word about grades. I'm happy to go over your midterm with you, but I can't change any scores during office hours. If you believe a question was graded incorrectly, write a short explanation and submit it to Professor Chang within one week.
