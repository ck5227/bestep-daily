BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-12
校園防災演練與地震應變

今日單字

evacuate v. 撤離；疏散
Everyone had to evacuate the library when the alarm went off.
assembly point n. 集合點
Please go to the assembly point on the basketball courts after the drill.
confiscate v. 沒收
The staff confiscated two electric cooking pots during the inspection.
in advance phr. 事先；預先
The exact time of the drill will not be announced in advance.
warden n. （樓層）引導員；管理員
Floor wardens help guide classmates to the nearest stairway.
inspection n. 檢查；視察
There will be a fire safety inspection of all dorm rooms next week.
extinguisher n. 滅火器
Do you know where the nearest fire extinguisher is in this building?
make-up session n. 補辦場次；補課
Students who miss the drill must attend the make-up session.

閱讀 Reading

1. When the fire alarm sounds, all students must ______ the building within three minutes.

  1. evaluate
  2. elevate
  3. evacuate
  4. eliminate
看答案與詳解

正解 C　evacuate 意為「撤離、疏散」，符合火警時離開大樓的情境，選 C。evaluate 評估、elevate 提升、eliminate 消除，皆不合語意。

2. To keep the drill realistic, the exact time will not be announced in ______, so please stay alert all week.

  1. progress
  2. addition
  3. general
  4. advance
看答案與詳解

正解 D　in advance 意為「事先」，事先不公布時間才需整週保持警覺，選 D。in progress 進行中、in addition 此外、in general 一般而言，皆不合語意。

From: Student Housing Office

To: All Residents of Dorm 3

Subject: Fire Safety Inspection and Night Drill

Dear residents,

To keep everyone safe during the winter months, staff will carry out a fire safety inspection of all rooms in Dorm 3 on November 18 and 19, between 2 p.m. and 6 p.m. You do not need to be in your room, but please make sure the area around your desk and bed is tidy enough for staff to check electrical outlets.

The following items are not allowed in rooms: electric cooking pots, heaters, candles, and extension cords without an on/off switch. Any of these items found during the inspection will be confiscated and kept at the Housing Office until the end of the semester. Residents with more than one item will also lose one point from their dorm conduct score.

In addition, a fire drill will be held on the night of November 20 at 10 p.m. All residents must take part. Anyone who cannot attend for a good reason must join the make-up session on November 27 at 7 p.m.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Student Housing Office

3. What is the main purpose of this email?

  1. To inform residents about an inspection and a drill
  2. To ask residents to buy new extension cords
  3. To announce new rules for dorm visitors
  4. To explain how to cook safely in the dorm
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信中說明 11/18–19 的消防安全檢查與 11/20 的夜間演練，選 A。信中並未要求購買延長線，也未提訪客或烹飪方法。

4. What will happen to a resident who has a heater and a candle in the room?

  1. They will be asked to move to another dorm.
  2. They must attend the make-up drill on November 27.
  3. They will pay a fine to the Housing Office.
  4. They will lose the items until the semester ends and lose a conduct point.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　違禁物會被沒收保管到學期末，且超過一件者扣宿舍操行一分；暖爐加蠟燭共兩件，選 D。補演練是給缺席者，信中未提罰款或換宿舍。

5. The word "confiscated" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. repaired
  2. taken away
  3. returned
  4. tested
看答案與詳解

正解 B　物品被 confiscated 後「保管在宿舍辦公室直到學期末」，可知意為「沒收、拿走」，選 B。修理、歸還、測試都與上下文不符。

Beyond the Drill: Do Students Really Know What to Do?

Every year, almost every student on campus takes part in the national earthquake drill. They crawl under desks, wait for the signal, and walk calmly to the sports field. But a new survey by the Student Association suggests that taking part is not the same as being prepared.

The survey asked 600 students about emergency safety. While 85 percent said they had joined at least one drill in the past year, only 35 percent could name the correct assembly point for their own dormitory. Fewer than one in four knew where the nearest fire extinguisher was in the building where they have most of their classes. "The drill feels like a ceremony," one sophomore wrote. "We know the exact time, so we just follow the crowd."

Safety experts say this is a common problem. "When people practice only in one place at one time, they learn a routine, not a skill," explains Dr. Grace Wu, who teaches disaster management. "Real emergencies happen at night, in the library, or in the shower."

In response, the Campus Safety Office plans several changes for next semester. First, at least one drill will be held without an announced time. Second, the campus app will add a map that shows each user the nearest exit and assembly point based on their location. Finally, the office hopes to train student floor wardens in every building.

Not everyone is pleased. Some professors worry that surprise drills will interrupt exams. The Safety Office says drills will not take place during the official exam weeks, and it will collect feedback after each one.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Why the national earthquake drill should be canceled
  2. How a professor became an expert in disaster management
  3. Why taking part in drills may not prepare students enough
  4. How the Student Association conducts its surveys
看答案與詳解

正解 C　文章開頭即指出「參加演練不等於準備好了」，並以調查與專家意見支持，再介紹改善措施，選 C。文中並未主張取消演練，A、B、D 皆為細節或離題。

7. According to the survey, what did most students NOT know?

  1. The date of the national drill
  2. The correct assembly point for their dormitory
  3. How to crawl under a desk
  4. The name of the Student Association president
看答案與詳解

正解 B　只有 35% 學生能說出自己宿舍的正確集合點，表示多數人不知道，選 B。文中說學生其實知道演練確切時間；C、D 未提。

8. What does Dr. Wu suggest about drills held at a fixed time and place?

  1. They teach students a habit rather than a real ability.
  2. They are the best way to prepare for emergencies.
  3. They should only take place at night.
  4. They are more effective than online training.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　吳博士說固定時間地點的練習只會學到「例行程序，而非技能」，選 A。她舉夜間、圖書館等例子是說明真實情況多變，並非主張只在夜間演練。

9. Which changes does the Campus Safety Office plan to make? Choose TWO.

  1. Holding drills during exam weeks
  2. Holding at least one drill without announcing the time
  3. Giving extra credit to students who join drills
  4. Adding a location-based exit map to the campus app
  5. Moving the assembly point away from the sports field
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　第四段提到將舉行至少一次不預告時間的演練，以及在校園 App 加入依位置顯示最近出口的地圖，選 B、D。文中明言考試週不演練；加分與改集合點皆未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I drilled a hole in the wall yesterday.
  2. Thursday morning, right after second period.
  3. Yes, the earthquake last night was pretty strong.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問地震演練何時舉行，B 給出具體時間最合理。A 用 drill（鑽孔）一字多義混淆；C 以 Yes 回答 When 問句，且談的是真實地震。

聽力原文

When is the earthquake drill this week?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. No, always use the stairs when you evacuate.
  2. The elevator is on the left side of the hall.
  3. Sure, I'll take it down to the copy shop later.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問疏散時能否搭電梯，A 直接回答應走樓梯，符合防災常識。B 只說電梯位置，答非所問；C 重複 take…down 製造混淆。

聽力原文

Should we take the elevator down during the evacuation?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The music was really beautiful.
  2. Yes, I'll set my alarm for seven.
  3. You should report that to the safety office.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　對方說練琴室聽不到警報，C 建議向安全辦公室反映最恰當。A 重複 music 但離題；B 把 alarm 誤當鬧鐘。

聽力原文

I couldn't hear the alarm in the music practice room.

4. Why was the dorm's assembly point changed?

  1. The basketball courts are closer to the main gate.
  2. The sports field became too crowded.
  3. The sports field is under repair.
  4. Last year's drill was canceled.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男子說去年操場太擠、點名花了快一小時，故改到籃球場，選 B。對話未提大門距離、整修或演練取消。

聽力原文

Excuse me, where do dorm residents gather after the drill? The sports field, right? Not this year. Our dorm meets on the basketball courts now. The sports field is only for the classroom buildings. Really? Why did they change it? Last year the field got way too crowded, and it took almost an hour to count everyone.

5. What will the man probably do this weekend?

  1. Join a drill in another building
  2. Visit the dentist
  3. Meet with Professor Lin
  4. Complete an online safety module and quiz
看答案與詳解

正解 D　教授說可用線上課程和小考補足，男子回答這週末會完成，選 D。看牙是星期四，並非週末；對話未提其他大樓演練或再約教授。

聽力原文

Professor Lin, I have a dental appointment on Thursday morning, so I'll miss the drill. Is that a problem? The drill counts toward your safety training requirement. But you can make it up by finishing the online module and the short quiz by next Friday. Great, thanks. I'll get it done this weekend.

6. What should people do when they hear the alarm this morning?

  1. Leave the building by the nearest stairs
  2. Stay inside unless told it is not a drill
  3. Go to the fourth floor to check the system
  4. Turn off all laboratory equipment
看答案與詳解

正解 B　廣播說這只是測試、不需離開大樓，除非聽到「這不是演練」才撤離，選 B。A 是真實警報時才做；C、D 廣播未提。

聽力原文

Attention, everyone in the Engineering Building. At ten fifteen this morning, the fire alarm system on the fourth floor will be tested. You may hear the alarm several times over about twenty minutes. This is only a test, so there is no need to leave the building. However, if you hear an announcement saying "This is not a drill," please leave immediately by the nearest stairway.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To report damage from a recent earthquake
  2. To announce changes to the class schedule
  3. To explain how the campus earthquake drill will work
  4. To recruit new members for the Red Cross
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者介紹演練時間、避難三步驟、集合與點名方式及樓層引導員招募，主旨是說明演練流程，選 C。紅十字會只是提供訓練，並非招募其會員。

8. Why should students bring their student ID?

  1. To open the gate to the assembly area
  2. So that attendance can be recorded
  3. To borrow an emergency kit
  4. To sign up as a floor warden
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說會在集合點掃描學生證點名，選 B。報名引導員是上網填表，與學生證無關；A、C 未提及。

9. What do floor wardens receive? Choose TWO.

  1. Priority course registration
  2. First aid training
  3. A free emergency kit
  4. Service-learning credit hours
  5. A discount at the campus bookstore
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　講者說引導員可接受紅十字會急救訓練，並獲得八小時服務學習時數，選 B、D。選課優先、急救包與書店折扣皆未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Kevin Chen from the Campus Safety Office. Because Taiwan has so many earthquakes, every student will take part in the national drill next Thursday at nine twenty-one in the morning. When you hear the alarm, remember three steps: drop, cover, and hold on. Get under a desk, protect your head and neck, and hold on until the shaking stops. Please don't run outside while the building is still shaking. Most injuries happen from falling objects near doorways and windows. After the shaking stops, your teacher will lead you to the assembly point shown on the map next to each classroom door. Leave your bags behind, but do bring your phone and your student ID, because we'll record attendance by scanning ID cards at the assembly point. This year we're also adding something new. Volunteers can sign up to become floor wardens, who help guide classmates out of the building. Wardens receive first aid training from the Red Cross and earn eight hours of service-learning credit. If you're interested, fill out the form on our website by this Friday.
