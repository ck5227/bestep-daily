校外租屋與租約須知

今日單字

lease n. 租約
Read every line of the lease before you sign it.
security deposit n. 押金；保證金
The landlord returned my security deposit after checking the room.
tenant n. 房客；承租人
All tenants must keep the hallway clear of bikes and boxes.
utilities n. 水電瓦斯等公用事業費
The rent is NT$7,000 a month, not including utilities.
ventilation n. 通風
A gas water heater needs good ventilation to be used safely.
scam n. 詐騙
The cheap apartment online turned out to be a scam.
subsidy n. 補助（金）
She applied for a rent subsidy from the city government.
pass on (to) phr. v. 轉嫁（費用）給
The landlord promised not to pass the repair costs on to us.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before signing the lease, Mia asked the landlord to ______ the broken window, and he agreed to do it within a week.

  1. require
  2. reply
  3. repair
  4. remain
看答案與詳解

正解 C　破掉的窗戶需要「修理」，repair 符合語意。require 要求、reply 回覆（需接 to）、remain 保持，皆不合。

2. Tenants who wish to end their lease early must give the landlord at least one month's written ______.

  1. notion
  2. notice
  3. notation
  4. nominee
看答案與詳解

正解 B　give written notice 為「書面通知」的固定說法。notion 觀念、notation 符號、nominee 被提名人，字形相近但語意不符。

From: Daniel Chen <dchen.rentals@mail.com>

To: Tenants of Building B, Lane 32

Subject: Water Heater Replacement and Rent Reminder

Dear tenants,

I'm writing to let you know that the old gas water heaters in Building B will be replaced with electric ones next Tuesday, December 1. A technician will need to enter each unit between 9 a.m. and noon. If you will not be home, please let me know by Sunday so that I can arrange for the building manager to let the technician in. Hot water will not be available while the work is being done.

This change was made after the fire department's inspection last month. Although the new heaters will cost a little more to run, they are much safer, and I have decided not to pass the extra cost on to you this school year.

Also, a reminder that December rent is due on the fifth. Please transfer the payment to the same account as before and send me a screenshot.

Best regards,

Daniel Chen

3. Why are the water heaters being replaced?

  1. Tenants complained about cold water.
  2. The old heaters cost too much to run.
  3. An inspection raised safety concerns.
  4. The power company required it.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段說更換是在消防局檢查之後，且新熱水器 much safer，可知是安全考量（C）。信中說新的反而較耗費用（B 錯），沒提到房客抱怨或電力公司。

4. What should tenants who will be away on Tuesday do?

  1. Leave their doors unlocked
  2. Reschedule the work for the afternoon
  3. Pay the technician in advance
  4. Inform Mr. Chen by Sunday
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中寫 If you will not be home, please let me know by Sunday（D），房東會請管理員開門，所以不用留門（A 錯）；沒有改時間或預付費用的說法。

5. The phrase "pass the extra cost on to" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. make someone else pay
  2. hide something from
  3. explain something to
  4. share a profit with
看答案與詳解

正解 A　pass the cost on to someone 指「把費用轉嫁給某人」，房東決定不讓房客負擔多出的電費，即 make someone else pay（A）。

Rising Rents Push Students Farther From Campus

As the fall semester nears its end, many students at Kaiyuan University are already searching for apartments for next year. This year, however, the hunt has become more stressful. According to a survey by the Student Association, the average monthly rent for a single room within walking distance of campus has risen to about NT$8,500, up nearly 15 percent from two years ago.

The survey, which collected responses from more than 1,200 students, found that one in three now lives more than five kilometers from campus. "I used to walk to class in ten minutes," said junior Kevin Huang. "Now I ride my scooter for half an hour each way." Several students also reported taking extra part-time jobs to cover housing costs, which they said left them less time for studying.

Students point to several causes. New MRT construction near the campus has attracted young professionals who are willing to pay higher rents, and some landlords have turned student rooms into short-term rentals for tourists. At the same time, the university's dormitories can house only about 40 percent of undergraduates, and freshmen and international students are given priority.

The university says it is responding. A new dormitory with 600 beds is scheduled to open in fall 2028, and the Office of Student Affairs has launched a rent subsidy program for students from low-income families. The office has also partnered with the city government to inspect off-campus rentals and publish a list of safe, fairly priced options. Still, Student Association president Grace Lo believes more can be done. "A building three years away doesn't help students who need a room this spring," she said. The association plans to meet with school officials next month to propose a temporary housing plan.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Why students are living farther from campus and how the school is responding
  2. How students can find good roommates
  3. The opening of a new MRT line
  4. The results of a Student Association election
看答案與詳解

正解 A　全文談房租上漲使學生住得更遠、原因，以及校方的因應，A 涵蓋主旨。MRT 只是原因之一（C 過窄），室友與選舉未提及。

7. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a reason for higher rents?

  1. Young professionals moving into the area
  2. Rooms being turned into tourist rentals
  3. Limited space in the dormitories
  4. A new tax on landlords
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第三段列出年輕上班族遷入、改作觀光短租、宿舍床位不足三項原因；房東新稅（D）文中沒有提到。

8. What does Grace Lo suggest about the new dormitory?

  1. It will be too expensive for most students.
  2. It will not meet students' immediate needs.
  3. It should be built closer to the MRT.
  4. It should give priority to seniors.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　她說 A building three years away doesn't help students who need a room this spring，意指新宿舍緩不濟急（B）。文中沒提到費用、地點或優先順序的意見。

9. Which TWO measures has the university already taken? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Lowering dormitory fees
  2. Running free shuttle buses
  3. Offering rent subsidies to low-income students
  4. Banning short-term rentals near campus
  5. Publishing a list of inspected rentals
看答案與詳解

正解 C、E　第四段提到學務處推出低收入學生租金補助（C），並與市府合作檢查校外租屋、公布安全合理的名單（E）。降宿舍費、接駁車、禁止短租皆未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The market is open until midnight.
  2. Not yet. I want my parents to read it first.
  3. Yes, I signed up for the night class.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否已簽夜市附近公寓的租約，B 表示還沒、想先給父母看，最合理。A 重複 market 只談營業時間；C 用 signed up 與 night 混淆，答非所問。

聽力原文

Have you signed the lease for the apartment near the night market yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. It's two months' rent, paid before you move in.
  2. The studio is very secure, with a guard at the door.
  3. I deposited the check at the bank yesterday.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問套房押金多少，A 回答兩個月租金、入住前付清，切題。B 用 secure 與 security 音近混淆；C 用 deposited 重複字但談的是存支票。

聽力原文

How much is the security deposit for that studio?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I shared my notes with him last week.
  2. The semester ends in January.
  3. Good idea. It would definitely cut our costs.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Why don't we... 是提議一起合租兩房，C 表示贊同並說能省錢，最自然。A 重複 share 卻談分享筆記；B 重複 semester 只說學期結束時間。

聽力原文

Why don't we share a two-bedroom place next semester?

4. What will the woman most likely do this afternoon?

  1. Attend a class at four
  2. View the room before another student
  3. Post an ad on the housing board
  4. Meet the man at a bank
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女子約三點去看房，而另一位學生四點才來，所以她會比對方先看房（B）。她的課兩點半結束而非四點（A 錯）；刊登廣告的是男子（C 錯）；沒提到銀行（D）。

聽力原文

Hi, I'm calling about the room you posted on the student housing board. Is it still available? It is, but another student is coming to see it at four this afternoon. Could I come by at three, then? I have class until two thirty. Sure. Just text me when you're outside the building.

5. What does the man plan to do?

  1. Buy a new air conditioner
  2. Move out of his apartment
  3. Point out a section of his lease to the landlord
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男子最後說 I'll show him that part of the contract，即向房東指出租約中的修繕條款（C）。他沒說要買新冷氣或搬家，A、B 皆無根據。

聽力原文

My landlord says I have to pay for fixing the air conditioner. Did you check your contract? Most leases say the landlord covers repairs unless the tenant caused the damage. It just stopped working by itself. I'll show him that part of the contract.

6. What is offered to workshop participants?

  1. A discount on their first month's rent
  2. A list of approved landlords
  3. A ride to visit apartments
  4. A free review of their rental contracts
看答案與詳解

正解 D　廣播提到 can also have their own contracts reviewed for free，即免費審閱租約（D）。房租折扣、房東名單、看房接送都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Attention, students. The Office of Student Affairs will hold a free rental workshop this Thursday at six thirty in the evening in Room two oh five of the Student Activity Center. A lawyer will explain common lease terms and how to avoid rental scams. Students who attend can also have their own contracts reviewed for free. Please register online by Wednesday, as seats are limited to sixty.

7. Who is the speaker most likely talking to?

  1. Students who will leave the dorms
  2. Landlords near the campus
  3. New dormitory staff
  4. Workers from the power company
看答案與詳解

正解 A　開頭說 many of you will move out of the dorms next semester，聽眾是即將搬離宿舍的學生（A）。內容是給租屋者的建議，不是對房東或員工說話。

8. Why does the speaker recommend taking photos on move-in day?

  1. To post the room online
  2. To compare electricity rates
  3. To help get the deposit back later
  4. To report a problem to the police
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說照片 can protect you when you move out and want your deposit back，即日後拿回押金的證據（C）。其他選項都不是她說的用途。

9. Which TWO things does the speaker advise students to do? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Pay a deposit online to hold a room
  2. Meet the landlord before sending any money
  3. Install their own smoke detectors
  4. Ask about unusually high electricity rates
  5. Sign the lease on their first visit
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　講者要學生 never transfer money before... met the landlord（B），並在電費遠高於電力公司時 ask about it before you sign（D）。A、E 與建議相反；她只說要確認有煙霧偵測器，沒叫學生自行安裝（C）。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. Since many of you will move out of the dorms next semester, I'd like to share a few tips about renting off campus. First, never transfer money before you've seen the place in person and met the landlord. Every year, several students lose their deposits to fake online listings with photos that look too good to be true. Second, read the contract carefully. In Taiwan, the security deposit can't be more than two months' rent, and the lease should clearly say who pays for utilities and repairs. If the landlord charges for electricity at a much higher rate than the power company does, ask about it before you sign. Also, take photos of every room on move-in day. These photos can protect you when you move out and want your deposit back. Finally, think about safety. Check that the building has working smoke detectors and that the gas water heater is not installed indoors without proper ventilation. Our office keeps a list of rentals that have passed a safety inspection, and you're welcome to drop by and look at it anytime.
