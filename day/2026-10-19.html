選課、候補與課程異動

今日單字

waitlist n. / v. 候補名單；列入候補
I'm third on the waitlist.
clash with phr. （時間）衝堂、衝突
The seminar clashes with my lab.
withdraw from phr. 停修、退出
She withdrew from the course.
mandatory adj. 強制的、必須的
Attendance is mandatory.
credit load n. 修課學分數
A heavy credit load can be stressful.
syllabus n. 課程大綱
The syllabus is on the course page.
over capacity phr. 超過容量、人數爆滿
The section is over capacity.
registrar n. 註冊組
Ask the Registrar's Office.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who wish to ______ from a course after the deadline must submit a petition to the Registrar's Office.

  1. withdraw
  2. withhold
  3. withstand
  4. without
看答案與詳解

正解 A　withdraw from a course = 停修。withhold 是「扣留」、withstand 是「承受」，都不接 from a course。

2. Attendance in the first week is ______; students who miss two sessions may lose their seat to someone on the waitlist.

  1. optional
  2. mandatory
  3. temporary
  4. flexible
看答案與詳解

正解 B　後半句說缺兩次課會被候補同學遞補，可見出席是強制的 mandatory。

From: Kevin Huang (Teaching Assistant)

To: BIO 101 students

Subject: New Thursday lab section

Dear BIO 101 students,

Because enrollment has exceeded our expectations, we are opening a fourth lab section on Thursdays from 3:10 to 6:00 p.m. in Room L-312. Students currently registered in the Tuesday afternoon section, which is over capacity, are encouraged to switch.

Requests will be processed on a first-come, first-served basis, and you will receive a confirmation within two working days. Please do not attend the Thursday section until your change has been confirmed, as lab safety rules limit the number of people in the room.

Best regards, Kevin

New Booking System Aims to End the Study-Room Scramble

For years, students at the main library have complained about the "study-room scramble": arriving early, only to find every group room already taken, often by a single person with a laptop. Starting this semester, the library has replaced its paper sign-up sheet with an app called RoomSpot.

Through RoomSpot, groups of three or more can reserve one of the library's 24 study rooms up to seven days in advance, for a maximum of three hours per day. Users must check in by scanning a QR code on the door within 15 minutes of their booking's start time; otherwise, the room is released to others. Individual students may still use a room, but only when no group has booked it.

Early data suggest the system is working. According to library director Grace Lee, room use during peak hours rose from 71 percent to 93 percent in the first month, and complaints dropped sharply. "Rooms are finally being used for what they were built for: working together," she said.

Not everyone is satisfied, however. Some graduate students argue that the three-hour limit makes it hard to prepare for long thesis meetings. International students also pointed out that the app was at first available only in Chinese; an English version was added in the second week after the International Student Association raised the issue.

The library plans to review the policy at the end of the semester and is collecting suggestions through an online survey.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A software company's new product
  2. A new study-room booking system and how people have reacted
  3. How to prepare for a thesis meeting
  4. The renovation of the main library
看答案與詳解

正解 B　全文介紹 RoomSpot 訂位系統、成效與反對意見，B 最完整。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, on Wednesday afternoon.
  2. I'll hold the door for her.
  3. Her office is on the third floor.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問的是「這週有沒有 office hours」，A 直接回答時間。B 用 hold 做同音陷阱；C 回答地點，沒回答「有沒有」。

聽力原文

Do you know if Professor Lin is holding office hours this week?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. It's in Room 204.
  2. It clashes with my lab section.
  3. I signed it yesterday.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Why 問句要回答原因。clash with = 時間衝堂。C 用 sign 重複字干擾，但意思是「簽名」。

聽力原文

Why didn't you sign up for the statistics elective?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Sure, I'll email it to you right now.
  2. The wait was about twenty minutes.
  3. I'd rather link the two ideas.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　請求句 Could you…? 最自然的回應是答應並說明做法。B、C 分別拿 wait、link 做字面陷阱。

聽力原文

Could you send me the link to the course waitlist form?

4. What must the woman do if a spot opens?

  1. Pay an extra lab fee.
  2. Confirm within a day.
  3. Visit the computer lab.
  4. Get the professor's signature.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生最後說 you'll have twenty-four hours to confirm，換句話說就是 within a day。題目常用同義改寫。

聽力原文

Hi, I'm trying to add Introduction to Data Science, but the system says the class is full. Right, it's capped at sixty because of the computer lab. But you can join the waitlist. Students usually drop in the first week. How will I know if I get in? You'll get an email, and you'll have twenty-four hours to confirm your spot.

5. What does the professor want the man to do?

  1. Read the syllabus and attend next Tuesday.
  2. Book a new flight.
  3. Form a project group by himself today.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　教授提到大綱在網路上，並強調下週二要分組 make sure you're there。B 是對話中的背景細節，C 時間錯（是下週二、且是在課堂上分組）。

聽力原文

Professor, I'm sorry I missed the first class. My flight back from Kaohsiung was cancelled. Did I miss anything important? We mainly went over the syllabus, and it's posted online. But groups for the term project will be formed next Tuesday, so make sure you're there.

6. Why has the deadline been changed?

  1. Many students complained about it.
  2. The registration system will be under maintenance.
  3. A new course has been added.
  4. The registrar's office is moving.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　開頭 Because of scheduled system maintenance 直接給原因。廣播題通常第一句就交代主旨。

聽力原文

Attention, all students. Because of scheduled system maintenance this weekend, the deadline for dropping a course without a W on your transcript has been moved from this Friday to next Monday at 5 p.m. Please note that online drop requests will not be available from Saturday morning until Sunday night.

7. Who is the speaker most likely talking to?

  1. Department chairs
  2. First-year students
  3. Peer tutors
  4. Visiting professors
看答案與詳解

正解 B　提到 first-year students 的學分規定、your first term，可推知聽眾是新生。

8. What does the speaker say about taking more than 25 credits?

  1. It needs approval from the department chair.
  2. It is required for the bilingual certificate.
  3. It costs an extra fee.
  4. It is only allowed in the second year.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　原文：you'll need written approval from your department chair。

9. Which TWO pieces of information does the speaker give?

  1. Peer tutoring is free and booked online.
  2. The library is open 24 hours.
  3. EMI courses count toward a bilingual certificate.
  4. All students must take a placement test.
  5. Advisors answer questions through online chat.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　5 選 2 題：講者提到 peer tutoring is free… book through the student portal，以及 EMI courses count toward the bilingual certificate。圖書館、分級測驗、線上聊天都沒提。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Dr. Chen from the Academic Advising Center. Before you finalize your schedules, I'd like to talk about credit load. Our university requires first-year students to take between sixteen and twenty-five credits each semester. If you want to go above twenty-five, you'll need written approval from your department chair, and honestly, I don't recommend it in your first term. Many of you are also considering EMI courses, that is, courses taught in English. These count toward the university's bilingual certificate, and they're a great way to build academic English. But they usually involve more reading, so balance them with courses in subjects you already feel confident about. One more thing. If you start to struggle, don't wait until midterms. Our peer tutoring program is free, and you can book a session through the student portal. Our office is open until seven on weekdays, so feel free to drop by after class.
