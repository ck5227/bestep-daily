今日單字

counselor n. 營隊輔導員；隊輔
Each counselor will lead a team of six children.
outreach n. 外展服務；推廣服務
The Youth Outreach Club runs reading camps in rural areas.
remote adj. 偏遠的
Schools in remote villages often have few teachers.
hands-on adj. 實際動手做的
The children loved the hands-on science activities.
priority n. 優先（權）
Students who attend the session will be given priority for interviews.
accommodation n. 住宿
Accommodation is provided, but please bring a sleeping bag.
grant v. 給予；准許
Online interviews will be granted only in special circumstances.
lasting adj. 持久的
A five-day camp may be too short to make a lasting difference.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who wish to be camp counselors must ______ a short video introducing themselves along with the application form.

  1. admit
  2. permit
  3. submit
  4. commit
看答案與詳解

正解 C　submit 意為「繳交、提交」，搭配申請表與影片最合理。admit 承認、permit 允許、commit 犯下／承諾，語意皆不符。

2. Because the camp is held in a remote village, counselors should be ______ and able to adjust quickly when plans change.

  1. flexibility
  2. flexible
  3. flexibly
  4. flexes
看答案與詳解

正解 B　be 動詞後並與 able 對等連接，需要形容詞 flexible（有彈性的）。flexibility 是名詞、flexibly 是副詞、flexes 是動詞，詞性皆不符。

From: Youth Outreach Club <outreach@university.edu.tw>

To: Winter Camp Applicants

Subject: Interview Arrangements for Winter Camp Counselors

Dear Applicants,

Thank you for applying to be a counselor at our Winter Reading Camp, which will take place from January 20 to 24 at Guangfu Elementary School in Nantou. We received 48 applications for only 16 positions, so we have decided to hold group interviews.

Interviews will take place on December 6 and 7 in Room 312 of the Student Activity Center. Please choose a time slot through the link below by December 2. Each interview lasts about forty minutes. You will work with other applicants to plan a simple reading activity for children aged eight to ten.

Please bring your student ID. If you are unable to attend on either day, reply to this email as soon as possible, and we will try to arrange an online interview. Note that online interviews are limited and will be granted only in special circumstances.

Best regards,

Kevin Huang, Camp Coordinator

3. Why is the club holding group interviews?

  1. The camp has been moved to a new school.
  2. Applicants need to meet the children first.
  3. Online interviews are no longer allowed.
  4. There are more applicants than available positions.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中說收到 48 份申請但只有 16 個名額，因此舉辦團體面試，故選 D。地點未更動，A 錯；仍可視情況安排線上面試，C 錯；B 未提及。

4. What will applicants do during the interview?

  1. Read a story aloud to a group of children
  2. Plan an activity together with other applicants
  3. Show a video they made about themselves
  4. Take a written reading test
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中說面試時要與其他申請者合作規劃一個簡單的閱讀活動，故選 B。面試對象不是孩童，A 錯；C、D 未提及。

5. The word "granted" in paragraph 4 is closest in meaning to

  1. given
  2. refused
  3. thanked
  4. assumed
看答案與詳解

正解 A　be granted only in special circumstances 意指「只在特殊情況下才會給予（核准）」，granted 最接近 given。refused 意思相反；thanked、assumed 不符語境。

More Students Spend Winter Break Teaching in Rural Schools

For many university students, winter break means sleeping late, catching up on dramas, or working part-time. But a growing number of students in Taiwan are choosing a different way to spend their vacation: volunteering as counselors at winter camps in rural areas. According to the university's Service Learning Office, the number of students joining such camps has nearly doubled over the past three years.

The camps usually last four to seven days and focus on subjects such as English, science, or reading. In many remote villages, schools have few teachers and limited resources, so children rarely get to take part in hands-on activities. "Our counselors bring energy and fresh ideas," said Ms. Chen, the principal of a small school in Yilan. "Some of our students still talk about the robot-building activity from last year."

The experience benefits the volunteers as well. Wang Yu-ting, a junior majoring in social work, said that leading a team of ten-year-olds taught her more about communication than any class had. "You can't just give a lecture. You have to think about how kids feel and keep them interested," she explained. Many students also say the experience helps them stand out when they apply for jobs or graduate programs.

However, organizers warn that running a camp is no vacation. Counselors must attend several training sessions, prepare lesson plans, and often sleep on classroom floors. Some camps have also been criticized for being too short to make a lasting difference. To address this, a few clubs now stay in touch with the schools all year round, mailing reading materials and holding online tutoring sessions.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. The growing trend of students volunteering at rural winter camps
  2. Why many rural schools are closing down
  3. How to plan a robot-building activity for children
  4. Changes to the university's winter break schedule
看答案與詳解

正解 A　全文從學生寒假到偏鄉擔任營隊隊輔的人數倍增談起，並說明對孩童與志工的影響及挑戰，故選 A。B、D 未提及；C 只是文中舉例的細節。

7. According to the article, why are the camps valuable for rural children?

  1. They replace regular classes during the semester.
  2. They provide free meals for the children's families.
  3. They help children prepare for entrance exams.
  4. They give children chances to do hands-on activities.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第二段說偏鄉學校師資與資源有限，孩子很少有動手做的機會，營隊正好補足，故選 D。營隊在寒假舉行，並非取代學期課程，A 錯；B、C 未提及。

8. What criticism of some camps is mentioned?

  1. They cost volunteers too much money.
  2. Counselors do not receive enough training.
  3. They are too short to have a long-term effect.
  4. Principals do not support them.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第四段指出部分營隊被批評時間太短，難以帶來持久影響（too short to make a lasting difference），故選 C。文中說隊輔須參加多次培訓，B 錯；校長給予肯定，D 錯；A 未提及。

9. According to the article, which TWO benefits do volunteers gain? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Better communication skills
  2. Extra course credits
  3. Free accommodation during the holidays
  4. An advantage when applying for jobs or graduate school
  5. A paid part-time position
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　第三段提到王同學學到比任何課程都多的溝通技巧，且許多學生表示經驗有助於求職或申請研究所時脫穎而出，故選 A、D。睡教室地板並非福利，C 錯；B、E 未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The sign near the main gate is new.
  2. I'd like to, but my family has a trip planned in January.
  3. She counseled me a lot last semester.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否報名寒假營隊隊輔，B 表示想參加但一月有家庭旅遊，最合理。A 以 sign 混淆 signing up；C 以 counseled 混淆 counselor，答非所問。

聽力原文

Are you signing up to be a counselor at the winter camp?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I applied some sunscreen before I left.
  2. The phone line is dead, so I'll call again later.
  3. This Friday at five in the afternoon.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　When 問截止時間，C 回答本週五下午五點，最合理。A 以 applied 混淆 application；B 以 line is dead 混淆 deadline，答非所問。

聽力原文

When is the deadline for the counselor application?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. That works. The lab is free that morning.
  2. I ran five kilometers yesterday.
  3. The second floor is closed for repairs.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Why don't we 是提議句，A 同意並說明實驗室當天早上有空，最合理。B 以 ran 混淆 run；C 重複 second 但答非所問。

聽力原文

Why don't we run the science activity on the second day of camp?

4. What will the man most likely do?

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To invite more children to join the camp
  2. To describe each experiment in detail
  3. To explain the rules and expectations for counselors
  4. To announce a change in the camp schedule

正解 C　林教授在第一次培訓中強調安全、不離開隊伍、與家長溝通等三項規範，主旨是說明隊輔守則與期待，故選 C。實驗下週才練習，B 錯；A、D 未提及。

8. What should a counselor do if he or she needs a break?

  1. Tell the children to wait quietly in the classroom
  2. Ask a floating counselor to take over
  3. Call the camp director for permission
  4. Bring the team back to the dorm

正解 B　教授說需要休息時請機動隊輔代班（ask one of the floating counselors to cover for you），故選 B。她強調絕不能讓隊伍單獨留下，A 錯；C、D 未提及。

9. Which TWO things are counselors asked to do? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Buy materials for the experiments
  2. Read the activity handbook
  3. Create a personal group chat with parents
  4. Design a flag for their team
  5. Hand in a health form
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　教授要求下週前先讀活動手冊，並在週五前繳交健康表，故選 B、E。與家長聯絡須用官方群組而非個人帳號，C 錯；A、D 未提及。

聽力原文

Good evening, everyone, and welcome to the first training session for our winter science camp. I'm Professor Lin, the camp director. Over five days, about sixty fifth and sixth graders from small schools in Pingtung will join us here on campus. Each of you will lead a team of six children, so you are not just a teacher. You are also a big brother or a big sister. Tonight I want to stress three things. First, safety comes before fun. Any activity that involves heat, chemicals, or water must be checked by a staff member before you begin. Second, never leave your team alone, not even for a quick phone call. If you need a break, ask one of the floating counselors to cover for you. Third, keep your communication with parents professional. Please use the official camp group chat, not your personal accounts. Next week, we'll practice the actual experiments, so please read the activity handbook before you come. And don't forget to hand in your health form by Friday.
