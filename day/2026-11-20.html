BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-20
校園資安與網路詐騙

今日單字

phishing n. 網路釣魚（詐騙）
The IT team warned students about a new phishing email that copied the school logo.
verify v. 確認；核實
Always verify the website address before you log in.
suspicious adj. 可疑的
If a message looks suspicious, forward it to the security team.
two-step verification n. 雙重（兩步驟）驗證
Turning on two-step verification takes only five minutes.
suspend v. 暫停；停權
Accounts that are not updated will be temporarily suspended.
target v. 以…為目標；針對
Scammers often target students who are looking for part-time jobs.
urgency n. 急迫（感）
Fake messages often create a sense of urgency to make you act quickly.
trick (sb.) into phr. v. 誘騙（某人）做某事
The fake recruiter tricked her into sharing her bank details.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before you enter your password, always ______ that the website address belongs to the university.

  1. deny
  2. predict
  3. verify
  4. borrow
看答案與詳解

正解 C　verify that... 意為「確認／核實⋯」，輸入密碼前應確認網址屬於學校。deny 否認、predict 預測、borrow 借用，皆不合語意。

2. The IT office warned that the fake message was designed to ______ students into sharing their bank details.

  1. remind
  2. trick
  3. allow
  4. invite
看答案與詳解

正解 B　trick someone into doing something 是固定用法，意為「誘騙某人做某事」。remind、allow、invite 都不能接 into + V-ing。

From: Computer and Network Center <cnc@university.edu.tw>

To: All Students

Subject: Required Password Update and Two-Step Verification

Dear students,

Following several recent phishing attempts targeting our campus, all student accounts must be updated by December 10. After that date, accounts that have not been updated will be temporarily suspended until the owner visits our service desk in person.

To update your account, log in to the student portal yourself, choose a new password of at least twelve characters, and set up two-step verification using the school's authenticator app. The whole process takes less than ten minutes.

Please note that this email does not contain any links. If you receive a similar message that asks you to click a link to update your account, it is a scam. Do not respond, and report it to security@university.edu.tw.

Thank you for helping us keep our campus network safe.

Computer and Network Center

3. What will happen to accounts that are not updated by December 10?

  1. They will be deleted permanently.
  2. Their owners will be charged a fee.
  3. Their passwords will be changed automatically.
  4. They will be suspended until the student visits the service desk.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第二段說未更新的帳號 will be temporarily suspended until the owner visits our service desk in person。信中是「暫停」而非永久刪除，也沒有收費或自動改密碼。

4. Why does the email point out that it contains no links?

  1. To keep the message short
  2. To help students tell real notices from fake ones
  3. Because the student portal is currently down
  4. Because links do not work on mobile phones
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第四段說若收到要求點連結更新帳號的信就是詐騙，藉此讓學生分辨真假通知。其他選項信中皆未提及。

5. The word "targeting" in the second paragraph is closest in meaning to

  1. aiming at
  2. ignoring
  3. protecting
  4. measuring
看答案與詳解

正解 A　phishing attempts targeting our campus 指「針對本校」的釣魚攻擊，target 在此為「以⋯為目標」，最接近 aiming at。

When a Job Offer Is Too Good to Be True

Every November, as students start searching for winter break jobs, the campus Career Center receives a wave of worried visitors. This year, the center has already handled more than forty reports of fake job offers, almost twice as many as last year. Most of the offers were posted in student social media groups and promised high pay for "simple online tasks."

One junior in the Department of Economics, who asked not to be named, said she was offered NT$1,500 an hour to "review products" from home. After she agreed, the recruiter asked for a photo of her student ID and her bank account details so that she could "receive her salary." Two days later, she discovered that her account had been used to move money from other scam victims. "I thought I was earning extra pocket money," she said. "Instead, I ended up talking to the police."

According to Professor Huang Mei-ling, who teaches information law, students are attractive targets because they are often short of money and tend to trust messages shared by classmates. "Scammers know that a post in a familiar group feels safer than a random advertisement," she explained. She added that lending a bank account to strangers, even without knowing its purpose, can lead to serious legal trouble.

To reduce the risk, the Career Center now checks every company before listing its jobs on the official job board. Staff also recommend that students never pay a "training fee," never hand over bank cards or passwords, and search the company's name online before any interview. Students who are unsure about an offer can book a free fifteen-minute consultation at the center.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A new law on part-time work for students
  2. How to write a strong resume for winter jobs
  3. A rise in fake job offers aimed at students and how to avoid them
  4. The most popular winter break jobs on campus
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文從假工作通報倍增、學生受害案例、教授分析到職涯中心建議，主旨是學生求職詐騙增加與防範。A、B、D 皆非重點。

7. What happened to the economics student?

  1. Her bank account was used to move money from other scam victims.
  2. She lost her student ID card.
  3. She paid a large training fee.
  4. She was banned from the Career Center job board.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第二段說 her account had been used to move money from other scam victims，帳戶淪為人頭帳戶。她只是提供學生證照片，並未遺失；訓練費出現在第四段建議中，不是她的經歷。

8. According to Professor Huang, why are students easy targets for scammers?

  1. They rarely use social media.
  2. They often need money and trust posts from classmates.
  3. They have not studied information law.
  4. They work too many hours during the semester.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第三段指出學生 often short of money and tend to trust messages shared by classmates。A 與文中學生常在社群看到貼文相反；C、D 未被提及。

9. Which TWO pieces of advice does the Career Center staff give? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Pay training fees only to well-known companies.
  2. Look up the company online before an interview.
  3. Share bank details only after signing a contract.
  4. Never give out bank cards or passwords.
  5. Apply only for jobs posted in student groups.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　第四段建議 search the company's name online before any interview 及 never hand over bank cards or passwords。文中說絕不付訓練費，A、C 與原文相違；E 的學生社群正是假工作的主要來源。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I passed the exam with a pretty good score.
  2. Yes, but I reported it to the IT office instead of replying.
  3. The mail room closes at five.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　對方問是否收到索取密碼的郵件，B 表示收到並已向資訊中心通報，最合理。A 的 passed 與 password 音近；C 的 mail room 重複 mail 字，皆為干擾。

聽力原文

Did you get that email asking for your student password?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. On the IT services page, under Account Help.
  2. Just set it on the table, please.
  3. The portal opens again next semester.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Where 問句問「地點」，A 指出在資訊服務網頁的帳號協助區，正確。B 的 set 與 reset 音近；C 重複 portal 但沒回答在哪裡重設。

聽力原文

Where can I reset my portal password?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Take two steps to the left.
  2. The verification letter arrived yesterday.
  3. Definitely. It makes it much harder for anyone to break in.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　對方詢問是否該開啟雙重驗證，C 肯定並說明能防止他人入侵帳號。A 重複 two steps 字面意思；B 重複 verification 但與帳號安全無關。

聽力原文

Should I turn on two-step verification for my school account?

4. What will the woman most likely do next?

  1. Contact the scholarship office
  2. Help the man change his password
  3. Check the status of his payment
  4. Delete his email account
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女子最後說 let's change it right now，接下來會協助男子更改密碼。獎學金只是詐騙郵件的藉口，A、C 都不是她要做的事；D 刪除帳號未被提及。

聽力原文

Hi, I clicked a link in an email that said my scholarship payment was on hold, and then it asked me to log in. Okay. Did you actually enter your password on that page? I did, about ten minutes ago. Then let's change it right now, and I'll also sign you out of every device.

5. What does the man suggest the woman do first?

  1. Call the club president
  2. Change her LINE password
  3. Buy the gift cards
  4. Save a record of the message
看答案與詳解

正解 D　男子說 but first take a screenshot，先截圖保存證據再封鎖。A、B 未被建議；C 正是詐騙者的要求，不可照做。

聽力原文

Someone messaged me on LINE pretending to be our club president. They asked me to buy gift cards for a prize. That happened to two other members this week. The real president never asks for money through private messages. So should I just block the account? Block it, but first take a screenshot and send it to the student affairs office so they can warn everyone.

6. What must students do in order to attend the workshop?

  1. Pay a small registration fee
  2. Bring their own laptop
  3. Register through the portal in advance
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播最後說 please register through the student portal by noon on Tuesday，須事先線上報名。工作坊是 free，A 錯；B 筆電未被提及。

聽力原文

Attention, all students. The Computer and Network Center will hold a free cybersecurity workshop this Wednesday from two to four in the afternoon in Room three oh five of the Information Building. Topics include spotting phishing emails, creating strong passwords, and protecting personal data on public Wi-Fi. Students who attend will receive two hours of general education credit. Seats are limited to sixty, so please register through the student portal by noon on Tuesday.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To announce a tuition refund
  2. To explain new exam rules
  3. To help students recognize and handle scam messages
  4. To introduce new IT staff members
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說明詐騙訊息增加、警訊特徵與處理方式，目的是教學生辨識與應對詐騙。A 的退費正是詐騙內容；B、D 未被談及。

8. According to the speaker, what happened last month?

  1. Over three hundred students received fake refund emails.
  2. The school changed its official logo.
  3. A staff member's account was hacked.
  4. The IT homepage was shut down.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者提到 Last month alone, more than three hundred students received fake emails claiming a tuition refund。B 只說詐騙信使用校徽；C 只說冒用職員姓名，並非帳號被駭。

9. Which TWO warning signs does the speaker mention? (Choose TWO.)

  1. The message pressures you to act quickly.
  2. The message contains spelling mistakes.
  3. The sender's address is not from the official school domain.
  4. The message is sent late at night.
  5. The message includes an attachment.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　講者列出的警訊為製造急迫感（sense of urgency）與寄件地址非學校官方網域，另一項是要求點連結登入。拼字錯誤、深夜寄送、附件皆未被提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Kevin Lin from the university's information security team. As the semester gets busier, we usually see a rise in scam messages, and this term is no exception. Last month alone, more than three hundred students received fake emails claiming that a tuition refund was waiting for them. These messages can look very convincing. They use our school logo and sometimes even a real staff member's name. But there are a few warning signs. First, they create a sense of urgency, like, your account will be closed in twenty-four hours. Second, the sender's address doesn't end in our official school domain. And third, they ask you to click a link and log in. Remember, our office will never ask for your password by email or by phone. If something looks suspicious, don't reply. Forward it to the security team's report address, which you can find on the IT homepage. And please turn on two-step verification before final exams start. It only takes about five minutes, and it's the single best way to protect your account.
