分組報告與團隊合作

今日單字

contribute (to) v. 貢獻；出力
Everyone is expected to contribute to the final report.
brainstorm v. 腦力激盪；集思廣益
Let's brainstorm ideas before we choose a topic.
coordinator n. 協調者；統籌人
The coordinator sends a reminder before every meeting.
rotate v. 輪流；輪替
We rotate the role of note-taker every week.
confidential adj. 機密的；保密的
Your answers on the form will remain confidential.
peer evaluation n. 同儕互評
The peer evaluation counts for ten percent of the grade.
free rider n. 搭便車者（不出力卻分享成果的人）
Nobody wants a free rider in their project group.
anonymous adj. 匿名的
The survey is anonymous, so please be honest.

閱讀 Reading

1. Because two members did not ______ to the final report, the group asked the professor to grade each person separately.

  1. attribute
  2. distribute
  3. contribute
  4. consist
看答案與詳解

正解 C　contribute to 意為「對…有貢獻」，符合沒出力的語意。attribute to 是「歸因於」，distribute 是「分發」，consist 須接 of。

2. The team spent the first meeting ______ ideas before choosing a topic for their presentation.

  1. postponing
  2. brainstorming
  3. apologizing
  4. graduating
看答案與詳解

正解 B　選題前先「腦力激盪」想點子最合理：brainstorm ideas。postpone ideas、apologize ideas 搭配不通，graduate 不接受詞。

From: Daniel Chou, Teaching Assistant

To: All students, Environmental Science 101

Subject: Group presentation schedule and peer review form

Dear all,

The schedule for the group presentations is now posted on the course website. Each group will have fifteen minutes to present, followed by five minutes of questions. Please arrive at least ten minutes before your time slot so that you can test your slides on the classroom computer.

If your group needs to swap time slots with another group, both groups must agree and email me by November 6. I cannot approve any changes after that date.

Also, please remember to submit the peer review form within 48 hours after your presentation. The form is confidential; your teammates will not see your comments. Late forms will not be accepted.

Best regards, Daniel

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To cancel an upcoming class
  2. To announce changes to group members
  3. To ask students to choose a topic
  4. To share presentation details and reminders
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中說明報告時程、換時段規則與互評表繳交期限，屬資訊與提醒。並未取消課程、更換組員或要求選題。

4. What must a group do to change its presentation time?

  1. Get another group's agreement and email the TA by November 6
  2. Ask the professor for permission in class
  3. Post a request on the course website
  4. Arrive ten minutes early on the presentation day
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第二段：both groups must agree and email me by November 6。提早十分鐘是為了測試投影片，與換時段無關。

5. The word "confidential" in paragraph 4 is closest in meaning to

  1. optional
  2. official
  3. private
  4. complicated
看答案與詳解

正解 C　後句 your teammates will not see your comments 說明內容保密，故 confidential = private。

Can an App Stop "Free Riders" in Group Projects?

For many university students, the words "group project" bring to mind one familiar problem: the free rider, a teammate who does little work but receives the same grade as everyone else. In a Student Association survey last spring, 68 percent of the 1,200 respondents said they had worked with at least one free rider, and nearly half said the experience made them dislike group assignments.

This semester, the Center for Teaching and Learning is testing a possible solution. Twelve courses across four colleges are using an online platform called TeamTrack. Students use it to divide tasks, set personal deadlines, and upload drafts. The platform automatically records who completed each task and when, and instructors can view a summary of each member's activity at any time.

Supporters say the tool makes contributions visible. "Before, I had to rely on students telling me who did what," said Professor Huang Ya-wen, who teaches business communication. "Now I can see the process, not just the final product." Several students also said they felt more motivated knowing their work was being recorded.

However, not everyone is convinced. Some students worry that the platform measures activity rather than quality. "Someone can upload ten small files and look busy, while the person who writes the most important section only uploads once," said Chen Po-han, a sociology junior. Others have raised privacy concerns and want to know who else can see the data.

The center will collect feedback from students and teachers at the end of the semester. If the results are positive, TeamTrack could be offered to all courses by next fall. In the meantime, the center recommends that groups continue to use written team agreements and regular peer evaluations.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A survey of students' favorite courses
  2. An online tool being tested to track group work
  3. A new business communication course
  4. A policy that bans group assignments
看答案與詳解

正解 B　全文圍繞 TeamTrack 平台的試行、支持與疑慮。問卷只是開頭引言，其他選項未提及。

7. According to the article, what can instructors do with TeamTrack?

  1. Grade presentations automatically
  2. Assign students to groups
  3. Check drafts for plagiarism
  4. See a summary of each member's activity
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第二段：instructors can view a summary of each member's activity at any time。其餘功能文中沒有提到。

8. Why is Chen Po-han critical of the platform?

  1. He thinks it shows how active students are, not how good their work is.
  2. He believes it is too difficult to use.
  3. He says teachers never check the data.
  4. He thinks it makes students less motivated.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　陳柏翰舉例上傳很多小檔案就看起來很忙，對應前句 measures activity rather than quality。D 與文中「學生更有動力」相反。

9. Which TWO statements are true according to the article? (Choose TWO.)

  1. All of the survey respondents disliked group work.
  2. TeamTrack is currently being tested in twelve courses.
  3. TeamTrack is already used in every college.
  4. Professor Huang thinks the tool is too expensive.
  5. Groups are still advised to use team agreements.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　第二段提到 Twelve courses 試用；末段建議 continue to use written team agreements。只有「近一半」不喜歡分組，目前僅四個學院試用，黃教授未談價格。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, the slide was fixed last week.
  2. Kevin volunteered to do it.
  3. It's on the third floor.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問題問「誰來報告」，Kevin 自願負責最切題。A 用 slide 字音重複誤導，C 回答地點，皆不符。

聽力原文

Has your group decided who's presenting the final slides?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Sorry, I have a lab until five.
  2. Because it was Thursday.
  3. I read that book in the library.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Why don't we... 是提議，用「我有實驗課到五點」婉拒最自然。B 把提議誤當成問原因，C 只重複 library。

聽力原文

Why don't we meet in the library study room on Thursday afternoon?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I'll conclude the meeting at six.
  2. It's due on Friday at noon.
  3. Mei-ling said she'd handle it.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問「誰負責寫結論」，答「美玲說她會處理」最合理。A 用 conclude 與 conclusion 字形相近誤導，B 回答截止時間。

聽力原文

Who's responsible for writing the conclusion?

4. What will the woman do if Brian does not respond?

  1. Write the literature review herself
  2. Remove him from the group chat
  3. Ask for a deadline extension
  4. Contact the professor the next morning
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女子說 If he still doesn't respond, I'll email the professor tomorrow morning。其他選項對話中都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Lisa, have you seen our shared document? Half of the literature review is still empty. I know. Brian hasn't replied to any of our messages since Monday. Should we tell Professor Lin? Let's give him until tonight. If he still doesn't respond, I'll email the professor tomorrow morning.

5. Why was the workshop moved?

  1. It was rescheduled to a later time.
  2. The original room was being repaired.
  3. More people registered than planned.
  4. The speaker needed a bigger screen.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男子說 more students signed up than expected，即報名人數超出預期。時間仍是兩點，所以 A 錯；B、D 未提及。

聽力原文

Excuse me, is this the room for the peer evaluation workshop? It was, but it moved to Room three oh two because more students signed up than expected. Oh, thanks. Does it still start at two? Yes, but they'll take attendance at the door, so don't be late.

6. What are students asked to include in the proposal?

  1. The names and ID numbers of all members
  2. A summary of each member's tasks
  3. A copy of the maintenance notice
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播要求 every member's name and student ID number appear on the first page。B、C 均未被要求。

聽力原文

Attention, students in Introduction to Marketing. The deadline for your group project proposal has been extended from Friday to next Monday at noon, because the online learning system will be down for maintenance this weekend. Please upload one file per group, and make sure every member's name and student ID number appear on the first page.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To assign students to project groups
  2. To explain the rules of the final exam
  3. To show how to design effective slides
  4. To offer tips for working in groups
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者開頭說 I'd like to spend a few minutes on how to work together effectively，全篇都在給團隊合作建議。設計投影片只是舉例。

8. According to the speaker, why should groups rotate the coordinator role?

  1. So that one member does not do all the organizing
  2. So that everyone learns to design slides
  3. Because the professor changes groups every two weeks
  4. Because the coordinator receives extra points
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者說 rotate... so that no single person ends up doing all the organizing。其他選項只借用 two weeks、slides 等字眼。

9. Which TWO things does the speaker say a team agreement should include? (Choose TWO.)

  1. How often the group will meet
  2. Which member will give the presentation
  3. How quickly members should reply to messages
  4. The topic of the final report
  5. The professor's office hours
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　團隊協議要決定 how often you'll meet 與 how quickly people should reply to messages（另有錯過截止日的處理）。報告人、題目、辦公時間都不在其中。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. Since your group projects make up forty percent of your final grade this semester, I'd like to spend a few minutes on how to work together effectively. First, at your very first meeting, write a team agreement. Decide how often you'll meet, how quickly people should reply to messages, and what happens if someone misses a deadline. Groups that do this usually have far fewer conflicts later on. Second, divide the work by skills, not just by sections of the report. For example, one person might be good at finding sources, while another is better at designing slides. And rotate the role of coordinator every two weeks, so that no single person ends up doing all the organizing. Finally, remember that at the end of the semester, each of you will complete an anonymous peer evaluation. Your individual grade can go up or down by as much as ten percent, depending on what your teammates report. If problems come up, please come to my office hours early. Don't wait until the week before the presentation.
