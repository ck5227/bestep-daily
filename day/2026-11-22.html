BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-22
校外參訪與田野調查

今日單字

waiver n. 免責同意書；（權利的）放棄
Every hiker had to sign a liability waiver before the trip.
preliminary adj. 初步的
Our preliminary findings suggest the beach is shrinking quickly.
reimburse v. 償還；核銷（費用）
The department will reimburse students for their bus fares.
qualify v. 符合資格
Taxi fares only qualify if you shared the ride with classmates.
consent form n. 知情同意書
Show the consent form before you start each interview.
erosion n. 侵蝕
Coastal erosion has changed the shape of the shoreline.
accommodate v. 通融；容納
Late arrivals cannot be accommodated because the bus leaves on time.
field notes n. 田野筆記
Write up your field notes the same evening while details are fresh.

閱讀 Reading

1. Because the trip involves hiking along a river, every participant must submit a signed liability ______ before departure.

  1. receipt
  2. transcript
  3. waiver
  4. reservation
看答案與詳解

正解 C　liability waiver 為「免責同意書」，用於有風險的活動，符合句意，選 C。receipt 收據、transcript 成績單、reservation 預約，都不與 liability 搭配。

2. Our observations at the night market were only ______, so we plan to go back next week and collect more systematic data.

  1. permanent
  2. preliminary
  3. mandatory
  4. fluent
看答案與詳解

正解 B　後半句說要回去蒐集更有系統的資料，表示目前觀察只是「初步的」preliminary，選 B。permanent 永久的、mandatory 強制的、fluent 流利的，語意不通。

From: Jason Lai <jlai@university.edu.tw>

To: Students in Urban Studies 201

Subject: Reimbursement for Field Trip Expenses

Dear all,

Thank you for your hard work during last week's visit to Dadaocheng. As promised, the department will reimburse your transportation costs for the trip. To receive the money, please fill out the reimbursement form on the course website and attach a screenshot of your MRT or bus records. Taxi fares will be covered only if you traveled with at least two classmates, so please list their names on the form.

All claims must be submitted by Friday, December 4. Because the department closes its accounts for the year in mid-December, late claims cannot be processed, and I'm afraid there will be no exceptions.

If you have lost your records or are unsure whether an expense qualifies, please stop by during my office hours on Wednesday afternoon.

Best,

Jason Lai

Teaching Assistant, Urban Studies 201

  1. If the ride took place late at night

5. In paragraph 4, the word "qualifies" is closest in meaning to

  1. is eligible
  2. is cancelled
  3. is explained
  4. is measured
看答案與詳解

正解 A　whether an expense qualifies 指某筆費用是否「符合資格」可申請，與 is eligible 同義，選 A。其他選項放入句中語意不通。

From Classroom to Coastline: Students Map Taiwan's Changing Shore

Every Saturday morning this semester, a group of twenty-three students from the Department of Geography has traded their lecture hall for the windy beaches of the Taoyuan coast. Armed with GPS devices, measuring tapes, and a borrowed drone, they are collecting data for a project that tracks how quickly the shoreline is moving.

The project began last spring, when Professor Huang Mei-ling noticed that the maps in her textbook no longer matched what she saw on the ground. "In some places, the beach has shrunk by more than ten meters in just five years," she explained. Instead of simply telling students about coastal erosion, she decided to let them measure it themselves.

The work is not always comfortable. Students start at six in the morning to avoid the afternoon heat and strong tides, and they must follow strict safety rules, including working in pairs and staying away from the water at high tide. "At first I thought it would be like a picnic," admitted junior Chen Yu-ting. "Then I spent four hours in the rain recording numbers. But seeing the changes with my own eyes made the problem real in a way a textbook never could."

The students' findings will not stay on campus. Their data will be shared with the local township office, which plans to use it when deciding where to plant protective vegetation. Several students will also present the results at a regional conference in January.

Professor Huang hopes the course will become a permanent part of the curriculum. However, she admits that funding is a challenge, since the drone and transportation costs are currently covered by a one-year grant that ends this summer.

6. Why did Professor Huang start the project?

  1. She received a request from the township office.
  2. The maps in her textbook no longer matched the real coastline.
  3. Students complained that her lectures were boring.
  4. A conference asked her department for new data.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段指出計畫起因是黃教授發現課本地圖與實地海岸不再相符，選 B。鄉公所是之後才使用資料，研討會是發表場合，都不是起因。

  2. They will help the township office plan where to plant vegetation.
  3. They will be displayed in the campus library.
  4. They will be presented at a regional conference.

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The museum opens at nine on weekdays.
  2. It was a really long trip last year.
  3. Yes, you'll need them to get the group discount.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問題是校外參訪是否要帶學生證，C 回答需要、可享團體優惠，最合理。A 重複 museum 只談開館時間；B 重複 trip 卻談去年的事，答非所問。

聽力原文

Do we need to bring our student IDs on the field trip to the museum?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Ms. Chen in the department office is handling it.
  2. I've been looking at the book you lent me.
  3. The bus stop is right outside the main gate.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問誰負責訂遊覽車，A 指出系辦陳小姐負責，直接回答「誰」。B 用 looking/book 與 booking 音近混淆；C 重複 bus 卻只談公車站位置。

聽力原文

Who's in charge of booking the bus for Saturday's site visit?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The farm is about an hour south of campus.
  2. Right, I'll print copies tonight so everyone can sign.
  3. No, I've never been to a farmers' market.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　對方提醒訪談前應先簽同意書，B 表示同意並今晚列印，回應合理。A 以 farm 與 farmers 相似字混淆；C 雖以 No 開頭，但談的是農夫市集，與同意書無關。

聽力原文

Shouldn't we get consent forms signed before we interview the farmers?

4. What will the students do on Thursday?

  1. Practice using equipment in the classroom
  2. Visit the wetland in the rain
  3. Meet the park rangers on the trail
  4. Take the day off from class
看答案與詳解

正解 A　教授說週四照常到教室，練習使用水質檢測工具，故選 A。參訪已延到下週二，B 錯；步道關閉，C 錯；週四仍要上課，D 錯。

聽力原文

Professor Wu, the forecast says heavy rain on Thursday. Are we still going to the wetland? No, the park rangers have closed the trails. We'll move the visit to next Tuesday morning. Then should we still meet on Thursday? Yes, come to the classroom at the usual time. We'll practice using the water testing kits so you're ready for the real thing.

5. What does the woman suggest Kevin do?

  1. Record the interviews again
  2. Delete some old files from his laptop
  3. Use the department's online storage
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女子建議把其餘檔案上傳到系上的共用雲端硬碟（shared drive），即 C。她沒有要他重錄或刪檔，A、B 皆無根據。

聽力原文

Kevin, did you back up the interview recordings from the temple visit? Only about half of them. My laptop ran out of space last night. You can upload the rest to the department's shared drive. Each group gets fifty gigabytes of storage.

6. What has changed from the earlier email?

  1. The departure time
  2. The meeting place
  3. The lunch arrangements
  4. The destination
看答案與詳解

正解 B　廣播說集合地點改為北門，「不是先前信中說的圖書館」，故為集合地點改變（B）。時間、午餐、目的地都沒有提到變動。

Attention, students joining the Hualien geology field trip. The bus will leave from the north gate at seven fifteen tomorrow morning, not from the library as stated in the earlier email. Please wear closed toe shoes and bring a hat and at least one liter of water. Lunch will be provided, but if you have any food allergies, please tell the teaching assistant before nine o'clock tonight. Late arrivals cannot be accommodated, since the bus must reach the coast before high tide.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To recruit volunteers for the fishing industry
  2. To announce a change in the trip's destination
  3. To review reports from last year's class
  4. To explain guidelines for upcoming fieldwork
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者開頭說要說明下個月在漁村「如何進行工作」，接著列出拍照、訪談、田野筆記等規範，主旨為 D。A、B、C 均未提及。

8. What should students do if a resident refuses to be interviewed?

  1. Offer the person a small gift
  2. Ask the village leader for help
  3. Thank the person and find someone else
  4. Try again the next day
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說 If someone says no, simply thank them and move on，並強調不要說服對方，故選 C。B、D 都屬於繼續勸說，與指示相反。

9. According to the speaker, which TWO things are students required to do? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Ask permission before photographing people
  2. Interview at least five residents
  3. Write field notes on the same evening
  4. Submit the report before leaving the village
  5. Record every interview on video
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　講者要求拍人前先詢問（A），並當晚寫完田野筆記（C）。訪談人數是至少兩位而非五位；報告是返校兩週後繳交；未要求錄影。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. Next month, our anthropology class will spend three days in a small fishing village in Pingtung, and today I want to go over how we'll work there. First, remember that we are guests. The village leader has kindly agreed to host us, so please don't wander into private homes, and don't take photos of people without asking first. Second, each of you will interview at least two residents about how the fishing industry has changed. Before every interview, explain the project, show the consent form, and make it clear that people can stop at any time. If someone says no, simply thank them and move on. Please don't try to persuade them. Finally, write up your field notes the same evening, while the details are still fresh. We'll gather at the community center every night at eight to share what we've learned. Your final report is due two weeks after we return, and it should include at least three direct quotations from your interviews.
