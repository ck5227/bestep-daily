諮商中心與壓力調適

今日單字

confidential adj. 機密的；保密的
What you tell your counselor is completely confidential.
cope with phr. v. 應付；處理
The workshop teaches students how to cope with exam stress.
overwhelmed adj. 不知所措的；承受不住的
I felt overwhelmed by three reports due in the same week.
postpone v. 延後；延期
The training session was postponed because of the fire drill.
anxiety n. 焦慮
This month's workshop focuses on test anxiety.
walk-in n. 未預約的來訪者；臨時現場服務
The center accepts walk-ins, but booking ahead is faster.
refer v. 轉介；轉診
After one session, she was referred to regular counseling.
put off phr. v. 拖延；延後
Stop putting off your report and start with a small step.

閱讀 Reading

1. If you feel overwhelmed, the counseling center can help you find healthy ways to ______ with stress.

  1. handle
  2. reduce
  3. relieve
  4. cope
看答案與詳解

正解 D　空格後接 with，只有 cope with（應付、處理）搭配正確。handle、reduce、relieve 都是及物動詞，直接接受詞，不加 with。

2. Everything you share in a counseling session is kept strictly ______ and will not appear on your academic record.

  1. confident
  2. confidential
  3. conventional
  4. considerate
看答案與詳解

正解 B　「保密的」是 confidential，符合不會出現在學業紀錄的語意。confident 是「有自信的」，拼字相近易混淆；conventional（傳統的）、considerate（體貼的）語意不合。

From: Student Counseling Center

To: Peer Listener Program Trainees

Subject: Training Session 2 – Date Change

Dear Peer Listener trainees,

Because the campus-wide fire drill has been scheduled for the same morning, the second training session originally planned for Saturday, November 14, has been postponed to Saturday, November 21. The time (9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.) and the location (Room 402, Student Activity Center) remain the same.

Please remember that trainees must attend all three sessions to receive the Peer Listener certificate, which also counts as eight hours of service learning. If you cannot attend on the new date, please reply to this email by November 17 so that we can arrange a make-up session with another group.

Before the session, please complete the online reading on active listening skills. We will be doing role-plays in pairs, so wear comfortable clothes and bring a water bottle.

Best regards,

Amy Huang

Peer Program Coordinator

3. Why was the second training session moved?

  1. The trainer is sick.
  2. The room is being repaired.
  3. Too few trainees registered.
  4. Another campus event is happening that morning.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中說 the campus-wide fire drill has been scheduled for the same morning，即當天早上有全校消防演練。地點不變，可見不是教室維修（B 錯）；生病與人數不足均未提及。

4. The word "postponed" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. canceled
  2. moved earlier
  3. delayed
  4. shortened
看答案與詳解

正解 C　從 11 月 14 日改到 11 月 21 日，是往後延，postponed = delayed。課程仍會舉行（非 canceled），時間長度也不變（非 shortened）。

5. What should a trainee who cannot attend on November 21 do?

  1. Reply to the email by November 17
  2. Attend the session on November 14
  3. Complete eight hours of service learning
  4. Submit a report on active listening
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第三段說無法出席新日期者須在 11 月 17 日前回信以安排補課。八小時是證書可抵的服務學習時數（C 錯）；閱讀只需完成線上教材，不用交報告（D 錯）。

Students Turn to Counseling as Midterm Pressure Peaks

In the two weeks after midterm exams, the university's Student Counseling Center received more than 300 appointment requests, almost twice the number from the same period last year. Director Lin Mei-hua says the increase is not necessarily bad news. "It shows that students are more willing to ask for help," she explained. "Ten years ago, many students felt ashamed to walk through our door."

Still, the rise in demand has created a problem: waiting time. Last month, some students had to wait up to two weeks for a first appointment. To shorten the wait, the center has introduced a "single-session" model. Instead of committing to a series of weekly meetings, students can book one 50-minute session that focuses on a specific issue, such as exam anxiety or a conflict with a roommate. If they need more support afterward, they can be referred to regular counseling.

The center has also trained 40 student volunteers as peer listeners. These volunteers do not give professional advice, but they are available in the library every weekday evening to talk with classmates who simply need someone to listen. "Sometimes talking to someone my own age feels less stressful," said Wang Yu-ting, a sophomore in the Department of Chemistry.

Not everyone is satisfied, however. Several graduate students say the center's opening hours do not fit their lab schedules, which often run late into the evening. In response, the center plans to add Saturday morning sessions starting next semester. Director Lin also encourages professors to mention the center's services in class, especially in the weeks before final exams, when stress levels usually rise again.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Why midterm exams should be canceled
  2. How students can become professional counselors
  3. How the counseling center is responding to higher demand
  4. The history of the university library
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文描述諮商需求倍增後，中心推出單次諮商、培訓同儕傾聽員並規劃週六時段等因應措施。A、B、D 皆非主旨。

7. According to the article, what is a "single-session" appointment?

  1. A group workshop for up to ten students
  2. A one-time meeting about a particular problem
  3. An online chat with a peer listener
  4. A weekly meeting for the whole semester
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段說學生可預約一次 50 分鐘、focuses on a specific issue 的會談，即針對特定問題的單次會談。它正是用來取代每週一系列會談（D 錯）。

8. Why does Director Lin see the rise in requests as partly positive?

  1. The center received more government funding.
  2. Fewer students failed their midterm exams.
  3. Professors sent more students to the center.
  4. Students are less ashamed to seek help than before.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　主任說 It shows that students are more willing to ask for help，並對比十年前學生 felt ashamed。文中未提經費、考試成績或教授轉介。

9. Which TWO changes has the center made or planned? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Hiring 40 new professional counselors
  2. Training students as peer listeners
  3. Closing the center on weekday evenings
  4. Adding Saturday morning sessions
  5. Requiring all students to attend counseling
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　第三段提到培訓 40 名學生志工擔任同儕傾聽員（B），第四段說下學期將增加週六上午時段（D）。40 人是學生志工而非專業諮商師（A 錯）；C、E 文中未提。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I'll count them again later.
  2. It's on the third floor of the Student Activity Building.
  3. Yes, the center was really helpful.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問諮商中心在哪裡，B 回答具體位置最合理。A 用 count 與 counseling 音近混淆；C 以 Yes 回答 Wh- 問句且答非所問。

聽力原文

Where is the counseling center located?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I pointed it out to him yesterday.
  2. The counselor's name is Ms. Lin.
  3. Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead is faster.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問是否需要預約，C 說明可臨時前往但預約較快，直接回應。A 以 pointed 與 appointment 音近混淆；B 重複 counselor 但沒回答問題。

聽力原文

Do I need an appointment to talk to a counselor?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Maybe you should try one of the stress workshops.
  2. The midterm starts at nine.
  3. The sleeping bags are on sale this week.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　對方說期中考後睡不好，A 建議參加紓壓工作坊是合理回應。B 重複 midterm、C 重複 sleeping，皆與失眠困擾無關。

聽力原文

I've been having trouble sleeping since midterms started.

4. What will the man probably do?

  1. Attend a morning session
  2. Come to the center on Thursday evening
  3. Change his class schedule
  4. Call back next Tuesday
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生說 Thursday works for me，而晚間時段在週二、週四，故會週四晚上來。他早上有課（A 錯），沒提要改課表或回電（C、D 錯）。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to sign up for a counseling session, but I have classes every morning. That's fine. We have evening sessions on Tuesdays and Thursdays until eight thirty. Thursday works for me. Can I ask for a male counselor? Sure, I'll note that on your form.

5. Why does the woman recommend the workshop?

  1. It is required for all students.
  2. It provides free meals.
  3. She found it useful herself.
  4. The man asked her about it.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女生說 I went last semester, and it really helped me，因親身受益而推薦。對話未提必修或供餐（A、B 錯），也是她主動提起而非男生詢問（D 錯）。

聽力原文

You look exhausted. Is everything OK? Not really. I have three reports due next week, and I keep putting them off. The counseling center has a time management workshop this Friday. I went last semester, and it really helped me break big tasks into small steps. Hmm, maybe I'll check it out.

6. What should students do if it rains on Wednesday?

  1. Come back on Thursday
  2. Register online in advance
  3. Go to the library lobby
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播說 In case of rain, the event will move to the lobby of the library。活動不需報名（B 錯），也沒有改到週四（A 錯）。

聽力原文

Attention, students. As part of Mental Health Week, the counseling center will hold a therapy dog event on the main lawn this Wednesday from noon to two p.m. In case of rain, the event will move to the lobby of the library. No registration is needed, but please wash your hands before and after petting the dogs.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To announce the midterm exam results
  2. To introduce the services the counseling center provides
  3. To recruit students as peer counselors
  4. To explain a new rule about class attendance
看答案與詳解

正解 B　整段依序介紹免費個別諮商、視訊諮商、英語諮商與團體工作坊，目的是介紹中心服務。期中考只是開場背景（A 錯），未提招募或出席規定（C、D 錯）。

8. How many free individual sessions can each student have per year?

  1. Five
  2. Six
  3. Eight
  4. Ten
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說 up to eight free one-on-one sessions each academic year。Ten 是每個工作坊的名額，屬干擾數字。

9. Which TWO services does the speaker mention? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Evening video sessions
  2. Free medical checkups
  3. Counseling in English for international students
  4. Private tutoring for exams
  5. Workshops for parents
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　講者提到晚間視訊諮商（A）及國際生可申請說英語的諮商師（C）。未提健康檢查或課業家教（B、D）；只說不會告訴家長，並無家長工作坊（E）。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Doctor Chen, one of the counselors at the Student Counseling Center. Since many of you have just finished your midterms, I'd like to talk about where you can get support this semester. First, every student can have up to eight free one-on-one sessions each academic year. Everything you share with us is confidential, which means we won't tell your professors or your parents without your permission, except in an emergency. Second, if you prefer not to talk face to face, we now offer video sessions in the evening, and international students can request a counselor who speaks English. Third, we run small group workshops every week. This month, the topics are test anxiety and healthy sleep habits. Each group has only ten seats, so please sign up early through the student portal. Finally, remember that you don't have to be in a crisis to visit us. Many students come simply because they feel a little lost. Asking for help early is a sign of strength, not weakness.
