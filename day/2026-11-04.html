運動中心與校運會

今日單字

postpone v. 延期；延後
The track events were postponed until Friday because of the rain.
undergo v. 接受（檢查、測驗）；經歷
All new players must undergo a basic fitness test.
minimize v. 使降到最低
The center set up a temporary room to minimize inconvenience.
renovate v. 整修；翻新
The fitness room will be renovated over winter break.
participation n. 參與；參加
Student participation in sports day nearly doubled this year.
enthusiastic adj. 熱衷的；熱情的
Not everyone on the track team is enthusiastic about the new events.
coordinator n. 協調員；統籌者
Ask the student activities coordinator if you want to volunteer.
on duty phr. 值班；執勤中
Lifeguards are on duty whenever the pool is open.

閱讀 Reading

1. Because of the typhoon warning, the outdoor events of sports day have been ______ until next Wednesday.

  1. prevented
  2. postponed
  3. provided
  4. promoted
看答案與詳解

正解 B　postpone ... until 表示「延期到…」，符合颱風警報的情境。prevent 阻止、provide 提供、promote 推廣，皆不能接 until 表示延後。

2. Before joining the varsity basketball team, every player must ______ a basic fitness test and a health check.

  1. qualify
  2. apply
  3. recover
  4. undergo
看答案與詳解

正解 D　undergo a test 指「接受測驗、檢查」，為及物動詞可直接接受詞。qualify、apply 需接 for，recover 意為康復，語意與用法皆不合。

From: Campus Sports Center

To: All Registered Students

Subject: Fitness Room Renovation and Temporary Arrangements

Dear Students,

We are pleased to announce that the fitness room on the second floor will be renovated from November 16 to December 6. New treadmills, rowing machines, and a stretching area will be installed. During this period, the fitness room will be completely closed.

To minimize inconvenience, a temporary workout space with basic equipment will be set up in Multipurpose Room B on the first floor. Because the space is smaller, each visit will be limited to 40 minutes, and no more than 20 people may use it at one time. Online reservations will be required starting November 14.

Students who have purchased a monthly personal training package will receive a one-week extension at no extra charge. We apologize for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience.

Campus Sports Center

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To explain a closure and the temporary arrangements
  2. To advertise new personal training packages
  3. To ask students to help move equipment
  4. To announce higher membership fees
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信中說明健身房整修期間關閉，並提供一樓臨時運動空間等替代安排，選 A。B 只提到既有私人教練方案延長一週；C、D 未提及。

4. The word "minimize" in the email is closest in meaning to

  1. explain
  2. measure
  3. reduce
  4. notice
看答案與詳解

正解 C　To minimize inconvenience 意為「把不便降到最低」，minimize 即 reduce。其他選項代入後語意不通。

5. What is true about the temporary workout space?

  1. It will open on November 14.
  2. Each visit there is limited in length.
  3. It is located on the second floor.
  4. It has the new rowing machines.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中說 each visit will be limited to 40 minutes，選 B。A 11 月 14 日是開始線上預約的日期；C 在一樓；D 新划船機是裝在整修後的健身房。

More Than Medals: Why Our Sports Day Is Changing

For decades, the annual sports day at our university has been dominated by a small group of athletes. Most students simply sat in the stands, checked their phones, or skipped the event entirely to catch up on sleep. This year, the Office of Physical Education hopes to change that.

According to Professor Chen Mei-ling, who leads the planning committee, participation fell to just 18 percent of undergraduates last year. "Students told us the events felt too competitive," she explained. "If you aren't a fast runner, there's no reason to show up." To address this, the committee has added several fun events, such as a three-legged race, a tug-of-war between departments, and a relay in which each team must include at least two members who have never joined a school competition.

Another major change is the introduction of a points system. Each department now earns points not only for winning but also for the number of students who take part. The department with the highest total will receive funding for a new student lounge.

Not everyone is enthusiastic. Some members of the track team worry that the new events will take time away from serious competition. "We train all year for this," said junior sprinter Lin Yu-hsuan. "I hope the hundred-meter final doesn't get pushed to the end of the day when everyone has gone home." The committee has promised that the main races will still be held in the morning.

Whether the changes succeed will be clear on November 25. For now, early registration numbers look promising: more than 1,200 students have already signed up, nearly double last year's figure at the same point.

6. What problem is the article mainly about?

  1. Too many injuries during races
  2. A lack of funding for the stadium
  3. Arguments between departments
  4. Low student participation in sports day
看答案與詳解

正解 D　文章開頭指出多數學生只在看台滑手機或乾脆不來，去年參與率只有 18%，改革皆為提高參與，選 D。其餘選項文中未提及為主要問題。

7. How will departments earn points under the new system?

  1. Only by winning races
  2. By both winning and having many participants
  3. By selling tickets to spectators
  4. By training all year round
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第三段說 not only for winning but also for the number of students who take part，選 B。A 的 Only 與原文相反；D 是短跑選手的話，與計分無關。

8. Why is Lin Yu-hsuan concerned?

  1. She may not be allowed to compete.
  2. The fun events are too difficult for her.
  3. Her main race might be held when few people are watching.
  4. The track team will lose its funding.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　她擔心百米決賽被延到傍晚大家都回家了，即觀眾很少，選 C。其他選項文中都沒有提到。

9. Which TWO changes to sports day are mentioned in the article? Choose TWO.

  1. Some relay teams must include first-time competitors.
  2. Spectators will have to buy tickets.
  3. Departments are rewarded for the number of participants.
  4. The hundred-meter final will move to the afternoon.
  5. The event will be held over two days.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　文中提到接力隊須含至少兩名從未參賽者，以及依參與人數給系所加分，故選 A、C。D 委員會承諾主要賽事仍在上午；B、E 未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, the staff scan it at the front desk.
  2. I study near the center of campus.
  3. The gym class starts at nine.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問使用健身房是否需帶學生證，A 回答需要並說明櫃檯會刷卡，最合理。B 重複 center 但答非所問；C 重複 gym 但談上課時間。

聽力原文

Do I need to bring my student ID to use the gym at the sports center?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The sign is right next to the stadium.
  2. Probably the relay and the long jump.
  3. It was a great day for sports.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問校運會要報名哪些項目，B 直接回答接力與跳遠。A 用 sign 與 signing 音近混淆；C 重複 day 與 sports，且時態錯誤。

聽力原文

Which events are you signing up for at sports day next week?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, it's close to the dorms.
  2. I'd rather swim in the evening.
  3. They're cleaning the filters before the competition.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Why 問原因，C 說明比賽前清洗過濾器，回答了原因。A 用 Yes 回答 Why 問句，且 close（靠近）與 closed 混淆；B 只重複 swim。

聽力原文

Why is the swimming pool closed this morning?

4. What will the man probably do?

  1. Practice for sports day on Friday evening.
  2. Reserve a court for Saturday morning.
  3. Book a court for two hours on Friday.
  4. Cancel his existing reservation.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　週五晚上場地已被校運練習占滿，女士建議週六早上，男士說 Saturday works for me，故選 B。C 週五已無場地且一次只能訂一小時；A、D 皆未提及。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to book a badminton court for Friday evening. Sorry, all the courts are reserved for sports day practice from six to nine. You could try Saturday morning, though. Saturday works for me. Can I book it for two hours? Students can only book one hour at a time, but you can add another hour if no one is waiting.

5. What will the man do at sports day?

  1. Run the five thousand meters.
  2. Go back to the health center.
  3. Work as a timekeeper.
  4. Coach the department team.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男士說 I'll keep time at the finish line，即擔任計時員，選 C。A 因腳踝受傷無法參賽；B 是已經去過的地方；D 他是告訴教練，不是當教練。

聽力原文

Are you still running the five thousand meters at sports day? I wanted to, but I hurt my ankle during practice on Monday. The doctor at the health center told me to rest for two weeks. That's too bad. Maybe you could help our department team in another way? Yeah, I've already told the coach I'll keep time at the finish line.

6. What is true about tomorrow's schedule?

  1. The morning track and field events will take place on Friday.
  2. The opening ceremony will start later than planned.
  3. All ball games have been canceled.
  4. The ceremony will be held on the outdoor track.

正解 A　廣播說上午田徑項目 postponed until Friday，選 A。B 開幕仍在八點半；C 球類照常進行；D 開幕改到室內體育館。

Attention, all students. Due to heavy rain expected tomorrow, the opening ceremony of the university sports day will move from the outdoor track to the indoor gymnasium. The ceremony will still begin at eight thirty. However, all track and field events scheduled for the morning are postponed until Friday. Ball games will continue as planned. Please check the sports day website for updated times.

7. What must students do before using the fitness room for the first time?

  1. Buy a semester pass.
  2. Show a health certificate.
  3. Pay a small entry fee.
  4. Attend a short safety session.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者說 you must complete a short safety session before your first visit，選 D。A 學期證是游泳池才需要；健身房免費，故 C 錯；B 未提及。

8. When does the sports center close on weekends?

  1. At six in the evening.
  2. At eight in the evening.
  3. At ten at night.
  4. At noon.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　週末開放時間為早上八點到晚上六點，選 A。C 晚上十點是平日的關門時間；B 的 eight 是週末開門時間，用來混淆。

9. According to the speaker, what do volunteers receive? Choose TWO.

  1. A free swimming pass.
  2. A free T-shirt.
  3. Priority court booking.
  4. Service learning hours.
  5. Lunch on sports day.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　講者說志工可獲得 free T-shirt，且時數可計入 service learning requirement，故選 B、D。游泳學期證需付費購買，其餘選項皆未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to the sports center orientation. I'm Linda, the student activities coordinator here. Let me start with the basics. The center is open from six in the morning to ten at night on weekdays, and from eight in the morning to six in the evening on weekends. To enter, simply scan your student ID at the gate. The fitness room is free for all students, but you must complete a short safety session before your first visit. These sessions run every Tuesday and Thursday at noon and last about thirty minutes. The swimming pool is a little different. You'll need to buy a semester pass, which costs five hundred dollars, and you must wear a swimming cap. Lifeguards are on duty at all times, but please don't swim alone late at night. Finally, we're looking for student volunteers to help with sports day later this month. Volunteers receive a free T-shirt, and they can count the hours toward their service learning requirement. If you're interested, sign up at the front desk by this Friday.
