失物招領與學生證補辦

今日單字

claim v. 認領；聲稱擁有
Please bring your student ID when you come to claim your umbrella.
unclaimed adj. 無人認領的
Unclaimed items will be donated after sixty days.
turn in phr. v. 交出；上繳
Someone turned in a black wallet at the front desk this morning.
replacement n. 補發品；替換物
A replacement ID card costs two hundred NT dollars.
deactivate v. 停用；使失效
Your old card is deactivated as soon as you report it missing.
belongings n. 個人物品；隨身財物
Don't leave your belongings unattended in the library.
valuable adj. 貴重的；有價值的
Label anything valuable with your name or email address.
circulation desk n. （圖書館）流通櫃台
Lost items found in the library are kept at the circulation desk.

閱讀 Reading

1. If you find a wallet on campus, please ______ it to the security office instead of leaving it where you found it.

  1. take off
  2. give up
  3. turn in
  4. put away
看答案與詳解

正解 C　turn in something to 某處 意為「把（拾獲物）交給」，符合把錢包交到校安中心。take off 脫下／起飛；give up 放棄；put away 收好，皆不合語意。

2. Students must show a valid ID to ______ any item from the lost and found, so that staff can confirm it belongs to them.

  1. borrow
  2. donate
  3. report
  4. claim
看答案與詳解

正解 D　claim 表示「認領」，出示證件以證明物品屬於自己。borrow 借用、donate 捐贈、report 通報，都與「確認物品屬於本人」不符。

From: Registrar's Office

To: Chen Yu-ting

Subject: Your student ID replacement request

Dear Ms. Chen,

We have received your request for a replacement student ID card, submitted on November 16. Unfortunately, we are unable to process it yet because the photo you uploaded does not meet our requirements. Photos must have been taken within the last six months and must show your full face against a plain white background. Sunglasses and hats are not allowed.

Please upload a new photo through the student portal by Monday, November 23. Once your photo is approved, your new card will be printed within three working days, and you will receive a text message when it is ready for pickup at Counter 4 of the Administration Building. The replacement fee of NT$200 can be paid at the counter in cash or with a mobile payment app.

Please note that your old card was deactivated when you reported it missing. If you find it later, it can no longer be used and should be returned to our office.

Sincerely, Registrar's Office

3. Why has Ms. Chen's request not been processed yet?

  1. She has not paid the fee.
  2. Her photo does not meet the requirements.
  3. She submitted it after the deadline.
  4. She reported the wrong card number.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中第一段說明無法處理是因為上傳的照片不符規定。費用是領證時在櫃台繳；11 月 23 日是補傳照片的期限，不是她遲交；卡號問題未提及。

4. The word "deactivated" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. printed
  2. replaced
  3. located
  4. switched off
看答案與詳解

正解 D　後文說舊卡「不能再使用」，可知 deactivated 指「停用」，即 switched off。printed 印製、replaced 更換、located 找到，都不符合「無法再使用」的語意。

5. What is true about the new card?

  1. It will be mailed to her dorm.
  2. It can be paid for only by credit card.
  3. She will receive a text message when it is ready.
  4. It was printed on November 16.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中說卡片可領取時會收到簡訊通知。卡片需到行政大樓 4 號櫃台領取，不是郵寄；可用現金或行動支付；11 月 16 日是申請日。

Lost and Found Goes Digital: New System Reunites Students With Their Belongings

For years, students at Ming Feng University who lost an umbrella or a calculator had only one option: walk to the security office, describe the item to a staff member, and hope it was sitting on a shelf in the back room. This semester, that process has changed. In September, the Student Affairs Office launched FindIt, an online lost and found system that lets students search for items by location, date, and category.

Under the new system, any staff member who receives a lost item takes a photo of it and uploads it within twenty-four hours. Valuable items, such as phones and wallets, are shown only as general descriptions to prevent false claims. Students who believe an item is theirs must answer a few questions, such as the color of the phone case or the contents of the wallet, before they can collect it.

The results have been encouraging. According to the Student Affairs Office, the return rate rose from 38 percent last spring to 61 percent in the first two months of this semester. "Before, many students simply gave up because they didn't know where to look," said Hsu Mei-ling, the system's project manager. "Now they can check from their phones in a minute."

Not everyone is satisfied, however. Some students say the photos are sometimes blurry, and items found in department buildings still take several days to appear online. The office plans to train department assistants to upload items directly by next semester. It is also considering a feature that would send automatic alerts when an item matching a student's description is uploaded.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A rise in thefts on campus
  2. A training program for department assistants
  3. Student complaints about blurry photos
  4. A new online system for finding lost items
看答案與詳解

正解 D　全文介紹學務處推出的線上失物系統 FindIt 及其成效與改進方向。系助理培訓與照片模糊只是第四段的細節；竊盜案增加未提及。

7. Why are valuable items shown only as general descriptions?

  1. To protect the privacy of staff members
  2. To prevent people from falsely claiming them
  3. Because their photos are often blurry
  4. Because they are stored in a different building
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段明說貴重物品只顯示概略描述是為了 prevent false claims（防止冒領）。照片模糊是學生對整體系統的抱怨，與此無關；員工隱私與存放地點未提及。

8. What can be inferred about students before FindIt was introduced?

  1. They had to pay a fee to claim their items.
  2. They could search for items by category.
  3. Many of them did not try to recover their lost items.
  4. Most of their items were returned within a day.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　專案經理說以前許多學生因不知去哪找而直接放棄，可推知很多人沒有嘗試找回。依類別搜尋是新系統的功能；收費與一天內歸還皆無根據，且舊歸還率只有 38%。

9. According to the article, which TWO changes is the office planning or considering? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Training department assistants to upload items
  2. Removing photos of all valuable items
  3. Moving the lost and found to the library
  4. Sending alerts when a matching item is uploaded
  5. Charging students a fee to use the system
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　第四段提到計畫下學期訓練系助理直接上傳，並考慮新增符合描述物品的自動通知。貴重物品本來就只顯示描述，並非新計畫；搬到圖書館與收費皆未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, the report was due last week.
  2. I think it was a brown leather one.
  3. You're in the right place. Please fill out this form.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　對方詢問是否在此通報遺失錢包，C 確認地點並請她填表最合理。A 重複 report 但指作業報告；B 描述錢包外觀，應是失主自己說的話，不是回應。

聽力原文

Excuse me, is this where I report a lost wallet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I picked it up at the bookstore.
  2. It should be ready in three working days.
  3. Your card number is on the back.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問「何時」可領補發學生證，B 回答三個工作天後，直接回應時間。A 重複 pick up 但答非所問；C 重複 card，未提到時間。

聽力原文

When can I pick up my replacement student ID card?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Turn left at the end of the hall.
  2. Let me check the shelf behind me.
  3. It's supposed to rain this afternoon.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　詢問今早是否有人交來黑色雨傘，B「我查一下後面的架子」是合理回應。A 用 turn 與 turned in 混淆；C 由 umbrella 聯想到下雨，但沒有回答問題。

聽力原文

Has anyone turned in a black umbrella this morning?

4. What will the woman most likely do next?

  4. Go to the department office

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To announce a new security system
  2. To give advice on preventing and handling lost items
  3. To explain how to apply for a student ID
  4. To recruit volunteers for the security office
看答案與詳解

正解 B　林警官先說明遺失物狀況，再提出四點建議，主旨是如何預防與處理遺失物。學生證只是建議之一；新系統與招募志工皆未提及。

8. According to the speaker, what happened last semester?

  1. Most lost items were returned within a week.
  2. The office moved to a new building.
  3. Forty-two umbrellas were stolen.
  4. Fewer than half of the items were claimed.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者說上學期收到八百多件物品，但不到一半被領回。42 是學生證的數量，不是雨傘；也沒有提到一週內歸還或搬遷。

9. Which TWO things does the speaker advise students to do? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Visit the office every weekday at noon
  2. Label valuable belongings with contact information
  3. Carry a spare student ID card
  4. Keep found items for sixty days
  5. Check the website before going to the office
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　講者建議在貴重物品貼上姓名或 email，並在親自前往前先查看線上失物頁面。中午是辦公室上傳照片的時間；保管六十天是辦公室的規定，不是給學生的建議；備用學生證未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Officer Lin from the Campus Security Office, and I'd like to spend a few minutes on something that affects almost every student at some point: losing things. Last semester, our office received more than eight hundred items, but fewer than half were ever claimed. The most common items were umbrellas, water bottles, and earphones, but we also received forty-two student ID cards. So here's what we recommend. First, put your name or email on anything valuable. A simple sticker makes it much easier for us to contact you. Second, if you lose something, check our online lost and found page before you visit us. We upload photos of new items every weekday by noon. Third, if you lose your student ID, report it on the student portal right away, so no one else can use it to borrow books or enter your dorm. Finally, please remember that we keep items for only sixty days. After that, they're donated or recycled.
