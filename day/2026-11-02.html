全英語授課（EMI）

今日單字

glossary n. 詞彙表；術語表
The professor posts a glossary of key terms before every lecture.
fluent adj. 流利的
You don't need to be fluent in English to do well in an EMI course.
rehearse v. 排練；預演
She rehearsed her presentation twice with a tutor.
subtitle n. 字幕
The lecture recordings come with English subtitles.
preview v. 預習；預先查看
Previewing the slides helps you follow the lecture more easily.
bilingual adj. 雙語的
Students may use a bilingual dictionary during the exam.
adjustment n. 調整
The teacher made a few adjustments after reading the survey results.
anonymously adv. 匿名地
Students can submit questions anonymously through the learning platform.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before each EMI lecture, Professor Wu posts a ______ of key terms with their Chinese translations.

  1. schedule
  2. glossary
  3. diploma
  4. receipt
看答案與詳解

正解 B　「附中文翻譯的關鍵詞」清單即 glossary（詞彙表）。schedule 是時間表、diploma 是文憑、receipt 是收據，都不合語意。

2. Students who find it hard to follow the lecture may ______ the recording later at their own pace.

  1. reject
  2. remain
  3. refund
  4. review
看答案與詳解

正解 D　跟不上課的學生可以之後依自己的步調「複習」錄影，review 正確。reject 拒絕、remain 保持、refund 退款，皆不合理。

From: Professor Daniel Kao

To: Students in Introduction to Economics (EMI)

Subject: Changes to our class starting next week

Dear all, Thank you for your hard work on the first quiz. Based on your feedback in the mid-semester survey, I am making a few adjustments to how our class runs.

Starting next Monday, I will speak more slowly and pause after each main section so that you can ask questions. I will also upload the slides by Sunday evening instead of on the morning of class. Several of you said the readings were too long, so I have shortened them and added a short video summary for each chapter.

Please note that the final project report must still be written in English. However, your group presentation may include up to five minutes of Chinese if you need to clarify a difficult concept. Feel free to come to my office hours on Wednesdays from 1 to 3 p.m. if you have any concerns. Best, Professor Kao

3. Why is Professor Kao changing the class?

  1. Because the quiz scores were low
  2. Because of students' responses to a survey
  3. Because the department asked him to
  4. Because the class is moving to a new room
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第一段 Based on your feedback in the mid-semester survey 說明是根據學生問卷回饋，答案 B。信中稱讚小考努力，並未說成績差。

4. In paragraph 1, the word "adjustments" is closest in meaning to

  1. complaints
  2. payments
  3. changes
  4. requirements
看答案與詳解

正解 C　adjustments 指「調整」，後文列出放慢語速、提早上傳投影片等改變，最接近 changes。

5. What is true about the final project?

  1. The report may be written in Chinese.
  2. It must be completed individually.
  3. It is due next Monday.
  4. Its written report must be in English.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第三段 the final project report must still be written in English，答案 D。只有口頭報告可用最多五分鐘中文；提到 group presentation，所以不是個人作業。

English-Taught Courses Grow, But Are Students Keeping Up?

Three years ago, only a handful of courses at our university were taught entirely in English. This semester, the number has risen to more than 120, part of a national plan to prepare students for international careers. But a new survey by the Student Association suggests that the rapid growth has left some students struggling.

Of the 850 students who responded, 62 percent said they understood "most" of what was said in their English-medium classes. However, nearly a third admitted that they often relied on classmates or translation apps to keep up, and 18 percent said they had avoided EMI courses altogether because they feared getting lower grades.

Professors see the issue from a different angle. "Many students understand more than they think," said Dr. Grace Lee, who teaches environmental science in English. "The real problem is confidence. They are afraid to speak up because they worry about making mistakes in front of their classmates." To address this, Dr. Lee lets students submit questions anonymously through the learning platform before each class.

The university has responded by expanding its support services. Starting next semester, every EMI course will be assigned a bilingual teaching assistant, and the Language Center will offer free weekly workshops on academic listening and presentation skills. The university is also considering a policy that would allow students to take one EMI course per semester on a pass/fail basis.

Not everyone is convinced. Some students argue that the courses should simply offer more explanations in Mandarin. Still, Student Association president Kevin Tsai believes the trend is positive. "Employers want graduates who can work in English," he said. "We just need the right support to get there."

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A national plan to hire more foreign professors
  2. Why the number of EMI courses has dropped
  3. How students are coping with English-taught courses and how the school is helping
  4. The results of a campus English proficiency test
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文談學生在 EMI 課的困難、教授看法與學校支援措施，答案 C。EMI 課程是增加而非減少；文中是問卷，不是英檢。

8. What does Dr. Lee believe is the main problem?

  1. Students do not know enough vocabulary.
  2. The reading assignments are too long.
  3. The learning platform is difficult to use.
  4. Students lack confidence to speak in class.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　Dr. Lee 說 The real problem is confidence，學生怕在同學面前犯錯而不敢發言，答案 D。學習平台只是她用來匿名收問題的工具。

9. Which TWO measures will the university introduce next semester? (Choose TWO.)

  1. A bilingual teaching assistant for every EMI course
  2. Required English tests for all first-year students
  3. Free weekly workshops on academic listening and presentations
  4. Anonymous question boxes in every classroom
  5. Removing grades from all EMI courses
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　第四段指出下學期每門 EMI 課配雙語助教（A），語言中心開免費工作坊（C）。匿名提問只是 Dr. Lee 個人做法；pass/fail 仍在考慮且只限一門，非取消所有成績。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. She's an entirely different teacher this year.
  2. I bought the English textbook at the campus bookstore.
  3. Mostly, but she reviews the difficult terms in Mandarin.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問統計課是否全英語授課，C 說「大部分是，但難的術語會用中文複習」，直接回答。A 重複 entirely 但答非所問；B 重複 English，只談買課本。

聽力原文

Is Professor Lin's statistics course taught entirely in English?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Sure, I'll email them to you after lunch.
  2. The slide on the playground is broken.
  3. Today's lecture was in Room five-oh-two.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　請對方寄投影片，A 答應午餐後寄出，最合理。B 的 slide 是「溜滑梯」，屬同字異義陷阱；C 重複 today's lecture 卻只說教室。

聽力原文

Could you send me the slides from today's lecture?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Because the group meets in the economics building.
  2. Good idea. How about Thursday evenings?
  3. I studied economics in high school.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Why don't we… 是提議而非真的問原因，B 表示贊成並提出時間。A 用 Because 回答是誤解句型；C 重複 economics 但無關。

聽力原文

Why don't we form a study group for our EMI economics class?

4. What does the man suggest the woman do?

  1. Switch to a class taught in Mandarin
  2. Practice with a tutor at a campus center
  3. Ask the professor for more time
  4. Record her presentation at home
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生建議去寫作與口說中心和家教「rehearse（排練）」，即 B。對話沒有提到換課、延期或在家錄影。

聽力原文

I'm worried about my presentation in the EMI marketing class. My English isn't very fluent. Have you tried the Writing and Speaking Center? They let you rehearse your presentation with a tutor. Really? Do I need to book in advance? Yes, usually at least two days ahead, through their website.

  2. Right after each class
  3. Every Friday

Excuse me, Professor Chen. I missed a few points in today's lecture because you spoke quite fast. Thanks for telling me. I upload the lecture recordings every Friday, and they have English subtitles. That would really help. Will the glossary be online too? Yes, I'll post it with this week's reading list.

  2. To announce changes to the graduation requirements

  4. To explain how to borrow books from the library

  1. A first-year student
  2. A program coordinator
  3. A teaching assistant
  4. A private language tutor

8. What does the speaker recommend students do?

  1. Record every lecture on their phones
  2. Take an English test before the semester
  3. Look over the slides before class
  4. Avoid using Mandarin during office hours
看答案與詳解

正解 C　她說 I strongly recommend previewing them（投影片），即 C。她明說 office hours 可用中文，所以 D 錯；A、B 未提及。

9. According to the talk, which TWO statements are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Teaching assistants hold office hours twice a week.
  2. Students must speak English during office hours.
  3. Students' accents will affect their grades.
  4. Some courses allow bilingual dictionaries during exams.
  5. Students can switch to a Mandarin section at any time.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　助教每週兩次 office hours（A）；許多課考試可用雙語字典（D）。office hours 可用中文、口音不計分，換班要三週後且須有中文班，故 B、C、E 錯。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to the first-year EMI orientation. I'm Doctor Huang, the coordinator of the English-Medium Instruction program. Starting next semester, about a third of your required courses will be taught in English. I know many of you feel nervous about that, so let me explain what support is available. First, every EMI course has a teaching assistant who holds office hours twice a week. You can ask questions in English or in Mandarin. The goal is understanding, not perfect grammar. Second, most professors share their slides at least one day before class, so I strongly recommend previewing them and writing down any unfamiliar terms. Finally, you won't be graded on your accent. Exams focus on subject knowledge, and in many courses you may use a bilingual dictionary during tests. If a course still feels too difficult after three weeks, talk to your advisor about switching to a Mandarin section, as long as one is offered. Any questions so far?
