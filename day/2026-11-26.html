教科書與影印著作權

今日單字

copyright n. 著作權
Sharing scanned textbooks online may violate copyright.
second-hand adj. 二手的
I saved a lot by buying second-hand books.
edition n. 版本；版次
Make sure you buy the latest edition of the textbook.
buyback n. 回收；買回
The bookstore holds a textbook buyback at the end of each semester.
course reserve n. 課程指定參考書
The book is on course reserve, so you can borrow it for three hours.
credit v. 註明出處；歸功於
Always credit the source of any image you use.
proof n. 證明；證據
You may need to show proof that you own the book.
uneven adj. 不一致的；參差不齊的
The quality of free online materials can be uneven.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who want to sell their old textbooks should make sure no pages are ______ before bringing them to the bookstore.

  1. missing
  2. folding
  3. lending
  4. printing
看答案與詳解

正解 A　no pages are missing 意為「沒有缺頁」，符合二手書收購條件，選 A。folding、lending、printing 放入句中語意不通。

2. If you use a chart from another study in your report, you need to ______ the original author in your reference list.

  1. delay
  2. credit
  3. borrow
  4. attend
看答案與詳解

正解 B　credit the original author 意為「註明原作者」，符合引用規範，選 B。delay（延遲）、borrow（借）、attend（出席）皆不合語意。

From: Campus Copy Center <copycenter@university.edu.tw>

To: All Students

Subject: Printing Services During Final Exam Weeks

Dear Students,

To meet the high demand during final exam weeks, the Copy Center will extend its hours from December 21 to January 9. We will be open from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. on weekdays and from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekends.

To reduce waiting time, please upload your files through our online ordering system at least one day in advance. Orders submitted online can be picked up at the express counter. Walk-in customers may have to wait more than thirty minutes during busy hours.

Please note that, in line with copyright regulations, we cannot copy more than one chapter of a book that is still in print. Staff may ask you to show proof that you own the materials or have permission to copy them.

Thank you for your cooperation, and good luck on your exams!

Campus Copy Center

5. The word "proof" in paragraph 4 is closest in meaning to

  1. evidence

正解 A　show proof that you own the materials 指出示「證明」，與 evidence 最接近，選 A。copy、payment、excuse 皆不符語境。

Open Textbooks: A Cheaper Way to Learn?

For many university students in Taiwan, the start of each semester means spending several thousand dollars on textbooks. Imported English textbooks are especially expensive, and some cost more than three thousand dollars each. As a result, a growing number of students skip buying books altogether, borrow old editions, or rely on illegal scanned copies shared online.

To address this problem, several universities have started to promote open textbooks. These are books written by experts and published under open licenses, which allow anyone to read, download, and even print them for free. Many open textbooks are also allowed to be edited, so teachers can add local examples or remove chapters they do not need.

Professor Lin, who teaches introductory psychology, switched to an open textbook last year. "My students used to come to class without the book," she said. "Now everyone has it on their phone or laptop from the first day." According to a survey she conducted, students' average quiz scores rose slightly, and fewer students dropped the course.

However, open textbooks are not a perfect solution. Some teachers worry that the quality is uneven, since not every book goes through the same review process as traditional textbooks. Others point out that open books in certain advanced fields are still hard to find, and that preparing new teaching materials takes a great deal of time.

The university library now offers small grants to teachers who want to adopt or create open materials. Librarians hope that within three years, at least twenty percent of first-year courses will use free textbooks.

6. What problem is described in the first paragraph?

  1. Textbooks are often out of date.
  2. Students cannot read English well.
  3. Libraries have too few computers.
  4. Textbooks are too expensive for many students.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第一段指出課本動輒數千元、進口原文書更貴，導致學生不買書或用盜版，故選 D。其他選項文中未提及。

7. What can teachers do with many open textbooks?

  1. Sell them to students for profit
  2. Change their content to fit the class
  3. Use them only in psychology courses
  4. Print them only with the author's payment
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段說 teachers can add local examples or remove chapters，即可依課程修改內容，故選 B。開放教科書是免費的，並未提販售或限定心理學課。

8. What happened after Professor Lin started using an open textbook?

  1. More students dropped her course.
  2. Students had the book from the start.
  3. Quiz scores fell slightly.
  4. She stopped giving quizzes.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　林教授說 Now everyone has it on their phone or laptop from the first day，故選 B。文中說成績略升、退選人數減少，與 A、C 相反。

9. Which concerns about open textbooks are mentioned? Choose TWO.

  1. Their quality is not always consistent.
  2. They are illegal to share online.
  3. They are hard to find in some advanced subjects.
  4. They cannot be read on phones.
  5. They cost more than imported books.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　第四段提到品質參差不齊（quality is uneven）以及進階領域不易找到，故選 A、C。開放教科書可合法分享、可用手機閱讀、免費，其餘選項皆與文意相反。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Try the second-hand book fair in the student center this week.
  2. I used it for my report last year.
  3. Statistics is my favorite subject, too.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問哪裡買得到二手統計課本，A 指出本週學生活動中心有二手書市集，最合理。B 以 used（使用過）混淆 used（二手）；C 重複 statistics 但答非所問。

聽力原文

Do you know where I can buy a used copy of the statistics textbook?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The library closes at ten tonight.
  2. I prefer the blue cover, actually.
  3. Five dollars a page, so it adds up quickly.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　How much 問價錢，C 回答每頁五元、很快就累積，最合理。A 答閉館時間；B 以 cover/color 相關字混淆，答非所問。

聽力原文

How much does it cost to print in color at the library?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I read the whole list of names.
  2. She said she'd upload it by Friday.
  3. The line at the copy shop was long.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問教授是否會把閱讀清單放上網，B 說她週五前會上傳，最合理。A 重複 read/list 但意思不同；C 以 line 混淆 online，答非所問。

聽力原文

Isn't the professor putting the reading list online?

4. Why can't the woman copy two chapters?

  1. The copy machine is out of order.
  2. The book belongs to the library.
  3. The shop limits copying because of copyright.
  4. Her study group has already copied them.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男生說 because of copyright rules 每位顧客只能印一章或一成內容，故選 C。對話未提機器故障；書是向圖書館借，是她之後的打算，並非不能印的原因。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to copy two chapters from this textbook for my study group. Sorry, because of copyright rules, we can only copy one chapter or up to ten percent of a book per customer. Oh, I see. Then just the first chapter, please. I'll ask the others to borrow the book from the library.

5. What is one reason the woman chose the e-book?

  1. It includes access to online homework.
  2. It is easier on her eyes.
  3. Her professor requires it.
  4. The printed copy was sold out.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　女生說 the access code for the online homework comes with it，故選 A。她承認看螢幕會累，故 B 錯；教授規定與紙本售完皆未提及。

聽力原文

Amy, did you buy the e-book version of the economics textbook? I did. It was about half the price of the printed one, and I can search for key words. But don't you get tired reading on a screen for hours? A little, but the access code for the online homework comes with it, so it saves me money.

6. What is true about books with a lot of highlighting?

  1. They will not be accepted.
  2. They will be bought for less money.
  3. They must be returned to the library.
  4. They can only be sold on the last day.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　廣播說 heavy highlighting 的書 will be accepted at a lower price，故選 B。不收的是缺頁的書，並非畫重點的書；其他選項未提及。

7. Why is the speaker giving this talk now?

  1. The library is changing its opening hours.
  2. Students are working on final projects.
  3. A new copyright law has just passed.
  4. Several students were recently fined.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說 As the semester comes to an end, many of you are preparing final reports and presentations，故選 B。新法通過、學生被罰款、開放時間皆未提及。

8. According to the speaker, what may be illegal even if it is free?

  1. Borrowing a book from course reserve
  2. Copying a short part of a book to study
  3. Sharing scanned chapters in a group chat
  4. Emailing the copyright office
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者指出 sharing scanned chapters in a class group chat may break the law, even if you don't charge any money，故選 C。影印少部分自用通常沒問題；借閱與寄信詢問皆合法。

9. What does the speaker recommend? Choose TWO.

  1. Using Creative Commons images
  2. Buying every textbook new
  3. Checking with the office when unsure
  4. Keeping reserve books overnight
  5. Paying for every picture online
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　講者建議使用 Creative Commons 授權圖片，並在不確定時 email our office，故選 A、C。指定參考書一次只能借三小時，不能過夜；她並未要求買新書或付費購圖。

聽力原文

Good morning, everyone. I'm Dr. Chen from the library's copyright office. As the semester comes to an end, many of you are preparing final reports and presentations, so I'd like to explain how to use other people's work legally. First, copying a short part of a book for your own study is usually fine. However, copying an entire textbook, or sharing scanned chapters in a class group chat, may break the law, even if you don't charge any money. Second, when you use images or charts from the Internet in your slides, you must give credit to the source. Better yet, look for pictures with a Creative Commons license, which tells you clearly how they can be used. Finally, remember that many expensive textbooks are available through the library's course reserve service. You can borrow them for three hours at a time, and some are also available as e-books for free. If you're not sure whether something is allowed, just email our office before you use it. We usually reply within two working days.
