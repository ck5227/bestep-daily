獎助學金與工讀申請

今日單字

waiver n. （費用等的）減免；棄權聲明
She applied for a tuition waiver because of her family's financial situation.
merit scholarship n. 成績優秀獎學金
The merit scholarship goes to the top five percent of each class.
work-study adj./n. 工讀（的）
He took a work-study position at the library to cover his living costs.
qualify (for) v. 有資格（獲得）
You need a GPA of at least 3.5 to qualify for this award.
overlap (with) v. （時間）重疊
Your shifts cannot overlap with your registered classes.
documented adj. 有文件證明的
Students with a documented financial need will be given priority.
barrier n. 障礙；阻礙
The long application form is a major barrier for busy students.
unclaimed adj. 無人申領的
Nearly twenty percent of the scholarship money went unclaimed last year.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who fail to meet the minimum attendance requirement will no longer be ______ for the work-study program.

  1. capable
  2. familiar
  3. eligible
  4. responsible
看答案與詳解

正解 C　be eligible for 表示「有資格參加」，符合出席不足就失去資格的語意，選 C。capable 搭配 of；familiar 搭配 with；responsible for 意為「負責」，語意不合。

2. Applicants must ______ all supporting documents into a single PDF file before uploading them.

  1. combine
  2. compare
  3. contain
  4. consider
看答案與詳解

正解 A　combine A into B 表示「把多個檔案合併成一個」，選 A。compare 比較、contain 包含、consider 考慮，都無法搭配 into a single file。

From: Office of Student Affairs

To: All Undergraduate Students

Subject: Work-Study Positions for the Spring Semester

Dear Students,

The Office of Student Affairs is now accepting applications for work-study positions for the spring semester. Openings are available at the library, the sports center, the IT help desk, and several academic departments. All positions pay an hourly wage and allow a maximum of fifteen hours per week.

Priority will be given to students who receive financial aid or who have a documented financial need. To apply, complete the online form on the student portal and attach your class schedule. Please note that work shifts cannot overlap with your registered classes, so make sure your schedule is accurate and up to date.

The application period runs from November 2 to November 20. Selected students will be notified by email in mid-December and must attend a two-hour training session before starting work.

Best regards, Office of Student Affairs

3. What is the main purpose of this email?

  1. To explain changes to financial aid rules
  2. To invite students to a training workshop
  3. To remind students to register for classes
  4. To announce job opportunities for students
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第一段即說明學務處開始受理下學期工讀職缺申請，主旨是公告工作機會，選 D。B 的訓練只是錄取後的細節；A、C 未提及。

4. Why must applicants attach their class schedule?

  1. To prove they are full-time students
  2. To make sure work shifts do not conflict with classes
  3. To calculate the amount of financial aid
  4. To choose a date for the training session
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段提到 work shifts cannot overlap with your registered classes，附課表是為了避免排班與上課衝突，選 B。其他選項文中都沒有提到。

5. The word "documented" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. officially recorded
  2. widely discussed
  3. carefully hidden
  4. recently increased
看答案與詳解

正解 A　documented financial need 指「有文件證明的經濟需求」，即正式記錄在案，選 A。其餘選項意思與「有證明」無關。

Scholarship Money Goes Unclaimed as Students Miss Out

Every year, a surprising amount of scholarship money at our university goes unused. According to a recent report by the Office of Student Affairs, nearly twenty percent of available scholarship funds were not awarded last academic year. The reason was not that students were ineligible, but that too few of them applied.

Officials believe there are several causes. Many students simply do not know the scholarships exist, since information is spread across department websites, bulletin boards, and email newsletters. Others assume that only students with top grades can win. "In fact, many of our awards focus on community service, leadership, or financial need rather than grades," said Chen Yi-ting, a scholarship coordinator. "Some funds received only one or two applications."

Another barrier is the application process itself. Students interviewed for this article described forms that required multiple recommendation letters and essays, often with deadlines that fell during midterm exams. "I started an application last spring, but I gave up because I was too busy studying," said Kevin Wu, a junior in mechanical engineering.

In response, the university plans to launch a single online scholarship platform next semester. Students will be able to create one profile and be automatically matched with awards they qualify for. The office will also hold monthly workshops on writing personal statements, and several departments have agreed to move their deadlines away from exam periods.

Ms. Chen encourages students not to wait for the new system. "Even a small scholarship looks great on a resume," she said. "And you can't win if you don't apply."

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A new rule that limits who can apply for scholarships
  2. How to write a strong personal statement
  3. Why some scholarship money is not awarded and what will change
  4. The rising cost of university tuition
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文先說明獎學金無人申領的原因，再介紹學校的改善措施，選 C。B 只是措施之一的細節；A、D 未提及。

7. According to Ms. Chen, what do many students wrongly believe?

  1. That scholarships must be paid back
  2. That scholarships are only for students with excellent grades
  3. That applications must be submitted on paper
  4. That international students cannot apply
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段：Others assume that only students with top grades can win，陳小姐隨即說明許多獎項看的是服務、領導或經濟需求，故選 B。其他選項文中未提及。

8. Why did Kevin Wu not finish his application?

  1. He was not eligible for the award.
  2. He could not find anyone to write a recommendation.
  3. He never saw information about it.
  4. He was too busy studying.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　Kevin 說 I gave up because I was too busy studying，選 D。B 的推薦信是文中提到的一般困難，但不是他放棄的原因；A、C 未提及。

9. Which changes does the university plan to make? Choose TWO.

  1. Raising the amount of each award
  2. Removing essay requirements
  3. Creating one online platform for scholarships
  4. Accepting applications only during exam periods
  5. Holding workshops on personal statements
看答案與詳解

正解 C、E　第四段提到將推出單一線上獎學金平台，並每月舉辦自傳寫作工作坊，故選 C、E。A、B 未提及；D 與「把截止日移開考試期間」相反。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The ship leaves at noon tomorrow.
  2. Yes, I uploaded everything on Tuesday night.
  3. My hand still hurts from all that writing.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否在截止前交了獎學金申請，B 說週二晚上已全部上傳，直接回答。A 用 scholarship 中的 ship 音近混淆；C 重複 hand 卻答非所問。

聽力原文

Did you hand in your scholarship application before the deadline?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I usually study at the library after class.
  2. It's a good position for taking photos.
  3. Ms. Lin in the Student Affairs Office is in charge of that.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問工讀職缺該找誰，C 指出學務處林小姐負責，回答了「誰」。A 重複 study、library；B 把 position 當「位置」解釋，皆答非所問。

聽力原文

Who should I talk to about the work-study positions at the library?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Right, so I'm not sure I can apply this time.
  2. Turn right at the next corner.
  3. The average student sleeps about six hours.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　確認申請門檻是否為 GPA 3.5，A 表示同意並說自己可能無法申請，最合理。B 把 right 當「右邊」；C 重複 average 但與成績門檻無關。

聽力原文

Your grade point average has to be at least three point five to qualify, right?

4. What does the man need to do?

  1. Pay his tuition by June.
  2. Bring a recommendation letter.
  3. Come back to the office in three months.
  4. Request an updated document.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　男生的證明是六月開的，超過三個月期限，女生說必須在十一月底前重新申請一份，故選 D。A 的 June、C 的 three months 都是對話中出現的字詞陷阱；B 未被提及。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to apply for the low-income tuition waiver. What do I need to bring? You'll need your household registration record and a certificate from your district office. The certificate has to be issued within the last three months. Mine is from June. Is that a problem? I'm afraid so. You'll have to request a new one before the end of November.

5. Why does the man work only eight hours a week?

  1. Because of a rule for international students.
  2. Because the help desk has limited funding.
  3. Because he wants to focus on his studies.
  4. Because the job pays very well.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男生說實驗課很忙，寧可顧好成績，故選 C。A 的國際生時數上限他明說不適用於自己；B、D 皆未提及。

聽力原文

I heard you got the job at the campus IT help desk. How many hours a week are you working? Only about eight. There's a twenty-hour limit for international students, but that doesn't apply to me anyway. So why only eight? My lab schedule is crazy this semester. I'd rather keep my grades up than earn a little extra money.

6. What should students who have already applied do?

  1. Submit the application again.
  2. Make sure they received a confirmation email.
  3. Visit the office before November seventh.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　廣播說已申請者不必重交，但要檢查學校信箱是否收到確認信，故選 B。A 與廣播內容相反；C 的十一月七日是原本的截止日，並未要求到辦公室。

聽力原文

Attention, students. The deadline for the Excellence in Learning Scholarship has been extended from November seventh to November fourteenth because of a problem with the online application system. If you have already submitted your application, you do not need to submit it again. However, please check your school email for a confirmation message. If you have not received one, contact the Office of Student Affairs at extension two three five.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A student volunteer coordinator
  2. A professor who leads a scholarship committee
  3. A research lab assistant
  4. An admissions officer
看答案與詳解

正解 B　開頭自我介紹：I'm Professor Huang, and I lead the department's scholarship committee，故選 B。其他身分皆未提及。

8. What is true about the merit scholarship?

  1. Students must submit a proposal.
  2. It requires thirty hours of volunteer work.
  3. Recipients are chosen without applying.
  4. It is only for graduate students.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　教授說 You don't need to apply，委員會自動選出各班前百分之五，故選 C。A 是研究助理計畫的條件；B 是社區服務補助的條件；D 未提及。

9. What is required to apply for the research assistant program? Choose TWO.

  1. A short proposal
  2. A one-page reflection
  3. A faculty member's signature
  4. Last semester's transcript
  5. Thirty hours of volunteer work
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　研究助理計畫需繳交 a short proposal 和 a faculty member's signature，故選 A、C。B、E 屬於社區服務補助；D 未被要求。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Professor Huang, and I lead the department's scholarship committee. Since many of you have asked about financial support, I want to explain the three main options we offer this year. The first is the merit scholarship, which is based entirely on last semester's grades. You don't need to apply. The committee automatically selects the top five percent of each class. The second option is the research assistant program. Students work ten hours a week in a professor's lab and receive a monthly payment. This is a great choice if you're thinking about graduate school, because you'll gain real research experience and possibly a strong recommendation letter. To apply, you'll need to submit a short proposal along with a faculty member's signature. Finally, there's the community service grant. To be eligible, you must complete at least thirty hours of volunteer work during the semester and write a one-page reflection. Please note that you can't receive the merit scholarship and the community service grant in the same semester. If you have any questions, my office hours are on Wednesday afternoons.
