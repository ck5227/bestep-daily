今日單字

postpone v. 延期
The midterm was postponed because of the typhoon warning.
proctor n. 監考人員
Hand your answer sheet to the proctor before you leave.
make-up exam n. 補考
You need a doctor's note to take a make-up exam.
extension n. （期限的）延長
She asked her professor for a one-week extension on the paper.
closed-book adj. 不可翻書的
The chemistry midterm is closed-book, but a periodic table is provided.
accommodate v. 配合；滿足（需求）
The instructor will try to accommodate your request.
assigned adj. 指定的；分配好的
Seats are assigned, so check the chart on the door.
rely on phr. v. 依賴
Don't rely too heavily on your notes during the exam.

閱讀 Reading

1. Professor Lin gave Mei an ______ on her term paper because she had a family emergency last week.

  1. excuse
  2. extension
  3. expansion
  4. exception
看答案與詳解

正解 B　give someone an extension 指「延長期限」，符合因家中急事晚交報告的情境。excuse 是藉口、expansion 是擴張、exception 是例外，搭配 on her paper 都不自然。

2. Please double-check your name and student number before you ______ your answer sheet to the proctor.

  1. admit
  2. commit
  3. permit
  4. submit
看答案與詳解

正解 D　submit ... to 表示「繳交給」，答案 D。admit 承認／准許進入、commit 犯（錯）、permit 允許，都是字形相近的陷阱。

From: Office of Academic Affairs

To: All Undergraduate Students

Subject: Midterm Exam Week Reminders

Midterm exam week runs from November 10 to November 14. To support students during this busy period, the main library will extend its opening hours until 2 a.m. from November 3 to November 14. The third floor will be a silent study zone, so please keep phone calls and group discussions to the study rooms on the first floor.

Study rooms can be reserved online for up to three hours per day. Because demand is expected to be high, a reservation will be canceled and released to other students if you do not check in within fifteen minutes of the start time.

Students who miss an exam for medical or family reasons must submit a Make-up Exam Request Form through the student portal within five working days. Your instructor will try to accommodate your request, but make-up exams are not guaranteed.

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce the opening of a new library
  2. To share services and rules for exam week
  3. To inform students that exam dates have changed
  4. To recruit volunteers for the library
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信件說明期中考週的圖書館延長開放、自習室預約規則與補考申請，答案 B。圖書館不是新開的、考試日期沒變、也沒招募志工。

4. The word "accommodate" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. refuse
  2. provide housing for
  3. meet the needs of
  4. delay
看答案與詳解

正解 C　上下文是老師會盡量 accommodate your request，意思是「配合、滿足」需求，答案 C。provide housing for 是 accommodate 的另一個意思（提供住宿），在此不適用；refuse 意思相反。

5. What happens if a student does not check in to a reserved study room on time?

  1. The reservation is given to other students.
  2. The student must pay a fine.
  3. The student is moved to the third floor.
  4. The reservation is extended by three hours.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第二段說十五分鐘內未報到 reservation will be canceled and released to other students，答案 A。文中沒提罰款；三樓是靜音區；三小時是每日預約上限。

Should Midterms Go Open-Note? One Department Tries Something New

This semester, the Department of Mechanical Engineering is testing a new approach to midterm exams. Instead of requiring students to memorize every formula, three of its required courses will allow students to bring one A4 page of handwritten notes into the exam room. Department chair Professor Liao says the goal is to shift attention from memory to problem-solving. "Engineers in the real world look things up all the time," she explained. "What matters is whether they know which tool to use and why."

The idea is not entirely new. Several universities in Taiwan and abroad already allow note sheets in certain courses. Research suggests that preparing a one-page summary can itself be a valuable way to review, because students must decide what information is most important. However, some studies have also found that students who rely too heavily on their notes during an exam perform worse than those who use them only as a backup.

Reactions on campus have been mixed. Chen Yu-ting, a second-year student, said the change has reduced her anxiety. "I used to spend hours memorizing equations that I'd forget a week later. Now I spend that time doing practice problems." Others are less enthusiastic. Some students worry that instructors will simply make the exams harder to balance out the extra help, and a few teaching assistants have asked how they will check that every note sheet follows the rules.

To address these concerns, the department will collect all note sheets at the end of each exam and will survey students when the semester is over. If the results are positive, the policy may be extended to all undergraduate courses next year.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Why engineering students prefer closed-book exams
  2. Why engineers no longer need to learn formulas
  3. A shortage of teaching assistants in one department
  4. A department testing a new exam policy and planning to evaluate it
看答案與詳解

正解 D　全文介紹機械系試行可帶一頁筆記的期中考，並說明各方反應與評估方式，答案 D。文章沒說學生偏好閉書考或 TA 不足；B 過度延伸系主任的說法。

7. According to the article, why can preparing a note sheet be helpful?

  1. It lets students share answers with classmates.
  2. It makes students decide what information is most important.
  3. It makes the exam shorter.
  4. It replaces the need for practice problems.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段說準備一頁摘要是很好的複習，because students must decide what information is most important，答案 B。其他選項文中都未提及，D 甚至與陳同學「改做練習題」相反。

8. What concern do some students have about the new policy?

  1. The exams may become more difficult.
  2. The note sheets are too small to read.
  3. Teaching assistants will grade unfairly.
  4. The policy will be canceled next year.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第三段說有學生擔心老師 make the exams harder to balance out the extra help，答案 A。TA 擔心的是如何檢查筆記，不是評分不公；文中說政策可能「擴大」而非取消。

9. What will the department do to evaluate the policy? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Collect the note sheets after each exam
  2. Ban note sheets in all other courses
  3. Survey students at the end of the semester
  4. Hire more teaching assistants
  5. Allow students to type their notes
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　最後一段提到會 collect all note sheets at the end of each exam（A）以及 survey students when the semester is over（C）。文中沒有禁止其他課程、增聘助教或允許打字筆記（規定是手寫）。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I'll cover for you if you're sick that day.
  2. Only up to chapter four, according to the syllabus.
  3. It's in Room four oh six.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問的是期中考範圍是到第六章還是前四章，B 直接回答「只到第四章」。A 重複 cover 但意思是「代班」；C 用 four/six 的數字做陷阱，回答的是教室。

聽力原文

Will the midterm cover chapters one through six, or just the first four?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Leave it in your bag at the front of the room.
  2. I'll phone you right after the exam.
  3. The exam starts at nine sharp.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問考試時手機放哪裡，A 回答「放在教室前方的包包裡」。B 重複 phone 當動詞「打電話」；C 回答時間，答非所問。

聽力原文

Where should I put my phone during the exam?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, I moved into the dorm on a Friday.
  2. It was a fine exam overall.
  3. Neither can I. Now it clashes with my lab.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　對方抱怨考試被改到星期五，C 表示同感並說明撞到實驗課，回應最自然。A 重複 moved 與 Friday 但講搬宿舍；B 用 fine 與 Friday 音近且時態不合。

聽力原文

I can't believe the exam was moved to Friday.

4. What does the man need to do by Thursday?

  1. Take a make-up exam
  2. Show his student ID at the TA office
  3. Apply for a new passport
  4. Pay a replacement fee
看答案與詳解

正解 B　助教最後說 bring your student ID to the TA office by Thursday，所以是 B。護照只是舉例的替代證件，對話中沒有補考或補辦費用。

聽力原文

Excuse me, I left my student ID in my dorm. Can I still take the midterm? You can, but you'll need to show another photo ID, like a passport or a driver's license. I have my health insurance card. It has my photo on it. That works for now. Just bring your student ID to the TA office by Thursday so we can confirm your identity.

5. What will the man probably do?

  1. Attend the review session at eight
  2. Skip his shift at the library
  3. Ask the professor for her notes
  4. Watch a recording of the session
看答案與詳解

正解 D　男生說 I'll just watch it tomorrow morning，it 指上傳的錄影，答案 D。他八點前要值班，沒有說要翹班或八點去；也沒提到跟教授要筆記。

聽力原文

Are you going to the review session tonight? I wanted to, but I have a shift at the library until eight. Professor Chen said she'll upload the recording to the course website afterward. Great, then I'll just watch it tomorrow morning.

6. What should students with a schedule conflict do?

  1. Email the department office before Friday
  2. Take the exam tomorrow as planned
  3. Go to a different exam room
  4. Contact the typhoon center
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播最後說有撞考的同學 email the department office before this Friday，答案 A。考試已延期，不是明天考；新考試教室不變，所以 B、C 錯。

Attention, students taking Introduction to Economics. Because of the typhoon warning, tomorrow's midterm exam has been postponed. The new date is Monday, November tenth, at the same time and in the same room. If you already have another exam at that time, please email the department office before this Friday.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To review important chemistry formulas
  2. To announce a change in the exam date
  3. To explain the rules for an upcoming exam
  4. To recruit new teaching assistants
看答案與詳解

正解 C　助教開頭說 I want to go over a few rules（期中考前說明規則），答案 C。內容沒有複習公式、改期或招募助教。

8. What will happen to a student who arrives twenty-five minutes after the exam begins?

  1. The student can sit in any empty seat.
  2. The student will not be allowed to take the exam.
  3. The student will lose twenty points.
  4. The student must email Professor Wu.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　規則是開考超過二十分鐘到就 won't be allowed in，二十五分鐘已超過，答案 B。twenty 是扣分陷阱；寄信給教授是生病時的規定。

9. According to the speaker, which TWO statements are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Students can choose their own seats.
  2. Calculators will be checked at the entrance.
  3. Students need to bring their own periodic table.
  4. A doctor's note is required for a make-up exam.
  5. Office hours are on Thursday mornings.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　助教說 We'll check every calculator at the door（B），並要求生病須在三天內交 doctor's note 才能補考（D）。座位是指定的、週期表由助教提供、office hours 在週二下午，故 A、C、E 錯。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Grace, one of the teaching assistants for General Chemistry. Since the midterm is only two weeks away, I want to go over a few rules so nobody gets surprised on the day. First, please arrive at least fifteen minutes early. Seats are assigned, and the seating chart will be posted on the classroom door. If you arrive more than twenty minutes after the exam begins, you won't be allowed in. Second, you may bring a basic calculator, but not one that can store formulas or connect to the internet. We'll check every calculator at the door. The exam is closed-book, but we'll provide a periodic table, so you don't need to bring your own. Finally, if you're sick on the exam day, you must email Professor Wu before the exam starts and hand in a doctor's note within three days. Otherwise, a make-up exam won't be possible. If you have any questions, come to my office hours on Tuesday afternoons.
