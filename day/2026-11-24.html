問卷調查與研究倫理

今日單字

informed consent n. 知情同意
Researchers must get informed consent before collecting any data.
anonymous adj. 匿名的
The survey is anonymous, so no one will know how you answered.
participant n. 參與者；受試者
Each participant will receive two hundred dollars as a thank-you.
questionnaire n. 問卷
Please keep your questionnaire under fifteen questions.
ethics review n. 倫理審查
Studies involving minors require a full ethics review.
penalty n. 處罰；不利後果
Participants can withdraw at any time without penalty.
reliable adj. 可靠的
Careless answers can make survey data less reliable.
track v. 追蹤
You can track the status of your application online.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before the interview began, each participant signed a ______ form agreeing to be recorded.

  1. complaint
  2. contest
  3. consent
  4. concert
看答案與詳解

正解 C　agreeing to be recorded 表示同意，consent form 為「同意書」，故選 C。complaint（抱怨）、contest（比賽）、concert（音樂會）拼字相近但語意不合。

2. To protect students' privacy, the researchers kept all survey responses completely ______.

  1. enormous
  2. ambitious
  3. available
  4. anonymous
看答案與詳解

正解 D　To protect students' privacy 提示應不具名，anonymous（匿名的）最合適，故選 D。available（可取得的）反而不利隱私；enormous、ambitious 語意不合。

From: Research Ethics Office <ethics@hsinchu-u.edu.tw>

To: All Undergraduate Students

Subject: New Online System for Student Research Review

Dear students,

Starting December 1, all applications for research ethics review must be submitted through our new online system instead of by email. The system allows you to upload your consent form and questionnaire, track the status of your application, and receive comments from reviewers directly.

Please note that projects for course assignments do not need a full review if they are anonymous and involve only adults. However, you must still complete a short self-check form, which takes about five minutes. Projects involving minors, medical information, or audio and video recordings require a full review, which typically takes ten working days.

A one-hour workshop on using the new system will be held on November 27 at 2 p.m. in Room 204 of the Administration Building. Seats are limited, so please register in advance on our office website.

Best regards,

Research Ethics Office

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To remind students of the final project deadline.
  2. To recruit students to serve as reviewers.
  3. To cancel an upcoming workshop.
  4. To announce a new way to submit applications.
看答案與詳解

4. Which project would most likely require a full review?

  1. A study that records interviews on video.
  2. An anonymous survey of adult classmates.
  3. A class project with only adult volunteers.
  4. A questionnaire that takes five minutes to finish.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信中指出涉及 audio and video recordings 的研究需完整審查，故選 A。匿名且僅限成人的課堂作業只需填自我檢核表；five minutes 指檢核表所需時間。

5. The word "track" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. design
  2. delete
  3. follow
  4. print
看答案與詳解

正解 C　track the status of your application 指追蹤申請進度，與 follow 意思最接近，故選 C。design、delete、print 都無法搭配 status 表達「掌握進度」。

Survey Fatigue: Why Students Stop Clicking "Start"

Every December, students at Taiwanese universities receive a flood of survey links. Classmates need data for final projects, graduate students are finishing their theses, and campus offices want feedback on their services. According to a recent study by the student union, the average undergraduate received eleven survey requests in the last three weeks of the fall semester. Researchers call the result "survey fatigue": people become so tired of questionnaires that they ignore them or rush through them without reading.

Survey fatigue is more than an annoyance. When fewer people respond, the results may not represent the whole student body. Worse, participants who answer quickly and carelessly can make the data unreliable. "A survey with three hundred careless answers is less useful than one with eighty thoughtful ones," says Dr. Grace Huang, who teaches research methods in the Department of Sociology.

Dr. Huang offers several suggestions for student researchers. First, keep questionnaires short; she recommends no more than fifteen questions. Second, be honest about the time required. Third, explain clearly why the research matters, since people are more willing to help when they understand the purpose. She also warns against offering large prizes, because they may attract participants who care only about the reward.

The student union is now testing a new approach. Instead of posting links in dozens of group chats, students can list their surveys on a single campus research board, where volunteers choose the studies that interest them. Early results are encouraging: surveys on the board have received more complete answers than those shared through social media.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. The history of the student union.
  2. How to design a graduate thesis.
  3. A problem caused by too many survey requests and ways to deal with it.
  4. Why prizes improve survey response rates.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文先說明問卷疲乏現象與影響，再提出黃教授的建議與學生會的新做法，故選 C。文中反對大獎品，D 與原文相反；A、B 只是枝節。

7. According to Dr. Huang, why are careless answers a problem?

  1. They make surveys take longer to finish.
  2. They make the data less reliable.
  3. They break university rules.
  4. They cost researchers extra money.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段指出草率作答 can make the data unreliable，黃教授也說三百份草率答案不如八十份用心的答案，故選 B。其餘選項文中未提。

8. What is true about the campus research board?

  1. It is run by the Department of Sociology.
  2. It is only open to graduate students.
  3. It offers large prizes to participants.
  4. It lets volunteers choose the studies they want to join.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　末段說明研究看板上 volunteers choose the studies that interest them，故選 D。看板由學生會試辦，非社會系；文中未限研究生，也未提供大獎品。

9. Which are suggestions that Dr. Huang gives to student researchers? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Limit a questionnaire to about fifteen questions.
  2. Post survey links in as many group chats as possible.
  3. Offer large prizes to attract more people.
  4. Explain why the research is important.
  5. Send surveys only in December.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　第三段建議題目不超過十五題，並清楚說明研究的重要性，故選 A、D。她反對大獎品（C）；B 是學生會想取代的做法；E 未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, the food here is pretty cheap.
  2. I filled up my water bottle already.
  3. Almost. I still need about twenty more responses.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問問卷是否已收集到足夠填答者，C 表示快了、還差約二十份，最合理。A 重複 food 談價格；B 以 filled up 混淆 fill out，答非所問。

聽力原文

Did you get enough people to fill out your survey on campus food?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, and the review can take up to two weeks.
  2. The committee meeting was a lot of fun.
  3. I'll interview for the job on Friday.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問訪談前是否需倫理委員會核准，A 回答需要並說明審查時間，最合理。B 重複 committee；C 重複 interview 但指求職面試，皆答非所問。

聽力原文

Do we need approval from the ethics committee before we interview the students?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I think it's in the second folder.
  2. Because it has to explain every possible risk to participants.
  3. About ten minutes, if you read quickly.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Why 問原因，B 說明同意書須解釋所有可能風險，最合理。A 回答位置；C 回答所需時間，誤把 so long 當成 how long。

聽力原文

Why is the consent form so long?

4. What will the man most likely do tonight?

  1. Ask the professor to extend the deadline.
  2. Repost the survey with the expected completion time.
  3. Offer participants a cash reward.
  4. Interview fifteen more students.

正解 B　男生最後說 I'll add that and post it again tonight，that 指填答所需時間，故選 B。對話未提延期、現金獎勵或訪談，fifteen 只是目前填答人數。

Professor Lin, I posted my survey link in a few class group chats, but only fifteen people have answered so far. That's quite low. Did you mention how long it takes? Students are much more willing to help when they know it's only five minutes. I didn't. I'll add that and post it again tonight.

8. According to the speaker, where should students store their data?

  1. On a personal laptop.
  2. On a USB stick.
  3. On the university's cloud drive.

正解 C　教授說 Store your data on the university's cloud drive, not on a personal laptop or a USB stick，故選 C；A、B 正是被排除的選項。

9. Which projects must be submitted to the research ethics office? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Projects that use the university's cloud drive.
  2. Projects involving participants under eighteen.
  3. Projects lasting more than ten working days.
  4. Projects with fewer than fifty responses.
  5. Projects that ask about family problems.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　教授指出涉及未滿十八歲者，或詢問健康、家庭問題等敏感主題的專題須先送審，故選 B、E。ten working days 是審查所需時間，非送審條件；A、D 未提及。

聽力原文

Before you start collecting data for your final projects, I want to go over a few ethical rules. First, every participant must give informed consent. That means you explain what the study is about, how long it will take, and what you will do with their answers before they agree to take part. They should also know they can stop at any time without any penalty. Second, protect people's privacy. Unless you truly need it, don't collect names, phone numbers, or student ID numbers. Store your data on the university's cloud drive, not on a personal laptop or a USB stick, and delete the raw data after the semester ends. Finally, if your project involves anyone under eighteen, or asks about sensitive topics such as health or family problems, you must submit your plan to the research ethics office first. The review usually takes about ten working days, so please don't wait until the last minute. If you're not sure whether your project needs a review, email me by this Friday.
