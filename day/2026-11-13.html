BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-13
學生會選舉與校務參與

今日單字

turnout n. 投票率；出席人數
The council hopes the turnout will reach at least 20 percent this year.
candidate n. 候選人
Each candidate must hold an open forum in their own college.
ballot n. 選票；投票
Several ballots were counted twice, so a recount was held.
petition n. 請願書；連署
Over three thousand students signed the petition for longer library hours.
nomination n. 提名
Candidates need fifty signatures on their nomination form.
eligible adj. 有資格的
Only registered undergraduates are eligible to vote in this election.
invalid adj. 無效的
The results were declared invalid because too few students voted.
substitute n. 代理人；替代者
If you can't attend, please send a substitute from your class.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before the student council can ______ the new budget, at least half of its members must be present at the meeting.

  1. approve
  2. appear
  3. apply
  4. approach
看答案與詳解

正解 A　approve the budget 指「通過預算」，語意最通順，選 A。appear 為不及物動詞；apply 與 approach 搭配 budget 不合語意。

2. The election results were ______ because several ballots had been counted twice, so a recount was held.

  1. delivered
  2. decorated
  3. disputed
  4. donated
看答案與詳解

正解 C　選票被重複計算導致重新計票，表示結果「受到質疑」，disputed 最合適，選 C。delivered（遞送）、decorated（裝飾）、donated（捐贈）皆不合語意。

From: Student Council Election Committee

To: All Undergraduate Students

Subject: How to Vote in the Student Council Election

Dear students,

Voting for this year's student council president and class representatives will take place online from 9 a.m. on Monday, November 23, to 5 p.m. on Wednesday, November 25. You can vote through the student portal by clicking the Election tab and logging in with your student ID and password.

Please note that each student may vote only once, and votes cannot be changed after they are submitted. If you have trouble logging in, visit the IT Help Desk on the first floor of the library. A paper ballot station will also be set up in the Student Center on Wednesday for students who are unable to vote online.

Results will be announced on the council's website on Thursday morning. For the election to be valid, at least 20 percent of eligible students must cast a vote, so we strongly encourage you to take part.

Election Committee

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To introduce the candidates
  2. To explain how and when to vote
  3. To announce the election results
  4. To recruit committee volunteers
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信件說明投票時間、方式、登入問題與紙本投票站，主旨是說明如何及何時投票，選 B。信中未介紹候選人，結果週四才公布，也未招募志工。

4. The word "cast" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. throw
  2. design
  3. perform
  4. submit
看答案與詳解

正解 D　cast a vote 指「投下一票」，即 submit a vote，選 D。throw 是 cast 的字面意思（丟擲），在此不適用。

5. What can a student who is unable to vote online do?

  1. Send a vote by email to the committee
  2. Ask the IT Help Desk to vote for them
  3. Use a paper ballot in the Student Center on Wednesday
  4. Vote on Thursday morning
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段說週三在學生活動中心設有紙本投票站，供無法線上投票者使用，選 C。資訊服務台只協助登入；週四是公布結果。

Low Turnout Puts Student Voice at Risk

Last spring, only 14 percent of eligible students voted in the student council election, the lowest figure in ten years. Because university rules require a turnout of at least 20 percent, the results were declared invalid, and the council had to hold a second election in May, just before final exams. That vote cost the council about 60,000 NT dollars and attracted only slightly more voters.

Council members say low turnout is not simply a sign that students do not care. In a survey of 1,200 students conducted in September, 41 percent said they did not know when the election was held, and 33 percent said they did not understand what the council actually does. Only 12 percent said they were not interested in campus affairs.

To change this, the council has introduced several measures for this year's election. Candidates must now hold at least one open forum in their own college, and a live-streamed debate has been added. Voting has also been extended from one day to three. In addition, the council now publishes a monthly summary of its work, including which proposals it has passed and how its budget is spent.

Not everyone thinks these steps will be enough. Dr. Hsu Mei-ling, who teaches political science, believes students vote when they see results. "If students can point to one clear change, such as longer library hours, they will feel that their vote matters," she said. She also suggested lowering the 20 percent requirement, which she called unrealistic for a university of this size. Council president Grace Wu disagreed, arguing that a lower requirement would weaken the council's authority when it speaks for students.

6. What happened as a result of last spring's low turnout?

  1. The council president resigned.
  2. A second election had to be held.
  3. Final exams were postponed.
  4. The council's budget was cut.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第一段說投票率未達 20%，結果無效，只好在五月重新選舉，選 B。期末考並未延期，文中也未提會長辭職或預算被刪。

7. According to the survey, what was the most common reason students did not vote?

  1. They were not interested in campus affairs.
  2. They did not understand the council's role.
  3. They did not know when the election took place.
  4. They were busy preparing for exams.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　調查中 41% 表示不知道選舉何時舉行，比例最高，選 C。不了解學生會功能為 33%，不感興趣僅 12%。

8. What can be inferred about Grace Wu?

  1. She thinks three-day voting is too long.
  2. She teaches political science.
  3. She plans to cancel the live-streamed debate.
  4. She wants to keep the 20 percent requirement.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　她反對降低門檻，認為會削弱學生會的代表性，可推知她希望維持 20% 規定，選 D。教政治學的是許教授；其他選項文中無據。

9. Which are NEW measures for this year's election? Choose TWO.

  1. Students can vote by paper ballot.
  2. Candidates must hold an open forum in their own college.
  3. The turnout requirement has been lowered.
  4. Voting has been extended to three days.
  5. Candidates receive funding from the council.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　第三段提到候選人須在所屬學院辦公開座談，且投票由一天延長為三天，選 B、D。降低門檻只是許教授的建議，尚未實施；紙本投票與經費補助文中未提。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The council room is on the third floor.
  2. I already counted the votes.
  3. Online voting opens next Monday at nine.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問投票何時開始，C 給出具體時間最合理。A 只說學生會辦公室位置，答非所問；B 重複 vote 一字製造混淆，且談的是計票。

聽力原文

When does voting for the student council election start?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. No, I'm too busy with my lab work this semester.
  2. I usually run in the park after class.
  3. The representative's office is closed today.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　run for 指「參選」，A 以太忙婉拒參選最恰當。B 把 run 誤當「跑步」；C 重複 representative 但離題。

聽力原文

Are you running for class representative this year?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Sure, I'll support the table while you move it.
  2. Why don't you watch the debate on Wednesday night?
  3. She decided to change her major last year.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　對方不知該支持哪位候選人，B 建議看週三的政見辯論最合理。A 把 support 當「撐住（桌子）」；C 重複 decide 但與選舉無關。

聽力原文

I can't decide which candidate to support.

4. What does the man suggest the woman do?

  1. Register as an undergraduate
  2. Volunteer for the campaign
  3. Bring a different ID card
  4. Run for office herself
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男子說研究生不能連署，但可以 help with his campaign as a volunteer，選 B。他沒有建議換證件、改註冊身分或自行參選。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to sign the nomination form for Kevin Chen. Is this the right place? Yes, but only registered undergraduates can sign. Can I see your student ID? Here it is. Oh, wait. I'm a graduate student, actually. Then I'm afraid you can't sign for an undergraduate candidate. You could still help with his campaign as a volunteer, though.

5. According to the woman, what is needed for the plan to be funded?

  1. Approval from the library director
  2. Volunteers to work night shifts
  3. Three thousand student signatures
  4. An extra fee from every student
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女子說若至少三千名學生連署線上請願，學校就會負擔費用，選 C。對話未提館長核准、志工值夜或向學生加收費用。

聽力原文

Did you read the student council's proposal about library hours? Yes. They want the library to stay open until two a.m. during finals. I like the idea, but who's going to pay for the extra staff? The university says it'll cover the cost if at least three thousand students sign the online petition.

6. Why was the debate moved to the Main Auditorium?

  1. Many more people registered than expected.
  2. The Student Center is being renovated.
  3. The candidates asked for a bigger stage.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播說超過四百人報名，需要更大的場地，選 A。未提學生活動中心整修，也不是候選人要求。

聽力原文

Attention, students. The student council candidates' debate will take place this Wednesday at seven p.m. in the Main Auditorium, not in the Student Center as announced earlier. More than four hundred people have registered to attend, so we needed a larger room. Seating is first come, first served, and doors open at six thirty. If you can't attend, the debate will be streamed live on the council's YouTube channel. Questions for the candidates can be submitted through the online form until Tuesday at midnight.

7. Who is the audience of this talk?

  1. Candidates for council president
  2. University committee staff
  3. First-year international students
  4. Newly chosen class representatives
看答案與詳解

正解 D　開頭即說 congratulations on being chosen as class representatives，聽眾是新任班代，選 D。講者本人才是學生會長，其他選項皆未提及。

8. According to the speaker, what did student committee members do last year?

  1. They raised money for new equipment.
  2. They helped postpone a cut to evening bus service.
  3. They changed the course schedule.
  4. They designed a campus safety survey.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說去年學生委員協助延後刪減晚間校車的計畫，直到完成調查，選 B（delay = postpone）。調查並非由學生設計，其餘選項只是重複談話中的字眼。

9. What does the speaker say representatives should do? Choose TWO.

  1. Read the documents before committee meetings
  2. Write a monthly report for the president
  3. Send a substitute if they cannot attend a meeting
  4. Visit every department once a semester
  5. Join at least two university committees
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　講者要求開會前仔細閱讀文件，且缺席時須派代理人並事先寄信，選 A、C。未要求寫月報、走訪各系或參加兩個委員會。

聽力原文

Good evening, everyone, and congratulations on being chosen as class representatives. My name is Grace Wu, and I'm this year's student council president. Tonight I want to explain what your role actually involves. First, you're the link between your classmates and the university. Once a month, you'll attend a representatives' meeting, where we collect concerns from every department, things like broken equipment, scheduling problems, or safety issues on campus. Second, some of you will be elected to sit on university committees. Students hold three seats on the Academic Affairs Committee and two on the Campus Planning Committee. These are real votes, so please read the documents carefully before each meeting. Last year, our student members helped delay a plan to cut evening bus service until a proper survey was done. Finally, a practical note. Representatives who attend at least eighty percent of the meetings will receive a service-learning certificate, which many of you can count toward graduation. If you have to miss a meeting, please send a substitute from your class and email our secretary in advance.
