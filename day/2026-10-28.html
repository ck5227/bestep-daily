校慶籌備與社團活動

今日單字

eligible adj. 有資格的
Clubs with fewer than fifteen members are not eligible for the grant.
grant n. 補助金
Our club applied for an activity grant to cover the cost of the concert.
budget plan n. 預算計畫
Please attach a detailed budget plan to your activity proposal.
priority n. 優先（考量）
Events involving community service will be given priority this year.
booth n. 攤位
The robotics club set up a booth near the main gate.
swap v. 交換
Can I swap shifts with you on Saturday?
guarantee v. 保證
We cannot guarantee a seat for late registrations.
commit to v. phr. 承諾投入；致力於
I can't commit to weekly meetings because of my part-time job.

閱讀 Reading

1. Members who fail to pay the annual fee by the end of October will no longer be ______ to vote in club elections.

  1. eligible
  2. available
  3. capable
  4. possible
看答案與詳解

正解 A　be eligible to + V 表示「有資格做…」，符合「未繳會費便無投票資格」。available 指可取得的；capable 須接 of；possible 不用於人。

2. The drama club's play was so popular that the organizers decided to ______ an extra show on Sunday afternoon.

  1. attend
  2. cancel
  3. schedule
  4. postpone
看答案與詳解

正解 C　演出大受歡迎，所以「安排」加演一場，schedule 最合理。attend 是參加者的動作；cancel、postpone 與「很受歡迎」的因果矛盾。

From: Student Volunteer Association

To: Anniversary Fair Volunteers

Subject: Your volunteer shift for the anniversary fair

Dear volunteers, thank you for signing up to help at the university's seventieth anniversary fair on Saturday, November 7. We received more than eighty applications this year, which is a record for our association.

Your shift assignment is attached to this email. Each shift lasts three hours. If you are unable to work your assigned shift, please find another volunteer to swap with you and let us know by Wednesday, November 4. We cannot guarantee that changes requested after that date will be accepted.

All volunteers must attend a short briefing at 8 a.m. on the day of the fair at the Student Activity Center. There you will receive a volunteer T-shirt, a name badge, and a lunch voucher. Volunteers who complete their shift will also earn four service-learning hours, which count toward graduation requirements.

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To recruit more volunteers for the fair
  2. To give volunteers details about their shifts and the event day
  3. To announce that the fair has been postponed
  4. To ask volunteers for feedback on last year's fair
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信件說明班表已附上、換班方式、當天說明會與福利，主旨是提供志工班表及當天資訊。信中提到報名已破紀錄，並非再招募；未提及延期或回饋。

4. What should a volunteer do if he or she cannot work the assigned shift?

  1. Contact the office on the morning of the fair
  2. Choose a new shift on the association's website
  3. Skip the briefing and arrive later
  4. Arrange a swap with another volunteer by November 4
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第二段指出須 find another volunteer to swap with you 並在 11 月 4 日前告知。其他選項文中均未提及，且當天才聯絡已超過期限。

5. The word "guarantee" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. promise
  2. review
  3. charge
  4. refuse
看答案與詳解

正解 A　cannot guarantee that changes ... will be accepted 意為「無法保證／承諾」變更會被接受，與 promise 最接近。review 是審查，charge 是收費，refuse 是拒絕，語意不符。

Fewer Members, More Ideas: How Campus Clubs Are Changing

A recent survey by the Office of Student Affairs found that the number of students who belong to at least one campus club has fallen by about twelve percent over the past five years. Of the 1,200 students who responded, many said they were still interested in club activities but simply did not have enough time.

The most common reason given was part-time work. Nearly forty percent of respondents said they work more than ten hours a week, often in the evenings when most clubs meet. Others pointed to heavier course loads, especially in programs that have added required credits in recent years. A smaller group said they now prefer online communities, where they can share hobbies without fixed meeting times.

Instead of giving up, several clubs have started to experiment. The Mountaineering Club, for example, introduced a "flexible membership" last spring. Students can join individual trips without committing to weekly meetings, as long as they complete the club's safety training. "We used to lose half of our new members by November," said club president Lin Yu-ting, a junior in civil engineering. "Now people stay because they can choose what fits their schedule."

Other clubs are joining forces. This semester, the Board Game Club and the English Conversation Club began holding a monthly game night where players may speak only English. Attendance at the first event was double what either club usually sees.

The university is also responding. Beginning next semester, a new program will allow students to list club leadership roles on an official co-curricular transcript, which can be shown to employers and graduate schools. Officials hope the change will encourage students to see club work as part of their education rather than something extra.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Students who are no longer interested in club activities
  2. How part-time jobs are hurting students' grades
  3. How campus clubs are finding new ways to deal with falling membership
  4. The university's plan to close several small clubs
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文先說明社團人數下降，再介紹社團與校方的因應做法，故選 C。第一段指出學生仍有興趣，A 錯；成績與關閉社團文中未提。

7. What does Lin Yu-ting suggest about the flexible membership?

  1. It has helped the club keep its new members.
  2. It has made the club's trips less safe.
  3. It was created by the Office of Student Affairs.
  4. It requires members to attend weekly meetings.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Lin 說過去到 11 月會流失一半新生，現在大家會留下來（Now people stay），表示彈性會員制有助留住新成員。仍須完成安全訓練，且不需每週出席；是社團自行推出。

8. What is the purpose of the co-curricular transcript?

  1. To replace students' academic transcripts
  2. To officially record students' club leadership experience
  3. To give students course credit for club activities
  4. To track attendance at club meetings
看答案與詳解

正解 B　最後一段說明此證明讓學生將社團幹部經歷列入正式紀錄，可供雇主與研究所參考。文中未說取代成績單、抵學分或記錄出席。

9. Which TWO reasons for the drop in club membership are mentioned in the article? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Part-time work
  2. High membership fees
  3. A decrease in the number of clubs
  4. Poor advertising by clubs
  5. Heavier course loads
看答案與詳解

正解 A、E　第二段指出最常見原因是 part-time work，其次為 heavier course loads（另有少數偏好線上社群）。會費、社團數量減少、宣傳不足均未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The sign on the club door is too small.
  2. I'm thinking about it, but their meetings clash with my lab.
  3. I took that photo of the library last week.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否要加入攝影社，B 表示考慮中但聚會時間和實驗課衝堂，最合理。A 用 sign 諧音混淆；C 重複 photo 卻答非所問。

聽力原文

Are you going to sign up for the photography club this semester?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. It costs about two hundred dollars to rent.
  2. At the main gate, I think.
  3. Kevin from the student association is.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　以 Who 詢問負責人，C 回答人名 Kevin 最恰當。A 回答價錢、B 回答地點，都不是回答「誰」。

聽力原文

Who's in charge of our club's booth at the anniversary fair?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Good idea. Let's book the studio for Thursday evening.
  2. Yes, the dancers wore red shirts last time.
  3. I've only seen that performance one time.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　提議演出前再排練一次，A 同意並提出預約練舞室，最合理。B 重複 dance(ers) 但答非所問；C 重複 one time、performance 造成混淆。

聽力原文

Shouldn't we rehearse the dance one more time before the performance?

4. What must the man do before going on the club's trip?

  1. Pay a membership fee
  2. Buy a pair of hiking boots
  3. Get a note from a doctor
  4. Attend a safety briefing
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女子說新成員必須先參加 safety briefing（安全說明會）才能參加登山行程。會費、登山鞋、醫生證明對話中都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to join the hiking club. Is it too late to sign up? Not at all. But because of the mountain trip next month, all new members have to attend a safety briefing first. Okay. When is the next one? This Thursday at seven in the evening, in room two oh five of the Student Activity Center.

5. What will the woman probably do next?

  1. Withdraw from the relay race
  2. Check the weather forecast again
  3. Ask someone to work her shift
  4. Sign up for a different event
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女子最後說 I'll have to ask someone to cover my shift，表示會找人代班。她沒有說要退出接力或改報其他項目；天氣預報是男子提到的改期原因。

聽力原文

The anniversary sports day is on Saturday, right? I signed up for the relay race. Actually, it's been moved to Sunday because heavy rain is expected on Saturday. Oh no, I work at the café on Sunday mornings. I'll have to ask someone to cover my shift.

6. According to the announcement, what is NOT allowed at the booths?

  1. Selling food that is already prepared
  2. Using portable gas stoves
  3. Setting up before the opening ceremony
看答案與詳解

正解 B　廣播明確指出 open flames, including portable gas stoves, are not permitted。販售已備好的食物是被要求的做法；社團本來就應在 7:30 到場布置。

聽力原文

Attention, students. The university's seventieth anniversary fair will be held on Saturday, November seventh, on the main sports field. The opening ceremony begins at nine a.m. Clubs that have reserved a booth should arrive by seven thirty to set up. Please note that open flames, including portable gas stoves, are not permitted at any booth. Clubs selling food must bring items that are already prepared. If you have any questions, please visit the Office of Student Affairs.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A professor of accounting
  2. A club president
  3. A community volunteer
  4. A staff member who advises clubs
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者自我介紹來自學務處（Office of Student Affairs），負責輔導所有社團（I advise all registered clubs），故選 D。她不是教授、社長或社區志工。

8. What is the deadline for spring activity grant applications?

  1. November second
  2. November twelfth
  3. November twentieth
  4. December twentieth
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說 The deadline for spring events is November twentieth。其他日期為發音相近的干擾選項。

9. Which TWO are required in order to apply for a grant? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Attending the advisor's office hours
  2. Having at least fifteen active members
  3. Holding an event open to the whole campus
  4. Submitting a budget plan with the proposal
  5. Getting a signature from a faculty member
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　申請資格為 at least fifteen active members 及 submit a budget plan。全校性活動只是「優先考量」而非必要條件；諮詢時間是自由參加；教師簽名未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to this semester's club leaders' meeting. I'm Ms. Chen from the Office of Student Affairs, and I advise all registered clubs on campus. Today I want to talk about funding, because many of you have asked how to get money for events in the spring. Every registered club can apply for an activity grant of up to fifteen thousand Taiwan dollars per semester. The deadline for spring events is November twentieth. To qualify, your club must have at least fifteen active members, and you need to submit a budget plan along with your activity proposal. Late applications will not be reviewed, so please don't wait until the last week. After your event, you must hand in all original receipts and a short report with photos within two weeks. Clubs that skip this step will not be eligible for funding next year. One more thing: this year, events that are open to the whole campus or involve community service will be given priority. If you'd like feedback on your proposal, my office hours are Tuesday and Thursday afternoons.
