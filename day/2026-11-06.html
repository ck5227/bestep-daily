畢業門檻與學分檢核

今日單字

graduation audit n. 畢業學分檢核
Check your graduation audit before spring registration opens.
accumulate v. 累積
You need to accumulate 128 credits to graduate.
petition n./v. 申請（書）；陳情
He filed a petition to have his summer course counted.
approve in advance phr. 事先核准
Overseas courses must be approved in advance by your department.
eligibility n. 資格
The audit is used to determine your eligibility to graduate.
exceptional adj. 例外的；特殊的（亦有「傑出的」之意）
Late corrections are accepted only in exceptional cases.
fall behind phr. v. 落後；跟不上
GradPath will warn you if you fall behind in any requirement.
sign off on phr. v. 簽核；批准
Your advisor has to sign off on the credit transfer form.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students must ______ at least 128 credits, including 30 general education credits, in order to graduate.

  1. accommodate
  2. accompany
  3. accuse
  4. accumulate
看答案與詳解

正解 D　accumulate credits 指「累積學分」，符合畢業門檻語意。accommodate 容納、accompany 陪伴、accuse 指控，都是字首相近的陷阱。

2. Courses taken at another university will not be counted unless they have been ______ by your department in advance.

  1. approved
  2. improved
  3. removed
  4. proved
看答案與詳解

正解 A　外校課程須事先經系上「核准」才會採計，approved 正確。improved 改善、removed 移除、proved 證明，皆字形相近但語意不合。

From: Registrar's Office

To: All Seniors

Subject: Your graduation audit and correction deadline

Dear Seniors,

Your preliminary graduation audit is now available on the student portal. It shows the credits you have completed, the courses you are taking this semester, and any requirements that are still unmet. Please review it carefully, as it will be used to determine your eligibility to graduate in June 2027.

If you find an error, such as a course listed in the wrong category or a missing transfer credit, submit a correction request through the portal by November 27. Requests must include supporting documents, for example an approval form signed by your department. After this date, corrections can be made only in exceptional cases, such as a system error.

Please note that students who expect to finish their final requirement during the summer session must also apply for August graduation by March 15.

Registrar's Office

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce the date of the graduation ceremony
  2. To ask seniors to review their audit and report any errors
  3. To explain how to register for summer courses
  4. To remind students to pay graduation fees
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中說明初步畢業檢核已上線，請學生仔細檢查並在 11 月 27 日前申請更正，故選 B。典禮日期、暑修選課與費用都沒有提到。

4. The word "exceptional" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. excellent
  2. expensive
  3. unusual
  4. ordinary
看答案與詳解

正解 C　後文舉例 such as a system error，指「特殊、例外的」情況，故選 unusual。exceptional 也可表示「傑出的」，excellent 正是這個字義陷阱；ordinary 意思相反。

5. What must a student who plans to finish in the summer session do?

  1. Submit a correction request by November 27
  2. Get a form signed by the department
  3. Take a summer course at another university
  4. Apply for August graduation by March 15
看答案與詳解

正解 D　最後一段說預計暑修完成最後要求的學生須在 3 月 15 日前申請八月畢業，故選 D。A、B 是發現錯誤時才需要做的事；C 文中未提。

Graduating in Four Years? Fewer Students Than You Think

At many Taiwanese universities, staying an extra semester or two has become almost normal. At our university, only 71 percent of last year's seniors graduated within four years, down from 80 percent a decade ago, according to the Office of Academic Affairs.

Some delays are planned. Students who go on exchange, take long internships, or add a double major often choose to stay longer. Others, however, are caught by surprise. "I found out in my last semester that two of my general education courses were in the same area," said Lin Wei-chen, a fifth-year student in journalism. "I had enough credits, but not the right ones." Missing English scores and forgotten PE credits are other common reasons.

There is also a practical side. Some students delay graduation on purpose to keep their student status, which allows them to apply for internships open only to current students or to postpone military service. Each extra semester, however, means more tuition, and the university has less space for incoming students.

To reduce unplanned delays, the university will launch an online tool called GradPath next semester. Unlike the current audit, which is updated only twice a year, GradPath will update every time a new grade is posted and will warn students if they fall behind in any requirement. Advisors will also be able to see their students' progress and contact those at risk.

Not everyone thinks technology is the answer. "A dashboard can show what's missing, but it can't tell you why you should take a course," said Professor Chou Hsiu-ling, who chairs the curriculum committee. She hopes students will still meet their advisors at least once a year.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. How to apply for a double major
  2. Why more students take longer to graduate, and a new tool to help
  3. The history of general education courses
  4. Changes to military service rules
看答案與詳解

正解 B　全文說明延畢比例上升的原因，並介紹 GradPath 新工具與不同看法，故選 B。雙主修與兵役只是文中提到的細節。

7. What was Lin Wei-chen's problem?

  1. She did not have enough credits.
  2. She failed her English test.
  3. Two of her courses were in the same general education area.
  4. She forgot to take PE.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　她說兩門通識課在同一領域，學分夠但「不是對的學分」，故選 C。A 與她的話相反；英文與體育是文中提到的「其他」常見原因，不是她的問題。

8. How is GradPath different from the current audit?

  1. It will replace academic advisors.
  2. It is available only to fifth-year students.
  3. It calculates tuition for extra semesters.
  4. It is updated whenever a new grade is posted.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第四段說現行檢核一年只更新兩次，GradPath 則每次有新成績就更新，故選 D。導師仍會使用它聯繫學生，並非被取代；B、C 文中未提。

9. According to paragraph 3, why do some students keep their student status? (Choose TWO.)

  1. To apply for internships open only to current students
  2. To pay lower tuition
  3. To postpone military service
  4. To keep living in the dormitories
  5. To make room for incoming students
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　第三段指出保留學籍可申請只限在學生的實習（A）以及延後服兵役（C）。文中說延畢反而要多繳學費，也會佔用新生名額，宿舍未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The auditorium is booked for the concert tonight.
  2. Yes, and it says I still need four general education credits.
  3. I usually run around the track after class.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否已在系統跑過畢業學分檢核，B 回答跑過並說明還缺通識學分，最合理。A 用 auditorium 與 audit 音近混淆；C 重複 run 但意思是「跑步」。

聽力原文

Have you run a graduation audit on the student portal yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, unless you've already passed an approved test.
  2. I transferred the files to your laptop.
  3. I applied for that job last week.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問英文畢業門檻是否也適用轉學生，A 回答適用並補充例外條件。B 重複 transfer 但指「傳檔案」；C 用 applied 與 apply 重複字干擾。

聽力原文

Does the English requirement apply to transfer students too?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The form is due at the end of the month.
  2. I signed up for that course online.
  3. Your department's academic advisor does.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Who 問由誰核准學分抵免表，C 回答系上的導師。A 回答期限而非人；B 用 signed up 與 signs off 字形相近混淆。

聽力原文

Who signs off on the credit transfer form?

4. Which of the following must the man include with his petition?

  1. A letter from his professor
  2. A copy of his passport
  3. A new graduation check
  4. The syllabus of the summer course
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女士說 Attach the course syllabus and your official transcript，故選 D。教授信、護照都沒有提到；graduation check 是他已經看過的結果，不是要附的文件。

聽力原文

Hi, my graduation check says I'm three credits short, but I took a summer course in Japan last year. I see it here. That course wasn't approved in advance, so it hasn't been counted yet. Is there anything I can do? You can file a petition. Attach the course syllabus and your official transcript from the Japanese university, and submit it by December first.

5. Why does the man prefer the volunteer option?

  1. It fits his weekend schedule better.
  2. It counts for more credits.
  3. His friend is volunteering too.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　男士說平日已排滿課、週末有空，志工時數可在週末完成，故選 A。對話沒有說志工學分較多，也沒提到朋友。

聽力原文

Are you taking the service learning course next semester? I have to. It's a graduation requirement, and I still haven't done it. You know there's another option, right? You can do thirty-six volunteer hours at an approved organization instead. That might be better. My weekdays are already full of classes, but I'm free on weekends.

6. What should students bring to the session?

  1. Their student ID card
  2. An appointment confirmation
  3. A printed copy of their graduation audit
  4. Their course registration form
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播說 Please print your graduation audit ... and bring it with you，故選 C。廣播明說不需預約，B 錯；學生證與選課單都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Attention, all seniors. The Registrar's Office will hold graduation check sessions next Tuesday and Wednesday from ten a.m. to four p.m. in the lobby of the Administration Building. Advisors will review your credits one on one and tell you exactly what is still missing before spring course registration opens on November twenty-third. Please print your graduation audit from the student portal and bring it with you. No appointment is necessary.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To announce changes to the graduation requirements
  2. To recruit students for English-taught courses
  3. To help seniors avoid problems that delay graduation
  4. To explain how to register for P E classes
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者開頭說要說明最常讓學生無法準時畢業的三件事，故選 C。規定本身沒有改變；英語授課與體育只是其中的提醒項目。

8. What does the speaker say about general education credits?

  1. They must come from several different areas.
  2. Students need forty of them.
  3. They can replace P E credits.
  4. They are checked only during finals.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者說三十個通識學分必須來自五個領域中至少四個，故選 A。總數是三十不是四十；體育學分另計，不能互抵。

9. According to the speaker, which TWO statements are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Test scores may take about a month to arrive.
  2. P E credits count toward the 128-credit total.
  3. Students must pass three English-taught courses.
  4. The English requirement can be met through department courses.
  5. Students should email the speaker during finals.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　講者提到成績約需一個月才會寄達（A），且可修系上兩門英語授課課程達到英文門檻（D）。體育學分不計入 128 學分；是兩門而非三門；他要大家在 11 月 20 日前、不要期末才寄信。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Professor Wang, and I advise fourth-year students in the Department of Business Administration. Since spring registration opens in just a few weeks, I want to go over the three things that most often stop students from graduating on time. The first is general education. You need thirty credits in total, but they must come from at least four of the five areas. Every year, a few students have plenty of credits, but all in the same area, so please check the distribution, not just the number. The second is the English requirement. You can meet it with an approved test score, or by passing two English-taught courses in our department. If you choose the test, give yourself time, because scores can take about a month to arrive. The third is physical education. Many of you forget that the two P E credits from your first year are required, and they don't count toward your total of one hundred twenty-eight. If anything on your audit looks wrong, email me before November twentieth, not during finals.
