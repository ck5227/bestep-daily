BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-15
寫作中心與英文寫作諮詢

今日單字

consultation n. 諮詢；面談
I booked a consultation with a writing tutor for Thursday.
proofread v. 校對
Always proofread your essay before you submit it.
thesis statement n. 論點句；主旨句
Your thesis statement should be clear and specific.
citation n. 引用；引文出處
Each citation must follow APA style.
paraphrase v. 改寫；換句話說
Paraphrase the author's idea and still cite the source.
alternative n. 替代方案；選擇
If Tuesday doesn't work, we can offer an alternative.
evidence n. 證據；佐證
Support your argument with evidence from reliable sources.
drop-in adj. 免預約的；隨到隨問的
The library will open a drop-in desk for quick questions.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before submitting your essay, ask a classmate to ______ it for spelling and grammar errors.

  1. publish
  2. memorize
  3. proofread
  4. translate
看答案與詳解

正解 C　for spelling and grammar errors 表示要「校對」，proofread 最恰當。publish 出版、memorize 背誦、translate 翻譯，都與檢查拼字文法不符。

2. Your introduction is clear, but the main argument needs more supporting ______ from reliable sources.

  1. emphasis
  2. entrance
  3. excuse
  4. evidence
看答案與詳解

正解 D　supporting evidence from reliable sources 指「來自可靠來源的佐證」，故選 evidence。emphasis 強調、entrance 入口、excuse 藉口，皆不合語意。

From: University Writing Center

To: Lin Pei-shan

Subject: Your Consultation Request – Rescheduling Needed

Dear Pei-shan,

Thank you for booking a consultation for your sociology research proposal. Unfortunately, your tutor, Kevin Hsu, has a scheduling conflict on Tuesday, November 24, and will not be available at 3 p.m. as originally planned.

We can offer you two alternatives: a session with Kevin on Wednesday, November 25, at 10 a.m., or a session with another tutor, Amy Tsai, at the original time on Tuesday. Amy also specializes in social science writing. Please reply to this email by Sunday to confirm your choice. If we do not hear from you, your booking will be cancelled automatically.

Best regards,

Writing Center Front Desk

3. Why was this email sent?

  1. To give feedback on a research proposal
  2. To change the arrangements for a scheduled consultation
  3. To introduce a new tutoring service
  4. To cancel a writing workshop
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中說原定家教週二下午三點無法出席，並提供兩個替代方案，目的是調整已預約的諮詢，故選 B。A 未提供回饋；C、D 信中沒提到新服務或工作坊。

4. The word "alternatives" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. requirements
  2. reminders
  3. options
  4. discounts
看答案與詳解

正解 C　後文列出「週三和 Kevin」或「週二和 Amy」兩種選擇，alternatives 意為「可替代的選項」，最接近 options。其他選項意為要求、提醒、折扣，不合上下文。

5. What will happen if Pei-shan does not reply by Sunday?

  1. Her booking will be cancelled.
  2. She will be assigned to Amy Tsai.
  3. Her session will move to Wednesday.
  4. Kevin will contact her by phone.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信末說 If we do not hear from you, your booking will be cancelled automatically，故選 A。B、C 是需要她回信確認的選項，不會自動安排；D 未提及。

Writing Center Sees Record Demand as AI Tools Change Student Habits

Visits to the university Writing Center have reached a record high this semester. According to the center's latest report, tutors held more than 1,800 consultations between September and mid-November, an increase of about 30 percent compared with the same period last year.

Center director Dr. Chen Hui-ling believes part of the increase is connected to the growing use of AI writing tools. "Many students now produce a first draft very quickly," she explained. "But they come to us because they are not sure whether the ideas are really their own, or whether the text sounds natural to an academic reader." Tutors have also noticed that students are asking more questions about citation rules and about how to describe their use of AI in an assignment.

To respond to this demand, the center has trained twelve new peer tutors, most of whom are graduate students from engineering, business, and the humanities. It has also added online sessions, which now make up nearly half of all bookings. Students say the online option is especially convenient during busy weeks when they cannot leave their labs.

However, the expansion has not solved every problem. Some students complain that popular time slots are fully booked two weeks in advance. Wang Tzu-yu, a third-year finance student, said she had to wait ten days for an appointment. "The feedback was excellent, but the waiting time was frustrating," she said.

Dr. Chen said the center plans to open a small drop-in desk in the main library next semester, where students can ask quick questions without a reservation. She also encouraged students to attend the center's Wednesday workshops, which can serve many students at once.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Rising demand for the Writing Center and how it is responding
  2. A new university rule that bans AI writing tools
  3. A student complaint about online classes
  4. A training program for engineering graduate students
看答案與詳解

正解 A　全文先報導諮詢量創新高，再說明原因及中心的因應措施與仍存在的問題，故選 A。B 文中沒有禁用 AI 的規定；C、D 只是細節或未提及。

7. According to Dr. Chen, why do many students who use AI tools visit the center?

  1. They cannot produce a first draft on their own.
  2. They want to check whether their writing is original and sounds natural.
  3. Their professors require them to visit.
  4. They want tutors to write their citations for them.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段引述 Dr. Chen：學生不確定想法是否真是自己的、文字對學術讀者是否自然，故選 B。A 與「很快產出初稿」相反；C、D 文中未提及。

8. What is suggested about the online sessions?

  1. They are available only on weekends.
  2. They are offered only to graduate students.
  3. They are helpful for students who are busy in their labs.
  4. They will be discontinued next semester.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第三段說學生認為線上諮詢在無法離開實驗室的忙碌週特別方便，故選 C。A、B、D 文中皆無根據。

9. Which TWO changes has the center made or planned? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Banning the use of AI tools in assignments
  2. Training new peer tutors
  3. Charging a fee for popular time slots
  4. Opening a drop-in desk in the main library
  5. Reducing the number of workshops
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　第三段說已培訓十二位新同儕家教，第五段說下學期將在總圖設立免預約服務台，故選 B、D。A、C 文中未提；E 與鼓勵學生參加週三工作坊的說法不符。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The center of campus is near the library.
  2. Sure, we have an opening at two thirty.
  3. I wrote it last Thursday.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　詢問能否預約週四下午的寫作中心諮詢，B 回答兩點半有空檔最合理。A 重複 center 但指「校園中心」；C 用 wrote 與 Writing、重複 Thursday 混淆，答非所問。

聽力原文

Could I book a consultation at the Writing Center for Thursday afternoon?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, she said it's too broad.
  2. I fed the cat before class.
  3. The thesis defense is in May.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問家教是否對論點句給回饋，A 說「她說太廣泛」直接回應。B 用 fed 與 feedback 音近混淆；C 重複 thesis 但談論文口試時間，答非所問。

聽力原文

Did the tutor give you any feedback on your thesis statement?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I copied the notes from a friend.
  2. Because the draft was printed yesterday.
  3. Good idea, I'll print one at the library.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Why don't you...? 是提出建議，C 接受建議並說會去圖書館印最恰當。A 重複 copy 但意思是抄筆記；B 把建議誤當成問原因，用 Because 回答並重複 draft、printed。

聽力原文

Why don't you bring a printed copy of your draft?

4. What will the man most likely do next?

  1. Ask the tutor to correct every sentence.
  2. Submit his paper without any changes.
  3. Look for a different tutor.
  4. Choose one section of his paper to work on.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女子說明家教不逐句修改，並建議「選最沒把握的部分開始」，男子也同意，故選 D。A 正是女子說不提供的服務；B、C 對話中未提及。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like the tutor to proofread my whole term paper before I submit it. Actually, our tutors don't edit papers line by line. We help you find patterns in your mistakes so you can fix them yourself. Oh, I see. So should I just pick one section to focus on? Exactly. Choose the part you're least confident about, and we'll start there.

5. What problem does the man have?

  1. A source is missing from his reference list.
  2. He used the wrong statistic.
  3. He has already missed the deadline.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　男子說「忘了把網站加進參考文獻清單」，故選 A。B 對話未說數據錯誤；C 他說今晚會在截止前修正，表示還沒錯過期限。

聽力原文

Jason, did you cite that statistic about plastic waste in your essay? I mentioned the website in the text, but I forgot to add it to my reference list. You'd better add it. Professor Lin takes points off for incomplete references. Thanks. I'll fix it tonight before the deadline.

6. What is true about the Writing Center during the final exam period?

  1. It will accept walk-in visitors.
  2. It will be open on Sundays.
  3. Students must book sessions in advance.
  4. Each session will last two hours.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播說期末期間不接受臨時現場諮詢，須至少提前一天上網預約，故選 C。A 與原文相反；B 只開放週一至週六；D 的 two 是每週可預約兩次，不是每次兩小時。

聽力原文

Attention, students. The Writing Center will extend its hours during the final exam period. Starting December seventh, consultations will be available from nine in the morning until nine at night, Monday through Saturday. Because demand is high, each student may book only two sessions per week. Walk-in visits will not be accepted during this period, so please make your reservation online at least one day in advance.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A first-year student
  2. The director of the Writing Center
  3. An exchange student
  4. A professor in the honors program
看答案與詳解

正解 B　說話者自稱 Dr. Chen，並說 I direct the center，故為寫作中心主任。A、C、D 都是她提到的使用者族群，不是她本人。

8. What should students do before a session?

  1. Check all of their grammar carefully.
  2. Attend a Wednesday workshop.
  3. Email the tutor a list of questions.
  4. Upload their draft and the assignment instructions.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　她說「來之前請把草稿和作業說明上傳到預約系統」，故選 D。A 文法是諮詢時有空才處理；B 工作坊是另外的活動，非必要；C 未提到寄信給家教。

9. Which TWO statements about the workshops are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. They are held on Wednesday evenings.
  2. They cost a small fee.
  3. One topic is avoiding plagiarism when paraphrasing.
  4. They are only for graduate students.
  5. They last forty-five minutes.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　原文說工作坊「每週三晚上」舉辦，主題之一是「改寫時避免抄襲」，故選 A、C。B 原文說 free；D 未限研究生；E 的四十五分鐘是一對一諮詢的長度。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to this orientation for the Writing Center. My name is Dr. Chen, and I direct the center. Many first-year students think we only help people whose English is weak, but that's not true. Graduate students, exchange students, and even top students in the honors program use our services every week. Here's how a typical session works. You'll meet a trained tutor for forty-five minutes. Before you come, please upload your draft and the assignment instructions to the booking system, so the tutor can read them in advance. During the session, the tutor will ask you questions about your argument and organization first. Grammar comes later, if there's time. We also run free workshops every Wednesday evening. This month's topics are writing a strong research proposal and avoiding plagiarism when you paraphrase. Seats are limited to twenty, so sign up early. Finally, remember that we never write or rewrite your paper for you. Our goal is to help you become an independent writer.
