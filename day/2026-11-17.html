語言交換與國際學伴

今日單字

coordinator n. 協調人；統籌者
The buddy coordinator arranged a shuttle bus for the new students.
familiarize v. 使熟悉（familiarize oneself with）
Please familiarize yourself with the campus map before orientation.
homesick adj. 想家的
Many exchange students feel homesick during their first winter abroad.
waiting list n. 候補名單
Because the program was full, Amy was placed on the waiting list.
alternative n. 替代方案
The office offers an online alternative for students who cannot attend.
guideline n. 指導原則；準則
The new guidelines ask partners to split each meeting evenly.
target language n. 目標語言（正在學習的語言）
Try to use your target language for at least half of each session.
take over phr. v. 接管；占主導地位
Without clear rules, English tends to take over the conversation.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before the first exchange session, participants are asked to ______ themselves with a few basic greetings in their partner's language.

  1. familiar
  2. familiarity
  3. familiarize
  4. familiarly
看答案與詳解

正解 C　are asked to 後接原形動詞，familiarize oneself with 表示「使自己熟悉」，選 C。familiar 是形容詞、familiarity 是名詞、familiarly 是副詞，都不能接在 to 後當動詞。

2. Because both partners had busy schedules, they decided to meet online ______ in person.

  1. instead of
  2. as well as
  3. in spite of
  4. because of
看答案與詳解

正解 A　兩人都很忙，所以選擇線上見面「而不是」面對面，instead of 最合理。as well as 表示「兩者都」，與忙碌的前提矛盾；in spite of、because of 語意不通。

Matching Results and Next Steps

From: Office of International Affairs

To: Spring 2027 Buddy Program Applicants

Subject: Matching results and next steps

Dear applicants,

Thank you for applying to the Spring 2027 International Buddy Program. We received 142 applications for 95 incoming exchange students, so some of you have been placed on a waiting list. Please check your matching result on the student portal by Friday, November 27.

If you have been matched, you must attend one training session before the end of the semester. Sessions will be held on December 3 and December 10 from 6:00 to 8:00 p.m. in Room 205 of the International House. Please register for your preferred date through the portal. Students who cannot attend either session should email us so that we can arrange an online alternative.

Matched volunteers will receive their buddy's contact information in early January. We encourage you to send a short welcome message before your buddy leaves for Taiwan, as early contact helps new students feel less anxious about their arrival.

Best regards, Office of International Affairs

3. Why have some applicants been placed on a waiting list?

  1. They missed the application deadline.
  2. They did not attend a training session.
  3. Their language test scores were too low.
  4. There were more applicants than incoming students.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中說收到 142 份申請，但只有 95 位交換生，因此部分人列入候補，選 D。截止日、訓練課程與語言成績都不是候補的原因。

4. What should a matched volunteer do if neither training date works?

  1. Apply again next semester
  2. Email the office
  3. Attend a session in January
  4. Ask another volunteer for notes
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第二段寫明兩場都無法參加的學生「應寄信給我們」，以安排線上替代方案，選 B。一月是收到學伴聯絡資料的時間，不是訓練時間。

5. The word "anxious" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. excited
  2. curious
  3. worried
  4. confused
看答案與詳解

正解 C　anxious 意為「焦慮的、擔心的」，及早聯絡能讓新生對抵達「比較不擔心」，最接近 worried。excited 是興奮，curious 是好奇，confused 是困惑。

Talk Partners: Why Language Exchange Works, and When It Doesn't

When third-year engineering student Tsai Yu-ting joined her university's language exchange program last spring, she expected to improve her Spanish quickly. Instead, after three meetings with her partner from Madrid, she noticed they were speaking almost entirely in English. "It was comfortable for both of us," she admitted, "but I wasn't learning anything."

Tsai's experience is common, according to Dr. Helen Brooks, who coordinates the program at the university's Language Center. "Language exchange is one of the cheapest and most enjoyable ways to practice speaking," she explained, "but without some structure, the stronger language usually takes over." This year, the center has introduced simple guidelines. Partners are asked to divide each meeting equally between the two languages and to agree on a topic in advance, such as food, travel, or a recent film. They are also given a short checklist to record new expressions they learn.

The early results look promising. In a survey of 120 participants at the end of the midterm period, 78 percent said the guidelines helped them use their target language more often. Several international students added that the program helped them make local friends, something they had found difficult in classes taught mainly in Chinese.

Still, Brooks warns that language exchange is not a replacement for formal study. "Your partner is a friend, not a teacher," she said. "They can tell you what sounds natural, but they may not be able to explain grammar rules." She recommends that students combine exchange sessions with regular courses or tutoring at the Writing Center.

As for Tsai, she now meets her partner every Wednesday at a café near campus. "We still laugh a lot," she said, "but now I actually leave each meeting with ten new Spanish words."

6. What was Tsai's problem at first?

  1. She and her partner mostly spoke English.
  2. Her partner often missed their meetings.
  3. She could not understand her partner's accent.
  4. The program did not offer Spanish.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第一段說見面三次後她發現兩人「幾乎全程講英文」，所以學不到西班牙文，選 A。文中沒有提到缺席、口音或課程不提供西班牙文。

7. According to Dr. Brooks, what usually happens when language exchange has no structure?

  1. Partners stop meeting after a few weeks.
  2. Students learn grammar more slowly.
  3. The meetings become too formal.
  4. The stronger language dominates.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　Brooks 說沒有架構時「較強的語言通常會主導（takes over）」，D 的 dominates 為同義改寫。其他選項文中都沒有提到。

8. What does Dr. Brooks recommend?

  1. Replacing regular courses with exchange sessions
  2. Combining exchange sessions with formal study
  3. Choosing partners who are trained teachers
  4. Meeting partners every day
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第四段她建議把交換時段與正式課程或寫作中心輔導「結合」，選 B。她明確說語言交換不能取代正式學習，也說夥伴是朋友不是老師，故 A、C 錯。

9. Which TWO guidelines has the Language Center introduced? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Dividing each meeting evenly between the two languages
  2. Meeting only inside the Language Center
  3. Choosing a topic before each meeting
  4. Taking a grammar test every month
  5. Writing a report for the coordinator
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　第二段列出的規則是「每次時間平分給兩種語言」與「事先約定主題」，選 A、C。文中的清單是給自己記錄新詞，不是交報告；見面地點與文法測驗都未規定。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I'd like to exchange this shirt for a smaller size.
  2. Yes, I registered on the website on Monday.
  3. It's on the sign by the door.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否已報名語言交換，B 回答「已在週一上網報名」最合理。A 重複 exchange 但指退換商品；C 用 sign 與 signed 字形相近混淆。

聽力原文

Have you signed up for the language exchange program yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The buddy coordinator arranged a shuttle bus.
  2. I picked the window seat.
  3. Around three in the afternoon.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問「誰」去機場接人，A 說明學伴協調人安排了接駁車，回應了接送安排。B 用 picked 與 pick up 混淆；C 回答時間，不是人。

聽力原文

Who's going to pick up the international students at the airport on Saturday?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Because the class was canceled.
  2. The center opens at eight.
  3. Sure, I'm free after four.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Why don't we…? 是提議而非詢問原因，C「好啊，我四點後有空」接受提議。A 誤把它當成問原因而用 Because 作答；B 重複 center 但答非所問。

聽力原文

Why don't we meet at the café near the language center after class?

4. What problem does the man mention?

  1. His partner often cancels their meetings.
  2. They can't find a place to meet.
  3. His partner is too busy on Thursdays.
  4. He rarely gets to practice Japanese.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　男子說對方一直切換成英文，所以「練不到日文」，對應 D。他們每週見兩次面，沒有取消或找不到地點的問題；Thursday 只是他打算提出建議的日子。

聽力原文

Hi Kevin, how's it going with your language partner from Japan? Pretty well. We meet twice a week, but she keeps switching to English, so I'm not getting much Japanese practice. Maybe you two could split each meeting in half, thirty minutes in each language. That's a good idea. I'll suggest it on Thursday.

5. What must the man submit in addition to the online form?

  1. A copy of his transcript
  2. A recommendation letter
  3. A one-minute video
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女子說除了線上表單，只需要「約一分鐘的自我介紹影片」，選 C。成績單與推薦信對話中都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Excuse me, I'd like to join the international buddy program. Is it too late? The first round closed last week, but we're opening a second round for students arriving in February. The deadline is December fifth. Great. Do I need anything besides the online form? Just a short self-introduction video, about one minute long.

6. What time should volunteers arrive?

  1. At six o'clock
  2. At six fifteen
  3. At six thirty
  4. At six forty-five
看答案與詳解

正解 B　晚宴六點半開始，志工要「提早十五分鐘」到場幫忙布置，所以是六點十五分，選 B。C 是晚宴開始時間，不是志工到場時間。

聽力原文

Attention, all buddy program volunteers. Tonight's welcome dinner for new international students has been moved from the student cafeteria to the second-floor lounge of the International House, because the cafeteria is being repaired. The dinner will still begin at six thirty. Please arrive fifteen minutes early to help set up the tables and name tags. Thank you.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A professor of Chinese
  2. A nurse at the student health center
  3. A staff member of the international office
  4. An exchange student from Japan
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者自我介紹「來自國際事務處（Office of International Affairs）」，選 C。健康中心只是她提到要協助學生辦保險的地點。

8. What should buddies do if their buddy seems stressed for a long time?

  1. Contact the international office
  2. Take the buddy on a hiking trip
  3. Help the buddy with homework
  4. Call the buddy's family
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者明確說若學伴想家或壓力大持續數週，要「聯絡我們辦公室，而不是自己解決」，選 A。健行是平常活動的例子；她也說學伴不是作業翻譯。

9. Which TWO tasks are buddies expected to help with during the first two weeks? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Translating homework assignments
  2. Opening a bank account
  3. Planning a night market tour
  4. Booking flights home
  5. Using the online course system
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　前兩週的任務包括開銀行帳戶、辦手機門號、辦保險，以及帶他們使用多為中文的線上課程系統，故選 B、E。講者強調不需翻譯作業；夜市是第一個月後的自由活動，訂機票未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and thank you for volunteering as international buddies this year. I'm Lisa from the Office of International Affairs. Each of you will be matched with one or two exchange students who arrive in February. Your main job during their first two weeks is practical: help them open a bank account, get a local phone number, and register for health insurance at the student health center. Many students also feel lost using the online course system, which is mostly in Chinese, so please walk them through it. After the first month, things become more relaxed. We ask you to meet your buddy at least twice a month, but where you go is up to you. Night markets and hiking trips are always popular. Please remember that you are not a tour guide, and you are not a translator for their homework. If your buddy seems homesick or stressed for more than a few weeks, contact our office rather than trying to solve it yourself. Finally, volunteers who complete the semester will receive eighteen hours of service-learning credit.
