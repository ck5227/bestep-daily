實驗室安全與研究室規範

今日單字

goggles n. 護目鏡
Always wear your goggles when you work with chemicals.
spill v./n. 灑出；溢出
If a chemical spills, report it to the lab manager right away.
activate v. 啟用；使生效
Your key card will be activated within two working days.
incident report n. 事故報告
Please fill out an incident report within twenty-four hours.
dispose of phr. v. 丟棄；處理掉
Unlabeled containers should be disposed of properly.
inspection n. 檢查；視察
The annual lab inspection will take place on Friday.
pilot program n. 試辦計畫
The pilot program was launched in six science buildings.
closed-toe shoes n. 包頭鞋
Closed-toe shoes are required in the lab at all times.

閱讀 Reading

1. Before starting the experiment, all students must ______ the safety instructions posted on the wall carefully.

  1. remind
  2. refuse
  3. review
  4. retire
看答案與詳解

正解 C　「仔細閱讀／複習牆上的安全說明」，review 最合適。remind 需接人當受詞；refuse（拒絕）、retire（退休）語意不通。

2. The lab was closed for a week after a small fire, but fortunately no one was ______.

  1. inspired
  2. injured
  3. invented
  4. insisted
看答案與詳解

正解 B　fortunately 暗示「幸好沒人受傷」，選 injured。inspired（受啟發）、invented（被發明）語意不合；insist 不能這樣用被動。

From: Dr. Mei-ling Wang

To: All members, Wang Lab

Subject: Lab inspection this Friday

Dear all,

The university's Environmental Safety Office will conduct its annual inspection of our lab this Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. The inspectors will check chemical storage, waste labels, and emergency equipment, and they may ask any of you basic safety questions.

Please spend some time this week tidying your bench. Every bottle must have a clear label showing its contents and the date it was opened. Any unlabeled containers should be disposed of properly. Ask Jason if you are unsure how to do this. Also, make sure the walkway to the emergency shower is completely clear; last year we lost points because boxes were blocking it.

We will hold a short meeting on Wednesday at 4 p.m. to go over common inspection questions. Attendance is required for first-year students and strongly encouraged for everyone else.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Mei-ling Wang

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To help lab members prepare for an inspection
  2. To introduce a new lab member
  3. To report the results of last year's inspection
  4. To change the time of a weekly meeting
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信中說明週五年度檢查並要求整理桌面、貼標籤、清空走道，目的在幫成員準備檢查，選 A。B、D 未提及；去年結果只是順帶一提。

4. The phrase "disposed of" in the email is closest in meaning to

  1. labeled
  2. opened
  3. returned
  4. thrown away
看答案與詳解

正解 D　沒有標籤的容器應該被妥善 disposed of（丟棄、處理掉），意思最接近 thrown away。labeled 正好是它們缺少的東西，opened、returned 不合文意。

5. What happened during last year's inspection?

  1. The lab failed because of missing labels.
  2. An inspector questioned only first-year students.
  3. The lab lost points because a walkway was blocked.
  4. The emergency shower was found to be broken.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中寫 last year we lost points because boxes were blocking it（通往緊急沖淋的走道），選 C。其他選項都是把文中字眼張冠李戴。

From Slides to QR Codes: Making Lab Safety Training Stick

When Lin Yu-ting started her master's program in chemistry last year, she finished the required online safety course in a single evening. "I clicked through the slides as fast as I could," she admits. "A week later, I couldn't remember where the nearest fire blanket was." Her experience is common. A survey by the university's Environmental Safety Office found that nearly sixty percent of new lab users could not correctly locate basic emergency equipment one month after training.

To address the problem, the office launched a pilot program this semester in six science buildings. Instead of relying only on online slides, the program places QR codes next to every eye wash station, fire extinguisher, and chemical cabinet. Scanning a code opens a one-minute video showing how to use that piece of equipment. In addition, new users now take a short "safety walk" with a senior lab member during their first week.

Early results are encouraging. In a follow-up check, eighty-five percent of participants in the pilot buildings found the emergency equipment correctly, compared with forty-two percent in other buildings. The number of reported minor accidents, such as small spills and burns, also dropped slightly.

Not everyone is convinced, however. Some professors worry that the safety walks take time away from research, especially in small labs with only one or two senior students. Others point out that the videos are currently available only in Mandarin, which makes them less useful for the growing number of international students on campus.

The office says it plans to add English subtitles by next spring. It will review the program at the end of the semester before deciding whether to expand it campus-wide.

6. What problem led to the pilot program?

  1. Lab equipment was often broken.
  2. Online courses were too expensive.
  3. Many new users forgot where safety equipment was.
  4. Too few students signed up for the safety course.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第一段調查指出近六成新使用者一個月後找不到基本緊急設備，因此推出試辦計畫，選 C。設備損壞、費用與報名人數皆未提及。

7. What happens when someone scans one of the QR codes?

  1. A short instructional video plays.
  2. The person's attendance is recorded.
  3. An alarm alerts the lab manager.
  4. A safety quiz appears on the screen.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第二段說 Scanning a code opens a one-minute video showing how to use that piece of equipment，選 A。其他功能文中沒有提到。

8. According to the article, what does the office plan to do by next spring?

  1. Expand the program to every building
  2. Cancel the safety walks
  3. Hire more senior lab members
  4. Add English subtitles to the videos
看答案與詳解

正解 D　最後一段 plans to add English subtitles by next spring，選 D。是否擴大到全校要等學期末檢討後才決定，故 A 錯。

9. Which TWO concerns about the program are mentioned? (Choose TWO.)

  1. The safety walks take time away from research.
  2. The QR codes are difficult to scan.
  3. The videos are too long to be useful.
  4. The videos are not yet available in English.
  5. The program is too expensive to run.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　第四段提到教授擔心安全導覽佔用研究時間（A），以及影片只有中文、對國際生不便（D）。掃碼困難、影片太長（其實只有一分鐘）與經費都沒提。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I think the train is running late.
  2. Not yet, but I'm planning to do it tonight.
  3. The lab is on the third floor.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否完成實驗室安全訓練，B 說還沒、今晚會做最合理。A 用 train 與 training 音近混淆；C 重複 lab 但答非所問。

聽力原文

Have you finished the lab safety training yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. It spilled all over my notes.
  2. Yes, I'll tell you later.
  3. Report it to the lab manager right away.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Who 問該通報誰，C 指出實驗室管理員。A 重複 spill 但描述別的事；B 用 Yes 回答 Wh- 問句，不合理。

聽力原文

Who should I tell if a chemical spills?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I left them in my locker. I'll go get them now.
  2. Because the team scored two goals.
  3. I'm wearing my new jacket.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問為何沒戴護目鏡，A 說明放在置物櫃、馬上去拿。B 用 goals 與 goggles 音近混淆；C 重複 wearing 但與護目鏡無關。

聽力原文

Why aren't you wearing your goggles?

4. What will the man probably do next?

  1. Finish the experiment before ten
  2. Ask a classmate to stay with him
  3. Request a new key card
  4. Move his experiment to another room
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生最後說 I'll ask Kevin if he can stay with me，因規定下班後不能獨自工作，故選 B。其他選項對話皆未提及。

聽力原文

Professor Lin, could I stay in the lab after ten tonight to finish my experiment? Only if someone else is there with you. The rules say no one works alone after hours. Okay, then I'll ask Kevin if he can stay with me.

5. Why can't the woman put her lunch in the lab fridge?

  1. It is already full.
  2. It is broken.
  3. It is only for samples and chemicals.
  4. It belongs to another lab.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男生說 That lab fridge is only for samples and chemicals，故選 C。冰箱滿了、壞了或屬於別的實驗室都沒有提到。

聽力原文

The fridge in our lab has a sign that says no food or drinks. Where can I put my lunch? There's a kitchen down the hall, next to the elevator. That lab fridge is only for samples and chemicals, so never put food in it, not even for a minute. Got it. Thanks!

6. What should people do if the alarm rings at two thirty on Thursday?

  1. Leave by the nearest stairway
  2. Call the building manager
  3. Go to the first floor
  4. Continue their work inside
看答案與詳解

正解 D　週四兩點到三點是測試時段，廣播說 There is no need to leave the building，故選 D。A 是其他時間聽到警報才要做的事；B、C 未提及。

聽力原文

Attention, everyone in the Science Building. The fire alarm system will be tested this Thursday between two and three in the afternoon. You may hear the alarm several times during this period. There is no need to leave the building during the test. However, if you hear an alarm at any other time, please exit immediately using the nearest stairway.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To introduce a new research project
  2. To explain safety requirements for new lab users
  3. To announce changes to the building hours
  4. To train students to repair lab equipment
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者是實驗室安全管理員，說明取得門禁卡前的三項要求及安全規定，故選 B。研究計畫、開放時間與維修設備都沒有提到。

8. How long does it take for a key card to be activated after the requirements are completed?

  1. About two hours
  2. Twenty-four hours
  3. Two working days
  4. One week
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說 your key card will be activated within two working days，故選 C。two hours 是線上課程時間，twenty-four hours 是填事故報告的期限。

9. According to the speaker, which TWO statements are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Students must practice using the eye wash station.
  2. Sandals are allowed on hot days.
  3. Chemical waste may go down the sink if it is diluted.
  4. Incident reports are used to punish careless students.
  5. An advisor must also sign the lab agreement.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、E　實作課要練習使用洗眼器（A），實驗室協議需指導教授簽名（E）。講者明說不准穿涼鞋、廢液不可倒水槽，報告也不是為了懲罰，故 B、C、D 錯。

聽力原文

Welcome to the Biology Department's research labs. My name is Grace Chen, and I'm the lab safety manager. Before any of you can get a key card, you'll need to complete three things. First, the online safety course, which takes about two hours. Second, a hands-on session with me, where you'll practice using the eye wash station and the fire extinguisher. And third, you need to sign the lab agreement, which your advisor must also sign. Once all three are done, your key card will be activated within two working days. Now, a few rules I really want to stress. Lab coats and closed-toe shoes are required at all times, so no sandals, even on hot days. Chemical waste never goes down the sink. Use the labeled containers by the back door instead. And if you have an accident, however small, please fill out an incident report within twenty-four hours. We use these reports to make the lab safer, not to punish anyone.
