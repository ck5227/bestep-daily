BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-05
學生餐廳與減塑環保

今日單字

reusable adj. 可重複使用的
Bring a reusable cup and get five dollars off your drink.
disposable adj. 拋棄式的；用完即丟的
The cafeteria no longer provides disposable lunch boxes.
deposit n. 押金；保證金
You can borrow a box for a fifty-dollar deposit.
refund n./v. 退款；退還
I was charged twice, so I asked for a refund.
portion n. （食物的）一份；份量
Half portions are cheaper and help reduce waste.
leftovers n. 剩菜；剩餘食物
The kitchen throws away kilograms of leftovers every day.
tackle v. 處理；著手解決
The university launched a new program to tackle food waste.
inform v. 通知；告知
Please inform the staff if you have a food allergy.

閱讀 Reading

1. To reduce waste, the cafeteria now gives a discount to students who bring their own ______ containers.

  1. disposable
  2. reliable
  3. reusable
  4. valuable
看答案與詳解

正解 C　為了減少垃圾而給自備容器的人折扣，應是「可重複使用的」reusable。disposable（拋棄式）意思相反；reliable、valuable 與減廢無關。

2. Students with food allergies should ______ the staff before ordering, as some dishes contain peanuts.

  1. insist
  2. invite
  3. involve
  4. inform
看答案與詳解

正解 D　inform sb. 表示「告知某人」，有過敏者點餐前應先告知店員。insist 須接 on；invite（邀請）、involve（牽涉）語意不合。

From: General Affairs Office

To: All Students and Staff

Subject: New water refill stations and cup-sharing program

Dear Students and Staff,

As part of our plan to become a plastic-free campus by 2028, the General Affairs Office will install fifteen new water refill stations this month, with at least one in every classroom building and dormitory. A map showing all of the locations is available on the campus app.

In addition, a cup-sharing program will begin on November 16 at all cafés on campus. Customers who do not have their own cup can borrow a stainless-steel cup by scanning their student or staff ID. Borrowed cups must be returned to any café or drop box within 48 hours. Users who fail to return a cup on time will be charged 150 dollars, which will be added to their student or staff account.

Single-use plastic cups will still be available for five dollars each until the end of the semester.

Best regards, General Affairs Office

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce higher drink prices at campus cafés
  2. To introduce new ways to cut plastic use on campus
  3. To ask for volunteers to clean the water stations
  4. To explain how to apply for a staff ID
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中介紹新增飲水機與共享杯計畫，都是為了邁向無塑校園，選 B。塑膠杯收五元只是細節，不是漲價主旨；C、D 未提及。

4. The word "charged" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. filled with energy
  2. accused of a crime
  3. made to pay
  4. rushed toward
看答案與詳解

正解 C　後文說 150 元會加到帳戶，可知 charged 指「被收費」，選 C。charge 也有充電、指控、衝鋒等意思，但不符此處上下文。

5. What will happen to a student who keeps a borrowed cup for three days?

  1. They will no longer be able to use the refill stations.
  2. They will have to buy a plastic cup.
  3. Their student ID will be suspended.
  4. They will have 150 dollars added to their account.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　借杯須在 48 小時內歸還，三天已逾期，會被收 150 元並記入帳戶，選 D。其餘處分文中都沒有提到。

Leftovers on the Menu: Campus Tackles Food Waste

Every weekday at around two o'clock, the kitchen staff at Ming-De Hall begin a task few students ever see: scraping uneaten rice, vegetables, and meat into large plastic bins. According to the General Affairs Office, the university's five dining halls throw away an average of 320 kilograms of food each school day, enough to fill about 1,000 lunch boxes.

To tackle the problem, the university launched a program called Clean Plate this semester. Each dining hall now offers half portions at a lower price, and students who finish their meals can scan a QR code at the tray-return counter to earn points. Every ten points can be exchanged for a free drink or a discount at the campus bookstore. In addition, unsold bread and side dishes are packed at closing time and sold through an app at sixty percent off.

The early results are promising. In the first six weeks, daily food waste fell by about 25 percent, and more than 3,000 students downloaded the app. "I used to throw away half my rice because the portions were huge," said Huang Pei-shan, a sophomore in nursing. "Now I order a half portion and save twenty dollars a day."

Still, some problems remain. Cafeteria owners say the half portions bring in less money, and a few have asked the university to lower their rent in return. Some students also complain that the discounted food is usually sold out within minutes of being posted, which makes it hard for anyone with late classes to get any.

The General Affairs Office says it will meet with cafeteria owners in December and may add more pickup times for discounted food next semester.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A university program to reduce food waste and how well it is working
  2. A new nursing course about healthy eating
  3. Why cafeteria rents on campus have increased
  4. How to download a campus shopping app
看答案與詳解

正解 A　全文介紹 Clean Plate 減少剩食計畫的做法、成效與問題，選 A。護理系學生只是受訪者；店家「要求」降租金，並非租金上漲；App 只是細節。

7. How can students earn points in the Clean Plate program?

  1. By buying discounted food through the app
  2. By bringing their own lunch boxes
  3. By finishing their meals and scanning a code
  4. By returning their trays before two o'clock
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段：students who finish their meals can scan a QR code ... to earn points，選 C。App 是買折扣食物用的；B 未提及；兩點是廚房清剩食的時間。

8. What can be inferred about students who have late classes?

  1. They receive extra points for finishing meals.
  2. They prefer to order half portions.
  3. They usually eat off campus.
  4. They often have trouble buying the discounted food.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第四段說折扣食物幾分鐘內就賣完，有晚課的人很難買到，選 D。其他選項文中沒有依據。

9. Which TWO problems with the program are mentioned? (Choose TWO.)

  1. The QR codes often fail to scan.
  2. Cafeteria owners earn less from half portions.
  3. Students dislike the taste of the discounted bread.
  4. The app is only available in Chinese.
  5. Discounted food sells out very quickly.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　第四段提到店家因半份餐收入減少（B），以及折扣食物很快賣完（E）。QR code 故障、麵包口味、App 語言都未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I'd like a second helping, please.
  2. The dinner was a little too salty.
  3. Only on Saturdays, until seven thirty.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問週末晚餐有沒有開，C 回答「只有週六，到七點半」最切題。A 用 second 重複字干擾（再來一份），B 重複 dinner 但只是評論口味。

聽力原文

Is the second-floor cafeteria open for dinner on weekends?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Five dollars off any drink.
  2. I'll bring it back tomorrow.
  3. Yes, that cup is mine.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　How much 問折扣金額，A 直接回答「任何飲料折五元」。B 重複 bring，C 重複 cup 並用 Yes 回答 How much 問句，都答非所問。

聽力原文

How much is the discount if I bring my own cup?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The snack bar is next to the bookstore.
  2. It's part of the new plastic reduction policy.
  3. I don't really like straw hats.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Why 問原因，B 說明是新的減塑政策。A 只重複 snack bar 回答地點；C 用 straw（吸管／草帽）同字異義陷阱。

聽力原文

Why doesn't the snack bar give out plastic straws anymore?

4. What does the woman tell the man to do?

  1. Pay for his lunch again with cash
  2. Come back to the cafeteria in three days
  3. Bring his receipt to another office
  4. Apply for a new student card
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女士說 Take your receipt to the General Affairs Office，即把收據拿到總務處，選 C。三個工作天是退款入帳的時間，不是要他回餐廳；A、D 未提及。

聽力原文

Excuse me, I paid for my lunch with my student card, but it looks like I was charged twice. Let me check. Yes, I see two payments at twelve fifteen. I'm sorry, but I can't give refunds here. So what should I do? Take your receipt to the General Affairs Office on the first floor. They'll return the money to your card within three working days.

5. Where will the food trucks be during the renovation?

  1. In front of the student union
  2. Near the library
  3. Inside the food court
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女士說 food trucks will park near the library，選 B。student union 是消息來源，不是地點；美食街正在整修所以 C 不可能。

聽力原文

Did you hear the food court is closing for renovation next month? Yeah, for all of December. I have no idea where I'll eat between classes. The student union said food trucks will park near the library at lunchtime. That's a relief. I hope they accept mobile payments.

6. What happens when students return a borrowed box?

  1. They get two dollars off their next meal.
  2. They receive a free drink.
  3. They must pay a cleaning fee.
  4. They get their deposit back.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　廣播說 The deposit will be returned when the box is brought back，即歸還時退還押金，選 D。折兩元是「自備容器」的優惠，A 混淆對象；B、C 未提及。

聽力原文

Attention, students. Starting next Monday, the cafeteria in the Student Activity Center will no longer provide disposable lunch boxes. Students who want to take food away may bring their own containers, or borrow a reusable box for a deposit of fifty dollars. The deposit will be returned when the box is brought back to any campus restaurant. Students who bring their own containers will also get two dollars off every meal.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A cafeteria chef
  2. The manager of the noodle shop
  3. A student representative on the dining committee
  4. A professor of nutrition
看答案與詳解

正解 C　開頭自我介紹 the student union's representative on the campus dining committee，選 C。麵店只是試辦地點，講者不是店經理或廚師。

8. What happened during the test at the noodle shop?

  1. Waiting times were cut by about half.
  2. Food waste fell by eighty kilograms.
  3. More students paid with cash.
  4. Lunch prices went down.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者說 waiting times dropped by about half，選 A。八十公斤是每天丟掉的食物量，不是減少的量；C、D 未提及。

9. Which TWO changes does the speaker mention for next semester? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Ordering ahead through the campus app
  2. A new restaurant opening near the library
  3. Free drinks for students who bring cups
  4. A smaller, cheaper meal size
  5. Longer opening hours at lunch
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　講者提到可用校園 App 提前一小時點餐付款（A），以及各餐廳提供較小、較便宜的份量（D）。新餐廳、免費飲料、延長營業時間都沒提到。

聽力原文

Good evening, everyone. I'm Jason Wu, the student union's representative on the campus dining committee. Thank you for coming to this open meeting. Last month, we asked more than nine hundred students about campus food, and two complaints came up again and again: long lines at lunchtime, and portions that are too large to finish. Let me start with the lines. Beginning next semester, you'll be able to order and pay through the campus app up to one hour in advance, and then pick up your meal at a separate counter. We tested this at the noodle shop in October, and waiting times dropped by about half. As for portions, every restaurant will offer a smaller size at a lower price, and rice refills will be free if you're still hungry. The kitchen staff told us they throw away almost eighty kilograms of food on a normal school day, so we hope this will cut waste. Finally, the committee is still collecting ideas, so please fill in the comment cards on your table before you leave.
