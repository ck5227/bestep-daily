BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-09
期中教學意見與課程回饋

今日單字

anonymous adj. 匿名的
The course survey is anonymous, so you can be honest.
constructive adj. 有建設性的
Please give constructive comments instead of just complaining.
aggregate adj. 合計的；彙整的
Teachers only see the aggregate results of the survey.
adopt v. 採納；採用
The professor decided to adopt two of our suggestions.
response rate n. 回覆率；填答率
The response rate rose to over 80 percent this semester.
pilot v. 試辦；試行
The center is piloting a new feedback program in 30 courses.
adjust v. 調整
She adjusted her grading method after reading our feedback.
extend v. 延長；延期
The deadline for the final project was extended by one week.

閱讀 Reading

1. Instructors are encouraged to ______ student feedback carefully and adjust their teaching where possible.

  1. remind
  2. require
  3. consider
  4. reflect
看答案與詳解

正解 C　consider feedback 指「認真考慮回饋」，選 C。reflect 須接 on 才能表達「反思」；remind、require 語意不合。

2. The survey results will be shared with the department in ______ form, so no individual student can be identified.

  1. aggregate
  2. private
  3. individual
  4. personal
看答案與詳解

正解 A　後句說無法辨識個別學生，故結果以「彙整、合計」(aggregate) 形式呈現，選 A。individual、personal 與句意相反；private 不合邏輯。

From: Office of Academic Affairs

To: All Undergraduate Students

Subject: Mid-semester teaching feedback survey now open

Dear students,

The mid-semester teaching feedback survey for the fall semester is now open and will remain available until 11:59 p.m. on Friday, November 13. The survey takes about five minutes per course and includes eight rating questions and two open-ended questions.

Please keep your comments specific and constructive. For example, instead of writing "This class is boring," try "More in-class discussion would help me stay focused." Comments that contain personal attacks or offensive language will be removed before the results are sent to instructors.

Instructors will receive a summary of the results next Wednesday, and many of them will discuss the changes they plan to make during class. If you have a concern that you would prefer not to share through the survey, you may contact your department's student representative or email this office directly.

Sincerely, Office of Academic Affairs

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce a new grading system
  2. To explain how final grades are calculated
  3. To invite students to elect a representative
  4. To remind students about a survey and how to give useful comments
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信件通知期中教學回饋問卷開放、截止時間，並說明如何寫出具體有建設性的意見，選 D。其他選項內容信中未提及或僅附帶一提。

4. The word "constructive" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. helpful
  2. brief
  3. formal
  4. positive
看答案與詳解

正解 A　constructive 指「有建設性的、有幫助的」，從例句（建議增加課堂討論）可知，選 A。positive 是常見陷阱，具建設性的意見不一定是正面讚美。

5. What will happen to comments that include offensive language?

  1. They will be forwarded to the student representative.
  2. They will be shown to instructors without names.
  3. They will be deleted before results are shared.
  4. They will be reported to the department head.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中說含人身攻擊或冒犯性用語的意見 will be removed before the results are sent to instructors，選 C。A 的學生代表是另一種反映管道，與此無關。

Do Course Surveys Really Change Anything?

Every semester, students at Harbor View University receive the same email asking them to complete their course evaluations. Yet according to a recent poll by the Student Association, only 42 percent of students believe their feedback actually leads to change. The rest say they fill out the surveys mainly because it lets them see their grades early, or they skip them entirely.

Some professors argue that students simply do not notice the changes. Dr. Grace Liao, who teaches introductory economics, says she adjusted her course twice last year based on survey comments. "I added weekly review videos and changed the way I grade group projects," she explained. "But I never told students these changes came from their feedback, so many of them assumed nothing happened."

Communication seems to be the missing link. This semester, the Center for Teaching and Learning is piloting a program called "You Said, We Did." Under the program, 30 instructors will spend five minutes of class time after the midterm survey explaining what they learned and which suggestions they will or will not adopt. Early results look promising: in pilot courses, survey response rates rose from about 55 percent to over 80 percent.

Not everyone is convinced, however. Some students worry that comments are not truly anonymous in small classes with fewer than ten people, where an instructor might recognize a student's writing style. In response, the center plans to hold back written comments in those courses until final grades have been submitted. "Students should feel safe being honest," said center director Mark Huang. "Otherwise, the whole system loses its value."

6. What does the Student Association poll show?

  1. 42 percent of students complete the surveys.
  2. Students want more questions on the surveys.
  3. Fewer than half of students believe their feedback leads to change.
  4. Most professors ignore student comments.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第一段說只有 42% 的學生相信回饋真的帶來改變，即不到一半，選 C。A 誤把 42% 當成填答率；D 文章未如此說。

7. According to Dr. Liao, why did students think nothing had changed?

  1. She only changed her other courses.
  2. She did not tell them the changes came from their feedback.
  3. The changes were too small to notice.
  4. Students did not read the course syllabus.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Liao 說 I never told students these changes came from their feedback，選 B。C 看似合理但她並未說改變太小；A、D 未提及。

8. What will happen in classes with fewer than ten students?

  1. The survey will be canceled.
  2. Students must sign their names.
  3. Comments will be sent only to the director.
  4. Written comments will be held until grades are submitted.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第四段說中心會 hold back written comments ... until final grades have been submitted，選 D。其餘選項文章皆未提及。

9. Which TWO statements about the "You Said, We Did" program are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. It is run by the Student Association.
  2. It involves 30 instructors.
  3. It replaces the end-of-semester survey.
  4. Instructors use class time to respond to feedback.
  5. It has lowered survey response rates.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　第三段說有 30 位教師參與（B），並會在課堂花五分鐘說明採納哪些建議（D）。計畫由教學發展中心主辦（A 錯）；填答率由 55% 升至 80% 以上（E 錯）；C 未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The course is full this semester.
  2. Yes, it only took about five minutes.
  3. I'll survey the area tomorrow.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否已填期中課程問卷，B「填了，只花約五分鐘」最合理。A 重複 course 但講課程額滿；C 把 survey 當動詞「勘查」混淆。

聽力原文

Have you filled out the midterm course survey yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. No names are collected, so feel free to be honest.
  2. I'll put it on your desk.
  3. Her name is Professor Chen.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問回饋表是否匿名，A 說不收集姓名、可放心誠實作答。B 重複 put 但答非所問；C 重複 name 但講教授名字。

聽力原文

Is the feedback form anonymous, or do we have to put our names on it?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Because the class was too slow.
  2. I posted the letter this morning.
  3. Good idea. Let's mention it in the comment box.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Why don't we...? 是提出建議，C 贊同並說要寫在意見欄最合理。A 誤把建議當成問原因；B 用 post（寄信）混淆 post the slides（上傳投影片）。

聽力原文

Why don't we ask the professor to post the slides before class?

4. What most likely caused the man's problem?

  1. He missed the deadline.
  2. He used an outdated link.
  3. He forgot his password.
  4. The survey closed early.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男子用了上學期 email 的連結登入舊系統，女子建議改用首頁的新連結，故選 B。截止日其實已延到週日，A、D 錯；C 未提及。

聽力原文

I tried to submit my course evaluation, but the system says the deadline has passed. Really? The deadline was extended to this Sunday. Did you log in through the old portal? Oh, I might have. I clicked the link from last semester's email. That's probably it. Use the new link on the university homepage instead.

5. What change will the professor make?

  1. Reduce the number of quiz questions
  2. Lower the weight of the quizzes
  3. Cancel the weekly quizzes
  4. Give the quizzes at the end of class
看答案與詳解

正解 A　教授說 I'll cut them down to ten questions，減少題數，選 A。他也說仍占百分之二十，故 B 錯；C、D 未提及。

聽力原文

Professor Wang, a lot of us wrote in the feedback that the weekly quizzes are too long. Yes, I read that. Starting next week, I'll cut them down to ten questions, but they'll still count for twenty percent of your grade. That's a relief. Thanks for listening to us.

6. What do students receive if they complete all of their surveys?

  1. Extra credit in each course
  2. A gift card for the campus store
  3. Earlier access to their final grades
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播說完成所有課程問卷者可提早三天查看期末成績，選 C。A 加分與 B 禮券均未提及。

聽力原文

Attention, all students. The Office of Academic Affairs reminds you that the mid-semester teaching feedback survey closes this Friday at midnight. Students who complete the survey for every course they are taking will be able to view their final grades three days earlier in January. You can find the survey on the student portal or in the campus app. All responses are anonymous, and instructors will only see summarized results.

7. What did many students complain about?

  1. The lecture room is too small.
  2. The final exam is too difficult.
  3. There are not enough examples.
  4. The professor talks too fast during examples.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　教授說 I speak too fast when I go through the statistics examples，選 D。C 重複 examples 但意思不同；A、B 未提及。

8. Why won't the professor remove the final project?

  1. The department requires it.
  2. The teaching assistant disagrees.
  3. It is too late in the semester.
  4. Most students voted to keep it.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　教授說 the project is required by the department，選 A。其他選項皆未提及；他只同意延後一週截止。

9. Which TWO changes will the professor make? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Shorten the final project
  2. Upload lecture recordings sooner
  3. Teach the class only in Chinese
  4. Move the review session to the weekend
  5. Let students practice problems in pairs
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　教授會在 24 小時內上傳錄影（B），並在每個例題後讓學生兩人一組練習（E）。期末專題只延期不縮短（A 錯）；複習課在週四晚上且中英並用（C、D 錯）。

聽力原文

Good morning, everyone. Before we start today's lecture, I want to share what I learned from your mid-semester feedback. First of all, thank you. Almost ninety percent of you responded, which is much higher than last year. Many of you said the lectures are useful, but that I speak too fast when I go through the statistics examples. So here's what I'm going to change. From now on, I'll pause after each example and give you two minutes to work through a similar problem with a partner. I'll also upload the lecture recordings within twenty-four hours, instead of waiting until the weekend. A few of you asked for more language support, since this is an English-taught course, so our teaching assistant, Amy, will hold an extra review session in both Chinese and English every Thursday evening. There was one request I can't agree to. Several people asked me to remove the final project. Unfortunately, the project is required by the department, but I will extend the deadline by one week. Okay, let's open the slides.
