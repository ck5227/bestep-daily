今日單字

notify v. 通知
The system will notify you by email if your grade drops below 60.
dispute v. 對…提出異議；爭論
If you want to dispute a score, explain which question was graded unfairly.
withdrawal n. 退選；撤回
The withdrawal deadline for this semester was last Friday.
regrade n./v. 重新評分
Regrade requests must be submitted through the LMS by November 7.
struggle v. 苦於應付；掙扎
Many first-year students struggle with time management.
in advance phr. 事先；提前
Please book tutoring sessions at least 24 hours in advance.
consequence n. 後果
The notice lists the consequences of failing a required course.
demanding adj. 要求高的；費力的
Organic chemistry is one of the most demanding courses in the department.

閱讀 Reading

1. If your midterm grade falls below 60, the system will automatically ______ you and your advisor by email.

  1. remove
  2. require
  3. attend
  4. notify
看答案與詳解

正解 D　notify sb. by email 表示「以電子郵件通知某人」，選 D。remove 移除、require 要求、attend 出席，都不能搭配 by email 表達通知。

2. Students who fall behind are strongly encouraged to seek help ______ rather than wait until the end of the semester.

  1. lately
  2. early
  3. rarely
  4. barely
看答案與詳解

正解 B　rather than wait until the end 對比「及早」求助，選 early。lately 最近、rarely 很少、barely 幾乎不，語意皆不合。

From: Kevin Huang (TA, Introduction to Economics)

To: ECON 101 Section B students

Subject: Extra office hours and midterm regrade requests

Dear all,

Now that midterm scores have been posted on the LMS, several of you have asked to go over your exams with me. To meet the demand, I will hold extra office hours next Monday and Wednesday from 3:00 to 5:00 p.m. in Room 412 of the Management Building, in addition to my regular Friday hours.

Please note that I cannot change any scores during office hours. If you believe a question was graded incorrectly, you must submit a regrade request through the LMS by November 7. Your request should state clearly which question you are disputing and why. Requests submitted after this date will not be considered.

Finally, if you scored below 60, Professor Wu would like to meet with you briefly. Please sign up for a ten-minute slot using the link on the course page.

Best, Kevin

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce changes to the final exam
  2. To apologize for errors in grading
  3. To cancel the regular Friday office hours
  4. To explain how students can get help and question their grades
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中說明加開輔導時間、如何申請重新評分，以及低分者與教授面談，選 D。沒有提期末考或道歉；週五時段是照常（in addition to），C 錯。

4. The word "disputing" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. challenging
  2. reviewing
  3. solving
  4. repeating
看答案與詳解

正解 A　dispute 指對評分「提出異議、質疑」，與 challenging 最接近。reviewing 只是複習檢視，沒有質疑之意；solving、repeating 不合。

5. What should a student who scored 52 on the midterm do?

  1. Visit Kevin on Friday to have the score raised
  2. Email the department office before November 7
  3. Sign up for a short meeting with Professor Wu
  4. Attend every extra office hour session
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中說 scored below 60 的同學要預約與 Wu 教授十分鐘面談，選 C。助教在輔導時間不能改分數，A 錯；B、D 未要求。

Early Warning System Draws Mixed Reviews

Last week, more than 1,200 undergraduates received midterm warning notices, the highest number in five years. The notices are sent automatically when a student's midterm grade falls below 60 or when a student misses more than one-third of class meetings.

The university introduced the early warning system in 2021 to reach struggling students before it is too late. According to the Office of Academic Affairs, students who met with an advisor after receiving a warning were twice as likely to pass the course as those who did not. "The notice is not a punishment," said Vice Dean Lisa Huang. "It is an invitation to talk."

Not everyone sees it that way. A survey by the Student Association found that 43 percent of students who received a notice felt embarrassed, and many admitted they never opened the email. "The message sounds like a court order," said Jason Lee, a sophomore in mechanical engineering. "It lists all the consequences but doesn't tell you where to get help until the very last line."

Some faculty members also question the 60-point rule. Professor Mark Tsai, who teaches organic chemistry, pointed out that in especially demanding courses, a midterm score of 55 may still be above the class average. "A single number doesn't tell the whole story," he said.

In response, Academic Affairs has announced that the notice will be rewritten in a friendlier tone, with links to tutoring and counseling services placed at the top. Starting next semester, instructors will also be able to add a personal comment before a notice is sent.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A new tutoring program for engineering students
  2. Changes to the university's grading scale
  3. Reactions to the university's midterm warning system
  4. Why more students are failing organic chemistry
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文介紹期中預警制度，並呈現行政、學生、教師的不同看法，選 C。A、D 只是文中細節或未提及；評分標準並未改變，B 錯。

7. According to the article, what happens when a student misses more than one-third of class meetings?

  1. The student must meet with the vice dean.
  2. The student automatically receives a warning notice.
  3. The student automatically fails the course.
  4. The student loses access to tutoring services.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　第一段說缺課超過三分之一時 notices are sent automatically，選 B。文中沒有說會直接被當、須見副院長或失去輔導資格。

8. Why does Professor Tsai criticize the 60-point rule?

  1. He thinks students should be warned even earlier.
  2. He would rather send the notices himself.
  3. He believes advisors are too busy to help.
  4. He feels it ignores how difficult some courses are.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　Tsai 教授指出在很難的課，55 分可能仍高於班平均，即單一分數忽略課程難度，選 D。其他選項文中未提。

9. Which TWO changes has the Office of Academic Affairs announced? (Choose TWO.)

  1. The passing score will be lowered to 55.
  2. Students will be allowed to opt out of notices.
  3. The notice will be written in a friendlier tone.
  4. Advisors will phone every student who gets a notice.
  5. Links to support services will appear at the top.
看答案與詳解

正解 C、E　最後一段提到通知改用較友善語氣（C），並把輔導與諮商連結放在最上方（E）。55 分只是教授舉例；選擇退出通知、導師逐一致電都沒有提到。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The warning signs were posted near the lab.
  2. I'll check out that book from the library tomorrow.
  3. Yes, and luckily nothing showed up in my portal.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問是否查過有沒有收到期中預警，C 回答查過且入口網站沒有通知，最合理。A 重複 warning 但講警告標誌；B 用 check out（借書）混淆 checked。

聽力原文

Have you checked whether you got a midterm warning notice yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Every Tuesday from two to four.
  2. In room three oh five of the Science Building.
  3. She held the door open for me.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Where 問地點，B 回答教室位置。A 回答時間（when）；C 重複 held 但意思無關。

聽力原文

Where are Professor Lin's TA office hours held this semester?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Sure, I could use some help with derivatives.
  2. Because I tutored him last week.
  3. The calculator is in my backpack.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Why don't we... 是提議，A 接受並說需要微分的協助。B 把 why 誤當成問原因；C 用 calculator 與 calculus 音近混淆。

聽力原文

Why don't we join the peer tutoring session for calculus tonight?

4. Why can't the woman withdraw from the course?

  1. She needs her advisor's signature first.
  2. The deadline has already passed.
  3. The course is required for her major.
  4. She has already dropped another course.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生說 the withdrawal deadline was last Friday，退選期限已過，選 B。導師簽名、必修、已退過別的課都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Hi, I got an email saying I'm at risk of failing Statistics. What should I do? First, book a meeting with your academic advisor. The system opens new appointment slots every Monday. Can I just drop the course instead? I'm afraid the withdrawal deadline was last Friday, so that's no longer an option. But the learning center offers free tutoring.

5. What does the woman ask the man to do?

  1. Bring a printed copy of his essay
  2. Come back later today as a walk-in
  3. Ask his professor for a referral
  4. Upload his essay a day before the session
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女生說 upload it at least one day in advance，選 D。A 是男生自己問的，女生用 Actually 否定；今天現場名額已滿，B 錯；C 未提及。

聽力原文

Excuse me, is this where I sign up for one-on-one sessions at the writing center? It is, but walk-in spots are full today. You can reserve a thirty-minute session online for next week. OK. Should I bring a printed copy of my essay? Actually, please upload it at least one day in advance so the tutor can read it first.

6. What may happen to students who skip the workshop?

  1. They may be allowed to take fewer courses next semester.
  2. They will lose their scholarships.
  3. They will receive another warning notice.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播最後說 may have their course load limited next semester，即下學期修課量可能受限，選 A。獎學金與再收預警都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Attention, first-year students. Midterm grade reports are now available on the student portal. If you have received two or more warning notices, you are required to attend an academic success workshop. Workshops will be held this Wednesday and Thursday at six thirty in the evening in the main auditorium. Please register by noon on Tuesday. Students who do not attend may have their course load limited next semester.

7. Who is the talk mainly intended for?

  1. Students who want to become peer tutors
  2. Teaching assistants planning their office hours
  3. Students looking for help after their midterms
  4. Professors who sent out warning notices
看答案與詳解

正解 C　開頭說期中成績公布後很多人問接下來怎麼辦，接著介紹各項輔導服務，對象是需要協助的學生，選 C。講者並非在招募小老師，也不是對助教或教授說話。

8. What must students do to use peer tutoring?

  1. Get a recommendation from their TA
  2. Book a session at least a day ahead
  3. Have earned an A in the course
  4. Pay a small registration fee
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說 book them ... at least twenty-four hours in advance，選 B。拿 A 的是小老師本人而非學生；課程免費，D 錯；A 未提及。

9. Which TWO statements are true according to the speaker? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Peer tutors are graduate students.
  2. Study skills workshops are held every two weeks.
  3. Next week's workshop is about note-taking.
  4. Students can visit TAs without an appointment.
  5. The center's services end in November.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　every other Thursday 即每兩週一次（B），TA 輔導時間 you don't need an appointment（D）。小老師是大四生（senior）非研究生；下週主題是時間管理；十一月前開始使用效果較好，不是服務在十一月結束。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Doctor Chen from the Learning Support Center. Since midterm results came out last week, many of you have asked what to do next, so let me explain the services we offer. First, our peer tutors are senior students who earned an A in the courses they tutor. Sessions are free, but you need to book them on the e-learning platform at least twenty-four hours in advance. Second, we run study skills workshops every other Thursday. Next week's topic is time management, which is the area where first-year students struggle the most. Third, if you've received a midterm warning, your course's teaching assistant is a great resource. Most TAs hold office hours twice a week, and you don't need an appointment. Just show up with specific questions. One last tip: don't wait until the final exam is only two weeks away. Students who start using our services before November usually see much better results. Thank you, and good luck.
