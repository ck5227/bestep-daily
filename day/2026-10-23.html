今日單字

transcript n. 成績單
The registrar's office can issue an English transcript in three working days.
recommendation letter n. 推薦信
Ask your professor for a recommendation letter at least two weeks before the deadline.
proficiency n. 熟練程度；精通
Most partner schools require proof of English proficiency.
accommodate v. 配合；通融；容納
We cannot accommodate requests received after November 12.
transfer v. （學分）抵免；轉移
Check whether your overseas credits will transfer before you register.
hybrid adj. 混合式的
The hybrid program combines online classes with a three-week campus visit.
withdraw v. 退出；撤回
Applicants who miss the interview will be considered to have withdrawn.
fraction n. 一小部分
A summer course costs only a fraction of a full semester abroad.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who wish to study abroad must ______ a copy of their passport along with the application form.

  1. admit
  2. permit
  3. submit
  4. commit
看答案與詳解

正解 C　「繳交」護照影本應用 submit。admit 承認、permit 允許、commit 犯下／承諾，皆不合語意。

2. Because the partner university has a limited number of dorm rooms, exchange students are ______ to apply for housing as early as possible.

  1. advised
  2. refused
  3. delayed
  4. prevented
看答案與詳解

正解 A　宿舍有限，故學生「被建議」及早申請，be advised to V 正確。refused 語意相反，delayed 不接 to V，prevented 應接 from V-ing。

From: Office of International Affairs <oia@univ.edu.tw>

To: Spring 2027 Exchange Applicants

Subject: Interview Schedule and Required Documents

Dear Applicants,

Thank you for applying to the Spring 2027 exchange program. All applicants who passed the document review will be invited to a fifteen-minute interview, which will take place from November 16 to 18. Your individual time slot will be posted on the OIA website by November 10.

The interview will be conducted mainly in English, so please be ready to explain why you chose your top three partner schools and how the experience fits your academic plans. If you have a class conflict, you may request a different time slot by emailing us no later than November 12. Requests received after this date cannot be accommodated.

Please also bring your original transcript and a printed copy of your study plan. Applicants who miss the interview without notice will be considered to have withdrawn from the program.

Best regards, Office of International Affairs

3. What is the main purpose of this email?

  1. To announce the final exchange results
  2. To give details about the upcoming interviews
  3. To introduce new partner schools
  4. To remind students to pay tuition

正解 B　全文說明面試日期、時段公告、改期方式與須攜帶文件，故選 B。結果尚未公布，也未提到新姊妹校或繳費。

4. The word "accommodated" in the third paragraph is closest in meaning to

  1. housed
  2. rejected
  3. arranged for
  4. reported
看答案與詳解

正解 C　此處指逾期的改期申請「無法配合安排」，accommodate 意近 arrange for。housed 是「提供住宿」的字面義，與上下文不符。

5. What should an applicant with a class conflict do?

  1. Email the office by November 12
  2. Send a video instead of attending
  3. Check the website after November 18
  4. Ask the course professor for permission to leave
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信中說有課程衝堂者須在 11 月 12 日前寄信申請改時段，故選 A。其餘做法信中均未提及。

Going Abroad for Less: Short-Term Programs Gain Popularity

For years, a full semester overseas was the standard exchange experience for students at our university. This year, however, the Office of International Affairs (OIA) reports that applications for short-term programs, such as summer schools, four-week language courses, and winter field trips, have risen by nearly forty percent, while applications for semester-long exchanges have stayed about the same.

OIA director Dr. Chen Mei-hua believes cost is the main reason. "A semester in Europe can cost a student more than three hundred thousand NT dollars once you add rent, flights, and daily expenses," she explained. "A four-week program costs a fraction of that, and students don't have to delay graduation." Many students also worry that courses taken abroad will not transfer, especially in departments with strict required courses, such as engineering and nursing.

Not everyone sees the trend as entirely positive. Professor David Morris of the Department of Foreign Languages argues that short programs rarely give students enough time to move beyond the "tourist stage." "In the first month, everything is exciting and new. The real learning, like solving problems on your own and building friendships with local students, usually happens later," he said.

To respond to these concerns, the OIA plans to launch a hybrid option next fall. Students will take an online course taught by a partner university during the semester and then spend three weeks on that campus in the summer. The office has also promised to publish a list of pre-approved overseas courses so that students can check whether their credits will transfer before they apply.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A new scholarship for engineering students
  2. The growing interest in short-term study abroad programs
  3. Problems between the university and its partner schools
  4. How to apply for a semester-long exchange
看答案與詳解

正解 B　首段即點出短期海外課程申請人數大增，全文圍繞此趨勢的原因與回應，故選 B。其餘選項只是細節或未提及。

7. Why does Professor Morris have doubts about short programs?

  1. They cost more than students expect.
  2. Credits from them rarely transfer.
  3. Their online courses are of lower quality.
  4. Students may leave before the deeper learning begins.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　Morris 認為真正的學習通常在第一個月之後才發生，短期課程難以超越「觀光階段」，故選 D。費用與學分是陳主任與學生的考量，不是他的疑慮。

8. According to the article, what has happened to applications for semester-long exchanges?

  1. They have increased by forty percent.
  2. They have stayed about the same.
  3. They have dropped sharply.
  4. They are now limited to nursing students.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　首段說學期交換的申請「維持差不多」，故選 B。增加百分之四十的是短期課程，A 混淆對象。

9. What does the OIA plan to do? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Combine online study with a short campus visit
  2. Cancel semester-long exchanges
  3. Publish a list of pre-approved courses
  4. Lower the cost of summer schools
  5. Require nursing students to study abroad
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　末段提到將推出「線上課程加三週實地研修」的混合方案，並公布預先核可的海外課程清單，故選 A、C。文中未提取消學期交換、降價或強制出國。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The exchange rate is pretty bad right now.
  2. Not yet. I'm still waiting for my recommendation letter.
  3. Yes, I turned on the heater this morning.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否已交出交換申請，B 說還在等推薦信最合理。A 用 exchange 重複字誤導成匯率，C 用 turned 混淆，皆答非所問。

聽力原文

Have you turned in your exchange application yet?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The registrar's office can print one for you.
  2. About two weeks ago.
  3. I translated the script for our drama club.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問「在哪裡」取得英文成績單，A 指出註冊組最切題。B 回答時間，C 用 script 與 transcript 音近字誤導。

聽力原文

Where can I get an English copy of my transcript?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Berlin is the capital of Germany.
  2. I'd rather take the train there.
  3. Actually, it was moved to the end of November.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　以否定問句確認截止日，C 更正為延到十一月底最合理。A 只重複 Germany，B 談交通方式，皆不相關。

聽力原文

Isn't the deadline for the Germany program next Friday?

4. What will the man probably do?

  1. Retake some courses to raise his GPA
  2. Choose a less competitive school as his first choice
  3. Give up on studying in Japan
  4. Ask a professor for a recommendation letter
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生最後說要把較小的大學列為第一志願，故選 B。他的 GPA 已達門檻，並未提到重修、放棄或找推薦信。

Hi, I'm thinking about applying to the exchange program in Japan, but my GPA is only three point one. The minimum for most partner schools is three point oh, so you qualify. But very competitive schools usually take students above three point five. I see. Then maybe I should put a smaller university as my first choice.

  4. Skip the English requirement this time

7. What must students do so that their overseas courses will count?

  1. Pay tuition directly to the host school
  2. Pass an English test before leaving
  3. Email the overseas professors in advance
  4. Have a course mapping form signed by the department chair
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者說出國前須填課程對應表並由系主任簽名，否則學分可能不被承認，故選 D。學費仍繳給本校，A 錯；B、C 未提及。

8. According to the speaker, what do many students find most difficult?

  1. The difficulty of the classes
  2. Finding affordable housing
  3. Feeling lonely when they first arrive
  4. Applying for a student visa
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者明言最難的不是課程，而是第一個月的孤單感，故選 C。A 正好被否定，B、D 未被描述為最困難之處。

9. Which TWO costs are the students responsible for? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Tuition at the host university
  2. Housing
  3. A course mapping fee
  4. Flights
  5. Club membership fees
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　講者說學生須自付住宿、機票與保險，故選 B、D。學費繳給本校而非交換學校，課程對應表與社團費用並未提到要付費。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and thanks for coming to today's exchange orientation. I'm Ms. Lin from the Office of International Affairs. First, let's talk about credits. Before you leave, you must fill out a course mapping form, and your department chair needs to sign it. If you skip this step, the courses you take overseas may not count toward your graduation requirements here. Second, money. As exchange students, you'll keep paying tuition to our university, not to the host school. However, you are responsible for your own housing, flights, and health insurance. Many of you are eligible for the Ministry of Education's study abroad scholarship, and the application period opens on November first. Finally, a word of advice. Students often tell me the hardest part isn't the classes. It's feeling lonely during the first month. So join a club or a language exchange as soon as you arrive. Next week, three students who just came back from Sweden, Korea, and Canada will share their experiences, and I strongly encourage you to attend.
