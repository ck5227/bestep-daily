學術誠信與抄襲防範

今日單字

plagiarism n. 抄襲、剽竊
Copying text from a website without citation is considered plagiarism.
cite v. 引用；註明出處
Remember to cite every source you quote in your paper.
paraphrase v. 改寫、換句話說
Try to paraphrase the author's idea in your own words.
similarity report n. phr. 相似度比對報告
The similarity report showed that my essay was eight percent similar to other sources.
acknowledge v. 承認；註明（來源）
You must acknowledge anyone whose ideas you used.
consult v. 請教、諮詢
If you're unsure about the rules, consult your instructor first.
offense n. 違規、過錯
A second offense may lead to failing the course.
generate v. 產生
The system will generate a report after you upload your file.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students must ______ all the sources they use in their papers, including websites and interviews.

  1. abandon
  2. acknowledge
  3. accumulate
  4. admire
看答案與詳解

正解 B　「註明／承認所使用的所有來源」，acknowledge 最合適。abandon（放棄）、accumulate（累積）、admire（欣賞）語意不通。

2. If you are not sure whether your work counts as collaboration or cheating, it is best to ______ your instructor before the deadline.

  1. consume
  2. conclude
  3. confess
  4. consult
看答案與詳解

正解 D　不確定時應在截止前「請教」老師，consult 正確。consume（消耗）、conclude（結論）不合；confess（坦承）暗示已犯錯，與「不確定」語境不符。

From: Amy Hsu (TA, Introduction to Sociology)

To: SOC101 students

Subject: Midterm paper submission and similarity reports

Hi everyone, this is a reminder that your midterm paper is due on Friday, November 6, at 11:59 p.m. Please upload it to the course page on the learning platform as a Word or PDF file. Papers sent by email will not be accepted.

After you upload your file, the system will automatically generate a similarity report. Don't panic if your score is not zero. Quotations, references, and common phrases are often highlighted. Professor Huang will manually review any report above 25 percent to decide whether there is a real problem.

You may submit as many times as you like before the deadline, and only the final version will be graded. If you would like feedback on your citations, I will hold extra office hours on Wednesday afternoon in Room 412. Best, Amy

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce a change in the paper topic
  2. To invite students to a sociology lecture
  3. To report students who plagiarized
  4. To explain how the paper will be submitted and checked
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信件說明繳交期限、上傳方式與相似度報告的處理方式。信中未提題目更改、演講，也沒有指名任何抄襲學生。

4. The word "generate" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. produce
  2. delete
  3. request
  4. hide
看答案與詳解

正解 A　系統上傳後會自動「產生」相似度報告，generate = produce。delete（刪除）、hide（隱藏）意思相反；request（要求）不符語境。

5. What is true about the similarity report?

  1. Any score above zero means the paper will fail.
  2. Students can upload their paper only once.
  3. Reports with high scores will be checked by the professor.
  4. It must be emailed to the TA.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段說超過 25% 的報告教授會親自審閱。信中說分數不是零不必驚慌（A 錯）、可多次上傳（B 錯）、email 繳交不受理（D 錯）。

Survey: Students Unsure Where Help Ends and Cheating Begins

A recent survey by the Student Association has found that many students at the university are confused about the rules of academic integrity. Of the 1,200 undergraduates who responded, nearly 40 percent said they were not sure whether using an AI tool to rewrite a paragraph counts as plagiarism, and almost a third admitted they had never read the university's integrity guidelines.

The results did not surprise Professor Lisa Wu, who chairs the Academic Integrity Committee. "Most cases we see are not about students trying to cheat," she said. "They are about students who don't know how to paraphrase or cite properly, especially when they are writing in English for the first time." She noted that the number of reported cases rose last year, mainly in courses taught in English.

To address the problem, the committee will launch two programs next semester. First, all first-year students will be required to complete a two-hour online module on citation and paraphrasing before they can register for second-semester courses. Second, the Writing Center will train twenty senior students as "integrity mentors" who can answer questions in Mandarin or English during evening hours.

Not everyone is convinced. Some students worry that the new module will simply become another box to check. "A video and a quiz won't teach me how to write," said Jason Lee, a sophomore in engineering. "What I need is feedback on my actual assignments." Professor Wu responded that instructors will also be encouraged to include short citation exercises in their courses.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A student who was punished for cheating
  2. Student confusion over integrity rules and the university's response
  3. A new AI tool developed by the Writing Center
  4. Changes to second-semester course registration
看答案與詳解

正解 B　全文先報導調查顯示學生對學術誠信規則困惑，再說明校方因應措施與學生反應。文中沒有個別受罰案例；選課只是線上課程的附帶條件。

7. According to Professor Wu, why do most integrity cases happen?

  1. Students deliberately buy essays online.
  2. Instructors do not explain their grading.
  3. The checking software makes too many mistakes.
  4. Students lack the skills to paraphrase and cite properly.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　吳教授說多數案例是學生 don't know how to paraphrase or cite properly，而非蓄意作弊，故 A 錯；B、C 文中未提。

8. Which TWO things will the university do next semester? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Require first-year students to complete an online module
  2. Ban AI tools in all courses
  3. Hire new English-speaking professors
  4. Offer evening help from trained student mentors
  5. Cancel some courses taught in English
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　第三段列出兩項計畫：大一必修線上引用模組，以及寫作中心培訓學長姐擔任晚間「誠信導師」。禁用 AI、聘新教授、取消英語授課皆未提及。

9. What concern does Jason Lee express?

  1. The module will take too much time.
  2. The mentors may not speak English well.
  3. An online module may not improve his actual writing.
  4. Engineering students rarely write papers.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　他說影片和測驗教不會寫作，他需要的是對實際作業的回饋，表示擔心線上模組無助於寫作。他沒抱怨時間太長或導師英文程度。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, the report came back at about five percent.
  2. No, I don't usually check my bag.
  3. The essay contest ends next week.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問交報告前是否跑過相似度比對，A 回答報告結果約 5% 最合理。B 重複 check 但語意無關；C 重複 essay 卻答非所問。

聽力原文

Did you run your essay through the similarity checker before you submitted it?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I sighted him at the library yesterday.
  2. The interview went really well, thanks.
  3. Your writing center tutor can help with that.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問該找誰請教如何引用訪談，C 指出寫作中心助教最合理。A 用 sighted 與 cite 同音混淆；B 重複 interview 但答非所問。

聽力原文

Who should I ask about how to cite an interview in my paper?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Sure, the old reporter retired last year.
  2. Not without asking the professor for permission first.
  3. This class starts at ten.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問能否重複使用舊報告內容，B 說須先徵得教授同意，切中「自我抄襲」規則。A 用 reporter 與 report 音近混淆；C 重複 class 但答非所問。

聽力原文

Isn't it okay to reuse parts of my old report for this class?

4. What does the professor ask the student to do?

  1. Drop the course
  2. Revise the report and cite the sources
  3. Meet the dean on Thursday
  4. Find a different website to use
看答案與詳解

正解 B　教授說 rewrite those sections... add the sources, and resubmit，即修改並註明出處。Thursday 是繳交期限，不是與院長見面；並未要求退選或換網站。

聽力原文

Professor Lin, I got an email saying my report was flagged for plagiarism. I really didn't copy anything. The software found that several paragraphs match a website, but there are no quotation marks or citations. Oh no. I took notes from that site and forgot where they came from. Then rewrite those sections in your own words, add the sources, and resubmit it by Thursday.

5. Why does the man refuse the woman's request?

  1. He hasn't finished his report yet.
  2. He thinks the TA would be upset.
  3. He is worried their reports might look too similar.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男生提到上學期有兩人因報告太相似而得零分，表示擔心被認定抄襲。他沒說報告未完成；TA 是女生後來提到的替代方案，非拒絕理由。

聽力原文

Kevin, can I borrow your lab report? I just want to see how you organized the results. I'd rather not. Last semester, two students got zeros because their reports looked too similar. Fair enough. Maybe I'll go to the TA's office hours instead.

6. What must students do to attend the workshop?

  1. Pay a small registration fee

  3. Arrive before two o'clock on Tuesday
  4. Sign up through the school app in advance

Attention, students. The Writing Center will hold a free workshop called Citing Sources the Right Way this Wednesday from two to four in the afternoon, in Room three oh five of the Humanities Building. You will learn how to paraphrase, use quotation marks, and format references in A P A style. Seats are limited to forty, so please sign up through the school app by noon on Tuesday. Students who attend will receive a certificate for their learning portfolio.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To explain academic integrity rules and the consequences of breaking them
  2. To introduce a new academic writing course
  3. To announce the dates of midterm exams
  4. To encourage students to use A I software
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者開場即說要談 academic integrity，並說明抄襲範圍、AI 規定與違規後果。期中只是背景；AI 只在教師允許下可用，並非鼓勵使用。

8. According to the speaker, what usually happens after a first offense?

  1. The student fails the course.
  2. The student is suspended for a semester.
  3. The student gets a zero on the assignment and takes an online course.
  4. The student must rewrite all of their papers.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說 For a first offense, you will usually receive a zero... and must complete an online integrity course。課程被當是「第二次」違規的後果；未提到停學。

9. Which TWO of the following does the speaker describe as plagiarism? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Asking a TA for help during office hours
  2. Submitting the same paper to two courses without permission
  3. Checking grammar with an approved A I tool
  4. Using someone else's ideas without giving credit
  5. Visiting the Writing Center before submitting
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　講者明確列出 using someone else's ideas without giving credit 與 submitting the same paper to two different courses without permission。經允許用 AI 查文法、去寫作中心都是被允許或鼓勵的行為。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Doctor Chen from the Office of Academic Affairs, and today I want to talk about academic integrity, especially now that midterm papers are due. Many students think plagiarism only means copying a whole essay. In fact, it also includes using someone else's ideas without giving credit, buying assignments online, and submitting the same paper to two different courses without permission. This semester, the university has also updated its policy on A I tools. You may use them to brainstorm or check your grammar, but only if your instructor allows it, and you must state clearly how you used them. Copying text from a chatbot and presenting it as your own work is treated the same way as copying from a book. So what happens if a case is reported? For a first offense, you will usually receive a zero on the assignment and must complete an online integrity course. A second offense may lead to failing the course. If you are unsure about any rule, please visit the Writing Center before you submit, not after.
