學術研討會與海報發表

今日單字

symposium n. 研討會；學術座談會
Eleven students presented posters at the research symposium.
abstract n. （論文）摘要
Please keep your abstract under three hundred words.
submission n. 投稿；提交（的作品）
The committee received over two hundred submissions this year.
rehearsal n. 彩排；預演
Let's do a final rehearsal before the session starts.
notify v. 通知
Please notify the office if you cannot attend.
handout n. 講義；分發的資料
She printed handouts with a QR code to her full report.
supervise v. 指導；監督
Professor Huang has supervised student researchers for years.
eligible adj. 有資格的
Students with one semester of research are eligible for the grant.

閱讀 Reading

1. Because of the large number of submissions, the organizing committee will only ______ abstracts that follow the required format.

  1. remind
  2. reflect
  3. review
  4. resign
看答案與詳解

正解 C　投稿量大，委員會只「審查」符合格式的摘要，review 最合語意。remind 提醒、reflect 反映、resign 辭職皆不通。

2. Ms. Chen's presentation was so ______ that several professors asked for a copy of her slides afterward.

  1. impressed
  2. impression
  3. impressively
  4. impressive
看答案與詳解

正解 D　so + 形容詞 + that，修飾「事物」令人印象深刻用 impressive。impressed 用於人「感到」佩服；impression 是名詞、impressively 是副詞。

From: Office of Undergraduate Research

To: Symposium Presenters

Subject: Final Reminders for Friday's Symposium

Dear Presenters,

Thank you for being part of this year's Undergraduate Research Symposium on Friday, November 27. Poster setup will take place from 8:30 to 9:15 a.m. in the Student Activity Center. Each presenter will be assigned a numbered board; please check your board number on the symposium website. Pins and tape will be provided, but please do not use glue, as it damages the boards.

Presenters are expected to stand by their posters during their assigned session so that judges and visitors can ask questions. If you are unable to attend, please notify us by Wednesday so that we can reassign your board. Certificates of participation will be issued to all presenters who attend the full session.

Best regards, Office of Undergraduate Research

3. What are presenters asked NOT to do?

  1. Use pins to hang posters
  2. Use glue on the boards
  3. Check the website
  4. Stand next to their posters
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中寫 please do not use glue, as it damages the boards。圖釘會提供、網站要查、要站在海報旁，都是被要求做的事。

4. The word "notify" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. invite
  2. forgive
  3. inform
  4. replace
看答案與詳解

正解 C　若無法出席請在週三前 notify us，好讓主辦重新分配看板，意思是「通知」，即 inform。

5. How can a presenter receive a certificate?

  1. By submitting the poster early
  2. By winning a judges' award
  3. By registering on the website
  4. By attending the whole session
看答案與詳解

正解 D　最後一句說證書發給 all presenters who attend the full session，關鍵是全程參與，與得獎或提早繳交無關。

Student Researchers Find Their Voice at Regional Conference

Last month, eleven undergraduates from our university presented their work at the Central Taiwan Student Research Conference, the largest event of its kind in the region. For many of them, it was their first time explaining their research to an audience outside their own department.

Lin Yu-ting, a third-year biology major, presented a poster on how water temperature affects the growth of local algae. "I practiced my explanation at least twenty times," she said. "But the real challenge was answering unexpected questions from people in completely different fields. A computer science student asked me how I collected my data, and it made me realize my method section needed to be clearer."

Faculty advisors say this kind of feedback is exactly why conferences matter. "In class, students mostly talk to people who already understand their topic," said Professor Huang Wei-ming, who has supervised student researchers for over a decade. "At a conference, they have to translate their ideas for a general audience. That skill is valuable whether they go on to graduate school or into industry."

Not every presentation went smoothly. Two students reported that their laptops failed to connect to the projector, forcing them to present without slides. "It was stressful," admitted engineering student Kevin Tsai, "but it taught me to know my material well enough that I don't depend on technology."

The Office of Undergraduate Research plans to double its travel grants next year, allowing up to twenty-five students to attend outside conferences. Applications will open in February, and students who have completed at least one semester of supervised research will be eligible.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A new research grant for graduate students
  2. Students' experiences presenting at a conference
  3. How to design an effective research poster
  4. A conflict between two academic departments
看答案與詳解

正解 B　全文圍繞十一位大學生到區域研討會發表的經驗與收穫。補助只在最後一段提及且對象是大學生；並非教人設計海報。

7. What did Lin Yu-ting learn from a question she was asked?

  1. Her topic was too similar to another poster.
  2. She needed to practice more times.
  3. Her explanation of her method was not clear enough.
  4. Computer science was a better field for her.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　資工系學生問她如何收集資料，讓她發現 my method section needed to be clearer。她已練習二十次，並非需要多練習。

8. According to Professor Huang, why are conferences valuable?

  1. Students learn to explain ideas to non-experts.
  2. Students can find jobs in industry there.
  3. Students receive higher grades for attending.
  4. Students meet professors from abroad.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　黃教授說學生在研討會必須 translate their ideas for a general audience，即學會向非專業者解釋。industry 只是說這項技能日後有用，不是在會場找工作。

9. Which TWO statements about next year's travel grants are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Only engineering students can apply.
  2. Up to twenty-five students may receive them.
  3. Students must have presented before.
  4. They will cover graduate school fees.
  5. Applications open in February.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　末段寫 up to twenty-five students 與 Applications will open in February。資格是完成一學期指導研究，不限工程系、不需發表過，也與研究所學費無關。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I posted the photos online last night.
  2. The symposium lasts two days.
  3. On the board next to booth twelve.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問海報要掛在哪裡，C 指出位置「十二號攤位旁的看板」最合理。A 用 posted 與 poster 音近混淆；B 重複 symposium 但談活動天數，答非所問。

聽力原文

Where should we hang our poster for the student research symposium?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The cash register is by the door.
  2. Not yet. The deadline is next week.
  3. It's in conference room three.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否已報名台中的研討會，B 回答「還沒，截止日在下週」直接回應。A 用 register（收銀機）與 registered 混淆；C 重複 conference 但指會議室位置。

聽力原文

Have you registered for the conference in Taichung yet?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I'd be happy to. Send it over tonight.
  2. It's a very abstract painting, isn't it?
  3. No, I didn't give it back.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Would you mind...? 是請求幫忙看摘要，A 欣然答應並請對方寄來最恰當。B 把 abstract 當「抽象的」；C 用 give 與 giving 重複字混淆，且 No 在此語意不通。

聽力原文

Would you mind giving me some feedback on my abstract?

4. When should the woman send her file to the print shop?

  1. By Monday
  2. By Wednesday
  3. By Friday
  4. Within three hours
看答案與詳解

正解 B　教授說印刷店需要三個工作天，要她「週三前」把檔案寄出。three 指的是工作天數而非小時，其他日期對話中未提及。

聽力原文

Professor Lin, the symposium guidelines say posters should be A zero size. Is that the same size we used last year? No, last year's posters were smaller. And remember, the campus print shop needs three working days, so send them your file by Wednesday. Got it. I'll finalize the charts tonight.

5. Why is the woman relieved?

  1. Her lab meeting was canceled.
  2. The rehearsal was moved to the morning.
  3. She doesn't have to present anymore.
  4. She no longer has a schedule conflict.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　女生的實驗室會議開到中午，原本擔心趕不上上午的報告；改到兩點十五分後就不衝突了。會議並未取消，彩排在一點，她仍需報告。

聽力原文

Did you see the new schedule? Our oral presentation got moved from the morning session to two fifteen. That's a relief, actually. My lab meeting runs until noon, so I was worried I'd be late. Great. Let's do a final rehearsal at one o'clock in the seminar room.

6. What is the main purpose of the announcement?

  1. To report a change in start time
  2. To introduce the keynote speaker
  3. To ask presenters to print new posters
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播重點是海報場次因展板布置延誤，從九點半「改到十點開始」。全文未提主講人，也沒有要求重印海報。

聽力原文

Attention, symposium participants. The poster session in the Student Activity Center will begin at ten o'clock instead of nine thirty because of a delay in setting up the display boards. Presenters may enter the hall from nine fifteen to hang their posters. Coffee and snacks will be served in the lobby during the wait. Thank you for your patience.

7. Who is the talk mainly intended for?

  1. Judges of the poster award
  2. Students presenting at a symposium
  3. Visitors to a science museum
  4. Staff at the campus print shop
看答案與詳解

正解 B　開頭說 many of you will be presenting at the undergraduate research symposium，聽眾是要發表海報的學生。評審只是被提及的對象。

8. What will happen if a student does not upload the PDF by Wednesday?

  1. The poster cannot be displayed.
  2. The student must present orally instead.
  3. The poster will not be eligible for an award.
  4. The student will lose course credit.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　最後提到錯過期限 your poster won't be considered for the best poster award，即失去評獎資格；並未說不能展示或影響學分。

9. Which TWO pieces of advice does the speaker give? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Make the title readable from a distance
  2. Include as many results as possible
  3. Read the poster text aloud to visitors
  4. Prepare handouts that link to the full report
  5. Bring a laptop to show extra slides
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　講者建議標題要「兩公尺外看得清楚」，並準備附 QR code 連到完整報告的講義。他說評審不看結果數量、也明確叫大家不要照念海報，筆電則未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. Since many of you will be presenting at the undergraduate research symposium next Friday, I'd like to share a few tips about poster presentations. First, remember that visitors usually spend less than a minute at each poster, so your title and main finding should be readable from about two meters away. Keep the text short and let your figures tell the story. Second, prepare a short spoken summary, about thirty seconds long, that explains why your question matters and what you found. Don't just read the poster aloud. The judges care more about how clearly you explain your methods than how many results you have. Third, print a few handouts with a QR code linking to your full report, so interested visitors can read more later. Finally, the deadline to upload a PDF of your poster to the symposium website is this Wednesday at midnight. If you miss it, your poster won't be considered for the best poster award.
