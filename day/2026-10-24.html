BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-10-24
實習與職涯中心

今日單字

internship n. 實習
She did a two-month internship at a software company in Hsinchu.
career fair n. 就業博覽會；校園徵才
More than eighty companies will set up booths at the career fair.
mock interview n. 模擬面試
The mock interview helped me answer questions more confidently.
tailor v. 使合適；量身調整
Always tailor your cover letter to the company you are applying to.
conduct v. 進行；實施
Volunteers will conduct twenty-minute practice interviews.
placement n. （實習或工作的）安排、職位
Interns will fill out a survey at the end of each placement.
supervisor n. 指導者；主管
Your training plan must be signed by a faculty supervisor.
first-come, first-served phr. 先到先得
Workshop seats are given on a first-come, first-served basis.

閱讀 Reading

1. Many companies prefer applicants who have some practical ______, such as a part-time job or an internship.

  1. expression
  2. experiment
  3. expansion
  4. experience
看答案與詳解

正解 D　由 such as a part-time job or an internship 可知指「實務經驗」experience（D）。expression 表達、experiment 實驗、expansion 擴張，字形相近但語意不合。

2. Please ______ your cover letter to each company instead of sending the same letter to everyone.

  1. tailor
  2. borrow
  3. ignore
  4. delay
看答案與詳解

正解 A　instead of sending the same letter 表示要依公司「量身調整」，tailor A to B 最符合（A）。borrow 借、ignore 忽略、delay 延後皆不合語意。

From: Career Development Center <career@univ.edu.tw>

To: All Registered Students

Subject: Mock Interview Day – Sign-up Now Open

Dear Students,

The Career Development Center will hold its Mock Interview Day on Thursday, November 12, from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. in the Student Activity Center. Volunteers from twelve local companies, including several engineering firms and a major bank, will conduct 20-minute practice interviews and give each student written feedback.

Spaces are limited to 60 students, and slots are offered on a first-come, first-served basis. To reserve a time, log in to the career portal and choose an available slot. Please note that your booking will not be confirmed until you upload an updated résumé. Business casual clothing is recommended.

If you can no longer attend, please cancel at least 48 hours in advance so that another student can take your place. Students who miss their slot without notice will not be allowed to book other career events this semester.

Best regards, Career Development Center

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To explain a new dress code for students
  2. To announce a job opening at a bank
  3. To ask local companies to send volunteers
  4. To invite students to sign up for practice interviews
看答案與詳解

正解 D　主旨與第一段都在介紹模擬面試日並開放報名（D）。服裝只是建議，銀行是志工來源而非職缺，信件對象是學生而非企業。

4. What must students do before their booking is confirmed?

  1. Upload an updated résumé
  2. Pay a deposit
  3. Get approval from an advisor
  4. Choose which company will interview them
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第二段說 booking will not be confirmed until you upload an updated résumé（A）。押金、顧問核准與指定公司皆未提及。

5. The word "conduct" in paragraph 1 is closest in meaning to

  1. behave
  2. transfer
  3. carry out
  4. record
看答案與詳解

正解 C　conduct practice interviews 指「進行、執行」面試，等同 carry out（C）。conduct 雖也有「行為」之意，但此處不是 behave。

More Students Turn to Internships Before Graduation

Walk past the Career Development Center on any weekday afternoon this semester and you will probably see a line of students waiting to speak with an advisor. According to the center's latest figures, 1,240 students applied for internships through the university last year, an increase of nearly 35 percent from two years earlier.

Center director Daniel Lin believes the rise reflects changing expectations in the job market. "Employers no longer look only at grades," he explains. "They want to see that graduates can work in a team, communicate clearly, and handle real deadlines. An internship is the easiest way to show that." He adds that some companies now hire up to half of their new employees from their own intern programs.

Students agree that the benefits go beyond an extra line on a résumé. Amy Chen, a senior majoring in information management, spent last summer at a software company in Hsinchu. "I learned more about project management in two months than in two years of classes," she says. "It also made me realize that I enjoy working with clients more than writing code, so I've changed my career plans."

Still, internships are not without problems. Some students report being given only simple office tasks, while others struggle to balance their internships with coursework. To address these concerns, the university introduced a new rule this year: every internship that earns academic credit must include a written training plan signed by both the company and a faculty supervisor. The center also plans to survey interns at the end of each placement and remove companies that receive repeated negative reviews.

For students considering an internship, Lin's advice is simple: start early. "The best positions are often filled by December," he warns.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A new software company in Hsinchu
  2. How to write a strong résumé
  3. The growing popularity of internships among students
  4. Changes to graduation requirements
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文從申請人數增加 35% 談起，討論實習的好處、問題與對策，主旨是實習日益受歡迎（C）。新竹公司只是例子，履歷與畢業門檻並非重點。

7. According to Daniel Lin, why do employers value internships?

  1. Interns cost less than full-time staff.
  2. They show that graduates have practical work skills.
  3. Students with internships usually have higher grades.
  4. The government requires companies to hire interns.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Lin 說雇主想看到團隊合作、溝通與處理期限的能力，實習最能證明（B）。他明說雇主不只看成績，成本與政府規定則未提及。

8. What can be inferred about Amy Chen?

  1. She plans to pursue a career that involves more contact with clients.
  2. She found her internship boring and unhelpful.
  3. She will definitely return to the same company after graduation.
  4. She prefers writing code to working with people.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Amy 說她發現自己較喜歡與客戶合作而非寫程式，因此改變職涯規劃，可推知她將走向與客戶接觸較多的工作（A）。D 與原文相反，B 與她的正面評價矛盾，C 無根據。

9. Which TWO steps has the university taken or planned to deal with problems in internships? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Limiting internships to the summer break
  2. Paying interns directly
  3. Requiring a signed training plan for credit-bearing internships
  4. Reducing interns' course loads
  5. Surveying interns at the end of each placement
看答案與詳解

正解 C、E　第四段提到：可抵學分的實習須附公司與指導老師簽署的訓練計畫，以及在每次實習結束時調查實習生，故選 C、E。其餘選項文中皆未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The bus fare is only fifteen dollars.
  2. I wore that jacket last Wednesday.
  3. Yes, I want to talk to a few tech companies.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問是否要去週三的就業博覽會，C 表示要去並說明目的最合理。A 用 fare 與 fair 同音混淆，B 重複 Wednesday 但答非所問。

聽力原文

Are you going to the career fair on Wednesday?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The career center has advisors who can do that.
  2. You can resume the meeting after lunch.
  3. I looked over the city from the tower.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問該找誰看履歷，A 指出職涯中心有顧問可協助。B 把 resume 當動詞「繼續」誤導，C 重複 looked over 但意思不同。

聽力原文

Who should I ask to look over my resume?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Because the interview was canceled.
  2. That would be great. How about seven?
  3. I practiced the piano when I was young.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Why don't we... 是提議，B 接受並提出時間最自然。A 誤把它當成問原因而用 Because 回答，C 重複 practice 但無關。

聽力原文

Why don't we practice for your mock interview together tonight?

4. Why can't the man submit the form?

  1. The deadline has already passed.
  2. He entered his grade in the wrong format.
  3. He forgot to attach his resume.
  4. The website is down for maintenance.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女子說 GPA 要用四分制而非百分比，男子輸入了 85，故是格式錯誤（B）。截止日、履歷附件與網站維修皆未提及。

聽力原文

Hi, I'm trying to sign up for the summer internship program, but the online form keeps saying my GPA is invalid. Oh, you need to enter it on a four point scale, not as a percentage. Ah, I typed in eighty-five. That's the problem. Convert it first, and then you can submit the form.

5. What will the man probably do this weekend?

  1. Travel with the hiring manager
  2. Attend the interview
  3. Research the company
  4. Cancel the interview
看答案與詳解

正解 C　男子說週末要 read up on their recent projects，即研究公司（C）。出差的是經理，面試改到下週五，也沒有取消。

聽力原文

Kevin, didn't you have an interview at that semiconductor company today? It got moved to next Friday. The hiring manager is traveling this week. Well, that gives you more time to prepare. True. I'm going to read up on their recent projects this weekend.

6. What has changed about the workshop?

  1. The start time
  2. The location
  3. The speaker
  4. The materials students should bring
看答案與詳解

正解 B　廣播說因投影機故障，地點從 205 教室改到學生活動中心三樓（B）。開始時間仍是兩點半，講者未提，帶履歷是原本的要求。

聽力原文

Attention, students. The Career Development Center's resume workshop scheduled for this afternoon in Room two oh five has been moved to the third floor of the Student Activity Center because of a projector problem. The workshop will still begin at two thirty. Please remember to bring a printed copy of your resume.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A company recruiter
  2. A staff member at the career center
  3. A department chair
  4. A student volunteer
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者自我介紹 I'm Daniel Lin from the Career Development Center，故為職涯中心人員（B）。他代表學校而非企業，也不是系主任或學生。

8. What does the speaker say about academic credit?

  1. It is given automatically to all interns.
  2. It is only available for paid positions.
  3. Students must get approval before the internship begins.
  4. Students should apply for it after the internship ends.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者強調要在實習開始前（before, not after）取得系上核准（C）。D 正好相反；A、B 未被提及。

9. Which TWO things must students do before applying? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Get a letter from a professor
  2. Upload a resume to the career portal
  3. Pay a registration fee
  4. Visit a company in person
  5. Attend an interview skills workshop
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　講者說申請前須做兩件事：上傳履歷到職涯平台，以及參加至少一場面試技巧工作坊，故選 B、E。推薦信、報名費與參訪公司都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to this year's internship orientation. I'm Daniel Lin from the Career Development Center. This year, more than eighty companies are offering summer internships through our office, from banks and hospitals to game studios. Most positions last eight weeks, and about two thirds of them are paid. Before you apply, there are two things you must do. First, upload your resume to the career portal so that one of our advisors can check it. Second, attend at least one of our interview skills workshops. Companies tell us that students who prepare this way are much more confident. Also, please remember that if you want academic credit for your internship, you need approval from your department before the internship starts, not after. Every year, a few students forget this step and lose the credit. Finally, our first application deadline is November twentieth, so please don't wait until the last minute.
