BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-28
畢業專題成果展

今日單字

capstone project n. 畢業專題；總結性專題
Our capstone project took the whole senior year to complete.
prototype n. 原型；樣品
The prototype kept crashing when we added more sensors.
booth n. 攤位；展位
Each booth includes one table, two chairs, and a poster board.
incorporate v. 納入；融入
We incorporated user feedback into the final design.
guarantee v. 保證；擔保
Late requests for equipment cannot be guaranteed.
prohibit v. 禁止
Heating devices are strictly prohibited in the exhibition hall.
marketable adj. 有市場性的；好賣的
Not every student project needs to be marketable.
represent v. 代表
The winning team will represent our university at the national competition.

閱讀 Reading

1. The judges were impressed by how the team ______ feedback from real users into the final design.

  1. eliminated
  2. postponed
  3. incorporated
  4. resembled
看答案與詳解

正解 C　incorporate A into B 意為「把 A 融入 B」，符合「把使用者回饋融入最終設計」。eliminate 刪除、postpone 延期、resemble 相似，皆不能接 into 表達此意。

2. Because space is limited, only teams that submitted their abstracts by the ______ will be given a booth at the showcase.

  1. headline
  2. outline
  3. guideline
  4. deadline
看答案與詳解

正解 D　by the deadline 表示「在截止期限前」，符合繳交摘要的語境。headline 標題、outline 大綱、guideline 準則，都是字形相近的干擾選項。

From: Capstone Committee <capstone@eng.university.edu.tw>

To: Senior Project Teams

Subject: Booth Assignments and Equipment Requests for the Showcase

Dear Teams,

Booth assignments for the Capstone Showcase on December 9 are now posted on the department website. Each booth includes one table, two chairs, and a poster board. If your project requires additional equipment, such as a monitor, extension cords, or a Wi-Fi connection, please fill out the online request form by December 3. Requests received after this date cannot be guaranteed.

Because the hall has a limited number of power outlets, teams whose projects run on batteries are encouraged to bring fully charged backup batteries. Heating devices and anything that produces smoke are strictly prohibited.

If you would like to swap booths with another team, both teams must agree and notify the committee in writing. We look forward to seeing the results of your hard work.

Best regards,

Capstone Committee

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce the winners of the showcase
  2. To give information about booths and equipment
  3. To invite teams to submit project abstracts
  4. To explain how projects will be graded
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信件主旨與內容都在說明攤位分配、設備申請與用電規定。全文未提得獎名單、摘要徵件或評分方式。

4. In the email, the word "guaranteed" is closest in meaning to

  1. promised
  2. delayed
  3. refused
  4. repaired
看答案與詳解

正解 A　cannot be guaranteed 指逾期申請「無法保證」會提供，guarantee 最接近 promise（承諾）。delayed 延遲、refused 拒絕、repaired 修理皆不符。

5. What must teams do if they want to change booths?

  1. Pay an extra fee to the committee
  2. Ask the committee to choose a new booth for them
  3. Get another team's agreement and inform the committee in writing
  4. Fill out the equipment request form by December 3
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中說若要換攤位，雙方隊伍都須同意並以書面通知委員會。未提費用，也不是由委員會另選；12 月 3 日是設備申請期限。

From Classroom to Marketplace: Capstone Projects Gain Industry Attention

For years, capstone projects at our university were seen mainly as a graduation requirement, something students completed, presented once, and then forgot. That view is changing. At last year's Capstone Showcase, more than forty companies sent representatives, nearly double the number from three years ago, and several student teams received offers to continue developing their projects with industry support.

One example is a team from the Department of Mechanical Engineering that designed a low-cost device to help elderly people stand up from a chair. After the showcase, a medical equipment company invited the team to test the device in two nursing homes in Taichung. "We built it for a class," said team leader Chen Yu-ting, "but now real people are using it, and their feedback is shaping every new version."

Professor Wang Mei-hua, who coordinates the program, believes this shift benefits students in several ways. "When students know that engineers from real companies will see their work, they take user needs much more seriously," she explained. "They also learn to explain technical ideas to people outside their field, which is a skill employers value highly."

However, not everyone sees only advantages. Some faculty members worry that projects may become too focused on commercial success. "A capstone should give students room to experiment and even fail," said Professor Lee Chih-ming. "If every project has to be marketable, students may avoid risky but creative ideas." To address this concern, the department now gives a separate award for the most innovative project, regardless of its business potential.

This year's showcase will take place on December 9, and organizers expect a record number of visitors.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. The growing industry interest in student capstone projects
  2. How to design devices for elderly people
  3. Why capstone projects should no longer be required
  4. A new grading system for engineering students
看答案與詳解

正解 A　標題與首段都指出業界對畢業專題的關注大增（參展企業近乎倍增），全文圍繞此趨勢的例子與正反看法。長者輔具只是例子；未主張取消專題或新評分制度。

7. What happened to Chen Yu-ting's team after the showcase?

  1. They started their own medical company.
  2. They won the award for the most innovative project.
  3. They were invited to test their device in nursing homes.
  4. They redesigned the device for hospitals in Taipei.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段說醫療器材公司邀請該隊到台中兩間護理之家測試裝置。文中沒說他們創業或得到創新獎，地點也不是台北醫院。

8. What is Professor Lee's main concern?

  1. Too few companies attend the showcase.
  2. Companies do not pay students enough.
  3. Students cannot explain technical ideas clearly.
  4. Students may avoid risky but creative ideas.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　李教授擔心若專題都要有市場性，學生會避開有風險但有創意的點子。參展企業其實變多；薪酬與表達能力都不是他提出的問題。

9. According to Professor Wang, which TWO benefits do students gain? (Choose TWO.)

  1. They pay more attention to what users need.
  2. They automatically receive higher grades.
  3. They learn to explain ideas to people outside their field.
  4. They are guaranteed jobs after graduation.
  5. They no longer need to write a final report.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　王教授說學生會更重視使用者需求（take user needs more seriously），並學會向領域外的人解釋技術概念。文中未提自動加分、保證就業或免交報告。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The show was canceled because of the rain.
  2. I'll post it on the class website later.
  3. Almost. We just need to fix the chart on the right side.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問專題成果展海報是否完成，C 回答「快好了，只剩右邊圖表要修」最合理。A 以 show 混淆 showcase；B 以 post 混淆 poster，答非所問。

聽力原文

Has your team finished the poster for the capstone showcase?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Lisa volunteered, since she wrote most of the code.
  2. The demonstration was on a Friday last year.
  3. I'd like the present wrapped in blue paper.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問誰要向評審展示成品，A 指出 Lisa 自願、因為程式多是她寫的，直接回答 Who。B 重複 Friday、以 demonstration 混淆 demo；C 把動詞 present 誤當名詞「禮物」。

聽力原文

Who's going to present our demo to the judges on Friday?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I usually work out at the gym in the evening.
  2. The exhibition hall is being repaired this month.
  3. Please move the chairs to the left side.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問成果展為何改到體育館，B 說明展覽廳本月整修，提供原因。A 只重複 gym；C 以 move 混淆 moved，都沒回答原因。

聽力原文

I can't believe the showcase has been moved to the gym. Why did they change it?

4. What will the man most likely do next?

  1. Buy a larger battery online
  2. Remove one of the sensors
  3. Ask Kevin for a power module
  4. Rewrite the program
看答案與詳解

正解 C　教授建議先向 205 室的 Kevin 借外接電源模組，故男生接下來最可能去找 Kevin。買電池是男生提議但被教授改為「先借」；拆感測器與改程式都未提到。

聽力原文

Professor Lin, our prototype keeps crashing whenever we connect more than three sensors. Have you checked the power supply? Three sensors might be the limit for that board. Not yet. Should we just buy a bigger battery? Try borrowing an external power module from the lab first. Kevin in Room two oh five can lend you one.

5. What does the woman suggest?

  1. Asking the organizers for more time
  2. Shortening the introduction of the talk
  3. Adding more details to the slides
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女生建議刪掉背景說明、直接講結果，也就是縮短簡報開頭部分。她沒有要求延長時間，細節則是放在海報上而非增加到投影片。

聽力原文

Did you see the email? Each team gets only eight minutes to present at the showcase. Eight? Our slides take at least twelve. Then let's cut the background section and go straight to the results. The judges can read the details on our poster.

6. What are students asked to do?

  1. Finish preparing their booths by twelve thirty
  2. Park in the lot behind the Student Activity Center
  3. Print posters wider than one meter
  4. Register for the event by Wednesday
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播說各組須在十二點半前布置好攤位。大樓後方停車場當天關閉；海報須符合約一公尺寬的展板，不能更寬；未提到報名期限。

聽力原文

Attention, all senior students in the College of Engineering. The Capstone Showcase will be held next Wednesday, December ninth, from one to five in the afternoon in the Student Activity Center. Every team must finish setting up its booth by twelve thirty. Posters must fit on the boards provided, which are about one meter wide. Please note that the parking lot behind the building will be closed that day, so visitors should use the parking area near the north gate.

7. Who will judge the showcase?

  1. Only professors from the department
  2. Professors and engineers from local companies
  3. Students from last year's winning team
  4. Officials from the national competition
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說五位評審是三位系上教授加兩位在地企業工程師。並非只有教授，也未提到學長姐或全國競賽官員。

8. Why does the speaker recommend practicing a two-minute summary?

  1. Each team has only two minutes at its booth.
  2. The summary must be uploaded to the learning platform.
  3. The judges prefer long, detailed lectures.
  4. The judges may ask any team member to speak.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者明說 because the judges may ask any member to speak，所以每位組員都要能講兩分鐘摘要。她反而說評審不要長篇演講；上傳的是期末報告，不是摘要。

9. Which TWO statements about the showcase are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. The winning team will compete nationally in March.
  2. Teamwork is not part of the scoring.
  3. It counts for thirty percent of the final grade.
  4. Every team will receive prize money.
  5. Final reports are due before the showcase.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　冠軍隊伍三月代表學校參加全國賽，且成果展佔學期成績百分之三十。團隊合作是評分項目之一；只有前三名有獎金；報告 12 月 14 日截止，在 12 月 9 日成果展之後。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. As your capstone coordinator, I want to explain how the showcase will be judged. This year we've invited five judges: three professors from our department and two engineers from local companies. Each judge will visit every booth and give scores in three areas: technical quality, teamwork, and communication. Communication is where many teams lose points. The judges are not looking for a long lecture. They want you to explain the problem you solved, show how your product works, and answer questions clearly. Practice a two-minute summary that everyone on your team can give, because the judges may ask any member to speak. The top three teams will receive prize money, and the winning team will represent our university at the national competition in March. Also, remember that the showcase counts for thirty percent of your final grade, so even if you don't win, your effort matters. Finally, please upload your final report to the learning platform by December fourteenth.
