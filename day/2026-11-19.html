校園創業競賽與育成中心

今日單字

incubator n. （新創）育成中心
The campus incubator offers free workspace to student startup teams.
pitch n./v. 提案簡報；向人推銷構想
Maya will pitch our app idea to the judges on demo day.
prototype n. 原型；試作品
We built a simple prototype in the makerspace to test our design.
mentor n. 導師；業師
Our mentor from industry meets with us every Wednesday.
underestimate v. 低估
Many new teams underestimate how much their server costs will be.
revise v. 修改；修訂
Please revise the budget section and upload it again by Friday.
demand n. 需求
Five hundred active users show strong market demand for the app.
certification n. 認證；證照
Food-safety certification is required before you can sell meals on campus.

閱讀 Reading

1. Our team decided to ______ the business plan after the mentor pointed out several weak assumptions.

  1. reveal
  2. resign
  3. revise
  4. reserve
看答案與詳解

正解 C　業師指出計畫中幾個薄弱的假設後，團隊決定「修改」計畫，revise 最合理。reveal 揭露、resign 辭職、reserve 預訂，皆不合語意。

2. The judges were impressed that the app had already attracted over five hundred active users, which showed strong market ______.

  1. supply
  2. demand
  3. decision
  4. permission
看答案與詳解

正解 B　已有五百多位活躍使用者，代表市場「需求」強，market demand 為固定搭配。supply 供給與句意相反；decision、permission 不與 market 搭配表達此意。

From: Office of Innovation and Entrepreneurship

To: Huang Jia-wei

Subject: Your Startup Challenge application

Dear Jia-wei,

Thank you for submitting your team's application for the Campus Startup Challenge. We have reviewed your materials, and your project, a mobile app that helps students share leftover cafeteria meals, has been selected for the second round.

Before the second round, each team must attend one of two pitch workshops, held on November 24 or November 26 from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. in Room 205 of the Management Building. Please register for one session through the online form by Saturday. In addition, your current budget does not include the cost of food-safety certification. Please revise this section and upload the updated file by December 1.

We look forward to seeing your pitch. Feel free to contact us if you have any questions.

Best regards,

Grace Wu, Program Coordinator

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce a new cafeteria policy
  2. To reject a team's application
  3. To invite students to judge a competition
  4. To tell a team that it has advanced and what to do next
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中通知團隊已進入第二輪（selected for the second round），並說明要參加工作坊、修改預算等後續步驟。並非拒絕通知，也與餐廳政策或擔任評審無關。

4. What does Ms. Wu ask the team to change?

  1. The name of their app
  2. The time of their pitch
  3. The budget section
  4. The workshop location
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中指出預算未包含食品安全認證費用，請他們 revise this section，即修改預算部分。其他項目信中皆未要求更改。

5. In the email, the word "selected" is closest in meaning to

  1. chosen
  2. delayed
  3. ignored
  4. copied
看答案與詳解

正解 A　has been selected for the second round 意為「被選入第二輪」，selected 與 chosen 同義。delayed 延後、ignored 忽略、copied 複製皆不符。

From Classroom Idea to Real Business: Student Startups on the Rise

Five years ago, only a handful of students at our university showed any interest in starting a business. This year, the Campus Startup Challenge received 92 applications, a record number. According to the Office of Innovation and Entrepreneurship, the increase is partly due to a new entrepreneurship course, which became an elective open to all departments in 2024.

One of last year's winners, a team of three engineering and design students, developed a low-cost sensor that warns farmers when soil becomes too dry. After the competition, the team joined the campus incubator, where they received office space and advice from experienced mentors. Today, their product is being tested on twelve farms in Yunlin County. "We learned more from failing in front of real farmers than from any textbook," said team leader Lin Pei-shan.

However, not everyone is convinced that the trend is entirely positive. Professor Chang Wen-hao of the Department of Economics warns that some students spend so much time on their startups that their grades suffer. "A startup can be a great learning experience," he said, "but students should not forget that their degree is their safety net." He suggests that the university offer academic credit for incubator work, so that students do not have to choose between their projects and their studies.

The office says it is considering the idea. In the meantime, it plans to add evening workshops on finance and legal issues next semester, since many teams have said these are the areas where they feel least prepared.

6. What is one reason given for the rise in applications?

  1. A larger cash prize for winners
  2. Free office space for every student
  3. A new entrepreneurship course open to all departments
  4. Pressure from the Department of Economics
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第一段說申請增加部分歸因於新的創業課程，自 2024 年起開放全校各系選修。辦公空間只提供給進入育成中心的團隊；文中未提獎金或經濟系施壓。

7. What can be inferred about the soil-sensor team?

  1. They have stopped working on their product.
  2. They improved their product through real-world testing.
  3. They are all students in the Department of Economics.
  4. They won first place two years in a row.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　產品正在雲林十二座農場測試，隊長也說從在農民面前失敗中學到更多，可推知他們透過實地測試改進產品。團隊成員來自工程與設計，且產品仍在進行中。

8. What does Professor Chang suggest?

  1. Limiting the number of teams that can apply
  2. Requiring students to graduate before starting a business
  3. Hiring more mentors from industry
  4. Giving academic credit for work in the incubator
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第三段張教授建議學校 offer academic credit for incubator work，讓學生不必在專案與課業間二選一。他並未主張限制隊伍或要求先畢業。

9. According to the article, which TWO statements are true? (Choose TWO.)

  1. The Startup Challenge received a record number of applications this year.
  2. The soil sensor is already sold in stores nationwide.
  3. Professor Chang believes startups have no educational value.
  4. Many teams feel unprepared in finance and legal matters.
  5. The entrepreneurship course is required for engineering students.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　第一段說今年 92 件申請創紀錄；第四段說許多團隊在財務與法律方面最沒準備。感測器仍在試驗階段；張教授認為創業是很好的學習經驗；創業課是選修而非必修。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. We started our company last year.
  2. Yes, teams now have until next Friday.
  3. The line outside was really long.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問題問截止日期是否延長，B 直接回答「是，延到下週五」。A 重複 start 字根但答非所問；C 的 line 與 deadline 音近，屬干擾選項。

聽力原文

Has the deadline for the startup competition been extended?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Throw the pitch a little higher.
  2. The judges sat near the stage.
  3. I think Maya should, since she's our strongest speaker.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Who 問句要回答「人」，C 指出由 Maya 上台提案最合理。A 的 pitch 是「投球」之意，屬一字多義陷阱；B 重複 judges 但說的是位置。

聽力原文

Who's going to pitch our idea to the judges?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Why don't we ask the makerspace for extra hours?
  2. It's about time for lunch.
  3. That type of paper is wrong.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　對方擔心原型來不及完成，A 提出向創客空間申請延長使用時間的解決辦法。B 重複 time；C 的 type 與 prototype 音近，皆不相關。

聽力原文

I'm not sure our prototype will be ready in time.

4. What must the man submit with his application?

  1. A business plan approved by a professor
  2. His teammate's course certificate
  3. Proof that he is a first-year student
  4. A letter from the incubator director
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女子最後說 Just attach her course certificate，即隊友的修課證明。其他選項（教授核可的計畫、新生證明、主任推薦信）對話中皆未要求。

聽力原文

Hi, I'm interested in the incubator program. Can first-year students apply? They can, but every team needs at least one member who has finished the entrepreneurship course. Oh, my teammate took it last spring. So we're fine? Yes. Just attach her course certificate to your application.

5. What problem did the mentor point out?

  1. The team underestimated its server costs.
  2. The app idea is not original.
  3. The marketing budget is too high.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　男子說 she thought we underestimated our server costs，問題在伺服器成本估太低。業師其實喜歡這個點子；刪減行銷費用是女子的猜測，被男子以 Actually 否定。

聽力原文

Did you see the feedback from the mentor session? Yeah. She liked our app idea, but she said our budget is unrealistic. So we need to cut the marketing costs? Actually, she thought we underestimated our server costs. We should raise that number.

6. What should teams do if they cannot come on the new date?

  1. Go to the student center tomorrow
  2. Present in a different room
  3. Email the organizing committee by Friday
  4. Withdraw from the competition
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播說無法出席新日期的隊伍 should email the organizing committee by Friday，以安排線上發表。明天的活動已延期；時間與教室不變；並未提到退賽。

聽力原文

Attention, all teams in the Campus Startup Challenge. Because of scheduled power maintenance in the student center, tomorrow's demo day has been moved to Monday, November thirtieth. The time and room will stay the same. Teams that cannot attend on the new date should email the organizing committee by Friday, so that we can arrange an online presentation slot.

7. What is the talk mainly about?

  1. Rules for renting office space on campus
  2. How to design professional slides
  3. The results of last year's competition
  4. An introduction to a startup support program
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者是育成計畫經理，介紹計畫提供的資源、選拔標準與申請建議，屬計畫說明會。投影片只是被拿來比較；未提租辦公室規則或去年成績。

8. According to the speaker, what is NOT required to join the program?

  1. A finished product
  2. A clear problem to solve
  3. Evidence that users want the solution
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者被問是否需要完成的產品時明確說 You don't。明確的問題與使用者需求的證據都是他們要找的條件。

9. Which TWO things does the speaker recommend doing before applying? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Interview at least twenty potential customers
  2. Prepare attractive presentation slides
  3. Agree on how ownership will be shared
  4. Raise fifty thousand dollars
  5. Find an industry mentor
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　講者建議先訪談至少二十位潛在顧客，並及早決定團隊持股分配。精美投影片反而被說不如真實訪談；五萬元與業師都是計畫提供的資源，不是申請前要做的事。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to the Innovation Incubator orientation. I'm David Lin, the program manager. Over the next six months, selected teams will receive free workspace on the third floor of the engineering building, a starting fund of fifty thousand dollars, and weekly meetings with a mentor from industry. Many students ask whether they need a finished product to join. You don't. What we look for is a clear problem, a team that works well together, and evidence that real users want your solution. There are two things I strongly recommend before you apply. First, talk to at least twenty potential customers and write down what they tell you. Judges are far more impressed by real interviews than by beautiful slides. Second, decide early how your team will share ownership. Disagreements about that have broken up more promising teams than any technical problem. Applications close on December fifteenth, and interviews will take place in the first week of January.
