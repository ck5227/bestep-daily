輔系與雙主修申請

今日單字

minor n. 輔系
She is majoring in history with a minor in digital media.
double major n. 雙主修
A double major often requires forty extra credits.
transcript n. 成績單
Please print your unofficial transcript before meeting your advisor.
approval n. 核准；同意
You need approval from your home department to add a minor.
postpone v. 延後
Kevin had to postpone his graduation by one semester.
requirement n. 必要條件；規定
The department explained the course requirements at the session.
flexibility n. 彈性
Many students choose a minor for its flexibility.
foundation n. 基礎
A double major gives you a deeper foundation in a second field.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who wish to add a minor must first get ______ from their home department.

  1. apology
  2. appearance
  3. approval
  4. approach
看答案與詳解

正解 C　申請輔系須先取得原系的「同意、核准」，approval 最合適。apology 道歉、appearance 外表、approach 方法，皆不合語意。

2. Because the double major requires so many credits, Kevin decided to ______ his graduation by one semester.

  1. prevent
  2. promote
  3. protect
  4. postpone
看答案與詳解

正解 D　雙主修學分多，所以將畢業「延後」一學期，postpone 正確。prevent 阻止、promote 促進、protect 保護，與 by one semester 搭配不合理。

From: Office of Academic Affairs

To: Wang Yu-ting

Subject: Your Double Major Application – Missing Document

Dear Yu-ting,

Thank you for submitting your application for a double major in Business Administration. After reviewing your file, we found that the study plan signed by your advisor is missing. Without this document, your application cannot be forwarded to the department for review.

Please upload the signed study plan to the student portal by 5 p.m. on Friday, December 11. If you are unable to meet your advisor before then, you may ask the department secretary to issue a temporary signature form. Late or incomplete applications will not be considered this semester, but you are welcome to reapply in May.

Best regards,

Office of Academic Affairs

3. Why was the email sent?

  1. To confirm that her application was approved
  2. To tell her that a document is missing
  3. To change the application deadline
  4. To introduce her new advisor
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中指出申請資料缺少導師簽名的讀書計畫，選 B。申請尚未送審，更談不上核准；截止日只是告知而非更改；也未介紹新導師。

4. The word "considered" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. reviewed
  2. respected
  3. calculated
  4. believed
看答案與詳解

正解 A　逾期或不完整的申請本學期將不被「審查、考慮」，即 reviewed，選 A。respected 尊重、calculated 計算、believed 相信，皆不符上下文。

5. What can Yu-ting do if she cannot meet her advisor in time?

  1. Submit a paper copy of the plan
  2. Email the dean directly
  3. Request a temporary form from the department secretary
  4. Wait until May to apply
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中說若無法及時見到導師，可請系秘書開立臨時簽名表，選 C。紙本與寫信給院長未提及；五月重新申請是錯過截止日的情況，並非此時的解決辦法。

More Students Choosing Minors Over Double Majors

The number of students applying for double majors at our university has dropped for the third year in a row, while applications for minors have nearly doubled, according to figures released last week by the Office of Academic Affairs. This year, 412 students applied for minors, compared with only 168 for double majors.

Officials say the change reflects how students are thinking about their future. "Employers today are looking for people who can combine skills from different areas," said Dean Hsu Wen-chi. "A minor in data science or design can make an engineering or literature graduate stand out, and it takes much less time than a second major." A double major at the university typically requires at least forty extra credits, which often delays graduation.

Students also point to flexibility. Lin Pei-shan, a third-year history major, chose a minor in digital media. "I wanted practical skills for my portfolio, but I didn't want to give up my exchange semester in Japan," she explained. "With a minor, I can do both."

However, not everyone sees the trend as positive. Professor Kuo Chih-ming of the Economics Department warned that some students choose minors simply to decorate their resumes. "A few courses won't make you an expert," he said. "If students are serious about a second field, a double major gives them a much deeper foundation."

To help students make better decisions, the university will launch an online tool next semester that compares the credit requirements and career outcomes of every minor and double major program.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A shift in students' choices between minors and double majors
  2. A new rule that limits double majors
  3. The career outcomes of history graduates
  4. A new exchange program in Japan
看答案與詳解

正解 A　全文圍繞輔系申請人數增加、雙主修減少的趨勢及各方看法，選 A。文中沒有限制雙主修的新規定；歷史系與日本交換只是舉例細節。

7. Why did Lin Pei-shan choose a minor?

  1. Her grades were too low for a double major
  2. Her advisor required her to
  3. She could still spend a semester abroad
  4. Digital media has no double major option
看答案與詳解

正解 C　林同學說不想放棄去日本交換一學期，修輔系可以兩者兼顧，選 C。文中未提成績、導師要求或是否有雙主修選項。

8. What concern does Professor Kuo express?

  1. Minors take too long to complete
  2. Students are applying too late
  3. Double majors cost too much
  4. Minors may not provide deep enough knowledge
看答案與詳解

正解 D　郭教授說「修幾門課不會讓你成為專家」，雙主修基礎更深，表示擔心輔系不夠深入，選 D。花太久時間是雙主修的問題；申請時間與費用未提及。

9. Which of the following are stated about double majors at the university? Choose TWO.

  1. They will be cancelled next year
  2. Applications have fallen for three years in a row
  3. They are more popular than minors
  4. They require an interview with the dean
  5. They usually require at least forty extra credits
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　第一段說雙主修申請人數連續三年下降，第二段說雙主修通常需多修至少四十學分，選 B、E。今年輔系申請人數較多，C 錯；取消與院長面談均未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Yes, the economy is doing well this year.
  2. I'm still comparing it with a double major.
  3. The minor injury healed quickly.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否決定申請經濟輔系，B 回答仍在和雙主修比較，最合理。A 用 economy 與 economics 音近混淆；C 重複 minor 但意思是「輕微的」，答非所問。

聽力原文

Have you decided whether to apply for a minor in economics?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. At the Office of Academic Affairs, on the second floor.
  2. I applied twice last year.
  3. Fill in the form with a black pen.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問「在哪裡」繳交申請表，A 給出地點最恰當。B 以 twice 呼應 double 製造混淆；C 重複 form，但說的是填寫方式而非地點。

聽力原文

Where do I hand in the double major application form?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The line at the cafeteria is too long.
  2. No, I don't mind at all.
  3. Yes, until next Friday.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　附加問句確認截止日是否延長，C 肯定並說明延到下週五。A 用 line 與 deadline 音近混淆；B 的 I don't mind 是回應請求的說法，不合語境。

聽力原文

The deadline for minor applications has been extended, hasn't it?

4. What does the woman suggest the man do?

  1. Raise his average before applying
  2. Begin preparing a study plan soon
  3. Choose a minor instead
  4. Talk to the department chair
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女子說今年還要交讀書計畫，建議「這週就開始寫」，選 B。男子平均 78 已超過 75 的門檻，不需提高成績；對話未提改申請輔系或找系主任。

聽力原文

Professor Lin, I'm thinking of adding a double major in computer science, but I'm worried about my grades. What's your current average? About seventy-eight. The department asks for at least seventy-five, right? That's right. But this year they also want a short study plan, so I'd start writing it this week.

5. Why will the woman probably take summer courses?

  1. To graduate a semester early
  2. Because summer courses are cheaper
  3. So she can keep all her major courses
  4. Because psychology is only taught in summer
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女子說暑修可以讓她「不必退掉任何主修課」，選 C。對話未提提早畢業或學費；男子只說「部分」輔系課在暑假開，並非只在暑假開。

聽力原文

I got accepted into the psychology minor, but my schedule next semester is already full. Some minor courses are offered in the summer session. You could take one or two then. Good idea. That way I won't have to drop any of my major courses.

6. What must students do to attend the session?

  1. Register online in advance
  2. Email a department representative
  3. Bring a copy of their transcript
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播說必須在週三中午前透過學生入口網站報名，選 A。各系代表是在現場回答問題，不需事先寄信；廣播未提到要帶成績單。

聽力原文

Attention, students. An information session on minors and double majors will be held this Thursday at two p.m. in the International Conference Hall. Representatives from twelve departments will answer your questions about requirements and course loads. Please note that seats are limited to two hundred, so you must sign up through the student portal by Wednesday at noon.

7. What is the talk mainly about?

  1. How to transfer to another department
  2. How to raise a low grade average
  3. How to apply to graduate programs
  4. How to choose between a minor and a double major
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者開頭即說要幫大家在輔系與雙主修之間做選擇，選 D。轉系與提高成績未提及；研究所只是舉例說明雙主修的優勢，不是主題。

8. Why might a double major cost students more money?

  1. Its application fee is higher
  2. Students often need an extra semester
  3. Its courses require expensive materials
  4. Students must take summer classes
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說雙主修常需多讀一學期，而多一學期就要多付學費，選 B。申請費、教材費與暑修都沒有提到。

9. What must students do before applying? Choose TWO.

  1. Meet with an advisor in their target department
  2. Attend a summer course
  3. Print an unofficial transcript
  4. Get a letter from their parents
  5. Pass an entrance exam
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　講者最後說申請前要和目標學系的導師面談，並印出非正式成績單，兩者都是必備，選 A、C。和家人討論只是建議，不需要家長信；暑修與入學考試未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. I'm Doctor Chen from the Office of Academic Affairs, and today I'd like to help you decide between a minor and a double major. A minor usually requires about twenty credits, so most students can finish it without staying in school longer. A double major, on the other hand, often requires forty credits or more, and many students need an extra semester to complete it. So how should you choose? First, think about your career goals. If you simply want to explore a new field, a minor is often enough. But if you plan to apply for jobs or graduate programs in that field, a double major will look stronger on your resume. Second, talk with your family, because an extra semester means extra tuition. Applications open on December first and close on December fifteenth. Before you apply, please meet with an advisor in the department you want to join, and print out your unofficial transcript. Both are required parts of the application.
