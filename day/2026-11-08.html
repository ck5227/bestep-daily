今日單字

waiver n. 免責同意書；棄權聲明
Please sign the liability waiver before the trip.
placement n. （實習、服務的）安排、派駐單位
Most of your hours should come from a long-term placement.
reflection n. 反思；心得
Students write a short reflection at the end of the semester.
alternative adj. 替代的；另外可選的
I can arrange an alternative shift on Saturday morning.
impact n. 影響；衝擊
The program has had a positive impact on children's reading.
resident n. 住民；居民
Our residents look forward to your Wednesday visits.
certificate n. 證書；證明
Certificates of service hours will be emailed next week.
coordinator n. 協調人；（活動）負責人
Grace Wu is the program coordinator at the center.

閱讀 Reading

1. All volunteers must sign a liability ______ before joining activities off campus.

  1. receipt
  2. deposit
  3. waiver
  4. margin
看答案與詳解

正解 C　liability waiver 指「免責同意書」，校外活動前須簽署，選 C。receipt 收據、deposit 押金、margin 邊緣，皆不符語意。

2. The tutoring program has had a positive ______ on the reading skills of children in rural areas.

  1. effort
  2. access
  3. appeal
  4. impact
看答案與詳解

正解 D　have a positive impact on 是固定搭配「對……有正面影響」，選 D。effort 努力、access 使用權、appeal 吸引力，都不與 on 搭配表示影響。

From: Kevin Chou, Volunteer Coordinator, Hope Senior Care Center

To: Student Volunteers, Fall Semester

Subject: Schedule update and reminders

Dear volunteers,

Thank you for your hard work this semester. Our residents look forward to your Wednesday visits, and several family members have told us how much they appreciate the conversation and music sessions you lead.

Starting November 18, our Wednesday afternoon session will begin at 2:30 instead of 2:00, because the residents' physical therapy schedule has been extended. The session will still last 90 minutes. If the new time conflicts with your classes, please let me know by November 13 so I can arrange an alternative shift on Saturday morning.

Also, a reminder: please do not bring outside food for residents, as many of them follow strict diets. Instead, feel free to bring board games, photos, or musical instruments. Finally, remember to sign in at the front desk on every visit. Your sign-in record is what we use to confirm your service hours with your university.

Best regards, Kevin Chou

3. Why is the Wednesday session changing?

  1. Volunteers requested a later time
  2. The residents' therapy now takes longer
  3. The front desk is closed until 2:30
  4. Saturday shifts have been canceled
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中說 because the residents' physical therapy schedule has been extended，物理治療時間延長，選 B。週六反而是提供的替代班次（D 錯）。

4. The word "alternative" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. additional
  2. earlier
  3. substitute
  4. permanent
看答案與詳解

正解 C　若新時間與課衝堂，可改排週六的班，alternative 指「替代的」，最接近 substitute，選 C。並非額外加班（additional）。

5. What are volunteers asked NOT to do?

  1. Bring musical instruments
  2. Share personal photos
  3. Sign in at the front desk
  4. Bring snacks for residents
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信中說 please do not bring outside food for residents，因許多長輩有飲食限制，選 D。樂器與照片是鼓勵攜帶的，簽到則是必須做的。

From Required Hours to Real Connections: Students Rethink Service Learning

When Riverside University introduced its service-learning requirement ten years ago, many students saw it as just another box to check. Undergraduates had to complete 36 hours of community service before graduation, and most simply collected hours wherever they could find them: a beach cleanup one weekend, a charity concert the next.

According to a survey released last month by the university's Service-Learning Center, that attitude is changing. Of the 1,200 students who responded, 64 percent said they had stayed with the same partner organization for at least one full semester, up from just 28 percent five years ago. More than half said their service experience had influenced their choice of major or career plans.

Center director Dr. Helen Tsai credits two policy changes. Three years ago, the university began limiting the number of hours students could earn from one-time events. It also added a required reflection report, in which students connect their service to what they learn in class. "When students have to explain what they learned, they start choosing work that actually means something to them," Tsai said.

Not everyone is convinced. Some students argue that long-term placements are difficult to fit around part-time jobs and lab schedules. "I support the idea, but I work twenty hours a week," said Jason Lee, a junior in electrical engineering. "Weekend-only options are limited." In response, the center plans to expand online volunteering, such as remote tutoring for rural schools, starting next spring.

For many participants, though, the benefits are clear. "At first I just wanted the hours," said Amy Lin, a sophomore who tutors at a senior center. "Now I visit even when I don't need to."

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Why the university increased the required service hours
  2. A new scholarship for student volunteers
  3. How students' attitudes toward service learning have changed
  4. Problems at a local senior center
看答案與詳解

正解 C　全文從「只為湊時數」談到調查顯示學生態度轉變及其原因，選 C。時數一直是 36 小時，並未增加（A 錯）。

7. What does Dr. Tsai suggest about the reflection report?

  1. It is too difficult for most students
  2. It encourages students to choose more meaningful service
  3. It replaced the hour requirement
  4. It is required only for one-time events
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Tsai 說學生必須說明學到什麼時，就會選擇對自己有意義的服務，選 B。報告是新增的要求，並未取代時數（C 錯）。

8. Why does Jason Lee mention his part-time job?

  1. To show that he has already finished his hours
  2. To criticize the reflection report
  3. To recommend a weekend event
  4. To explain why long-term placements are hard for him
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第四段指出長期服務難以配合打工，Jason 以每週工作二十小時為例，選 D。他說 I support the idea，並未批評反思報告。

9. Which TWO changes does the article give as reasons for the shift in student attitudes? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Higher pay for student workers
  2. A limit on hours from one-time events
  3. More weekend-only placements
  4. An online tutoring program launched last year
  5. A required reflection report
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　第三段 Tsai 歸功於兩項政策：限制單次活動時數、新增反思報告，選 B、E。線上家教是明年春天才要擴大（D 錯）；週末選項反而有限（C 錯）。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The beach is very clean this time of year.
  2. Not yet, but I'm planning to do it today.
  3. I signed the form for my dorm room.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問是否已報名淨灘，B「還沒，但今天打算報名」最合理。A 重複 beach、clean 但答非所問；C 用 signed 混淆 signed up，內容無關。

聽力原文

Have you signed up for the beach cleanup this Saturday?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. About two hours by bus.
  2. We'll serve lunch at noon.
  3. Thirty-six, I think, but you should check the handbook.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問畢業需要幾小時服務學習，C 回答時數並建議查手冊。A 重複 hours 但講車程；B 用 serve 與 service 音近混淆。

聽力原文

How many service-learning hours do we need to graduate?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. That's a good idea. I enjoy working with kids.
  2. The school is at the end of the street.
  3. Because I volunteered for the campus tour yesterday.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Why don't you...? 是提出建議，A 接受建議最合理。B 重複 school 但講位置；C 把建議誤當成問原因，並用 tour 與 tutoring 音近混淆。

聽力原文

Why don't you volunteer at the elementary school tutoring program?

4. What will the man probably do next?

  1. Send another email to the club president
  2. Register on the volunteer portal
  3. Sort donated food items
  4. Wait for the president to arrive
看答案與詳解

正解 C　女子說會幫他手寫補上名字，並請他到後面房間整理罐頭（sorting cans），故選 C。A、B 都不需要再做；D 對話未提及。

聽力原文

Hi, I'm here for the food bank shift, but I don't see my name on the list. Did you register through the student volunteer portal or by email? By email. I sent it to the club president last week. Ah, email sign-ups are added by hand. Let me write your name in now, and you can start sorting cans in the back room.

5. What does the professor allow the group to do?

  1. Skip the community visit
  2. Submit the report later
  3. Write a shorter introduction
  4. Visit the community on Friday
看答案與詳解

正解 B　教授說 I'll give your group until next Wednesday，即延後繳交報告，選 B。教授只要求在引言中說明變動，並非縮短引言（C）；拜訪改到週六，並未取消（A）。

聽力原文

Professor Lin, our service-learning reflection report is due Friday, but our community visit was moved to Saturday because of the typhoon warning. In that case, I'll give your group until next Wednesday. Just mention the change in your introduction. Thank you. We'll do that.

6. What has changed from the original plan?

  1. The location of the water stations
  2. The way certificates are delivered
  3. The length of the race
  4. The time volunteers should arrive
看答案與詳解

正解 D　廣播說報到時間是 seven fifteen，而非原傳單上的 seven thirty，故選 D。證書以 email 寄送並未說有變更；水站位置與賽程長度都沒提到改變。

聽力原文

Attention, volunteers for tomorrow's charity run. Please arrive at the north gate of the sports field by seven fifteen a.m., not seven thirty as stated on the original flyer. Water station volunteers should pick up their vests and gloves at the student affairs tent. Breakfast will be provided after the race, and certificates of service hours will be emailed by the end of next week.

7. Who is the speaker?

  1. A program coordinator
  2. A professor of social work
  3. A student club president
  4. A nurse at a senior care center
看答案與詳解

正解 A　講者自我介紹 I'm Grace Wu, the program coordinator，選 A。安養中心只是合作單位之一，並非講者身分。

8. What is new this semester?

  1. Students need more total service hours
  2. One-time events can count for only a limited number of hours
  3. The training session is now optional
  4. Students may choose any organization they like
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說單次活動的時數 no more than twelve，即有上限，選 B。總時數仍是三十六小時（A 錯）；訓練是 must attend（C 錯）。

9. According to the speaker, which TWO items are needed before hours are officially recorded? (Choose TWO.)

  1. A letter from a professor
  2. A reflection written by the student
  3. A photo of the service activity
  4. An evaluation form from the supervisor
  5. A visit during office hours
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　講者說學生寫 reflection、督導填 evaluation form，Only when both are submitted 才登錄時數，選 B、D。辦公時間只是有問題時可來詢問，並非必要條件。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to the Service-Learning Center orientation. I'm Grace Wu, the program coordinator. As you know, every undergraduate needs to complete thirty-six hours of service before graduation, but this semester we're making a few changes. First, hours from one-time events, like a single beach cleanup, will now count for no more than twelve of your total hours. We want you to build a real relationship with one partner organization, so most of your hours should come from a long-term placement. Our partners this year include a senior care center, two rural elementary schools that need online English tutors, and a local animal shelter. Before you start, you must attend a two-hour training session and sign a safety agreement. At the end of the semester, you'll write a short reflection, and your supervisor will fill out an evaluation form. Only when both are submitted will your hours be officially recorded. If you have any questions, my office hours are Tuesday and Thursday afternoons.
