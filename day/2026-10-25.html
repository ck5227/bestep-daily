健康中心與流感疫苗

今日單字

vaccine n. 疫苗
The flu vaccine is free for all registered students this year.
symptom n. 症狀
Please report any flu symptoms to the health center online.
appointment n. 預約；約診
I made an appointment with the doctor for this afternoon.
postpone v. 延後
Students with a fever should postpone their vaccination until they recover.
chronic adj. 慢性的；長期的
Tell the nurse if you have a chronic illness such as asthma.
spread v. 散播；傳染
Wear a mask in common areas so you don't spread the virus.
consent form n. 同意書
Students under 20 must upload a signed parental consent form.
sick-leave note n. 病假證明
Ask the doctor for a sick-leave note during your visit.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who feel sick should stay in their dorm rooms to avoid ______ the virus to others.

  1. catching
  2. receiving
  3. spreading
  4. affecting
看答案與詳解

正解 C　spread A to B 表示「把病毒傳給他人」，故選 C。catch 是自己被傳染；receive 與 affect 都不能接 the virus to others 的結構。

2. If you have a ______ illness such as asthma, please tell the nurse before you receive the vaccine.

  1. chronic
  2. sudden
  3. brief
  4. casual
看答案與詳解

正解 A　氣喘屬於長期的「慢性」疾病，chronic illness 為固定搭配，故選 A。sudden 和 brief 意思與氣喘不符，casual 不用來形容疾病。

From: Campus Health Center <health@univ.edu.tw>

To: All Undergraduate Students

Subject: Free Flu Vaccination – Book Your Slot by November 6

Dear Students,

This year, the Health Center is offering free flu vaccines to all registered students. Vaccinations will be given from October 28 to November 13 in Room 105 of the Health Center. To reserve a time, please log in to the student portal and select "Health Services." Online booking closes on November 6.

Before your appointment, you must complete a short health questionnaire on the portal. Students who are currently running a fever will be asked to postpone their vaccination until they have recovered. Please also bring your student ID; without it, we cannot record your vaccination in your health file.

Students who are under 20 years old must upload a signed parental consent form. If you have questions, call extension 2210 or visit the front desk between 9 a.m. and 4 p.m.

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To announce new opening hours of the Health Center
  2. To explain how to treat the flu at home
  3. To ask students to update their health insurance
  4. To invite students to book a free vaccination
看答案與詳解

正解 D　信件主旨與第一段都在說明免費流感疫苗及如何預約，故選 D。信中沒有提到新的營業時間、居家照護或更新保險。

4. The word "postpone" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. cancel
  2. delay
  3. report
  4. repeat
看答案與詳解

正解 B　發燒的學生要等康復後再接種，postpone 意為「延後」，與 delay 相同，故選 B。cancel 是取消，與「康復後再來」不符。

5. What must ALL students do before their appointment?

  1. Fill out a health questionnaire online
  2. Upload a parental consent form
  3. Call the Health Center
  4. Visit the front desk
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信中說所有人預約前「必須」在系統上填寫健康問卷，故選 A。家長同意書只限未滿二十歲者；打電話與到櫃台只是有問題時的選項。

Mental Health Days? University Rethinks Sick Leave

Every autumn, the campus health center gets busier. Last October, it recorded more than 1,800 visits, about 30 percent more than in a typical spring month. Most students came in with colds, sore throats, or the flu. But according to center director Dr. Lin Mei-hua, a growing number arrived with a different problem: stress.

"Around midterms, we see students who haven't slept properly in days," Dr. Lin said. "Some have headaches or stomach problems, but the real cause is anxiety about grades." Under current rules, however, a sick-leave note can only be issued for physical illness, so these students often feel they have no choice but to attend class.

This semester, the Student Association has proposed a change. Under its plan, each student could take up to two "wellness days" per semester without a doctor's note. Students would simply submit a short form online at least one day in advance, and professors would not be allowed to lower participation grades for those absences.

Supporters say the policy would encourage students to rest before small problems become serious ones. "Right now, people come to class sick because they're afraid of losing points," said association president Wu Jia-hao. "That's bad for them and for everyone sitting next to them."

Not everyone is convinced. Some faculty members worry that students will use the days to extend long weekends or skip exams. In response, the proposal excludes exam days and requires students who miss a group presentation to arrange a replacement date with their professor.

The University Council will vote on the plan in December. If approved, it could take effect as early as next spring.

6. What did Dr. Lin notice about recent visits to the health center?

  1. Fewer students came in with the flu.
  2. Most visits happened in the spring.
  3. More students came in because of stress.
  4. Students waited too long to see a doctor.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第一、二段指出林主任發現越來越多學生是因「壓力」前來就診，故選 C。文中說秋季比春季忙，且感冒流感仍是多數，A、B 錯誤；D 沒有提到。

7. Why do some stressed students attend class even when they feel unwell?

  1. They cannot get a sick-leave note for stress.
  2. Their professors require a doctor's visit first.
  3. The health center is closed during midterms.
  4. They want to present with their group.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第二段說目前病假證明只能開給生理疾病，壓力引起的不適無法請假，學生只好去上課，故選 A。其他選項文中並未提到。

8. What is true about the proposed wellness days?

  1. They require a doctor's note.
  2. They can be requested on the same day.
  3. Students may take up to three per semester.
  4. They cannot be used on exam days.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第五段說提案排除考試日，故選 D。第三段指出不需醫師證明、且須至少提前一天申請，A、B 錯；每學期最多兩天而非三天，C 錯。

9. Which TWO concerns or conditions are mentioned regarding the proposal? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Students would lose participation points.
  2. Some teachers fear students will make weekends longer.
  3. The health center would need more staff.
  4. The policy would start this semester.
  5. Students missing a group presentation must reschedule it.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　第五段提到部分教師擔心學生拿來延長週末，且缺席分組報告者須與教授另約時間，故選 B、E。提案明訂不得扣參與分數；人力未提及；最快明年春季才實施。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. At the health center, on the first floor of the Student Union.
  2. I think I caught a cold last week.
  3. Yes, he took a great shot in the last game.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　問校內哪裡能打流感疫苗，A 直接回答地點最合理。B 只用相關的感冒字眼答非所問；C 重複 shot 但指投籃，意思不同。

聽力原文

Where can I get a flu shot on campus?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I didn't see the doctor's car outside.
  2. No, the coffee here is too strong.
  3. I know. I made an appointment for this afternoon.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　對方建議去看醫生，C 表示已預約下午門診，回應最自然。A 重複 see 和 doctor 但意思不符；B 用 coffee 與 cough 發音相近來混淆。

聽力原文

You should see a doctor about that cough.

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The clinic opens at eight thirty.
  2. Yes, and your health insurance card too.
  3. I'd rather study in the library.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問看診是否需帶學生證，B 回答要，並補充也要帶健保卡。A 只講開診時間未回答問題；C 用 study 與 student 字形相近混淆。

聽力原文

Do I need to bring my student ID to the clinic?

4. When will the woman most likely get her vaccine?

  1. Thursday morning
  2. Thursday afternoon
  3. Friday afternoon
  4. Friday morning
看答案與詳解

正解 D　男子說週四已額滿、週五早上有空檔，女子表示週五早上可以，並被告知十點回來，故選 D。週四已滿；週五下午她有實驗課。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to sign up for the free flu vaccine. Is there still space this week? Thursday is fully booked, I'm afraid, but we have openings on Friday morning. Friday morning works. I only have a lab in the afternoon. Great. Just fill out this health form and come back at ten.

5. What does the man suggest the woman do?

  1. Get a note from the health center
  2. Ask a classmate to give the presentation
  3. Go to the economics class anyway
看答案與詳解

正解 A　男子建議她向健康中心申請病假證明，再寫信請教授延到下週報告，故選 A。並未提到請同學代報，也沒有建議她照常上課。

聽力原文

Hey, are you okay? You look really tired. I've had a fever since yesterday, and I have a presentation in my economics class tomorrow. You should ask the health center for a sick-leave note. Then you can email your professor and ask to present next week.

6. What is available at the front desk?

  1. Flu tests
  2. Thermometers
  3. Free masks
  4. Hand sanitizer
看答案與詳解

正解 C　廣播最後說櫃台到週日晚上提供免費口罩，故選 C。乾洗手是放在電梯與共用廚房，不是櫃台；流感快篩與體溫計都沒有提到。

聽力原文

Attention, residents of Dorm Building C. Because several students have reported flu symptoms this week, the dormitory office will place hand sanitizer at every elevator and in all shared kitchens. Residents who have a fever are asked to wear a mask in common areas and to report their symptoms to the health center online. Free masks are available at the front desk until Sunday evening. Thank you for helping keep our community healthy.

7. Why does the speaker mention midterms?

  1. Classes are often canceled during that time.
  2. Students are more likely to get sick then.
  3. The health center closes during exams.
  4. The vaccine is only offered after midterms.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　護理師說期中考時學生睡得少、吃得差，所以生病的人更多，故選 B。其他選項（停課、健康中心關閉、考後才打疫苗）都沒有提到。

8. According to the speaker, why should students not wait to get vaccinated?

  1. The free vaccines will run out soon.
  2. Walk-ins will end next week.
  3. The price will go up in December.
  4. The vaccine needs time to become effective.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者說疫苗大約需要兩週才能完全產生保護力，所以不要拖到十二月，故選 D。並沒有說疫苗會用完或漲價；週三下午的現場接種也沒說會停止。

9. Which TWO things does the speaker say about the campus clinic? (Choose TWO.)

  1. A doctor is there every morning.
  2. It is open on weekends.
  3. Visits are free for all students.
  4. Students should bring their health insurance card.
  5. Sick-leave notes can be requested by email.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　講者說每天早上都有醫師駐診，且出示健保卡看診只要一百五十元，故選 A、D。診所只在平日開放；看診要收費（免費的是疫苗）；病假證明須在看診時當場申請，不能事後補。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to this short health talk. I'm a nurse at the campus health center. As the weather gets colder, we usually see more students with colds and the flu, especially around midterms, when many of you are sleeping less and eating badly. So, first, the flu vaccine. It's free for all registered students this year, but you must book a time through the student portal. Walk-ins are only accepted on Wednesday afternoons. Remember, it takes about two weeks for the vaccine to fully protect you, so please don't wait until December. Second, if you do get sick, our clinic is open from eight thirty to five on weekdays. A doctor is available every morning, and a visit costs only one hundred fifty dollars if you show your health insurance card. If you need a sick-leave note for a class, ask the doctor during your visit. We can't write one later, after you've already recovered. Finally, wash your hands often and stay home if you have a fever. Your classmates will thank you.
