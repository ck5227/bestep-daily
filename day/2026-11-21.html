請假規定與作業遲交

今日單字

waive v. 免除（規定、費用、罰則）
The instructor agreed to waive the late penalty after seeing the repair receipt.
certificate n. 證明書
You must upload a medical certificate if you miss more than one day of class.
extension n. 延期；展延
She asked for a three-day extension on her final project.
penalty n. 處罰；扣分
The penalty for late work is ten percent per day.
absence n. 缺席；缺課
Check your absences on the student portal before the final exam.
make up phr. v. 補做；補考
He was allowed to make up the quiz the following Tuesday.
documented adj. 有文件證明的
Late requests are accepted only for documented emergencies.
course load n. 修課負擔；修習學分量
Her advisor suggested reducing her course load next semester.

閱讀 Reading

1. If you miss the final exam because of illness, you must provide a medical ______ within three days.

  1. certainty
  2. combination
  3. certificate
  4. celebration
看答案與詳解

正解 C　medical certificate 為「診斷證明書」的固定搭配，故選 C。certainty（確定性）字形相近但語意不符；其餘選項無法與 medical 搭配。

2. The professor agreed to ______ the late penalty because the student's flight had been canceled.

  1. wave
  2. weave
  3. wage
  4. waive
看答案與詳解

正解 D　waive the penalty 意為「免除罰則」，符合因班機取消而通融的語意，故選 D。wave（揮手）與 waive 同音，是典型音近干擾；weave、wage 語意不合。

From: Dr. Amy Hsu <amyhsu@university.edu.tw>

To: Students in Introduction to Sociology

Subject: Extension Requests for the Final Project

Dear students,

Several of you have asked about extensions for the final project, which is due on December 18. To keep things fair, I will only consider requests that are submitted through the course website at least five days before the deadline. Please explain your reason briefly and suggest a realistic new date.

Extensions will generally be granted for illness, family emergencies, or schedule conflicts with official university events. Heavy workloads in other courses are understandable, but they are not, by themselves, a valid reason. Requests made after the deadline will not be accepted unless there is a documented emergency.

If your request is approved, you will receive a confirmation email within two working days. Please keep this email, as it serves as your record.

Best regards,

Dr. Amy Hsu

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To change the topic of the final project
  2. To explain how to request an extension for the final project
  3. To announce that the deadline has been moved
  4. To remind students about office hours
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中說明延期申請的方式、期限與可接受的理由，故選 B。C 截止日仍是 12 月 18 日，並未更改；A、D 未提及。

4. Which reason, on its own, will NOT be accepted for an extension?

  1. A family emergency
  2. A conflict with an official university event
  3. A heavy workload in other courses
  4. A documented illness
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段指出其他課程的繁重課業「本身並不是正當理由」，故選 C。A、B、D 皆列為通常會核准的理由。

5. The word "realistic" in paragraph 1 is closest in meaning to

  1. imaginary
  2. official
  3. flexible
  4. achievable
看答案與詳解

正解 D　suggest a realistic new date 指提出「可行、做得到的」新日期，與 achievable 最接近，故選 D。imaginary（想像的）意思相反。

Leave Requests Go Digital, but Something Is Missing

When the Office of Academic Affairs moved all leave requests online two years ago, many students welcomed the change. Instead of chasing signatures from their advisor, department office, and instructor, they could now fill out a single form on their phones. According to the office, the average processing time dropped from five days to less than two. Students who are sick or dealing with a family emergency can even submit a request after they return, as long as they upload supporting documents within a week.

However, a recent survey by the Student Association suggests that the system is not working equally well for everyone. Of the 1,200 students who responded, about 70 percent said the online process was faster. Yet nearly a quarter complained that instructors often approved requests without giving any guidance on missed work. "My leave was approved in an hour, but I still had no idea how to make up the lab," said one third-year chemistry student.

Some faculty members admit the problem. Professor Wu Ming-hui of the History Department says the system makes approval "almost too easy." "In the past, a student had to talk to me in person, so we naturally discussed what they had missed. Now I just click a button," she explained. She now sends a short message to every student whose leave she approves, asking them to visit her office hours within a week.

The Student Association has proposed two improvements: adding a required section where instructors list makeup arrangements, and sending automatic reminders before makeup deadlines. The Office of Academic Affairs says it will test both features in selected departments next semester before deciding whether to use them campus-wide. Until then, students are encouraged to contact their instructors directly as soon as their leave is approved.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. Students prefer paper forms to online leave requests.
  2. The online leave system is faster but has created a communication gap.
  3. Professors want to stop approving leave requests.
  4. The Student Association plans to replace the leave system.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　全文先說線上系統加快速度，再指出老師核准後缺乏補課指引的溝通落差，故選 B。A 與 70% 認為較快相反；D 學生會只提出改善建議，並非取代。

7. What did the chemistry student complain about?

  1. The request took too long to be approved.
  2. The instructor rejected the request.
  3. The form was difficult to use on a phone.
  4. The student did not know how to make up the missed lab.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　學生說假一小時就核准，卻不知道如何補做實驗，故選 D。A 與「一小時核准」相反；B、C 文中未提及。

8. What does Professor Wu suggest about the old system?

  1. It was faster than the online system.
  2. It required fewer signatures.
  3. It naturally led to conversations about missed work.
  4. It was preferred by most students.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　吳教授說過去學生須當面找她，因此自然會討論缺漏的課業，故選 C。A 與第一段處理時間縮短相反；B 舊制反而要收集多個簽名。

9. Which TWO statements are true according to the article? (Choose TWO.)

  1. About 70 percent of the survey respondents found the online process faster.
  2. The online system was introduced last semester.
  3. The proposed features will first be tested in some departments.
  4. All instructors must now meet students in person.
  5. The survey was conducted by the Office of Academic Affairs.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　第二段指出約七成受訪者認為較快（A），末段說新功能下學期先在部分系所試行（C）。B 系統兩年前上線；D 無此規定；E 調查由學生會進行。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The lab is on the third floor, next to the elevator.
  2. Yes, I emailed her and asked to make it up next week.
  3. I'll tell you more about Friday's party later.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　對方問是否已告知教授將缺席週五實驗課，B 表示已寄信並詢問下週補做，最合理。A 重複 lab 但答非所問；C 重複 Friday，與請假無關。

聽力原文

Have you told Professor Lin you'll miss Friday's lab?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I left my notebook in the classroom.
  2. Just call me if you feel better.
  3. You'll need a note from the clinic for anything longer than a day.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問病假是否需要診斷證明，C 直接說明超過一天需診所證明。A 的 notebook 與 note 音近；B 重複 call，但與請假規定無關。

聽力原文

Do I need a doctor's note for sick leave, or is a phone call enough?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Great, that gives me the weekend to revise it.
  2. I pushed the door, but it wouldn't open.
  3. The paper towels are in the cabinet.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　期末報告截止日延到週一，A 回應多了週末可修改，最自然。B 的 pushed 與 pushed back 字面重複；C 的 paper towels 重複 paper，皆為干擾。

聽力原文

The deadline for the term paper has been pushed back to Monday.

4. What should the man do first?

  1. Take the makeup quiz this week
  2. Submit a leave request online with his hospital documents
  3. Ask Ms. Chen to contact his instructor
  4. Return to the hospital for a new certificate
看答案與詳解

正解 B　女子說回校七天內可線上請假並上傳出院文件，之後才由老師決定是否補考，故先做 B。A 補考尚未確定；C、D 對話未提及。

聽力原文

Hi, Ms. Chen. I was in the hospital last week, so I missed the quiz in Economics. I'm sorry to hear that. Did you apply for leave through the online system? Not yet. I wasn't sure whether I could still do that after the fact. You can, within seven days of returning to class. Upload your discharge papers, and your instructor will decide about a makeup quiz.

5. What will happen if Jason shows a repair receipt?

  1. He can submit the report next week.
  2. He will receive full credit no matter when he submits.
  3. He will not lose points for the first late day.
  4. He can rewrite the report with a partner.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　老師說出示維修收據可免除第一天的遲交扣分（waive the penalty for the first day），故選 C。B 過度推論，之後幾天仍會扣分；A、D 未提及。

聽力原文

Jason, your lab report was due yesterday. Are you planning to turn it in? I am. My laptop crashed on Sunday night and I lost half of it. Is there any penalty? Normally it's ten percent off for each day late. But if you can show me a repair receipt, I'll waive the penalty for the first day.

6. What is required for a leave request for an official university event?

  1. Submission at least three working days before the event
  2. A signed note from the coach on the day of the event
  3. A medical certificate from the health center
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播指出公假須於至少三個工作天前提出，並附邀請函或名單，故選 A。B 當天送件不會核准；C 是病假所需文件。

聽力原文

Attention, first-year students. Starting this semester, leave requests for official university events, such as sports competitions or student conferences, must be submitted at least three working days in advance. Requests submitted on the day of the event will not be approved. Please attach the official invitation or team list when you apply. For questions, contact the Office of Student Affairs at extension two two one five.

7. What is the main purpose of the talk?

  1. To announce the final exam schedule
  2. To introduce a new student portal
  3. To explain attendance and leave rules before finals
  4. To recruit students for a health survey
看答案與詳解

正解 C　導師開頭即說要在期末考前說明出缺勤與請假規定，故選 C。A 只提到期末考將近，未公布時程；B 學生系統只是查缺席的工具。

8. According to the speaker, what may happen to a student who misses more than one third of the class hours?

  1. They must repeat the course next summer.
  2. They may not be allowed to take the final exam.
  3. They will automatically lose six points.
  4. They must meet the advisor every week.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說缺課超過三分之一，老師可拒絕讓學生參加期末考，故選 B。C 的 six 是事假時數上限，被挪用成干擾。

9. Which TWO statements are true according to the talk? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Personal leave must be requested before the class.
  2. Sick leave must be requested one week in advance.
  3. Approved leave means assignments are cancelled.
  4. Students with long-term problems may discuss reducing their course load.
  5. Students can take unlimited personal leave.
看答案與詳解

正解 A、D　講者說事假須於課前申請（A），長期問題可早點來談減修（D）。B 病假可事後申請；C 作業不會因請假取消；E 事假每科每學期上限六小時。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. Since final exams are about four weeks away, I'd like to go over our attendance and leave rules before things get busy. First, if you're absent for more than one third of the class hours in any course, the instructor can refuse to let you take the final exam. That rule is strict, so keep track of your own absences on the student portal. Second, there are different types of leave. Sick leave and family emergency leave can be requested after you return, but personal leave must be requested before the class. Personal leave is limited to six class hours per course each semester. Finally, approved leave does not cancel your assignments. You still need to contact your instructor and agree on a new deadline. If you're facing a long-term problem, such as a serious illness, come see me early. We can talk about options like reducing your course load instead of failing a class.
