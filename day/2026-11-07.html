BESTEP 校園英文練習 2026-11-07
研究所推甄與推薦信

今日單字

recommendation letter n. 推薦信
Most programs require two recommendation letters from professors.
recommender n. 推薦人
Give your recommenders at least three weeks to write the letters.
study plan n. 讀書計畫
She rewrote her study plan to focus on machine learning.
remind (sb. of sth.) v. 提醒（某人某事）
Could you remind me of the deadline next week?
incomplete adj. 不完整的
Incomplete applications will not be reviewed.
forward v. 轉寄；轉交
The office will forward your file to the department.
selection n. 篩選；選拔
Only thirty applicants passed the first-stage selection.
permanent adj. 永久的；不變的
Choosing a job now doesn't have to be a permanent decision.

閱讀 Reading

1. Applicants who pass the first ______ will be invited to an interview in early December.

  1. selection
  2. election
  3. collection
  4. reflection
看答案與詳解

正解 A　first-stage selection 指「第一階段篩選」，通過後才參加面試，選 A。election 是選舉、collection 是收集、reflection 是反思，字形相近但語意不合。

2. Professor Huang agreed to write the letter, but she asked me to ______ her of the deadline a week in advance.

  1. remember
  2. remind
  3. recall
  4. review
看答案與詳解

正解 B　remind sb. of sth. 是「提醒某人某事」，選 B。remember、recall 是自己記得或回想，不接 sb. of；review 是複習、審查，皆不合句構。

From: Office of Graduate Admissions

To: All Applicants, 2027 Recommended Admission

Subject: Missing documents and the November 18 deadline

Dear Applicants,

Our records show that more than sixty applications are still incomplete. The most common problems are missing recommendation letters and study plans uploaded in the wrong format. Please note that all files must be in PDF and no larger than five megabytes.

Recommendation letters must be submitted by the recommenders themselves through the online system. Letters sent to us by applicants, in paper or by email, cannot be accepted. You can check the status of each letter under "My Application." If a letter shows "Not Received" after November 14, we recommend contacting your recommender.

The final deadline for all documents is 5 p.m. on Wednesday, November 18. Incomplete applications will not be forwarded to the departments for review. If you have any questions, please contact us at extension 2231 before the deadline.

3. What is the main purpose of this email?

  1. To remind applicants to complete their applications
  2. To announce the results of the interviews
  3. To introduce a new online application system
  4. To ask professors to write more letters
看答案與詳解

正解 A　信中指出仍有六十多份申請不完整，並說明繳件規定與截止時間，目的是提醒申請者補齊資料，選 A。面試結果、新系統皆未提；收件人是申請者而非教授（D）。

4. What is true about recommendation letters?

  1. Applicants may email them to the office.
  2. They must be sent in paper form.
  3. Recommenders must upload them online.
  4. They are due on November 14.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　信中說推薦信須由推薦人本人透過線上系統繳交，選 C。申請者自行寄送（紙本或 email）不予受理，故 A、B 錯；11 月 14 日只是建議聯絡推薦人的時間點，截止日是 18 日（D 錯）。

5. In paragraph 4, the word "forwarded" is closest in meaning to

  1. passed on
  2. paid back
  3. put off
  4. pointed out
看答案與詳解

正解 A　forward 在此指「轉送」，不完整的申請不會轉交各系審查，與 passed on 意思最接近，選 A。paid back 償還、put off 延後、pointed out 指出，皆不合語境。

Grad School or a Job? Seniors Face a Tough Choice

Every November, the fourth-floor computer lab in the library becomes unusually crowded. Many seniors are there finishing their study plans and checking their recommended admission applications, hoping to secure a place in a master's program before spring. But a recent survey by the Student Affairs Office suggests that fewer students are choosing this path than before.

According to the survey of 1,200 seniors, 38 percent plan to apply for graduate school this year, down from 46 percent five years ago. The biggest drop was in engineering and business, where many students said they could find good jobs with a bachelor's degree. "Several companies offered me a position after my internship," said Kevin Tsai, an industrial engineering major. "I can always go back to school later, maybe in a part-time program."

Other students still see a master's degree as necessary. Wen-Ting Lu, a biology senior, explained that most research jobs in her field require at least two years of graduate training. "For me, it's not really a choice," she said. "If I want to work in a lab, I need the degree."

Career counselor Amy Hsieh believes students should think less about trends and more about their own goals. "Some students apply just because their friends are applying," she said. "That's when they struggle in the first year." She suggests that seniors talk with both professors and working graduates before deciding. The Career Center also offers one-on-one sessions to help students compare the costs and benefits of each option.

Whatever they decide, Hsieh reminds seniors that the decision is not permanent. More universities now offer evening and weekend master's programs designed for people who are already working.

6. What does the survey show?

  1. Most seniors now plan to study abroad.
  2. Engineering students prefer graduate school.
  3. The number of seniors has dropped by half.
  4. Fewer seniors plan to apply for graduate school.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第二段指出計畫申請研究所的比例從 46% 降到 38%，選 D。B 與原文相反，工程與商科降幅最大；A、C 未提及。

7. Why does Wen-Ting Lu plan to attend graduate school?

  1. Her friends are all applying.
  2. She did not receive any job offers.
  3. Jobs in her field usually require the degree.
  4. Her professor strongly encouraged her.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　她說她領域的研究工作多半需要至少兩年研究所訓練，想在實驗室工作就需要學位，選 C。A 是諮商師批評的情況；B、D 文中未提。

8. According to Amy Hsieh, when do students often have difficulty in graduate school?

  1. When they apply only because others are applying
  2. When they choose a part-time program
  3. When they work during their first year
  4. When they change their major
看答案與詳解

正解 A　她說有些學生只因朋友申請就跟著申請，That's when they struggle in the first year，選 A。B、C、D 都不是她提出的原因。

9. Which TWO things are mentioned as options for students? Choose TWO.

  1. Joining a company and studying part-time later
  2. Taking a gap year to travel
  3. Getting individual advice at the Career Center
  4. Transferring to another university
  5. Applying for a government scholarship
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　Kevin 提到先就業、日後再讀在職專班（A），職涯中心也提供一對一諮詢（C）。旅遊、轉學、政府獎學金文中皆未提及。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I'd be glad to. When is the deadline?
  2. The graduation ceremony is in June.
  3. I recommend the beef noodles at the cafeteria.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　請老師寫推薦信，A 答應並詢問截止日期，最合理。B 用 graduation 與 graduate 音近混淆；C 重複 recommend 但意思是推薦食物，答非所問。

聽力原文

Could you write me a recommendation letter for my graduate school application?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. My flight is planned for next Monday.
  2. The system crashed during the upload.
  3. Not yet. I'm still revising the second section.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問讀書計畫是否已上傳，C 表示還沒、仍在修改第二部分，最自然。A 重複 plan 但談的是航班；B 重複 system、upload，但說的是系統當機，並未回答自己的進度，且語意不連貫。

聽力原文

Have you uploaded your study plan to the application system yet?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Because the research building is closed today.
  2. That's a good idea. I'll email her this afternoon.
  3. She lives much closer to the campus.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　Why don't you...? 是建議句型，B 接受建議並說下午寄信，最恰當。A 誤把建議當成問原因並重複 research；C 重複 she、much 但與建議無關。

聽力原文

Why don't you ask Professor Lin instead? She knows your research much better.

4. What will the woman most likely do by Monday?

  1. Submit her application online
  2. Send the professor some documents
  3. Meet the professor in his lab
  4. Ask the department to change the deadline
看答案與詳解

正解 B　教授請她週一前寄成績單和讀書計畫草稿，她答應並會附上專題清單，故選 B。截止日是系上改的，她並未要求更改（D）；A、C 對話未提及。

聽力原文

Professor Chen, I'm sorry to bother you, but the department changed the letter deadline. It's now November twentieth instead of the thirtieth. Ten days earlier? That's a bit tight for me this month. Can you send me your transcript and a draft of your study plan by Monday? Of course. I'll also include a list of the projects I did in your lab.

5. What surprised the woman during the interview?

  1. She was asked to speak in English.
  2. The interview was shorter than expected.
  3. The professors did not ask about her project.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　她說被要求用英文說明大學專題，而她沒準備（wasn't ready for that），故選 A。C 與對話相反，教授正是問了專題；B 未提及面試長短。

聽力原文

How did your interview at the Institute of Data Science go? The first part was fine, but then they asked me to explain my undergraduate project in English. I wasn't ready for that. Well, you still answered it, right? A lot of people get nervous in that part.

6. What are students asked to bring to the workshop?

  1. A recommendation letter
  2. Their student ID card
  3. Copies of their résumé
  4. A printed study plan
看答案與詳解

正解 C　公告最後說 Bring two printed copies of your résumé，故選 C。推薦信、讀書計畫雖與推甄相關但公告並未要求攜帶；學生證未提及。

聽力原文

Attention, seniors. The Career Center will hold a mock interview workshop for graduate school applicants this Wednesday at six thirty p.m. in Room two oh five of the Student Activity Center. Faculty members from three colleges will act as interviewers and give you feedback. Space is limited to forty students, so please sign up on the student portal by Tuesday noon. Bring two printed copies of your résumé.

7. Who is the speaker most likely talking to?

  1. New freshmen at orientation
  2. Professors writing letters
  3. Parents of senior students
  4. Students applying to graduate school
看答案與詳解

正解 D　開頭說 many of you are applying to graduate programs this month，聽眾是準備申請研究所的學生，選 D。演講對象不是寫信的教授（B），也未提新生或家長。

8. According to the speaker, what kind of recommender is usually better?

  1. A professor who is well known in the field
  2. A teacher who knows the student's work well
  3. A department chair with a high position
  4. A former classmate now in graduate school
看答案與詳解

正解 B　講者說與其找不熟的名教授，不如找指導過你專題或小班授課、了解你的老師，選 B。A 正是講者說「通常較沒用」的選項；C、D 未提及。

9. What does the speaker advise students to do? Choose TWO.

  1. Write the recommendation letter themselves
  2. Ask recommenders at least three weeks in advance
  3. Apply to no more than three programs
  4. Avoid contacting recommenders after the deadline
  5. Enter recommenders' email addresses in the system early
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　講者建議至少在截止前三週提出請求（B），並及早在系統輸入推薦人信箱（E）。A、C 未提及；D 與原文相反，講者說可禮貌提醒，結果出來後也要感謝並告知去向。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone. Since many of you are applying to graduate programs this month, I want to talk about recommendation letters. First, choose your recommenders carefully. A short letter from a famous professor who barely knows you is usually less useful than a detailed letter from a teacher who supervised your project or taught you in a small class. Second, give your recommenders enough time. I suggest asking at least three weeks before the deadline. When you ask, prepare a simple information sheet: the programs you are applying to, the deadlines, your transcript, and two or three specific things you did in their class or lab. This helps them write concrete examples instead of general praise. Finally, remember that most online systems send the request to your recommender only after you enter their email address. So please do that early, and check the system a few days before the deadline to make sure every letter has arrived. A polite reminder is fine. And after the results come out, don't forget to thank them and let them know where you will study.
