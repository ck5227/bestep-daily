Wait for the applause before letting late guests in.

The photography exhibition will stay open for an extra week.

Not everyone was enthusiastic about the idea at first.
funding n. 資金；經費
The office will provide funding for a better sound system.

閱讀 Reading

1. Students who want to perform in the year-end talent show must ______ a short video of their act by December 5.

  1. attend
  2. refuse
  3. submit
  4. respond
看答案與詳解

正解 C　想參加才藝表演者須在期限前「繳交」表演影片，submit 最合適。attend 參加、refuse 拒絕、respond 回應（需接 to），語意或用法皆不符。

2. The photography exhibition was so ______ that the gallery decided to keep it open for an extra week.

  1. popular
  2. temporary
  3. nervous
  4. careful
看答案與詳解

正解 A　展覽因「很受歡迎」而延長一週，popular 符合因果。temporary 暫時的與延展相矛盾；nervous、careful 用來形容人。

From: University Arts Center <artscenter@campus.edu.tw>

To: All registered students

Subject: Free Pottery Workshop – Limited Spots

Dear Students,

To celebrate the end of the semester, the Arts Center will host a free pottery workshop on Saturday, December 12, from 2 p.m. to 5 p.m. in Room 108 of the Fine Arts Building. No experience is needed. An instructor from a local studio will guide you step by step as you make your own cup or small bowl.

Because we have only twenty pottery wheels, spaces are limited. Please register through the student portal by December 8. Participants should wear clothes they don't mind getting dirty. Finished pieces need about two weeks to dry and be fired in a kiln, so you can collect yours from the Arts Center office after the winter break begins.

If you register but can no longer attend, please cancel at least two days in advance so that someone on the waiting list can take your place.

Best regards, Arts Center Team

3. What is the main purpose of the email?

  1. To recruit pottery instructors
  2. To announce a free workshop
  3. To sell student artwork
  4. To explain new rules for the Fine Arts Building
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信件開頭即宣布藝文中心將舉辦免費陶藝工作坊，並說明報名方式，故選 B。講師來自校外工作室，並非招募；未提販售作品或大樓規定。

4. When can participants pick up their finished pieces?

  1. At the end of the workshop
  2. Before December 8
  3. Two days after the workshop
  4. After the winter break begins
看答案與詳解

正解 D　第二段說作品需約兩週乾燥與燒製，可在寒假開始後到辦公室領取，故選 D。December 8 是報名截止日；two days 是取消的提前時間。

5. The word "fired" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to

  1. dismissed
  2. heated
  3. shot
  4. praised
看答案與詳解

正解 B　fired in a kiln 指放進窯裡「燒製」，即高溫加熱，故選 B。dismissed（解雇）、shot（射擊）是 fire 的其他意思，在此不符。

Lunchtime Concerts Turn the Library Lobby into a Stage

Every Wednesday at twelve-thirty this semester, the quiet lobby of the main library has turned into a small concert hall. The series, called Music at Noon, was started by Chen Yu-ting, a third-year music major who noticed that many students skipped the evening concerts at the Arts Center. "Students are busy at night with part-time jobs and club meetings," she explained. "But almost everyone walks past the library at lunchtime."

Each concert lasts only thirty minutes. Performers so far have included a string quartet, a Taiwanese folk band, a jazz pianist, and even a group of engineering students who play traditional Chinese instruments. Audience members can sit on the floor or simply stop and listen on their way to class. Food is not allowed in the lobby, but many students bring a drink and stay for the whole show.

Not everyone was enthusiastic at first. Some library staff worried that the music would disturb students studying on the upper floors. To solve this problem, the organizers agreed to keep the volume low, avoid drums, and finish by one o'clock. Library director Wang Mei-ling says she has received no complaints since the second week. "In fact, our visitor numbers on Wednesdays have gone up by about fifteen percent," she added.

The series has been so successful that the Student Affairs Office will provide funding next semester to pay performers a small fee and buy a better sound system. Chen hopes to add a monthly open-mic session so that students who have never performed in public can give it a try. Students interested in performing can apply through the Music at Noon page on the student portal.

6. Why did Chen Yu-ting start Music at Noon?

  1. To raise money for the Arts Center
  2. Because the library asked her to plan events
  3. Because many students missed evening concerts due to busy schedules
  4. To prepare for her own graduation recital
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第一段說她發現許多學生晚上忙打工與社團而錯過晚間音樂會，故選 C。文中未提募款、圖書館邀請或畢業音樂會。

7. What concern did some library staff have at first?

  1. The music might bother people studying upstairs.
  2. Students would leave food in the lobby.
  3. Too few people would attend.
  4. Performers would want to be paid.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　第三段說部分館員擔心音樂會 disturb students studying on the upper floors，故選 A。禁止飲食是規定而非館員的疑慮；其餘未提。

8. According to the article, what will happen next semester?

  1. The concerts will move to the Arts Center.
  2. The series will be held every day.
  3. Drums will be allowed in the lobby.
  4. Performers will receive a small fee.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　最後一段說學務處下學期將提供經費 to pay performers a small fee，故選 D。文中未說移地、天天舉辦或開放鼓組。

9. Which of the following did the organizers agree to do? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Keep the volume low
  2. Charge a small entrance fee
  3. End the concerts by one o'clock
  4. Hold an open-mic session every week
  5. Move the concerts to the evening
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　第三段主辦方同意 keep the volume low, avoid drums, and finish by one o'clock，故選 A、C。open-mic 是陳同學「希望」每月舉辦，並非每週；未提收費或改晚上。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The concert hall is next to the library.
  2. Only a few, so you'd better book them today.
  3. I'd rather sit on the left side.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問週五管弦樂音樂會是否還有票，B 回答只剩幾張、最好今天訂，最合理。A 答地點；C 以 left（左邊）混淆 left（剩下），答非所問。

聽力原文

Are there still tickets left for Friday's orchestra concert?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. Someone from the design club volunteered.
  2. It's on display until next Sunday.
  3. I posted it on the website this morning.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Who 問人，A 說設計社有人自願負責，最合理。B 答展期；C 以 posted 混淆 poster，皆答非所問。

聽力原文

Who's designing the poster for the art exhibition?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. The play was about two sisters.
  2. I've seen that theater from the bus.
  3. No need. Our seats are already reserved.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　問是否該早點到劇場搶好位子，C 說不必、座位已預訂，最合理。A 談劇情；B 重複 theater 但無關。

聽力原文

Shouldn't we get to the theater early to find good seats?

4. What is true about the roommate's ticket?

  1. It will be free.
  2. It will cost the full price.
  3. It must be picked up on Saturday.
  4. It is already sold out.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　室友不是本校學生，男生說 her ticket will be the regular price，故選 B。學生才享半價，對話未提免費、取票時間或售完。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like two tickets for the dance performance on Saturday night. Is there a student discount? Yes, it's half price, but each ticket holder has to show a valid student ID at the door. Oh, my roommate isn't a student here. She works downtown. Then her ticket will be the regular price, four hundred dollars.

5. Why is the choir changing its rehearsal plans?

  1. The concert was moved to Thursday.
  2. The music building is closed for repairs.
  3. Several choir members have an exam on Thursday.
  4. Their usual room will be used for something else.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　男生說原本的排練教室週四要用來辦考試複習，故選 D。音樂會在下週；音樂館仍有空教室；exam review 是借用教室的活動，並非團員要考試。

聽力原文

Lisa, the choir's usual rehearsal room is being used for an exam review session on Thursday. Really? Our concert is next week. Can we practice in the music building instead? I already asked. They have a practice room free on Wednesday evening. That works. Let's tell everyone in the group chat.

6. What has changed about the concert?

  1. Its start time
  2. Its location
  3. Its ticket price
看答案與詳解

正解 B　廣播說音樂會因大雨由大草坪改到學生活動中心，故選 B。開始時間仍是七點半；票價未提及。

聽力原文

Attention, everyone. Tonight's jazz concert on the main lawn has been moved indoors to the Student Activity Center, because heavy rain is expected this evening. The concert will still begin at seven thirty. Please note that food and drinks are not allowed inside the hall. Thank you.

7. Who is the speaker most likely talking to?

  1. Ticket buyers
  2. Music professors
  3. Student volunteers
  4. Professional musicians
看答案與詳解

正解 C　開頭感謝大家 volunteering with the University Arts Center，並說明帶位工作與服務學習時數，對象是學生志工，故選 C。

8. What should ushers do when a guest arrives late?

  1. Seat the guest in the back row right away.
  2. Send the guest to the ticket office.
  3. Ask the guest to come back for a later show.
  4. Let the guest in during the applause between pieces.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　講者說 wait until the applause between pieces before you let them in，故選 D。其餘做法皆未提及。

9. What benefits does the speaker mention for volunteers? (Choose TWO.)

  1. Free tickets to a spring show
  2. Payment for each shift
  3. Hours that count toward a requirement
  4. A free meal before each show
  5. Priority seating at the concerts
看答案與詳解

正解 A、C　做滿四場可獲春季音樂劇免費票兩張，時數也可抵服務學習，故選 A、C。未提酬勞、餐點或優先座位。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, and thank you for volunteering with the University Arts Center this semester. As you know, our year-end concert season starts next Friday, with six performances over two weeks. Most of you will work as ushers. That means checking tickets, helping guests find their seats, and making sure the aisles stay clear. Please arrive forty-five minutes before each show. You'll receive a black volunteer T-shirt today, and we ask that you wear it with dark pants so the audience can easily recognize you. During the performance, don't use your phones, even to check messages. If a guest arrives late, wait until the applause between pieces before you let them in. Finally, every volunteer who works at least four shows will get two free tickets to the spring musical, and your hours also count toward the service-learning requirement. Before you leave today, please sign up for your shifts on the sheet by the door.
