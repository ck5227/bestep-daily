今日單字

extend v. 延長；擴大
The library has extended its opening hours during exam week.
overdue adj. 逾期的
You can't borrow anything new while you have an overdue book.
renew v. 續借；更新
You can renew your books online twice.
reserve v. 預約；保留
I'll reserve a group study room for Thursday.
interlibrary loan n. 館際合作借閱
I got the article through interlibrary loan in four days.
circulation desk n. 流通櫃台（借還書處）
Laptops can be borrowed at the circulation desk.
pilot program n. 試辦計畫
The 24-hour study zone is part of a six-week pilot program.
respondent n. （問卷）受訪者
Nearly 70 percent of respondents said the library closed too early.

閱讀 Reading

1. Please remember to ______ your books on time, or you will have to pay a late fee.

  1. remind
  2. repeat
  3. return
  4. replace
看答案與詳解

正解 C　「準時歸還書籍，否則要付逾期費」，return（歸還）最符合。remind 提醒、repeat 重複、replace 替換，語意不通。

2. The library has ______ its opening hours during exam week, so it now stays open until midnight.

  1. expired
  2. excluded
  3. exhausted
  4. extended
看答案與詳解

正解 D　後半句說現在開到午夜，表示開放時間「延長」了，選 extended。expired 過期、excluded 排除、exhausted 耗盡，皆不合語境。

From: Circulation Services <circ@lib.hsinhua-u.edu.tw>

To: Kevin Lin <s1120457@hsinhua-u.edu.tw>

Subject: Your reserved item is ready for pickup

Dear Kevin,

The book you reserved, Research Methods for the Social Sciences (3rd edition), has been returned and is now being held for you at the circulation desk on the first floor. Please pick it up by Monday, October 26. After that date, your hold will be cancelled and the book will be offered to the next student on the waiting list.

Remember to bring your student ID card. If a friend is picking up the book for you, he or she will need your ID card as well as a note signed by you. Please also note that reserved items cannot be renewed, so the loan period for this book is 14 days instead of the usual 30.

Best regards,

Circulation Services, Hsin-Hua University Library

3. Why was this email sent?

  1. To remind Kevin that a book is overdue
  2. To tell Kevin that a book he requested is available
  3. To ask Kevin to join a waiting list
  4. To inform Kevin about an unpaid fine
看答案與詳解

正解 B　信中說他預約的書已歸還並在櫃台為他保留，故選 B。信件未提到逾期、罰款，也不是邀他排隊候補。

4. What will happen if Kevin does not pick up the book by October 26?

  1. He will have to pay a fee.
  2. The library will mail the book to him.
  3. Another student may borrow it.
  4. His loan period will be shortened.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第一段指出逾期未取，保留會取消並提供給候補名單上的下一位學生，故選 C。A、B 未提及；借期 14 天是因預約書不能續借，與是否準時取書無關。

5. The word "held" in paragraph 1 is closest in meaning to

  1. carried
  2. organized
  3. delayed
  4. kept
看答案與詳解

正解 D　being held for you 指「為你保留」，與 kept 意思最接近。carried 拿著、organized 整理、delayed 延誤，都不符合上下文。

Library Tests 24-Hour Study Zone

Starting next Monday, Hsin-Hua University Library will open a 24-hour study zone on the first floor as part of a six-week pilot program. The decision follows a student survey last spring in which nearly 70 percent of respondents said the library closed too early, especially during exam periods.

The new zone, which used to be the periodicals area, has been redesigned with 120 seats, extra power outlets, and brighter lighting. Between 10 p.m. and 8 a.m., the rest of the building will be closed, and students will enter the zone through a separate door on the east side by tapping their student ID cards. A security guard will be on duty all night, and cameras have been installed near the entrance.

Library director Dr. Wen-Ting Chou said the pilot is meant to test whether the service is worth the cost. "Keeping any space open overnight means paying for staff, security, and electricity," she explained. "If fewer than 40 students use the zone on an average night, we will have to reconsider."

Not everyone is convinced. Some students worry that the zone will become noisy late at night, and a few professors have raised concerns about students' sleep. In response, the library has made the zone a silent area: phone calls and group discussions are not allowed, and food is limited to snacks in sealed containers.

Students are encouraged to share feedback through a short online form, which will be available until the end of the pilot. The library will announce in December whether the zone will become permanent.

6. What is the article mainly about?

  1. A trial of an overnight study area at the library
  2. The results of a survey about library books
  3. A new security system for campus buildings
  4. Professors' complaints about student sleep
看答案與詳解

正解 A　全文介紹圖書館為期六週的 24 小時自習區試辦計畫，故選 A。問卷、保全與教授的擔憂都只是文中的細節。

7. How will students enter the study zone at night?

  1. Through the main entrance with a guard's permission
  2. By booking a seat online in advance
  3. Through a side door using their ID cards
  4. By showing a special pass from the director
看答案與詳解

正解 C　第二段說夜間學生要從東側的獨立入口刷學生證進入，故選 C。文中沒有提到需經警衛同意、線上訂位或特別通行證。

8. What will most likely happen if an average of 30 students use the zone each night?

  1. The zone will add more seats.
  2. The library may reconsider the program.
  3. The zone will become permanent in December.
  4. The library will close earlier during exams.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　館長表示若平均每晚少於 40 人使用就必須重新考慮，30 人低於門檻，故選 B。A、C 與此推論相反，D 未提及。

9. Which TWO rules apply in the study zone? Choose TWO.

  1. Group discussions are allowed after midnight.
  2. Phone calls are not permitted.
  3. Students may bring full meals.
  4. Only snacks in sealed containers are allowed.
  5. Students must leave by 2 a.m.
看答案與詳解

正解 B、D　第四段說自習區禁止講電話與小組討論，食物限密封容器裝的點心，故選 B、D。A 與規定相反；C 不能帶正餐；E 該區是 24 小時開放。

聽力 Listening

聽力音檔請到線上作答頁播放；以下為題目選項，聽力原文在詳解中。

1. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I returned it late last week.
  2. Yes, until midnight through Friday.
  3. The midterm is in Room three oh five.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　問題詢問期中考週圖書館是否開到很晚，B 直接回答「開到午夜」。A 重複 late 但答非所問；C 重複 midterm 卻在講考場。

聽力原文

Do you know if the library stays open late during midterm week?

2. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. On the second floor, next to the copy machines.
  2. Sure, the lecture notes were helpful.
  3. Yes, that's a really nice print.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　Where 問地點，A 回答「二樓影印機旁」。B 重複 lecture notes 但沒回答地點；C 用 print 當名詞（印花／版畫），屬同字異義陷阱。

聽力原文

Where can I print my lecture notes in the library?

3. （請選出最適當的回應）

  1. I booked a flight for Thursday.
  2. Because it's too quiet in there.
  3. Good idea. I'll reserve one online now.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　Why don't we… 是提議，C 表示同意並馬上線上預約。A 重複 book 和 Thursday 但內容無關；B 把提議誤當成詢問原因。

聽力原文

Why don't we book a group study room for Thursday?

4. Why can't the man borrow the book?

  1. The book is reserved for another student.
  2. His student ID card has expired.
  3. The library is about to close.
  4. He has a book that is past its due date.
看答案與詳解

正解 D　館員說他有一本上週一就到期的統計課本尚未歸還，即逾期，故選 D。對話未提到預約、學生證過期或閉館。

聽力原文

Hi, I'd like to borrow this book, but the machine says I can't. Let me check. It looks like you still have a statistics textbook that was due last Monday. Oh no, I completely forgot about that. Can I bring it back tomorrow? Sure. But you won't be able to check out anything new until it's returned and the late fee is paid.

5. What does the man suggest the woman do?

  1. Visit another university's library in person.
  2. Submit a request on the library website.
  3. Ask her professor for a copy.
  4. Buy a subscription to the journal.
看答案與詳解

正解 B　男生建議使用館際合作，在圖書館網站填寫申請表，故選 B。文章是由他校提供，不需親自前往（A）；教授與訂閱都沒提到。

聽力原文

I need a journal article for my report, but our library doesn't subscribe to that journal. You can use the interlibrary loan service. Just fill out the request form on the library website, and they'll get a copy from another university. How long does that usually take? About three to five working days, so I'd send the request soon.

6. What is the main purpose of the announcement?

  1. To inform students about a temporary closure.
  2. To announce new permanent opening hours.
  3. To explain how to reserve a quiet room.
看答案與詳解

正解 A　廣播說四樓週六因更換地毯暫時關閉，並建議改用一樓閱覽室，故選 A。開放時間並非永久更改，也沒有說明預約方式。

Attention, please. The library's fourth floor will be closed this Saturday from nine a.m. to three p.m. for carpet replacement. Students who need a quiet place to study may use the reading room on the first floor, which will stay open until ten p.m. We apologize for any inconvenience.

7. Who is the talk mainly for?

  1. Graduate students writing theses
  2. New and international students
  3. Library staff in training
  4. Writing Center tutors
看答案與詳解

正解 B　開頭即說這是給大一新生與國際生的圖書館導覽，故選 B。其他身分都未被提及為聽眾。

8. What does the speaker say about the Writing Center?

  1. It charges a small fee.
  2. It is located on the first floor.
  3. It gets busy before final exams.
  4. It only helps with Chinese papers.
看答案與詳解

正解 C　講者說預約在期末前很快額滿（fill up quickly before finals），故選 C。預約免費、位在三樓、協助英文報告，所以 A、B、D 皆錯。

9. According to the speaker, which TWO things can students do? Choose TWO.

  1. Borrow books for sixty days
  2. Renew books online
  3. Print documents for free
  4. Get a chat reply within one hour
  5. Borrow a laptop for a few hours
看答案與詳解

正解 B、E　講者提到書可線上續借兩次，且流通櫃台可借筆電最多四小時，故選 B、E。借期是三十天不是六十天；線上諮詢是一個工作天內回覆；免費列印未提及。

聽力原文

Good afternoon, everyone, and welcome to the library orientation for first-year and international students. I'm Grace, one of the reference librarians. First, your student ID card is also your library card, so you don't need to register separately. Undergraduates can borrow up to twenty books for thirty days, and you can renew them online twice, as long as no one else has reserved them. Second, many students don't realize we offer more than books. On the third floor, you'll find the Writing Center, where tutors can help you organize an English paper. Appointments are free, but they fill up quickly before finals, so book early. We also lend laptops and phone chargers for up to four hours at the circulation desk. Finally, if you can't find a source, please don't give up. Use the chat box on our website, and a librarian will reply within one working day. Now, let's walk over to the self-checkout machines.
